# Community-only Sweep

A **separate** research sweep, started 2026-09-28.

**Only one thing counts here: the community.** We ignore water, climate, mountains, sea, distances, land prices and legal van/yurt rules on purpose. This way we see places the main search filtered out.

Results stay **here**. They are **not** mixed into [`../shortlist.md`](../shortlist.md) or [`../places/`](../places/).

## What counts (community must-haves)

From [`../us/profile.md`](../us/profile.md), community part only:

| # | Criterion | Weight | What a 5 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **People**: spiritual, do inner work, but grounded. No show-off | 3 | Many kind, aware people; yoga, meditation, healing, but normal family life |
| C2 | **Projects to join**: retreats, eco farms, healing, education, cooperatives | 3 | Many small projects, some take volunteers or new members |
| C3 | **Young families + schooling**: kids nearby, alternative / free / Waldorf / forest schools, home-school groups | 3 | Many families; 2+ alternative schools or an active home-school network; home schooling legal |
| C4 | **Integration with locals**: newcomers live in the normal village, locals are kind, traditions alive | 3 | Newcomers and locals mix at markets, festivals, school, work |
| C5 | **Like-minded neighbors nearby**: density of newcomers around one hub | 2 | Dozens of households within 20–40 min |
| C6 | **Open door**: easy for newcomers to arrive and get to know people (events, market, Workaway, co-working, groups) | 2 | Weekly market, regular events, active online groups |

**Total** = sum of (score × weight). **Max = 80.**

## Deal-breakers (still apply)

- Closed intentional community, or strong guru structure.
- Show-off / performative spiritual scene.
- No real contact with local people.

## Flags (note, don't drop)

- 🏫 Home schooling legal status (see [legal overview](../regions/legal-overview.md)).
- 🫧 Strong expat bubble.
- 🏘️ Place is an intentional community itself (OK only if it is **open** and **integrated** with the area).
