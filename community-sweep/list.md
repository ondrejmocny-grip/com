## What this sweep tells us

1. **The UK and France have the richest community scenes.** But the UK needs a visa, and France has strict home-schooling rules and needs French.
2. **Many strong places are cold or rainy** (Millevaches, Bretagne, Wendland, Ireland, Czechia). The main search filtered them out.
3. **Czechia is the real new find:** legal home schooling, parent-run schools, forest clubs, parents nearby. But it is Czech families, not international, and less spiritual.
4. **Iberia keeps its top places** (Órgiva, Benfeita, São Luís). No new big hub was found there.
5. **Turkey has good people but always fails on schools.** Urla is the only rural + alternative-school mix.
6. **In both lists:** Órgiva, Benfeita, São Luís, Diois, West Cork, Totnes, Kaz Dağları. They are strong on community and also passed the other filters (at least partly).
