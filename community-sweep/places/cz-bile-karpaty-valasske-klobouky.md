# Bílé Karpaty: Valašské Klobouky – Hostětín – Uherský Brod (Czechia)

- **Status:** lead
- **Hub town / area:** Valašské Klobouky, Uherský Brod, Bojkovice / Hostětín, Moravské Kopanice (Starý Hrozenkov, Žítková), Březová
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** no

## In short
East Moravia. **Living folk traditions**, meadows and orchards, a famous **eco-village project (Hostětín)**, many eco farms, and **parent-run community schools** for home-schooled kids. Strong for integration with locals. Fewer "spiritual newcomers" than Český ráj, *but a real earth-based, folk-healer culture (Žítková "bohyně"). Guess on how alive this is today.*

## People (C1)
- Earth-based culture: traditions, orchards, old fruit varieties, "Tradice Bílých Karpat" network of farmers and crafts people. [tradicebk.cz](https://www.tradicebk.cz/cs/drzitel/centrum-veronica-hostetin)
- Žítková is known as the land of the **"žítkovské bohyně"** (folk healers). [Veronica](https://hostetin.veronica.cz/rodinna-dovolena-turisticke-zazitky-v-bilych-karpatech-kraji-zitkovskych-bohyni)
- *Guess:* yoga / meditation scene is small; people are more "eco + folk" than "spiritual".

## Families and schooling (C3)
- Alternative schools ([alternativniskoly.net](https://www.alternativniskoly.net/zlinsky/), read 2026-09-28):
  - **Komunitní škola Pomněnka**, Valašské Klobouky – started 2015 by parents from forest club **Vrběnka**. ~30 kids, grades 1–5, 3,500 CZK / month. Kids are in *individuální vzdělávání*. [regionvalassko.cz](https://www.regionvalassko.cz/v-komunitni-skole-se-neznamkuje-jak-probiha-vyuka/), [FB](https://www.facebook.com/KomunitniSkolaValasskeKlobouky/)
  - **komuNITKA**, Uherský Brod – community group for home-schoolers. [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rndr-blanka-svobodov%C3%A1/)
  - **Školy Březová ("školy na konci světa")** – village school with ~1,376 pupils, known for **distance learning** for Czech kids at home or abroad. Useful for world schooling. [Seznam Zprávy](https://www.seznamzpravy.cz/clanek/skola-z-jineho-vesmiru-na-konci-sveta-tady-se-uci-sest-let-dalkove-111096?noredirect=1), [skolybrezova.eu](https://www.skolybrezova.eu/o-nas-2)
  - Nearby: ZŠ Na Dědině (Ostrožská Nová Ves), Komunitní škola Sova (Slušovice), Waldorf ZŠ Jasan (Želechovice near Zlín). [alternativniskoly.net](https://www.alternativniskoly.net/zlinsky/)
- Home schooling legal? ✅ Czechia (§ 41). [§ 41](https://www.pracepropravniky.cz/zakony/skolsky-zakon-uplne-zneni/paragraf-41/)

## Integration with locals (C4)
- Pomněnka kids take part in **Fašanky** (carnival) with the town. [nasevalassko.info](https://www.nasevalassko.info/foto-video/videogalerie/item/6690-val-klobouky-fasanky-13-2-2018-detsky-lesni-klub-vrbenka-komunitni-skola-pomnenka.html)
- Eco projects in Hostětín were pushed by the **village office itself** (since the 1990s). Locals and eco people are the same people here. [Veronica](https://hostetin.veronica.cz/obec-hostetin)
- Traditions (costumes, music, fruit) are strong. *Guess:* newcomers are welcome if they join village life.
