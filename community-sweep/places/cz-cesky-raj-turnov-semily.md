# Český ráj: Turnov – Semily – Malá Skála (Czechia)

- **Status:** lead
- **Hub town / area:** Turnov and Semily (Liberec region), villages around Malá Skála, Rovensko, Radostná pod Kozákovem
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** no

## In short
Maybe the **densest Waldorf / alternative-school scene in rural Czechia**. A state Waldorf school in Semily (grades 1–9 plus secondary), a parent-run Waldorf community school with ~76 kids, and many forest clubs. Families move here for the schools. About 1 h from Prague, ~1.5 h from Ondrej's family *(guess, depends where they live)*.

## People (C1)
- Many parents follow **Waldorf / anthroposophy** ideas (art, crafts, seasons, festivals). Source: [Lesves](https://lesves.cz/).
- Yoga and meditation classes exist in Turnov (e.g. Jogajitka). [skvelecesko.cz](https://www.skvelecesko.cz/joga/turnov)
- *Guess:* the scene is more "Czech alternative family" than "international spiritual". Grounded, not show-off.

## Families and schooling (C3)
- Alternative schools (list checked 2026-09-28, [alternativniskoly.net](https://www.alternativniskoly.net/liberecky/)):
  - **Waldorfská ZŠ a SŠ Semily** – state Waldorf school, primary and secondary. [ČŠI](https://portal.csicr.cz/School/600099296)
  - **Lesves** – Waldorf primary school in Turnov (26 kids) + community school in Malá Skála (**76 kids, grades 1–9**, fee 4,000 CZK / month). Started 2021 because Semily school was full. [lesves.cz](https://lesves.cz/)
  - Waldorf kindergartens in **Turnov, Semily, Rovensko pod Troskami**.
  - Community schools V Roubence (Radostná), Lesves; forest clubs in Sedmihorky, Semily, Lomnice.
- Home-school groups: the Lesves community school **is** a home-school group (kids are in *individuální vzdělávání*). [lesves.cz](https://lesves.cz/)
- Home schooling legal? ✅ Czechia: *individuální vzdělávání* (§ 41). Parent needs maturita (grades 1–5) or a degree (6–9). Exams twice a year. [§ 41](https://www.pracepropravniky.cz/zakony/skolsky-zakon-uplne-zneni/paragraf-41/)

## Integration with locals (C4)
- Newcomers live in normal towns and villages. The Waldorf school in Semily is a **public (state) school**, so alternative and local families mix. [ČŠI](https://portal.csicr.cz/School/600099296)
- The city of Turnov has run a Waldorf kindergarten for a long time. This shows local support. [lesves.cz/zs](https://lesves.cz/zs/)
