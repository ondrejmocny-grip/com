# Jindřichovice pod Smrkem, Frýdlantsko (Czechia, near PL border)

- **Status:** lead
- **Hub town / area:** Jindřichovice pod Smrkem, Frýdlant, Nové Město pod Smrkem; across the border Jelenia Góra (PL, *~1 h, guess*)
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** no

## In short
A small border village that became a **hub for free schooling / unschooling** and eco life: a free school (Ronja), an unschooling "laboratory" (SvobodUm), a living open-air museum (Lunaria), wind turbines and eco houses. A real, small scene. Cold, remote north — the old filter would drop it.

## People (C1)
- Self-directed learning people, eco builders, crafts people. SvobodUm offers yoga, singing, dancing, talks. [svobodauceni.cz](https://www.svobodauceni.cz/svobodum/)
- Parents did **nonviolent communication** training in the cross-border project. [Liberecký deník 2023-01-15](https://liberecky.denik.cz/ctenar-reporter/svobodna-skola-ronja-z-jindrichovic-ma-za-sebou-velmi-pestre-mesice-20230115.html)
- Actor **Jaroslav Dušek** (well-known Czech spiritual public figure) built a superadobe house here. [Českolipský deník](https://ceskolipsky.denik.cz/zpravy_region/jindrichovice-nejsou-jen-vetrne-7491.html). *Guess:* this can pull fans and a little "celebrity" feeling. Check in person.

## Families and schooling (C3)
- **Svobodná škola Ronja** – Sudbury / Summerhill style, ~20 kids aged 5–14 (2019). Not a registered school: kids are home-schooled by law. [frydlantsko.eu 2019](https://www.frydlantsko.eu/2019/12/16/rebelska-skola-jindrichovicich-smrkem/), [Liberecký deník](https://liberecky.denik.cz/ctenar-reporter/svobodna-skola-ronja-z-jindrichovic-ma-za-sebou-velmi-pestre-mesice-20230115.html)
- **SvobodUm** – unschooling stays for families. [svobodauceni.cz](https://www.svobodauceni.cz/svobodum/)
- Cross-border: **Mandala Wolna Szkoła Demokratyczna**, Jelenia Góra (PL) – organized home education Tue–Thu. [odk.jgora.pl](https://odk.jgora.pl/mandala-wolna-szkola/)
- Liberec region has many more (Sudbury school Rádlo, Waldorf classes Liberec). [alternativniskoly.net](https://www.alternativniskoly.net/liberecky/)
- Home schooling legal? ✅ Czechia (§ 41) [§ 41](https://www.pracepropravniky.cz/zakony/skolsky-zakon-uplne-zneni/paragraf-41/). ✅ Poland also legal (*edukacja domowa*, yearly exams). [HSLDA Poland](https://hslda.org/post/poland)
