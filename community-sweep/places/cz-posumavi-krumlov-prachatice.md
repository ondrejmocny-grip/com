## In short
Foothills of Šumava. Several small alternative schools, one of them **international** (NeŠkola, Český Krumlov). Village schools with mixed ages and folk traditions. A spread-out scene, not a strong hub. *Spiritual / project side not well checked.*

## Families and schooling (C3)
- ([alternativniskoly.net](https://www.alternativniskoly.net/jihocesky/), read 2026-09-28)
  - **NeŠkola**, Český Krumlov – kids from Krumlov, Kaplice, Velešín, Horní Planá, České Budějovice; families from Poland, Brazil, Ukraine; opening a 1st grade. [noviny.ckrumlov.cz](https://noviny.ckrumlov.cz/cz/neskola-spojuje-rodiny-i-kultury/)

  - **Komunitní svobodná škola Desky**; Montessori in **Frymburk** and **Prachatice**; forest kindergarten **Volary**.
  - Nearby: Svobodná lesní škola Ráj na Zemi (Pracejovice), Doma v lese (Libníč, near České Budějovice). [domavlese.cz](https://www.domavlese.cz/svobodna-zakladni-skola.html)
