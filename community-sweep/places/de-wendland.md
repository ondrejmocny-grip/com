# Wendland (Lüchow-Dannenberg, Lower Saxony, Germany)

- **Status:** lead
- **Hub town / area:** Lüchow, Hitzacker, Dannenberg, Gartow + round villages (Rundlinge). *~2 h from Hamburg and Berlin (guess).*
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** no

## In short
Rural district that became Germany's biggest "alternative countryside" through 40+ years of anti-nuclear protest (Gorleben). Communes, farm co-ops, artists, organic farms. Two free schools (Waldorf + democratic-style) are a main reason families move here. A public agency helps newcomers settle. More political/eco than spiritual. ❌ Home schooling illegal in Germany.

## People (C1)
- Some of Germany's first commune experiments (inspired by Kommune 1 Berlin). "In Wendland there is no standard — everything is possible." [Willkommen im Wendland](https://willkommen-im-wendland.de/leben-im-wendland/gemeinschaften/index.html), [wendland-net](https://wendland-net.de/post/gemeinsam-wohnen-auf-grossen-hoefen-14785)
- Lebensgemeinschaft Mützen: 6 ha farm co-op (since Jan 2020), 7 people, based on "mindfulness, spirituality and sustainability". [Wendland im Wandel](https://wendland.imwandel.net/seite/lebensgemeinschaft-muetzen-7/)
- *Spiritual side exists but smaller than the political/art side (guess).*

## Families and schooling (C3)
- Alternative schools: **Freie Waldorfschule Hitzacker** (to class 12, 300+ pupils), **Freie Schule Wendland** (Grabow, free/democratic, 50+ pupils, since 2015/16), LERNORT Waldorf (Nieperfitz). Forest kindergartens in Gartow and Wadweitz. "The two free schools are a main reason many parents move here." [Wendlandleben Familie](https://www.wendlandleben.de/leben/familie.html), [Schullandschaft](https://willkommen-im-wendland.de/arbeit-und-bildung-im-wendland/schullandschaft/index.html), [Freie Schule Wendland](https://www.freie-alternativschulen.de/schule/freie-schule-wendland.html)
- Home-school groups: ❌ none (illegal).
- Home schooling legal? ❌ Illegal in Germany since 1918 (Schulpflicht). ECHR upheld it in *Wunderlich v. Germany* (Jan 2019). [HSLDA](https://hslda.org/post/wunderlich-vs-germany), [Strasbourg Observers](https://strasbourgobservers.com/2019/02/05/wunderlich-v-germany-enforcing-compulsory-home-schooling/)

- **Agentur Wendlandleben** (public, since Feb 2017): free help for newcomers; open meeting for "new, old and soon Wendlanders" **1st Thursday each month, 18:00**. [Wendlandleben](https://www.wendlandleben.de/ueber-uns.html), [Vernetzung](https://www.wendlandleben.de/leben/vernetzung.html)
