# Alto Aragón repopulated villages: Guarguera (Aineto, Ibort, Artosilla) + La Fueva (Caneto) (Spain)

- **Status:** lead (weak — small and fragile)
- **Hub town / area:** Sabiñánigo (for Guarguera), Aínsa / Boltaña (for Sobrarbe and La Fueva). Huesca province, Pyrenees foothills.
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** no (cold mountain area; the old filter would drop it)

## In short
Abandoned villages rebuilt since the 1980s by young people from all over Spain. Very young population, own small public schools, a strong self-help spirit. But these are almost "new villages": few old locals, assembly politics, and a school fight with the regional government. 🏘️

## People (C1)
- Artiborain association (since 1986) rebuilt Aineto, Artosilla, Ibort. Founders wanted a life "closer to nature, less competitive, more collaborative, simpler". Today **~160 people**, many children (25 in Ibort). [Guarguera Viva](https://www.asociacionguargueraviva.es/asociacion-artiborain/), [nuevospueblos, 2012](http://nuevospueblos.blogspot.com/2012/01/aineto-artosilla-e-ibort-asociacion.html)
- Ibort: 80 people, oldest person 64. Crafts, natural cosmetics shop, a builder of traditional houses. [ZG, 2019-07-24](https://www.zgrados.com/pueblos-rehabitados/)
- Caneto (La Fueva, Sobrarbe): repopulated in the 1980s; now **almost half the people are children**. [El Salto, 2023-11-12](https://www.elsaltodiario.com/opinion/pueblos-vivos-traves-escuela-rural)
- Spiritual style: *eco, practical, political more than spiritual (guess).*

## Families and schooling (C3)
- **CP Valle del Guarga** in Aineto: one-class public school since 1987; 70+ children educated there. [Guarguera Viva](https://www.asociacionguargueraviva.es/asociacion-artiborain/)
- **O Chinebro (Caneto)**: public classroom since 2019, 21 children (3–12), active/free methods, bio-built rooms. [El Salto](https://www.elsaltodiario.com/opinion/pueblos-vivos-traves-escuela-rural)
  - Nov 2023 the government closed it; court (TSJA) reopened it Jan 2024. [Hoy Aragón, 2024-04-09](https://www.hoyaragon.es/articulo/noticias-aragon/escuela-caneto-reabierta/20240409175310047658.html)
  - **April 2026:** still no proper school building; ~20 children waiting 7+ years. [escuelarural.net, 2026-04-17](https://escuelarural.net/caneto-sigue-sin-escuela/)
- Home schooling legal? ⚠️ Grey zone in Spain. Here the path is small public rural schools.

## Integration with locals (C4)
- ⚠️ The villages were empty, so the "locals" are mostly other newcomers. Old locals live in Sabiñánigo, Aínsa and nearby villages.
- ⚠️ Tensions with neighbour villages about building styles; internal conflicts from assembly rule. [ZG](https://www.zgrados.com/pueblos-rehabitados/)
- ✅ Opposition parties and people from the region supported Caneto in the school fight. [arainfo](https://arainfo.org/caneto-seguira-siendo-un-pueblo-vivo-y-o-chinebro-una-escuela-con-futuro/)
