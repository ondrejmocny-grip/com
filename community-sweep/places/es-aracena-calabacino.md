# Sierra de Aracena: Alájar, El Calabacino, Castaño del Robledo (Spain)

- **Status:** lead
- **Hub town / area:** Aracena (town). Core: Alájar + hamlet El Calabacino, Castaño del Robledo, Fuenteheridos, Linares de la Sierra.
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** yes → [es-aracena-alajar](../../places/es-aracena-alajar.md)

## In short
A quiet, family-heavy scene. El Calabacino is an old hamlet rebuilt by newcomers: about half of its ~100 people are children. The kids keep the Alájar village school open. More Spanish, less hippie than Órgiva. Smaller, fewer retreats.

## People (C1)
- El Calabacino: just over 100 people, **half are minors**; birth rate far above the area. [Huelva24, 2024-06-11](https://www.huelva24.com/provincia/sierra/unica-ecoaldea-provincia-huelva-20240611033805-nth.html)
- Calls itself "a family of families": own houses, no single leader. Workshops in music, circus, nonviolent communication, permaculture, bio-building. [RIE](https://ecoaldeas.org/el-calabacino/)
- Newcomers are a known group in the whole Sierra (study of Fuenteheridos). [AIBR, 2024](https://aries.aibr.org/articulo/2024/19/6393/los-neorrurales-frente-al-turismo-de-base-local-y-la-crisis-rural-estudio-de-caso-en-la-sierra-de-aracena-andalucia-espana)
- Spiritual style: *earth-based, artistic, low-key. Guess.*

## Families and schooling (C3)
- Alternative schools: La Semilla (free school), Sendas (Montessori, Aracena). Public rural schools CPR Tresfuentes (Alájar) and CPR Adersa 1 use "learning community" methods. [ludus Huelva](https://ludus.org.es/es/projects?province_id=5)
- Calabacino children keep the **Alájar public school open**. [Huelva24](https://www.huelva24.com/provincia/sierra/unica-ecoaldea-provincia-huelva-20240611033805-nth.html)
- Home-school groups: *not found online; likely informal (guess).*
- Home schooling legal? ⚠️ Grey zone in Spain. *La Semilla's official status: ask (guess: not registered).*

## Integration with locals (C4)
- Alájar town council **voted to support** legalising El Calabacino as "rural habitat of social and ecological interest". [La Voz del Sur, 2021-10-31](https://www.lavozdelsur.es/actualidad/ecologia/calabacino-ecoaldea-en-mitad-parque-natural-camina-hacia-regularizacion_266812_102.html)
- Newcomer kids go to the village school with local kids. [Huelva24](https://www.huelva24.com/provincia/sierra/unica-ecoaldea-provincia-huelva-20240611033805-nth.html)
- ⚠️ 2017: court cases and demolition orders against some families (Natural Park rules). [Diario de Huelva, 2017-12-26](https://www.diariodehuelva.es/articulo/provincia/alajar-abre-expedientes-derribo-seis-familias-la-ecoaldea-calabacino/20171226001414167208.html). This is the state, not the village.
- Traditions: chestnuts, ham, romerías. *General knowledge.*
