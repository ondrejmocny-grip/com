# La Vera: Villanueva, Valverde, Talaveruela (Spain)

- **Status:** lead
- **Hub town / area:** Jaraíz de la Vera (market town). Core villages: Villanueva de la Vera, Valverde de la Vera, Talaveruela, Madrigal, Losar.
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** yes → [es-la-vera](../../places/es-la-vera.md)

## In short
Living Spanish villages with strong traditions, two small free schools and a real local food movement (farmers, towns, schools together). The newcomer scene is quieter and more Spanish than in Andalusia. Good integration, fewer "spiritual" projects.

## People (C1)
- Two parent-led free schools show a base of young families with alternative values. [ludus Cáceres](https://ludus.org.es/es/projects?province_id=36)
- A Tibetan Buddhist centre (Thubten Dhargye Ling, Gelugpa, led by a lama) runs yoga and nature retreats near Villanueva. [budismotibetanolavera.com](https://budismotibetanolavera.com/yoga-y-naturaleza/). *Its members seem to come mostly from Madrid, not live here (guess).*
- Spiritual scene: *small, nature-based. Guess.*
- Population grew in 2024 (+116, to 24,169). Villanueva grew. [A la Vera de Gredos, 2024-12-29](https://alaveradegredos.com/2024/12/29/la-poblacion-en-la-vera-aumento-en-116-personas-en-2024-y-se-situa-en-24-169-habitantes/)

## Families and schooling (C3)
- Alternative schools: **Isla Tortuga** (3–12, Montessori + democratic + forest school), **Maisha** (3+). [ludus](https://ludus.org.es/es/projects?province_id=36)
- Public school CEIP in Piornal (next valley) is a "learning community". [ludus](https://ludus.org.es/es/projects?province_id=36)
- Home-school groups: *not found online (guess: informal).*
- Home schooling legal? ⚠️ Grey zone in Spain. *Isla Tortuga's legal status: ask.*

## Integration with locals (C4)
- Isla Tortuga brings **local people** to teach workshops. [ludus](https://ludus.org.es/es/isla-tortuga)
- The food council mixes town halls, local farmers and schools. [La Vera nos alimenta](https://www.laveranosalimenta.org/alianza/)
- Strong traditions: Pero Palo carnival (Villanueva), Los Empalaos (Valverde), pimentón. *General knowledge, verify.*
- Spanish needed. No expat bubble.
