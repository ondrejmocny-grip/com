# Mallorca inland: Binissalem – Sencelles – Bunyola (Balearic Islands, Spain)

- **Status:** lead
- **Hub town / area:** Binissalem (Raiguer). Around: Sencelles and Biniali (Es Pla), Alaró, Bunyola, Santa Maria. All ~15–25 min from each other *(guess)*.
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** partly — the main search looked at the Tramuntana coast (dropped for price/water): [es-mallorca-tramuntana](../../places/es-mallorca-tramuntana.md). This inland area is new.

## In short
Mallorca has a real alternative-school movement, and it clusters in the inland towns, not on the coast. Sa Llavor (100+ pupils, officially approved) is in Binissalem; three more projects are close. Farm villages with Catalan (Mallorquí) culture. Risk: rich-newcomer and show-off side of the island.

## People (C1)
- Permaculture farms and newcomers: e.g. Can Bustan in Sencelles — an **Israeli–Turkish couple** (Shaul Shaham, Gulnur Bayraktar), 1 ha, 200+ fruit trees, animals. [Última Hora, 2020-09-24](https://www.ultimahora.es/noticias/sociedad/2020/09/24/1199657/vida-100-100-natural-permacultura-crece-mallorca.html)
- Sa Llavor's "holistic pedagogy" looks at the child's emotional and **spiritual** needs. [sallavor.es](https://www.sallavor.es/escuela/escuela-2/?lang=en)
- *Adult spiritual scene: yoga and retreat places exist on the island, but many are tourist retreats (guess).*

## Families and schooling (C3)
- **Sa Llavor** (Binissalem): first **officially approved** infant + primary centre with holistic pedagogy in the Balearics; 100+ pupils; also secondary. [sallavor.es](https://www.sallavor.es/escuela/escuela-2/?lang=en)
- **Escola Nexes** (Biniali, free/living), **EEI Els Molins** (Sencelles, Reggio Emilia), **Ses Milanes** (Bunyola, forest school), Nau Escola (Palma). [ludus Baleares](https://ludus.org.es/es/projects?autonomy_id=4)
- Home-school groups: *not found (guess).*
- Home schooling legal? ⚠️ Grey zone in Spain. Sa Llavor is a legal option.
