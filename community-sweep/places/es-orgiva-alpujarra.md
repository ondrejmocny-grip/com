# Órgiva and the western Alpujarra (Spain)

- **Status:** lead
- **Hub town / area:** Órgiva (~7,000 people). Satellite villages 10–40 min: Soportújar, Cáñar, Carataunas, Lanjarón, Pampaneira, La Taha (Pitres, Ferreirola).
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** yes → [es-orgiva-alpujarra](../../places/es-orgiva-alpujarra.md), [es-la-taha-alpujarra](../../places/es-la-taha-alpujarra.md), dropped part: [es-beneficio-orgiva](../../places/es-beneficio-orgiva.md)

## In short
The biggest and most mixed alternative scene in Spain. Many families, many projects, a legal Waldorf school and a big weekly market. Risk: a loud "hippie" fringe and fewer old locals. Best for community in all of Spain, *Claude's guess*.

## People (C1)
- People from **72 countries**. 26% foreign, 38% from other parts of Spain, 35% born locally (town data, 2025-12). [Euro Weekly News, 2025-12-01](https://euroweeklynews.com/2025/12/01/tiny-spanish-mountain-village-where-a-third-of-its-population-are-expats/)
- Yoga, meditation, kirtan, ecstatic dance, shamanic and healing work, retreats. [La Guía de la Alpujarra](https://www.laguiadelaalpujarra.com/vida-alternativa-en-la-alpujarra/)
- A Sufi community, a Buddhist monastery nearby, weekly satsang meetings. [Routinely Nomadic](https://routinelynomadic.com/orgiva-spain/) *(blog, date not clear)*. The Buddhist centre is O Sel Ling (Soportújar) — *general knowledge, verify*.
- Grounded: many are farmers, builders, school founders. *But part of the scene drifts (camps, Beneficio). Guess from sources.*

## Families and schooling (C3)
- Alternative schools: **Árbol Madre** — officially authorised private school (Junta code 18015791), Waldorf-based, 3–11 years (site checked 2026-09-27). [arbolmadre.org](https://arbolmadre.org/). Earlier many families **left** because there was no legal alternative primary. [Goteo](https://en.goteo.org/project/arbol-madre-holistic-international-project). Also Montessori Alpujarras, Proyecto Espiral, and a Waldorf school project "Escuela Waldorf de la Alpujarra" in formation. [ludus](https://ludus.org.es/en/a-c-escuela-waldorf-de-la-alpujarra)
- Home-school groups: *informal groups very likely, not found online (guess).*
- Secondary (12+): public IES only, *guess*.
- Home schooling legal? ⚠️ Grey zone in Spain. [legal overview](../../regions/legal-overview.md)

## Integration with locals (C4)
- The mayor says openly that diversity is good for the town. [Olive Press, 2022-06-26](https://www.theolivepress.es/spain-news/2022/06/26/olive-press-meets-orgiva-mayor-raul-orellana-to-find-out-why-this-spanish-town-in-la-alpujarra-south-of-granada-is-the-worlds-best/)
- Local traditions alive: acequias (UNESCO-recognised), terraces, fiestas. [El Independiente de Granada](https://www.elindependientedegranada.es/economia/unesco-premia-regantes-alpujarra-sierra-buen-uso-agua-con-acequias-careo)
- ⚠️ Only ~1/3 born locally. *Deep contact with old locals is easier in the high villages (La Taha) than in town. Guess.*

## Like-minded neighbors (C5)
- ~2,300 foreigners in the town alone + Spanish neorrurales. Satellite villages add more. [Euro Weekly News](https://euroweeklynews.com/2025/12/01/tiny-spanish-mountain-village-where-a-third-of-its-population-are-expats/)

## Open door (C6)
- **Thursday market** — the biggest in the Alpujarras; people from the hills come to sell. [Routinely Nomadic](https://routinelynomadic.com/orgiva-spain/)
- Health-food shops full of event flyers; many workshops. *Same source.*
- Knowmads program, many Workaway hosts. Easy to arrive.

## Deal-breaker check
- Closed community / guru? The town is open. Beneficio commune is closed → avoid (already dropped in main search). *Some guru-type groups may exist; choose carefully (guess).*
- Show-off? ⚠️ Some "hippie tourism" and drifting fringe. Not a luxury scene.
- Contact with locals? Yes, but thinner in town. 🫧 Moderate expat bubble.
