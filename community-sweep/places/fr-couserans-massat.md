# Couserans and Arize valley: Saint-Girons, Massat, Campagne-sur-Arize (Ariège, France)

- **Status:** lead
- **Hub town / area:** Saint-Girons (market town). Massat basin, Biert, Montbrun-Bocage, Arize valley (Campagne-sur-Arize, La Bastide-de-Sérou).
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** yes → [fr-couserans-saint-girons.md](../../places/fr-couserans-saint-girons.md)

## In short
Green Pyrenean valleys where hippies and néo-ruraux have lived in old hamlets since the late 1960s. Many families, light housing (yurts, cabins), a Steiner school on a biodynamic farm, a nature school and an Occitan school. Newcomers saved village schools. **Weak side:** some poverty / marginal fringe; projects are small and informal.

## People (C1)
- First hippie wave late 1960s in empty hamlets of the Massat basin, then newcomers "with very varied profiles". [Le Journal Toulousain](https://www.lejournaltoulousain.fr/societe/dossier-lhabitat-legerlourd-de-consequence-35227/)
- Massat has had autonomy projects (energy, food) for 40 years. [les-cabanes.com](https://www.les-cabanes.com/robinsons-cabane-vie-alternative/libres-enfants-de-l-ariege-vivre-en-autonomie-complete/)

## Families and schooling (C3)
- Alternative schools:
  - **Chant'Arize** (Steiner-Waldorf, Campagne-sur-Arize): 60–90 children, nursery to middle school; started 1993 with 9 families on the Portecluse farm. [ecole-chant-arize.fr](https://ecole-chant-arize.fr/), [Fédération Steiner-Waldorf](https://pedagogie-waldorf.fr/ecole/ecole-chantarize/)
  - **École Pleine Nature** (Petits Pas association, Couserans). [petitspascouserans.org](https://petitspascouserans.org/fr/ecole-pleine-nature/) *(site did not load 2026-09-27)*
  - **Calandreta deth Coserans** (Occitan immersion). [main file](../../places/fr-couserans-saint-girons.md)
- Home-school groups: **Liberté Éducative Ariège (LEA)**, founded Aug 2022 to defend free choice of instruction. [instructionenfamille.org](https://instructionenfamille.org/associations/liberte-educative-ariege/)
- Massat school got pupil numbers back to 1956 levels thanks to newcomers. [les-cabanes.com](https://www.les-cabanes.com/robinsons-cabane-vie-alternative/massat-une-ecole-qui-repasse-a-un-taux-de-frequentation-de-1956/)
- Home schooling legal? ⚠️ France: yearly authorisation since 2022; 23% refused in 2024-25. [demarchesadministratives.fr](https://demarchesadministratives.fr/actualites/ecole-a-la-maison-combien-de-demandes-instruction-en-famille-ont-ete-refusees)
