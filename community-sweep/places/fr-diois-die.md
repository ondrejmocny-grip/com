# Diois and Drôme valley (Biovallée): Die, Saillans, Crest (France)

- **Status:** lead
- **Hub town / area:** Die (upper valley). Lower valley: Saillans, Crest. The Biovallée brand covers 91 communes, 56,000 people. [Diois Tourisme](https://www.diois-tourisme.com/en/pays-diois/the-biovallee/)
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** yes → [fr-die-diois.md](../../places/fr-die-diois.md)

## In short
One of the oldest and biggest néo-rural valleys in France (since the 1970s). Organic farming is normal here. Many schools with other pedagogies, co-ops, shared housing and a new transition "third place" in Die. For community only, this is **the strongest all-round place in France** we found. **Weak side:** mostly French, eco-political more than spiritual; popular, so a bit crowded.

## People (C1)
- Néo-ruraux from the 1970s built organic farming in the valley. [Biovallée](https://biovallee.net/transition-agroecologique-1/)
- Valley has "a local tradition of welcoming" protesters, resisters and néo-ruraux. [Cairn / Ethnologie française, 2004](https://cairn.info/revue-ethnologie-francaise-2004-1-page-23.htm)
- Drôme +20,000 people in 2013–2018, mostly by people moving in. [Médias Citoyens Diois, 2021-01](https://mediascitoyens-diois.info/2021/01/drome-toujours-plus-de-neo-ruraux-dans-la-vallee/)
- *Spiritual side: yoga, meditation, healing exist; main tone is ecology and solidarity. Guess.*

## Families and schooling (C3)
- Alternative schools:
  - **Terresdenfance** (Die): associative, active pedagogy + nature, ages 3–8, since 2013. [terresdenfance.fr](https://terresdenfance.fr/)
  - **École Buissonnière du Diois** (primary). [ecole-buissonniere-diois.org](https://www.ecole-buissonniere-diois.org/) *(site did not load 2026-09-27, per main file)*
  - **École du Colibri** (Les Amanins, La Roche-sur-Grâne): 35 pupils, cooperative pedagogy, under state contract; info evening for 2026-27 was 8 Jan 2026. [lesamanins.com](https://www.lesamanins.com/ecole-du-colibri/), [ac-grenoble](https://bv.ac-grenoble.fr/carteforpub/uai/ecole-elementaire-privee-le-colibri-ass-les-amanins-la-roche-sur-grane). *~45 min from Die, guess.*
  - **L'Archipel** (Allex): self-managed school, Montessori + forest class. [archipel-drome.com](https://archipel-drome.com/) *~1 h from Die, guess.*
- Home-school groups: *likely active, not found online. Guess.*
- Home schooling legal? ⚠️ France: yearly authorisation since 2022; 23% refused in 2024-25; 79% accepted for 2025-26. [demarchesadministratives.fr](https://demarchesadministratives.fr/actualites/ecole-a-la-maison-combien-de-demandes-instruction-en-famille-ont-ete-refusees), [Assemblée nationale](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/17/questions/QANR5L17QE12272)

## Integration with locals (C4)
- 50 years of mixing; organic farming became the local norm. [Biovallée](https://biovallee.net/transition-agroecologique-1/)
- Big market Wednesday + Saturday in Die (~120 stalls). [main file source: camping-pinede.com](https://www.camping-pinede.com/fr/tourisme-drome/marches/)
- *Some tension about house prices and "too many newcomers" is possible. Guess.*

## Like-minded neighbors (C5)
- Very dense. 1,000+ associations in the Biovallée; co-op shop La Carline has 620 member families. [Diois Tourisme](https://www.diois-tourisme.com/en/pays-diois/the-biovallee/)

## Open door (C6)
- Markets, festivals, co-working, many WWOOF / Workaway hosts, local citizen media. [mediascitoyens-diois.info](https://mediascitoyens-diois.info/)
