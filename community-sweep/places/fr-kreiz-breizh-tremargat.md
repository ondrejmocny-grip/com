# Centre Bretagne (Kreiz Breizh): Trémargat, Mellionnec, Rostrenen (France)

- **Status:** lead
- **Hub town / area:** Rostrenen (seat of the Kreiz-Breizh community of communes: 23 communes, 18,800 people). Villages: Trémargat (~200 people), Mellionnec (~400), Lanrivain, Saint-Nicolas-du-Pélem, Gouarec. Carhaix is the bigger town nearby (*~30 min, guess*). [tresordesregions](https://tresordesregions.mgm.fr/epci.php?epci=242200715&region=53), [France Bleu](https://www.francebleu.fr/infos/culture-loisirs/mellionnec-dans-le-centre-bretagne-400-habitants-et-bientot-une-ecole-de-cinema-3803076)
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** no (*rainy, inland — main search would drop it*)

## In short
Inland Brittany lost its people. From the 1970s young néo-ruraux farmers came. In **Trémargat** they run the village since 1995 with real participatory democracy. Mellionnec has a documentary film festival and a busy village life. Breton culture is strong and alive. **Weak side:** small alternative school offer; spiritual side not visible online.

## People (C1)
- Trémargat: neo-rural farmers since the 1970s; since 1995 the "newcomers" run the council with **participatory democracy**, values: ecology, mutual help, degrowth. [Autogestion](https://autogestion.asso.fr/tremargat-laboratoire-dalternatives-et-de-democratie-participative-a-ciel-ouvert/), [Basta!](https://basta.media/Reportage-Tremargat)
- Rostrenen area: "strong spirit of ecology and cooperation". [Côtes d'Armor – Tout vivre](https://toutvivre.cotesdarmor.com/decouvrir-nos-villes/rostrenen-kreiz-breizh/)
- 2026 travel essay: young people arriving in Rostrenen with art projects; also a remark on "barefoot but wealthy" baba-cools. [Openfield, 2026-06](https://www.revue-openfield.net/2026/06/25/centre-bretagne-mellionnec-gouarec-rostrenen/)
- *Spiritual side: likely yoga, healing, Celtic / earth-based interest, but not documented. Guess.*

## Families and schooling (C3)
- Trémargat got younger: **about 30 children** (170 people). [Reporterre](https://reporterre.net/Tremargat-le-village-des)
- Mellionnec has its own nursery + primary school. [mellionnec.bzh](https://mellionnec.bzh/decouvrir-mellionnec/)
- Alternative schools:
  - **Diwan** (Breton-immersion, associative) schools in Carhaix and Bourbriac; Diwan high school in Carhaix since 1997. [education.gouv.fr](https://www.education.gouv.fr/annuaire/29270/carhaix-plouguer/ecole/0292021p/ecole-primaire-privee-diwan.html), [Wikipedia Diwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diwan_(school))
  - A couple planned a new **alternative school** in Centre Bretagne (radio report, *date not shown*). [RKB](https://www.rkb.bzh/emissions/abadennou/kreizh-breizh-projet-de-creation-dune-ecole-alternative/)
  - *No Steiner / Montessori / democratic school found in Kreiz Breizh itself.*
- Home-school groups: *not found online. Guess: some IEF families.*
- Home schooling legal? ⚠️ France: yearly authorisation since 2022; 23% refused in 2024-25. [demarchesadministratives.fr](https://demarchesadministratives.fr/actualites/ecole-a-la-maison-combien-de-demandes-instruction-en-famille-ont-ete-refusees)

## Integration with locals (C4)
- Integration is easier because newcomers **share values close to the old farming world**. [Reporterre](https://reporterre.net/Tremargat-le-village-des)
- Newcomers were elected by the village, which shows trust. [Basta!](https://basta.media/Reportage-Tremargat)
- Breton language, music and *fest-noz* dances are alive (*guess, typical for Centre Bretagne*).

## Like-minded neighbors (C5)
