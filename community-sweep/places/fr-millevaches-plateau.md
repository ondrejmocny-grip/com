# Plateau de Millevaches / Montagne limousine: Faux-la-Montagne, Eymoutiers, Tarnac, Felletin (France)

- **Status:** lead
- **Hub town / area:** Eymoutiers (gateway town) and Felletin as service towns. Villages: Faux-la-Montagne, Tarnac, Gentioux-Pigerolles, Peyrelevade, Royère-de-Vassivière, Lacelle. Three departments: Creuse, Corrèze, Haute-Vienne. Faux-la-Montagne is ~20 min from Eymoutiers and ~1 h 15 from Limoges. [Laou](https://www.laou.fr/decouvrir-les-villes/la-creuse/faux-la-montagne/)
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** no (*main search probably filtered it out: cold, wet, high plateau*)

## In short
The best-organised newcomer "territory" in France. Since the 1970s people came to the empty plateau and built co-ops, a local newspaper, a newcomer welcome system and a "union" of inhabitants. Newcomers and old farming families work together. **Weak side:** the scene is more **political / eco / DIY** than spiritual. Alternative schooling is small.

## People (C1)
- A 2021 study calls the Montagne limousine a "high place" of social and ecological alternatives: **70+ alternative projects** in ~20 communes with ~12,000 people. Faux-la-Montagne alone: 27+ initiatives for 389 people. [Belgeo, 2021](https://journals.openedition.org/belgeo/48884)
- The study says **both newcomers and long-time locals** take part. It is not only activists from outside. [Belgeo, 2021](https://journals.openedition.org/belgeo/48884)
- Young workers keep arriving, drawn by "mutual help" and services. [Laou](https://www.laou.fr/decouvrir-les-villes/la-creuse/faux-la-montagne/)
- *Spiritual side: small. People are grounded, kind, practical, often left / anarchist-leaning (Tarnac). Yoga, healing, meditation exist but are not the centre. Guess.*

## Families and schooling (C3)
- Village schools are alive. Faux-la-Montagne primary: 4 classes, 63 pupils (26 in nursery). [college-lycee.com](https://college-lycee.com/ecole-primaire-faux-la-montagne/Faux-la-Montagne-0230226W) *(date not shown)*
- Alternative schools:
  - **Calandreta Montanhiera** (Tarnac): secular associative school, Freinet method, classes **in a yurt in nature**, Occitan optional. Fundraising article Nov 2021. [Syndicat, 2021](https://syndicat-montagne.org/creation-dune-ecole-calandreta/), [net1901](https://www.net1901.org/association/CALANDRETA-MONTANHIERA,1000105393.html). *Open in 2026? Not confirmed — ask.*
  - Since autumn 2020, **~20 families** from several villages worked on a new associative school. [Rencontres éducation 2020-21 (PDF)](https://www.montagnelimousine.net/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/RENCONTRES-EDUCATION.pdf)
- Home-school groups: the Syndicat education group planned "support tools for home schooling" (Nov 2020). Contact: education-plateau@ilico.org. [syndicat-montagne.org](https://syndicat-montagne.org/category/education/)
- Association "Les P'tits Bouts" for families of the plateau. [journal-ipns.org search result](https://www.journal-ipns.org/)
- Home schooling legal? ⚠️ Since 2022 France needs a yearly **authorisation**, 4 reasons only. Refusals: 11% (2022-23), **23% (2024-25)**. For 2025-26, 79% of requests accepted. [demarchesadministratives.fr](https://demarchesadministratives.fr/actualites/ecole-a-la-maison-combien-de-demandes-instruction-en-famille-ont-ete-refusees), [Assemblée nationale Q 12272](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/17/questions/QANR5L17QE12272)

## Integration with locals (C4)
- The alternatives grew **with** the local farming world, not against it. [Belgeo, 2021](https://journals.openedition.org/belgeo/48884)
- Faux-la-Montagne is "hyper-rural" but a **counter-example** of services closing. [Laou](https://www.laou.fr/decouvrir-les-villes/la-creuse/faux-la-montagne/)
- *Traditions: cattle farming, forests, Occitan (Limousin). Guess on how alive.*

## Like-minded neighbors (C5)
- Dense: 70+ projects in ~20 communes. [Belgeo, 2021](https://journals.openedition.org/belgeo/48884) *Likely hundreds of newcomer households across the plateau (guess).*

## Open door (C6)
- The Syndicat runs a **structured welcome for newcomers**: meetings on your project, links with inhabitants, visits of projects, **temporary housing** before you settle, 4 info points. [search summary of syndicat-montagne.org](https://syndicat-montagne.org/)
- Yearly **summer camp** of the Syndicat in Faux-la-Montagne (e.g. July 2024). [fauxlamontagne.fr](https://fauxlamontagne.fr/evenement/camp-dete-du-syndicat-de-la-montagne-limousine/), [citoyliens.fr](https://citoyliens.fr/%C3%A9v%C3%A8nement/camp-dete-du-syndicat-de-la-montagne-limousine/)
- Welcome of refugees too ("welcome without subordination"). [Terrestres, 2024-02](https://www.terrestres.org/2024/02/19/accueillir-ici/)

## Deal-breaker check
- Closed community / guru? ✅ No. Open network of villages.
- Show-off? ✅ No. Very low-key, practical.
- Contact with locals? ✅ Yes, strong.
