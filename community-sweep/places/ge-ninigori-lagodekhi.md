# Ninigori / Lagodekhi – Ecovillage Georgia (Georgia) 🏘️

- **Status:** lead (weak, young project)
- **Hub town / area:** Ninigori village, Lagodekhi municipality, Kakheti. Foot of the Greater Caucasus. ~2.5 h from Tbilisi.
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** no (Georgia only in [other-europe.md](../../regions/other-europe.md))

## In short
A young, open ecovillage (bought 6.5 ha in 2023) started by **Georgian and international** people, inside a normal village with a school and shops nearby. Plans a kindergarten and a child-led school. Home schooling is *probably legal* in Georgia. **But:** small (~20 people), run by volunteers, few families yet, and we don't know how locals see it. The old climate filter would have skipped this (cold winters, inland).

## People (C1)
- ~20 Georgian + international people; values "trust, cooperation and care"; aim 10–30 households. [GEN](https://ecovillage.org/ecovillage/ecovillage-georgia/), [site](https://ecovillagegeorgia.ge/)
- Residents do communal tasks a few hours per week + regular meetings. No leader / guru mentioned. [site](https://ecovillagegeorgia.ge/)
