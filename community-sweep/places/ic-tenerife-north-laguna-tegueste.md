# Tenerife north: La Laguna – Tegueste – Tacoronte (Canary Islands, Spain)

- **Status:** lead
- **Hub town / area:** San Cristóbal de La Laguna (university town). Around it: Tegueste, Tacoronte, El Sauzal, edge of Anaga.
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** yes → [ic-tenerife-north-tegueste](../../places/ic-tenerife-north-tegueste.md)

## In short
The best cluster of alternative schools in the Canaries: four within ~15 min. Parent-run schools, eco co-ops and social farms, mostly Canarian people. Not a "hippie" scene — more like a green, local, family network near a city.

## People (C1)
- Parents and teachers run the schools together (El Lucero since 2010). [crianzarespetuosa](https://crianzarespetuosa.info/educacion-alternativa/santa-cruz-de-tenerife/tegueste/asociacion-waldorf-el-lucero/)
- Anthroposophy group active on Tenerife. [antroposofiagrancanaria.org](https://antroposofiagrancanaria.org/otras-islas/tenerife/)
- Spiritual style: *Waldorf, yoga, ecology; low-key. No single leader found (guess).*

## Families and schooling (C3)
- Alternative schools (all ~15 min apart):
  - **El Lucero** Waldorf, Tegueste, 3–12, self-managed association, 15+ years, enrolment open. [ellucero.es, checked 2026-09-28](https://www.ellucero.es/)
  - **Alaire** (free/living, La Laguna, 3–12), **Kaleide International** (democratic, La Laguna), **Raíces y alas** (free, La Laguna, from 18 months). [ludus Canarias](https://ludus.org.es/es/projects?autonomy_id=5)
  - El Caracol (Waldorf, Arona, south). [ludus](https://ludus.org.es/es/projects?autonomy_id=5)
