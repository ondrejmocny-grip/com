# Cloughjordan + East Clare (Ireland) 🏘️

- **Status:** lead
- **Hub town / area:** Cloughjordan ecovillage (Co. Tipperary) inside the normal village of Cloughjordan. Nearby: East Clare Steiner scene (Scarriff, Tuamgraney, Mountshannon), *~45–60 min away (guess)*.
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** yes (East Clare only) → [link](../../places/ie-east-clare.md)

## In short
Ireland's best-known ecovillage (67 acres, 120+ residents, 50+ eco homes) sits at the edge of an ordinary Irish village and is well mixed with it. Community farm feeds 75 families from the ecovillage **and** the town. Across Lough Derg, East Clare has Ireland's oldest Steiner school. Good pair: ecovillage for projects, East Clare for schools.
