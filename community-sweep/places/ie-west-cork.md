# West Cork (Skibbereen, Ballydehob, Clonakilty, Bantry) (Ireland)

- **Status:** lead
- **Hub town / area:** Skibbereen (2,903 people, 2022) + Ballydehob, Schull, Clonakilty, Bantry, Enniskeane. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skibbereen)
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** yes → [link](../../places/ie-west-cork.md)

## In short
"Blow-ins" (artists, crafts people, Germans, Dutch, English) have lived among Irish farmers here since the 1960s–70s. Busy Saturday market, a democratic (Sudbury) school, an eco-learning centre, a Buddhist retreat centre. Locals and blow-ins have mixed for 50 years. Home schooling legal.

## People (C1)
- 1960s–70s wave of artists and craftspeople ("Ballydehob Bohemians"). [Irish Times](https://www.irishtimes.com/culture/art-and-design/bohemian-blow-ins-crafting-an-arts-movement-in-west-cork-1.4205730)
- Coole Mountain traveller community (~200 people, 2010). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coole_Mountain)
- Dzogchen Beara (Allihies): Buddhist retreat centre, weekend retreats all year. ⚠️ Former director Sogyal Rinpoche (Rigpa) retired in 2017 after abuse allegations; centre goes on. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dzogchen_Beara), [RTÉ 2025](https://www.rte.ie/culture/2025/0708/1485646-inside-dzogchen-beara-the-rocky-road-to-spiritual-enlightenment/), [Wikipedia Sogyal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sogyal_Rinpoche)
- *More artsy / crafty / eco than "spiritual scene"; yoga and ceremony exist (guess).*
