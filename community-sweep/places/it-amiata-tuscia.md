# Monte Amiata + Colline del Fiora + Alta Tuscia: Arcidosso, Pitigliano, Farnese (Italy)

- **Status:** lead
- **Hub town / area:** Arcidosso (Amiata, Merigar). Pitigliano (Steiner school). Farnese / Selva del Lamone (libertarian school). Families come from Farnese, Montefiascone, Canino, Bolsena, Gradoli, Tuscania. [Italia che cambia, 2021-01](https://www.italiachecambia.org/2021/01/cresce-albero-della-tuscia-comunita-educante-libertaria-lamone/) *Arcidosso–Pitigliano ~50 min, Pitigliano–Farnese ~20 min (guess).*
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** partly → [it-amiata-arcidosso.md](../../places/it-amiata-arcidosso.md)

## In short
The best community mix we found in Italy, but spread over a big area. Amiata has a 40-year Buddhist (Dzogchen) community living in normal villages. South of it, Pitigliano has a Steiner school and Farnese a libertarian "educating community" run by parents. **Home schooling is legal in Italy.** **Weak side:** no single hub; family scene is small; little online proof of a newcomer network.

## People (C1)
- Merigar West (Arcidosso), since 1981. Many practitioners moved to the villages, "above all Arcidosso". [Viverealtrimenti](https://viverealtrimenti.com/merigar/), [merigar.it](https://www.merigar.it/en/dzogchen-community/)
- Summer retreats bring 1,000+ people. [Il Giunco, 2017](https://www.ilgiunco.net/2017/06/23/ritiro-alla-comunita-dzogchen-di-merigar-e-lincontro-piu-importante-dellanno)
- ⚠️ Teacher lineage (founder Namkhai Norbu died 2018). *People live in their own homes; not a closed group. Check "inner circle" feeling on site.* [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Namkhai_Norbu)
- Farnese: founders of Albero della Tuscia came from Rome (they ran a libertarian nursery there), parents of four. [Italia che cambia](https://www.italiachecambia.org/2021/01/cresce-albero-della-tuscia-comunita-educante-libertaria-lamone/)

- Home-school groups: *not found locally.* Italy has ~17,000 home-schooled pupils, most in the North + Tuscany. [chiesacattolica.it](https://educazione.chiesacattolica.it/istruzione-parentale-per-17mila-in-italia/)
- Home schooling legal? ✅ Yes (*istruzione parentale*): yearly notice + yearly exam. [D.Lgs 62/2017 art. 23](https://www.notiziedellascuola.it/legislazione-e-dottrina/indice-cronologico/2017/aprile/DLGS_20170413_62/cap4-art23). ⚠️ Social-services case with a neo-rural family in Abruzzo (2025) shows off-grid families can get checked.
