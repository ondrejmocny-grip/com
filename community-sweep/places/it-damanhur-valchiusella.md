# Damanhur, Valchiusella: Vidracco, Baldissero (Piedmont, Italy)

- **Status:** dropped (2026-09-28) — **reason: strong guru structure** (deal-breaker). See below.
- **Hub town / area:** Vidracco, Valchiusella, ~50 km north of Turin. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federation_of_Damanhur)
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** no
- 🏘️ Intentional community itself.

## In short
Famous spiritual federation (since 1975), with underground temples, its own constitution and currency. Big, active, has families and projects. **But** it was built around one founder, members take new names, and ex-members and researchers describe cult-like control. Fails our "no strong guru structure" rule.

## Why dropped (deal-breaker check)
- Closed community / guru? ❌
  - Founded 1975 by Oberto Airaudi ("Falco"); ~800 members by 2000; own constitution and currency; members take **animal names**, live in houses of 10–20. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federation_of_Damanhur)
  - A 2007 assessment saw traits of psychologically abusive cults (suppressing criticism, distance from families). Ex-members call leaving "torturous". [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federation_of_Damanhur)
  - 2008 report: marriages as fixed-term contracts, pregnancies need approval of the house group. [Cabinet Magazine, 2008](https://www.cabinetmagazine.org/issues/30/bartas.php) *(older source; may have changed)*
  - After Falco's death (2013) it became "more outward-facing". [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federation_of_Damanhur)
- Show-off? ⚠️ Strong spiritual tourism around the temples. [damanhur.org](https://damanhur.org/)
- Contact with locals? ⚠️ Better now: locals use its shop, café, clinic. [The Dark Atlas (search summary)](https://thedarkatlas.com/posts/damanhur-underground-temples-italy)
