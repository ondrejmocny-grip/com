# Ourika valley – worldschool families (Morocco)

- **Status:** lead
- **Hub town / area:** Tnine Ourika (Monday souk) and Tahannaout, Al Haouz, ~30–60 min from Marrakech. Madrasa Dunya is in the Amazigh village **Belhouane** (Aghbalou side).
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** yes → [ma-ourika-tahannaout.md](../../places/ma-ourika-tahannaout.md)

## In short
The best **family / schooling** option in Morocco: a licensed, full-time worldschool hub (Madrasa Dunya) inside a normal Amazigh village, plus permaculture farms and Marrakech's worldschool network. **But** the settled newcomer scene is thin, many families only stay weeks, and a luxury "Marrakech villa" crowd lives nearby.

## People (C1)
- Worldschool families (Waldorf / Montessori / democratic style) come and go. [Worldschooly](https://worldschooly.com/hub/madrasa-dunya-worldschool/)
- Permaculture: Marrakesh Organics (since 2014; PDC, natural building, yoga, meditation). [marrakeshorganics.com](https://marrakeshorganics.com/about/)
- ⚠️ Luxury resort-farms and villas on the Route de l'Ourika. [The Ranch](https://theranch.ma/en/home/)
- *Grounded long-term people: few, guess.*

## Families and schooling (C3)
- Alternative schools: Madrasa Dunya (ages ~1–18, licensed hub). Montessori schools in Marrakech (e.g. Amana Montessori). [Amana](https://amana-montessori.blog4ever.com/)
- Home-school groups: worldschool networks active in Marrakech and Essaouira (co-learning, outings). [Worldly Tribe](https://worldlytribe.com/morocco-a-cultural-gateway-for-digital-nomad-families/)
- Home schooling legal? ⚠️ No law for foreigners; tolerated. School duty is for Moroccan children. [morocco note](../../regions/morocco.md), [Expat Exchange](https://www.expatexchange.com/ctryguide/20958/81/Morocco/Education-in-Morocco-Homeschooling-in-Morocco)

## Integration with locals (C4)
- The hub sits in a real Amazigh village and uses **local facilitators** for culture exchange. [Worldschooly](https://worldschooly.com/hub/madrasa-dunya-worldschool/)
- Monday souk, terraces, irrigation channels — traditions alive. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ourika_Valley)
- ⚠️ Language: Tashelhit, Darija, French. We speak none well → harder than Turkey.
