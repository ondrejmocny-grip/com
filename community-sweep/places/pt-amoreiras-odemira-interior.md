# São Martinho das Amoreiras / Aldeia das Amoreiras (Portugal)

- **Status:** researching (deep dive 2026-09-28)
- **Hub town / area:** São Martinho das Amoreiras parish, inland Odemira (Baixo Alentejo). ~1,047 people (Census 2021). Villages in the parish: **São Martinho das Amoreiras** (parish seat, main life), **Aldeia das Amoreiras** (oldest hamlet, 4 km away, the old Transition pilot), **Amoreiras-Gare** (train station village), Conqueiros, Corte Malhão. ## In short
- A small, old Alentejo parish that was dying. In the last ~10 years it came back to life. RTP (April 2026) says it has **more than 50 nationalities**, a sushi bar, an international restaurant, festivals and markets. [RTP Linha da Frente, 2026-04-09](https://www.rtp.pt/programa/tv/p48339/e10)
- **Main reason: Mooji.** The guru Mooji has his ashram **Monte Sahaja** inside this parish (since ~2012). Many newcomers came to follow him. The village restaurant Casa Nirvana calls itself "a gift of Monte Sahaja". [Sul Informação / Postal, 2025-09](https://postal.pt/nacional/japao-sem-sair-do-alentejo-este-restaurante-parece-uma-tasca-alentejana-mas-serve-comida-japonesa-que-podia-estar-em-toquio/), [Casa Nirvana](https://casanirvana.pt/about/)
- Tamera is a second magnet, ~15–20 min west (Relíquias). *Guess: fewer Tamera people live in this village than Mooji people.*
- Good news: a young **civic scene** grew in 2025–2026: a new association (Amoreiras.info), a community theatre, a village festival, work on the old Casa do Povo. Newcomers act in Portuguese plays about village life.
- Big question for us: **is the scene too centred on one guru?** This is a deal-breaker risk to check in person.

## People (C1)
- Spiritual density is **high**. But one path is dominant: **Advaita / self-inquiry with Mooji**. That fits "meditation" on our list, but it is a **guru structure**.
- Monte Sahaja is **by invitation only**. You need 6+ months on his online programme first. Not open for casual visits. [mooji.org – Monte Sahaja](https://mooji.org/monte-sahaja), [mooji.org news](https://mooji.org/news)
- **Controversy:** In 2019 a journalist (Be Scofield) wrote about claims of abuse, sexual relations with students and cult-like control. Mooji's side calls it slander. Two devotee suicides in 2017 are reported. *We cannot judge this from far away.* [GuruMag, 2019-03-04](https://www.gurumag.com/becoming-god-inside-moojis-portugal-cult/), [Mooji response](https://mooji.org/monte-sahaja-response-to-allegations-of-abusive-cult)
- **Tamera** (~200 people, 170 adults + 30 kids) is ~6 km west of Amoreiras-Gare as the crow flies, ~15–20 min by car *(guess)*. Own strong founders' ideology. [search result: Tamera](https://www.tamera.org/about/), [Lidador](https://lidadornoticias.pt/en/odemira-comunidade-tamera-gente-do-mundo-encontrou-paraiso-no-concelho/)
- **Grounded signs (good):** newcomers run restaurants, a theatre, a festival, a chess club, work to renovate the Casa do Povo. They act in Portuguese. [Amoreiras.info festival page, 2026](https://amoreiras.info/festival/), [allevents – Amoreiras Fest 2025](https://allevents.in/events/amoreiras-fest/200028166715417)
- **Show-off risk:** festival line-up is mostly "sangha" musicians (Anandi, Omraj & Devapriya, Sam Garrett, ShaManTra). *Guess: warm and heart-centred, but a bit of one-flavour "satsang culture". Check if it feels open to non-followers.*
- Other paths (yoga, permaculture, NVC, shamanic): *more in São Luís / Tamera area than here (guess).*

## Integration with locals (C4)
- RTP (2026): newcomers "feel integrated and, above all, well welcomed", even while still learning Portuguese. "New and old residents live happily." [RTP Ensina](https://ensina.rtp.pt/artigo/a-aldeia-das-cinquenta-nacionalidades/)
- Locals were **sceptical at first**, then came out of curiosity (sushi bar story). [Postal, 2025-09-21](https://postal.pt/nacional/japao-sem-sair-do-alentejo-este-restaurante-parece-uma-tasca-alentejana-mas-serve-comida-japonesa-que-podia-estar-em-toquio/)

## Deal-breaker check
- **Closed community / guru?** ⚠️ **Strong guru structure in the parish (Mooji, Monte Sahaja).** The ashram is closed/gated. The **village itself is open** and normal. The key question: can a non-follower family feel at home, or is all social life "sangha"? Tamera (founder ideology) is also near. → **Not dropped, but the #1 thing to check on the trip.**
- **Show-off?** Not visible. Festival and theatre look sincere and low-cost (tickets from €3). *Some "satsang culture" style — check.*
- **Contact with locals?** ✅ Yes: school, Casa do Povo, Portuguese theatre, markets, feasts.
- 🫧 Flag: expat/sangha bubble risk.
- 🏫 Home schooling legal ✅.
