- **Status:** lead (low confidence — few fresh sources)

- **Added:** 2026-09-28

## In short
A dry, inland Alentejo parish the old water/heat filter would drop. RTP (2026) calls it "the village of fifty nationalities": the village school has children again, and newcomers "feel integrated and, above all, well welcomed". It was a Transition Town pilot (2010). Many newcomers are *probably* linked to Tamera. The old community centre closed in 2015.

## People (C1)
- "Immigrants who came from all over the world"; ~50 nationalities. [RTP Ensina, 2026](https://ensina.rtp.pt/artigo/a-aldeia-das-cinquenta-nacionalidades/)
- *Likely many ex-Tamera or Tamera-friendly people (peace work, ecology, relationship work). Guess — verify on the trip.*

## Families and schooling (C3)

## Integration with locals (C4)
- New and old residents live together; families feel "integrated and well welcomed" even while learning Portuguese. [RTP Ensina, 2026](https://ensina.rtp.pt/artigo/a-aldeia-das-cinquenta-nacionalidades/)
- Transition work was done **with** the Junta and the local improvement committee. [Transição Portugal](http://www.transicaoportugal.net/iniciativas-de-transicao/portugal/aldeia-das-amoreiras/)
- Traditions: S. Bento chapel, village feast first weekend of July. [JF São Martinho](https://www.jf-saomartinhodasamoreiras.pt/pages/579?news_id=8)
