# Benfeita / Açor valleys, Arganil (Portugal)

- **Status:** lead
- **Hub town / area:** Benfeita parish (Arganil), with nearby villages Luadas, Esculca, Pardieiros, Sardal. Côja and Arganil town ~15–25 min *(guess)*.
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** yes → [pt-benfeita-arganil.md](../../places/pt-benfeita-arganil.md)

## In short
A mountain valley with 200+ newcomer adults and ~70 children living in **normal villages**, not behind a gate. They run a learning community (Folha Verde), a food co-op, a tool bank and a forest festival. After the big fire of August 2025 the group worked together for weeks, and locals now "trust us more". Closest thing to "Bayramiç, but in Portugal".

## People (C1)
- 200+ adults, ~70 children, from ~20 countries plus Portuguese. English is often the shared language. [permaculturinginportugal.net](https://www.permaculturinginportugal.net/projects/community/) *(date of count unknown)*
- Skills: builders, teachers, artists, chefs, holistic health people. [folhaverde.pt](https://folhaverde.pt/)
- Very practical and grounded. After the 2017 fires they organised a tool bank, clean-up and crowdfunding. [permaculturinginportugal.net](https://www.permaculturinginportugal.net/projects/community/)
- Spiritual life: *low-key and mixed (holistic health, nature). No leader, no single path found. Guess.*

## Families and schooling (C3)
- Alternative schools: **Folha Verde** (since 2018). Kindergarten 3+ and "Big Kids" 6–~14. Bilingual PT/EN, small groups, one forest-school day a week. Supports home-educating families. [folhaverde.pt](https://folhaverde.pt/) (checked 2026-09-28)
- Old Waldorf kindergarten **Caracol ao Sol** (Barril de Alva, Arganil) **closed in 2020**. [caracolaosol.org](http://caracolaosol.org/)
- Home-school groups: the Folha Verde families *are* the home-school network. ~70 children in the valleys.
- Home schooling legal? ✅ Portugal, *ensino doméstico*. Parent needs a bachelor's degree (Decreto-Lei 70/2021). [DGE](https://www.dge.mec.pt/ensino-individual-e-ensino-domestico)

## Integration with locals (C4)
- The parish itself became an **Eco-Freguesia** (June 2019) together with the newcomers. [permaculturinginportugal.net](https://www.permaculturinginportugal.net/projects/community/)
- After the August 2025 fire: "the fire reduced cultural divisions between local and foreign residents" and "people trust us more" (ArBOR president). [Campeão das Províncias, 2025-09-02](https://www.campeaoprovincias.pt/2025/09/02/comunidade-da-benfeita-defende-reflorestacao-com-especies-autoctones-apos-incendio-de-agosto/)
- ⚠️ Local Portuguese population is small and old *(guess)*. So "locals" are few.
