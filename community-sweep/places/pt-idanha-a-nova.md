# Idanha-a-Nova (Portugal)

- **Status:** lead (medium confidence)
- **Hub town / area:** Idanha-a-Nova town and villages (Idanha-a-Velha, Monsanto, Medelim, Penha Garcia). Penamacor next door.
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** yes → [pt-idanha-a-nova.md](../../places/pt-idanha-a-nova.md) (low priority there: heat, cold nights, no sea)

## In short
An old, empty border region where the **council itself invites newcomers** (programme "Recomeçar", 2015–2025) and calls itself a Bio-Region. A parent-led learning community (Sementes do Interior) and a Clonlara campus serve neo-rural families. Community pull is real, but the scene is spread out and partly tied to the Boom festival crowd.

## People (C1)
- "Citizens of the world, national and international, who exchange big cities for the countryside". [Diagnóstico Social Idanha, 2024](https://www.idanha.pt/media/7595/diagnosticosocial_in_10-09-2024_final.pdf) *(via search summary)*
- More people move in than out since 2019. [Reconquista](https://www.reconquista.pt/articles/demografia-idanha-a-nova-regista-segundo-ano-de-fluxos-migratorios-positivos)
- ⚠️ Boom Festival (every 2 years) brings a psytrance / party crowd. *Some residents come from that scene. Guess.* [Boom](https://www.boomfestival.org/environment/socio-economic)

## Families and schooling (C3)
- Alternative schools:
  - **Clonlara School – Idanha campus**: with the council since 2022; grades 1–5 first; **still in licensing** with the Ministry. [clonlara.org](https://clonlara.org/pt/campus-pt-idanha-a-nova/)
  - **Sementes do Interior** learning community (partner of Clonlara). [clonlara.org](https://clonlara.org/pt/campus-pt-idanha-a-nova/)
  - Quinta da Bazágueda (Penamacor, next door) listed as a learning project. [alternativeschoolsinportugal.com](https://www.alternativeschoolsinportugal.com/)
- Council support: free childcare "Idanha +Bebé". [URBACT](https://urbact.eu/good-practices/restart-integrated-strategy-attracting-residents)
- Home schooling legal? ✅ [DGE](https://www.dge.mec.pt/ensino-individual-e-ensino-domestico)

## Integration with locals (C4)
- Council actively wants newcomers; 300 families showed interest in the first 6 months of Recomeçar. [search summary of Recomeçar coverage](https://www.publico.pt/2015/03/26/local/noticia/camara-de-idanhaanova-apoia-quem-quiser-recomecar-no-concelho-1690368)
- Strong traditions: Monsanto, Idanha-a-Velha, UNESCO City of Music. [Food4Sustainability](https://www.food4sustainability.org/post/why-idanha-a-nova)
- *Locals are very old; newcomers fill empty villages. Guess: kind but thin daily contact.*
