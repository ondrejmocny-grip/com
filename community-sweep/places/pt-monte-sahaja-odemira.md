## In short
Well-known spiritual centre around the teacher Mooji. **Dropped: strong guru structure.** Former members made public claims of abuse and cult-like control; the ashram denies them. Either way, the whole place is built around one teacher. Listed here only so we do not look at it again.

## Why dropped
- Deal-breaker: **strong guru structure**. Mooji lives at Monte Sahaja; the community centres on him. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mooji)
- Former members' allegations (abuse, control). [Guru Magazine](https://www.gurumag.com/becoming-god-inside-moojis-portugal-cult/)
- Ashram's reply (denies the claims). [mooji.org](https://mooji.org/monte-sahaja-response-to-allegations-of-abusive-cult)
