# Penela – Espinhal – Rabaçal (Portugal)

- **Status:** lead
- **Hub town / area:** Penela municipality (Coimbra district): Espinhal and Rabaçal villages. Lousã / Serra da Lousã ~20–30 min *(guess)*. Coimbra ~30 min *(guess)*.
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** yes → [pt-penela-lousa.md](../../places/pt-penela-lousa.md)

## In short
The strongest **schooling** hub in rural Portugal we found. Two alternative learning communities (Waterfall Valley in Espinhal, Cerejeiras in Rabaçal) pull international families into normal Portuguese villages. Families join village festivals and markets. The spiritual side is quieter than in the south.

## People (C1)
- Waterfall Valley: holistic learning "mind, body, emotions, spirit", yoga in the programme; multicultural families. [waterfallvalleyed.org](https://www.waterfallvalleyed.org)
- Families take part in teaching, cooking, gardening. [waterfallvalleyed.org](https://www.waterfallvalleyed.org)
- *Spiritual scene beyond the schools: unknown, probably family-focused and grounded. Guess.*

## Families and schooling (C3)
- Alternative schools:
  - **Cerejeiras** (Rabaçal): ages 3–14, ~50 children, max 12 per group, Portuguese first, **integration programme** for families new to Portugal. Places for 2025–26 limited. [cerejeiras.eu](https://www.cerejeiras.eu/) (checked 2026-09-28)
  - **Waterfall Valley** (Espinhal): 4 days a week. [waterfallvalleyed.org](https://www.waterfallvalleyed.org)
- Home-school groups: both schools work under *ensino doméstico*. [alternativeschoolsinportugal.com](https://www.alternativeschoolsinportugal.com/schools/cerejeiras)
- Home schooling legal? ✅ (bachelor's degree rule). [DGE](https://www.dge.mec.pt/ensino-individual-e-ensino-domestico)

## Integration with locals (C4)
- Waterfall Valley takes part in **local festivals, markets, workshops and village events**; its house is inside Espinhal village. [waterfallvalleyed.org](https://www.waterfallvalleyed.org)
- Cerejeiras teaches in Portuguese and helps with Portuguese language. [cerejeiras.eu](https://www.cerejeiras.eu/)
- Villages are mostly Portuguese. [main file](../../places/pt-penela-lousa.md)
