# Järna / Ytterjärna (Södertälje, Sweden)

- **Status:** lead
- **Hub town / area:** Järna (6,153 people, 2020) + Ytterjärna village. South of Stockholm, in Södertälje municipality. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C3%A4rna,_S%C3%B6dert%C3%A4lje_Municipality)
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** no

## In short
Sweden's anthroposophic centre since the 1930s. Several Waldorf schools, biodynamic farms, a big culture house, care homes, an eco bank. The movement is the **biggest employer in the municipality (~2,000 jobs)**. Huge for families and projects. But: one path only (anthroposophy), and ❌ home schooling almost impossible in Sweden.

## People (C1)
- Anthroposophy centre since the 1930s: schools, care, biodynamic farms. [Biveros](https://biveros.com/jarna/), [Ytterjärna](https://www.ytterjarna.se/om-platsen)
- *Mostly anthroposophic culture (Steiner). Kind and serious, but one worldview; other paths (yoga, shamanic, tantra) less visible (guess).*

## Families and schooling (C3)
- Alternative schools: **Örjanskolan** (Waldorf, F–12), **Solvikskolan** (Waldorf), **Mariaskolan**, Järna friskola, Mora Park. Six private + seven public preschools in Järna. Järna Naturbruksgymnasium (eco farming high school). [Södertälje kommun](https://www.sodertalje.se/kommun-och-politik/om-kommunen/sodertaljes-kommundelar/jarna/skola-i-jarna/), [Örjanskolan](https://orjanskolan.se/), [Solvikskolan](https://solvikskolan.se/), [Biveros](https://biveros.com/jarna/)
- Swedish free schools (friskolor) are **tax-funded, no fees** *(general Swedish rule, guess not checked for each school)*.
- Home-school groups: ❌ none.
- Home schooling legal? ❌ almost not. Since Skollagen 2010 it needs "synnerliga skäl" (very special reasons); religious / philosophical reasons do not count. [sv.Wikipedia](https://sv.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hemundervisning), [Riksdagen motion](https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/motion/hemundervisning_gy02ub239/)
