# Zaježová (Pliešovce, near Zvolen), Slovakia 🏘️

- **Status:** lead
- **Hub town / area:** Zaježová (part of Pliešovce village), Zvolen ~30 min; Banská Štiavnica *~45 min (guess)*
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** no

## In short
Scattered hill farms ("lazy") in the Javorie mountains. Since 1989 city people moved here and built a **loose, open community ("Zaježka")**: ~80 adults and 45 kids, a community school, a forest kindergarten, a food co-op. It is not a closed ecovillage — people own their own houses in a normal municipality. 🏘️ flag.

## People (C1)
- Eco, crafts, natural building (straw bale, clay). Community does **meditation**, celebrations, work brigades. [zajezka.sk](https://zajezka.sk/content/zaje%C5%BEovsk%C3%A1-komunita)
- Most residents work remotely. [startitup.sk 2020](https://www.startitup.sk/na-slovensko-mame-miesto-kde-ludia-zabudaju-na-konzum-a-ziju-v-sulade-s-prirodou/)

## Families and schooling (C3)
- **Zaježovská škola** – eco-community school, grades 1–5, founded by Živica, parents help. ~14 pupils in 2018. [zivica.sk](https://zivica.sk/projekt/zajezovska-skola/), [zajezka.sk](https://zajezka.sk/content/zaje%C5%BEovsk%C3%A1-komunita)
- **Forest kindergarten** in the old village office. [zajezka.sk](https://zajezka.sk/content/zaje%C5%BEovsk%C3%A1-komunita)
- Young families have been moving in from cities. [sk.wikipedia](https://sk.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaje%C5%BEov%C3%A1)
- Home schooling legal? ⚠️ Slovakia: legal (*individuálne vzdelávanie*, since 2008) for primary level, **but the teaching adult needs a teacher degree** for that level (or you hire one). Exams every 6 months. Kids abroad: yearly exams. [HSLDA Slovakia, 2025-02-10](https://hslda.org/post/slovakia)

## Integration with locals (C4)
- ~200 people live in Zaježová, mostly **old locals**; newcomers live among them. [startitup.sk](https://www.startitup.sk/na-slovensko-mame-miesto-kde-ludia-zabudaju-na-konzum-a-ziju-v-sulade-s-prirodou/)
