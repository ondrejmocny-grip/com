# Datça peninsula (Turkey)

- **Status:** lead — **we are here now (Sept 2026)**, easy to check on site.
- **Hub town / area:** Datça town (İskele, ~14,800 people). Villages: Hızırşah, Reşadiye, Kızlan, Eski Datça, Yaka, Sındı.
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** yes → [tr-datca.md](../../places/tr-datca.md)

## In short
A big "leave İstanbul" scene in a real small town, with village coops, a strong civic life (Kent Konseyi) and a few healing / retreat projects. One alternative kindergarten (Mor Kanguru) with active parents. **Weak points:** many retirees and second-home people, few projects for ages 6+, no home schooling.

## People (C1)
- Many white-collar İstanbul people moved here. [Hürriyet](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/kelebek/stil/unlu-oyuncu-datcaya-yerlesmisti-koy-hayati-kolay-degil-42652057)
- Local paper (2026-09-08): some newcomers "flee the city" but bring city habits. [Datça Havadis](https://datcahavadis.com/datcaya-gelen-kacis-hikayeleri/)
- Permaculture teacher Emre Rona lives here, works with Buğday and the Kent Konseyi. [Buğday](https://www.bugday.org/blog/soylesiye-davetlisiniz-yarinin-icin-suyuna-sahip-cik/)
- Active civic / ecology people: Kent Konseyi (14 work groups), water group, "Dayanışma Datça". [dayanisma-datca.org](https://www.dayanisma-datca.org/), [Su Dostu Datça](http://sudostudatca.blogspot.com/)
- *Spiritual scene smaller than Kaz Dağları; more civic and cultural (guess).*

## Families and schooling (C3)
- Alternative schools: **Mor Kanguru** kindergarten (town): Reggio + Montessori + Waldorf mix, play and nature, parents join workshops (e.g. Fathers' Day woodwork). [morkanguru.com](https://www.morkanguru.com/), [datca-haber](https://www.datca-haber.com/haber/babalar-gunune-ozel-etkinlik-minik-eller-ve-guclu-kollar-ayni-tezgahta-bulustu-6976)
- For ages 6+: ❌ only public schools (Kızlan, Reşadiye, town). No BBOM in Muğla. [BBOM](https://www.baskabirokulmumkun.org/okullar/)
- Home-school groups: ❌ none found.
- Kids' nature activities exist (WWF seagrass SUP workshop). [Datça Havadis](https://datcahavadis.com/datcada-cocuklar-deniz-cayirlarini-kesfediyor-kim-korkar-deniz-cayirlarindan-sup-atolyesi-basliyor/)
- Home schooling legal? ❌ Illegal in Turkey. [HSLDA](https://hslda.org/post/turkey)

## Integration with locals (C4)
- Real town with farm villages; almond and olive harvest, silk weaving in Hızırşah, Almond Blossom Festival. [Yarımada Kafası](https://www.yarimadakafasi.com.tr/koyler/)
- ⚠️ Friction: villa pools vs villagers on water tankers. [Datça Havadis](https://datcahavadis.com/datcanin-susuzluk-drami-havuzlu-villalar-cimli-bahceler-ve-o-garibim-esekler/)
- We speak Turkish.

## Like-minded neighbors (C5)
- *Many newcomer households all around the town (dozens within 15 min), but mixed: retirees, summer people, some families (guess).*

## Open door (C6)
- Markets: Saturday town market (main meeting place), Wednesday producers' market, Thursday Palamutbükü. [indatca](https://indatca.com/cumartesi-pazari/), [PazarMap](https://pazarmap.com/mugla/datca/)
- Walking group open to newcomers; Kent Konseyi groups; Workaway. [datcadetay](https://www.datcadetay.com/datca-yuruyus-gruplari.html)

- Closed community / guru? ✅ No. (⚠️ "Datça Ekolojik Köyü" coop sites are down — check if closed.)
- Show-off? ⚠️ Some upmarket retreats and villa life.
- Contact with locals? ✅ Yes.
