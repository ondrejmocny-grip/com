# Kaz Dağları: Bayramiç (north) + Küçükkuyu / Adatepe (south) (Turkey)

- **Status:** lead
- **Hub town / area:** Both sides of Mount Ida (Kaz Dağı), Çanakkale province. North: **Bayramiç** town and its villages (Yeniköy, Toluklar, Muratlar, Mollahasanlar, Zeytinli). South: **Küçükkuyu** / Ayvacık with Adatepe, Yeşilyurt and villages around.
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** yes → [bayramic-canakkale.md](../../places/bayramic-canakkale.md) (our benchmark), [tr-kazdaglari-kucukkuyu.md](../../places/tr-kazdaglari-kucukkuyu.md)

## In short
The oldest and biggest "leave the city" scene in Turkey. Mostly **Turkish** newcomers (İstanbul, İzmir), some foreign families. Many small projects: seed groups, natural farms, yoga camps, TaTuTa farms. It is spread over many normal villages, not one closed place. **Weak point: schooling.** No alternative school, and home schooling is illegal.

## People (C1)
- Kaz Dağları is known for retreats: women's circles, meditation, yoga, breathwork, permaculture courses. [Altınoluk camps page](https://www.altinolukcadirkamplar.org/kamp/kazdaglari-yoga/)
- Bayramiç Yeniköy group: permaculture, natural farming with local seeds, "self-sufficient village" goal. Founded by "new villagers" from the city. [EkoHarita](https://www.ekoharita.org/project/bayramic-yenikoy-kazdaglari-ekolojik-yasam-ve-tohum-dernegi/), [BirGün](https://birgun.net/haber-detay/bayramic-te-bir-ekolojik-koy-112190.html)
- Adatepe / Yeşilyurt: saved from emptiness in the 1980s by intellectuals, artists, city people. [Biz Evde Yokuz](https://www.bizevdeyokuz.com/adatepe-koyu-kaz-daglari-gezilecek-yerler/), [Çok Okuyan Çok Gezen](https://cokokuyancokgezen.com/adatepe-koyu/)
- ⚠️ Mixed with **wealthy retirees and villa people** (Adatepe draws "high-income families"). [AA](https://www.aa.com.tr/tr/yasam/kazdaglarinda-kentlilerin-yasadigi-koy-/912318)
- *Our own experience (Bayramiç): kind, grounded people who build projects. This is our benchmark for C1.*

## Families and schooling (C3)
- Families exist: Mexican–American family with 3 kids (Muratlar), Turkish family with 2 small kids (Mollahasanlar), Swiss–Turkish farming family ("our kids were never so happy"). [Hürriyet, 2020-12-04](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/seyahat/galeri-amerikadan-kaz-daglarina-koy-yasami-suruyorlar-41679669), [Hürriyet, 2020-11-12](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/seyahat/galeri-isvicreyi-birakip-kazdaglarina-yerlestiler-cocuklarimiz-hic-olmadigi-kadar-mutlu-41660162)
- Alternative schools: ❌ none found in Çanakkale province (BBOM list, 2026-09-28). Nearest BBOM: İzmir-Bornova (*~3 h, guess*). [BBOM](https://www.baskabirokulmumkun.org/okullar/)
- Public village schools only (e.g. Küçükkuyu İlkokulu). [MEB](https://kucukkuyu.meb.k12.tr/)
- Home-school groups: ❌ none found online. *Informal parent play groups are likely, ask on site (guess).*
- Home schooling legal? ❌ **Illegal in Turkey.** Our kids would be Turkish. [HSLDA](https://hslda.org/post/turkey), [Turkey note](../../regions/turkey.md)
- *Idea: this is a place where a small parent-run forest group could be started by us (guess).*

## Integration with locals (C4)
- Newcomers live **inside normal villages**; villagers taught the foreign families how to farm. [Hürriyet, 2020-12-04](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/seyahat/galeri-amerikadan-kaz-daglarina-koy-yasami-suruyorlar-41679669)
- ⚠️ Some friction: villa owners vs villagers' animals; land disputes. [Paradigma Akademi PDF, 2022](https://www.paradigmaakademiyayinlari.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/kaz-daglari-ve-geleneksel-ekolojik-bilgi.pdf)
- Toluklar: ~70% of land bought by city people. [Köylerim, 2025-02-07](https://www.koylerim.com/bu-koyu-koyluler-terk-etti-sehirliler-kesfetti-bayramic-toluklar-koyu-349252h.htm) → *some villages are "taken over", choose a mixed one (guess).*
- Traditions alive: Yörük culture, olive harvest, seed swaps. Local municipality supports the seed festival.
- We speak Turkish → big plus.

## Like-minded neighbors (C5)
- Dozens of newcomer households in many villages on both sides of the mountain (Bayramiç, Ayvacık, Küçükkuyu, Edremit side). *Probably the highest density in Turkey (guess, based on our visit + press).*

## Open door (C6)
- Markets: Küçükkuyu Friday organic market, Ayvacık Tuesday + Friday. [kucukkuyu.com](https://www.kucukkuyu.com/organik-halk-pazari/), [AESO](https://www.aeso.org.tr/carsilar-ve-pazarlar/)
- Bayramiç farmers' market + yearly seed festival. [cevreciyiz](http://www.cevreciyiz.com/etkinlik-detay/484/bayramic-koylu-pazari-ve-yerel-tohum-takas-senligi-)
- Workaway, TaTuTa, yoga camps = easy first step.
- *We already know people in Bayramiç.*

## Deal-breaker check
- Closed community / guru? ✅ No. Loose network of villages.
- Show-off? ⚠️ Some villa / weekend people in Adatepe, Assos. Not the main scene.
- Contact with locals? ✅ Yes, daily village life.

## Not scored here (just FYI, one line each)
- Water: Bayramiç dam ~11% (Sept 2026); fire risk; land prices high. See main files.
