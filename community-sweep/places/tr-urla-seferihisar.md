# Urla + Seferihisar villages (İzmir peninsula, Turkey)

- **Status:** lead
- **Hub town / area:** Urla (~82,000 people, 2025) and Seferihisar (Cittaslow since 2009). Villages: Bademler, Orhanlı, Sığacık, Tepecik, Ulamış. İzmir city 45–60 min *(guess)*.
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** yes → [tr-seferihisar-urla.md](../../places/tr-seferihisar-urla.md) (dropped there for water, fire, low hills — ignored here)

## In short
The best **schooling mix** we found in Turkey: Waldorf-inspired kindergarten and play groups in Güzelbahçe (next to Urla), a BBOM school in İzmir-Bornova, plus a strong eco-culture scene (Doğa Okulu, Cittaslow, village markets). Many İzmir / İstanbul people move here. **Risk:** it is also a retiree and weekend-home area, less spiritual than Kaz Dağları.

## People (C1)
- Many people from İstanbul, Ankara, İzmir move to Urla and Seferihisar; **retirees** are a big group too (2025 surveys). [Yeni Ankara](https://www.yeniankara.com.tr/turkiye-haberleri/turkiyede-insanlarin-buyuk-sehirlerden-tasindigi-ilceler-181839)
- Akyaka-style creative / eco people; "another farming is possible" movement in Seferihisar. [Son Dakika](https://www.sondakika.com/guncel/haber-seferihisar-da-baska-bir-tarim-mumkun-8520962/)
- *More eco-cultural and intellectual than spiritual. Yoga and healing exist but smaller than Kaz Dağları (guess).*

## Families and schooling (C3)
- Alternative schools:
  - **Kumkurdu Anaokulu**, Güzelbahçe (next to Urla): Waldorf-inspired kindergarten; founders did the 3-year ESDD Waldorf training. [kumkurduanaokulu.com](https://www.kumkurduanaokulu.com/)
  - Waldorf-based play group in Güzelbahçe–Urla, running since 2014. [search summary / alternatifokullar](http://alternatifokullar.com/tag/waldorf-izmir/)
  - **BBOM Renkli Orman** kindergarten + primary, Yakaköy, Bornova (*~1 h, guess*). [BBOM](https://www.baskabirokulmumkun.org/okullar/)
  - Nature-focused private kindergarten in Urla. [anaokulu.com.tr / search summary](https://www.anaokulu.com.tr/anaokullari/izmir-urla/)
- Home-school groups: ❌ none found online.
- Home schooling legal? ❌ **Illegal in Turkey.** [HSLDA](https://hslda.org/post/turkey)
- *Many young families in Urla because İzmir jobs are close (guess). The Waldorf parent circle in Güzelbahçe is likely the best "tribe" entry (guess).*

## Integration with locals (C4)
- Seferihisar was Turkey's **first Cittaslow** (2009). Sunday **Sığacık producer market**: only villagers' own food and crafts, many women producers. [Seferihisar Belediyesi](https://seferihisar.bel.tr/tepecik-pazari-acildi/), [Beyaz Ev Sığacık](https://sigacikpansiyon.net/2019/09/23/sigacik-koy-pazari/)
- Bademler: Tahtacı Türkmen village with a 1962 development cooperative and a village theatre. Traditions strong. [Wikipedia TR](https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bademler,_Urla)
- Orhanlı: imece, shared ovens, weddings cooked together. [İz Gazete](https://www.izgazete.net/orhanli-koyu-seferihisarin-ekolojik-ve-kulturel-hazinesi)
- We speak Turkish.
