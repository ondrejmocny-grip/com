# Stroud and the Five Valleys (Gloucestershire, England)

- **Status:** lead
- **Hub town / area:** Stroud (market town where five valleys meet, west edge of the Cotswolds). Also Nailsworth, Painswick, Chalford, Slad nearby. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stroud)
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** no

## In short
A working, "bohemian" activist town, not a pretty tourist town. Extinction Rebellion started here in 2018. Very strong family and Waldorf scene: one Steiner-inspired school, 4+ Steiner kindergartens and several home-ed groups. Big Saturday farmers' market. *Maybe more grounded and less show-off than Totnes (guess).*

## People (C1)
- "Bohemian town of activists"; long an "incubator for activism". XR founded here May 2018. [NPR 2019](https://www.npr.org/2019/10/07/767711216/how-a-small-english-town-spurred-the-group-thats-reshaping-global-climate-protes), [Wikipedia XR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extinction_Rebellion)
- Buddhist groups: Plum Village sangha (Thich Nhat Hanh line), Triratna Stroud. [Plum Village UK](https://plumvillage.uk/group/stroud-sangha/), [Triratna Stroud](https://www.triratna-stroud.com/)
- Hawkwood: retreat centre above Stroud, arts, ecology, wellbeing, therapy courses. Steiner roots. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hawkwood_College)
- *Mix: eco-activists, artists, Steiner parents, therapists. More political and practical than "New Age" (guess).*

## Families and schooling (C3)
- Alternative schools (list from Waldorf in Stroud, seen 2026-09-28): **Acorn School** (Steiner-inspired, 6–18, since 1991), **The Grove** (Waldorf home-ed programme 6–18), **The Lantern** (part-time 6–10), kindergartens **Kindytree, The Lindens, One End Street, Sunlands** (0–6). [Waldorf in Stroud](https://waldorfinstroud.co.uk/), [Sunlands](https://www.sunlands-stroud.co.uk/)
- ⚠️ Old Wynstones Steiner school closed after Ofsted 2020; relaunch failed 2021. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wynstones_School)
- Home-school groups: Stroud District Home Education Group, Gloucestershire Home Educators, Lantern. [Homeschool Hall](https://app.homeschoolhall.com/l/stroud-district-home-education-group), [Lantern](https://lanternhomeeducation.co.uk/)
- Home schooling legal? ✅ England: legal. Children's Wellbeing and Schools Act 2026 adds a register + visits, *earliest 2027*. [Home Ed Daily](https://www.thehomeeddaily.co.uk/Childrens-Wellbeing-and-Schools-Act-2026-what-happens-now)
- ⚠️ Private schools pay VAT on fees since Jan 2025 → fees up, some Steiner schools closed in the UK. *Acorn fees may rise (guess).*

## Integration with locals (C4)
- Real working town with old mills, council houses, local families. XR itself started in a council house. [Punchline Gloucester](https://www.punchline-gloucester.com/articles/aanews/extinction-rebellion-a-gobal-protest-movement-that-started-in-a-stroud-council-house)
- *Newcomers from London/Bristol push prices up; some local tension likely (guess).*
