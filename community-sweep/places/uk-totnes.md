# Totnes and Dartington (South Devon, England)

- **Status:** lead
- **Hub town / area:** Totnes (~9,000 people *guess*) + Dartington estate + villages (Staverton, Broadhempston, Ashprington).
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **Also in main search?** yes → [link](../../places/uk-totnes.md)

## In short
The best-known "alternative town" in England. First Transition Town (2006), Steiner school since 1980, Dartington and Schumacher College. Very dense scene of families, healers and eco projects. Risk: some show-off / "wellness shop" side and rich incomers.

## People (C1)
- Known since the 1990s for retreat centres, organic farming, mind/body therapy, peace work. [Christopher Titmuss](https://www.christophertitmussblog.org/a-transition-in-totnes)
- Transition Town Totnes, founded 2006 (Rob Hopkins, Naresh Giangrande). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_Hopkins)
- Schumacher College: accredited courses closed Aug 2024; now run by Satish Kumar Foundation (July 2025), short courses go on. [Dartington](https://www.dartington.org/dartingtons-schumacher-college-closes-academic-courses/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schumacher_College)
- ⚠️ *Some performative wellness and money (guess).*

## Families and schooling (C3)
- Alternative schools: **South Devon Steiner School** — open, 249 pupils, fees £6,657–10,878/yr (Good Schools Guide, June 2026). It cut ~10 staff after VAT on fees (2025). [Good Schools Guide](https://www.goodschoolsguide.co.uk/uk-schools/profile/south-devon-steiner-school), [Totnes Today](https://www.totnes-today.co.uk/news/south-devon-steiner-school-to-lay-off-up-to-10-staff-and-reduce-fees-745146). **Park School** (Dartington, 3–12, alternative). [Park School](https://www.parkschoolonline.com/)
- Home-school groups: Outdoors Group home-ed at Broadhempston woodland. [Outdoors Group](https://www.outdoorsgroup.co.uk/totnes-forest-school/)
- Home schooling legal? ✅ England. New register *from 2027 earliest*. [Home Ed Daily](https://www.thehomeeddaily.co.uk/Childrens-Wellbeing-and-Schools-Act-2026-what-happens-now)
