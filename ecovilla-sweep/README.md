# Ecovilla-pattern Sweep

A **separate** research sweep, started **2026-09-28**.

**Question:** Are there projects in Europe, Turkey or Morocco that work like **La Ecovilla / Ecovilla San Mateo** in Costa Rica?

Results stay **here**. They are **not** mixed into [`../shortlist.md`](../shortlist.md), [`../places/`](../places/) or [`../community-sweep/`](../community-sweep/).

The model project is analysed in [`ecovilla-costa-rica.md`](ecovilla-costa-rica.md).

## The Ecovilla pattern (E1–E10)

| # | Point | Good for us? |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | **Developer-led, planned eco-neighbourhood** (one founder/company, masterplan) | ⚠️ conflict 💶 |
| E2 | **Private lots for sale + co-owned common land** (condominium / HOA, monthly fee) | ❌ conflict 🔒 |
| E3 | **Most land kept as shared nature** (reserve, food forest, river); homes on a small part | ✅ |
| E4 | **Own alternative school on or next to the site** | ✅✅ key |
| E5 | **Many young families**, many nationalities | ✅ (⚠️ expat bubble) |
| E6 | **Shared food system**: food forest, gardens, harvest share, animals | ✅ |
| E7 | **Shared buildings**: yoga shala, community kitchen, co-working, playground | ✅ (simple, not luxury) |
| E8 | **Light governance**: circles / consent, no guru, own finances | ✅ |
| E9 | **Near a normal farming town**, ~1 h to capital/airport, sea close | ✅ |
| E10 | **Part of a valley cluster**: several projects + local villages close, weekly market, rentals for newcomers | ✅✅ key |

### What matters most for us

1. **E4** school, **E5** families, **E10** cluster — these are the real value.
2. **E3, E6, E8, E9** — nice, and match our wishes.
3. **E1, E2** — how Ecovilla makes money. For us these are **risks**, not goals. Best case: a project with E3–E10 but a **lease / join / rent** option instead of E1–E2.

## Scoring rule

- **Score = number of points met, x/10.** Half points allowed (0.5 = partly).
- Also write **"Our-fit score" = points met out of E3–E10 (x/8)**, because E1–E2 are not what we want.
- **Our deal-breakers still apply** (see [`../us/criteria.md`](../us/criteria.md)): closed community or guru, show-off scene, no real contact with locals. A place can score 10/10 and still be dropped.

### Flags (always note)

| Flag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🏘️ | Planned / intentional community (not a normal village) |
| 🔒 | Gated or closed (buy-in only, no lease, no open door) |
| 💶 | Real-estate-driven (sales webinars, brokers, luxury marketing) |
| 🏫 | Home schooling legal status in that country (see [`../regions/legal-overview.md`](../regions/legal-overview.md)) |

La Ecovilla itself: **~9/10** on the pattern, **7/8** our-fit, flags 🏘️ 🔒 💶 🏫. Verdict: model to learn from, **not** a place for us.
