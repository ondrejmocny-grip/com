## 0. Which project is "the" Ecovilla?

It is **one company with several projects**. The same founder and team run all of them. Brand: **"Ecovilla"** (website laecovilla.com). [S1][S3]

| Project | Short name | Where | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **La Ecovilla (Original)** | LEV | Machuca Valley, San Mateo, Alajuela | Built, full (since 2012) | The famous one (Netflix "Down to Earth", 2020). ~17 ha, 43 lots [S3][S4][S9] |
| **Ecovilla San Mateo** | ESM | 10 min from LEV, San Mateo, Alajuela | Selling and building (launched 2023) | ~200–220 ha, plan for 300 families. **This is the main project now** [S2][S3][S5] |
| Ecovilla Cabo Blanco | ECB | Near Santa Teresa, Nicoya peninsula | Interest list only | Newest. "Blue Zone" marketing [S3] |
| Ecovilla Sicily (Noto) | — | Noto, Sicily, Italy | Planned ("launch 2025") | First Ecovilla in Europe, with partner "Vida Design" [S14]. *Current status not verified.* |

Other names you may see:
- "Ecovilla Costa Rica" = old name / website of LEV (ecovillacostarica.com). [S15]
- "La Ecovilla San Mateo" = the ESM project (sanmateo.laecovilla.com). [S16]
- **Alegría Village** = a **different** project in the same valley (70 ha, 135+ residents, 34 countries). Often confused. Similar type. [S17]
- **Machuca Valley** = the name of the valley and a website that links all local projects (LEV, ESM, Alegría, Tacotal, and the Tico villages Maderal and La Libertad). [S18]

In this file, "Ecovilla" means **LEV + ESM** together, unless one is named.

### Legal model

- **Private ownership of lots.** Families buy a lot, build their own house. [S4][S5][S8]
- ESM = **condominium** (Costa Rican "condominio"): private lot + co-ownership of common areas (reserve, river, pools, sport park, yoga shalas, kitchen, gardens). [S8][S11]
- **HOA fee** each month. Most decisions happen at **neighbourhood HOA** level. [S8]
- Foreigners can buy. No residency needed. [S8]
- IC.org: "one-time fee, investment, or share purchase" to join. Independent finances (no shared income). [S5][S10]
- Not a co-op. Not rent-based.

### Prices (USD)

| Item | Price | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Lot in ESM | "from $120,000" | Search snippet only [S11]. *Not seen on an official page. Official site says lot prices are "shared via a call".* |
| Finished home (LEV/ESM) | ~$700,000 to $1M+ | [S11][S19] |
| Building cost | $800–1,500 per m² (standard). $2,000+ per m² (luxury) | Official FAQ [S8] |
| HOA fee (LEV) | ~$350 / month | Listing text on re.cr [S20] (date of listing: 2024, *guess from listing ID*) |
| HOA fee (ESM) | Not published | [S8] |

*Guess: a very small house (60 m² × $1,000) + cheapest lot = ~$180,000 + HOA $350/month. That is about our full loan, plus HOA on top. And Costa Rica, not Europe.*

## 2. How it works

### Governance

| Source | What it says |
|---|---|
| Official FAQ [S8] | "Circle-based, representative system". "Consent-based decision-making" (not full consensus). Neighbourhood HOA does most decisions |
| Casa Clan [S11] | "do-ocracy", circles, consent, defined conflict resolution |
| IC.org LEV [S10] and ESM [S5] | "Majority vote", "Representative/Democratic". Committees for topics (permaculture, architecture…) |
| Residents (2021/2023) [S7] | Neighbour conflicts not solved. They suggested an outside mediator |

*Guess: it is a sociocracy-inspired layer on top of a legal condominium/HOA. The developer still holds a lot of power in ESM while it sells lots.*

### Shared infrastructure

| Thing | LEV | ESM |
|---|---|---|
| Food | Food forest (planted 2010), organic community garden, weekly harvest share [S4][S7][S9] | Permaculture garden per neighbourhood. IC.org: 33–66% food self-produced [S2][S5] |
| Water | Machuca River on site (swimming) [S9] | River Park with swimming holes [S2]. Water system included [S8] |
| Energy | Grid | IC.org: 66%+ own energy (solar, wind, hydro, biomass) [S5]. Underground power, fibre internet [S8] |
| Waste | Compost, recycling [S20] | BioNest ecological wastewater treatment [S8] |
| Buildings | Rancho, yoga shala, chlorine-free / saltwater pool, sports court, playground, library [S9][S20] | Per neighbourhood: yoga shala, organic garden, rancho (co-working). Shared: 25 m chlorine-free pool, sport park (tennis, padel, pickleball, soccer, basketball), Art Village, community kitchen, Village Center with weekly farmers' market, 25 km trails [S2][S8] |
| Animals | — | Rescue dairy cows, mini cows, mini donkeys, horses, bees [S5] |

### Building rules

- Own architect/builder, or turnkey house from the developer. [S11]
- Design review by an "architecture" committee (LEV). [S10]
- *Exact eco-standards (materials, size limits) not published. Guess: design guidelines exist but are not strict natural-building rules.*
- Critics say many "ecovillages" in Costa Rica use cement, need cars, run air conditioning. [S13] *Not said about Ecovilla by name.*
- Pets allowed "under supervision to protect wildlife". [S11]
- *Van / yurt / tiny house: not mentioned. Guess: not allowed as a permanent home in a condominium like this.*

### Rental and trial stays

- Rentals through **ecovillarental.com** for both LEV and ESM. [S8][S21]
- Short and monthly stays exist (examples: $2,500–4,500/month). [S9]
- A family stayed 1 month (Dec 2020–Jan 2021) as a "try it" stay. [S7]
- *No formal "try before you buy" program found.*

### Volunteering

- *No volunteer / Workaway program found* on official pages. [S8][S9]
- IC.org: "open to visitors, call to schedule a visit". [S10]
- Webinars and live Q&A with the founder (sales channel). [S1][S22]
- Ecovillage Tours runs paid tours to the valley. [S23]

## 4. Community life and spirituality

### Facts

- Daily life: workshops, yoga, music, potlucks, spontaneous gatherings. [S2]
- Events: markets, hikes, swimming, yoga, Eco Festival (Feb 2024: ~80 of 150 future ESM residents came). [S12][S27]
- "Ceremonies" and "circles" mentioned by a resident. [S27]
- Residents' jobs: remote workers, freelancers, therapists, healers, entrepreneurs, consultants, writers, teachers. [S8]
- Local offer: organic and vegan food, massage, IV therapy. [S7]
- A travel guide calls ESM "conservative": no radical social, religious or political ideals. Mostly single-family homes. [S12]
- Rules: quiet after 10 pm; some rentals: after 8 pm. [S7]
- Netflix called it a "ritzy eco-village" (2020). [S28]

### Our reading

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Guru? | **No guru.** Strong founder/developer, but no spiritual leader [S1][S12] |
| Spiritual? | Soft New Age / wellness: yoga, healing, ceremonies, holistic health. No one path [S2][S8][S27] |
| Grounded or show-off? | *Mixed. Family life is real (school, kids, potlucks). But the marketing is luxury wellness (padel, pools, $700k homes, Netflix). Guess: risk of "wellness lifestyle" more than deep inner work.* |
| Stable? | Residents named "too many transient visitors", "community loses its essence", "too few kids/adults" in LEV [S7] |

## 5. Relation with locals / the town

### San Mateo

- Small farming town, fruit region, ~45 min to San José, ~1 h to the airport, <40 min to the Pacific beaches (Jacó area). [S2][S12]
- Normal Tico town, not a tourist town. [S12]
- Tico villages right next to the projects: **Maderal** and **La Libertad**. Local people from La Libertad helped the first settlers. [S18]

### Do Costa Ricans live inside?

- A resident blog: "families from all over the world… including Ticos". [S12] *Guess: a few, not many. No numbers found.*

### Jobs and links

- Company says it creates jobs for locals: construction, landscaping, community management, hospitality, maintenance. [S29]
- Weekly farmers' market at the Village Center for local farmers and artisans. [S8][S29]

### Criticism

- A resident (Nicole Reese, 2024) first **refused** an offer in Costa Rica because many "conscious communities" there do **not integrate Costa Ricans beyond maintenance and kitchen staff**. She later moved to La Ecovilla and said they try to avoid eco-gentrification. [S27] (Her words about the general problem, not proof about Ecovilla.)
- Same author, general article: eco-villages drive up land prices, treat local workers as "staff" not "members", greenwashing (cement, cars, AC). [S13]
- Costa Rica in general: rural and coastal gentrification. 76.9% of Costa Ricans think foreigners push up property prices (National University survey). [S30][S31]
- Residents: "it felt like we were in our own little bubble". [S7]

*Guess: Ecovilla is an international bubble with friendly contact to the town (market, jobs, school). It is **not** gated like a security estate, but it is a private condominium with high prices, so locals mostly enter as workers or sellers.*

## 6. Strengths and weaknesses vs. our profile

### Deal-breakers check

| Our deal-breaker | Ecovilla | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Closed intentional community | Private condominium, buy-in needed. Open to visitors and renters, but not "open" like a village | ⚠️ **Close to a deal-breaker** 🏘️🔒 |
| Strong guru structure | No guru. Developer-led | ✅ OK |
| Show-off spiritual scene | Luxury wellness marketing, Netflix fame, padel and pools | ⚠️ Risk 💶 |
| No real contact with locals | Tico villages next door, market, jobs. But few Ticos as residents | ⚠️ Weak |
| Water | River, rainy tropics | ✅ OK |
| Mountains | Hills of the Central Valley edge (300–450 m) [S9] | ✅ OK-ish |
| Dying village | No, active valley | ✅ OK |

### Strengths (for us)

- Many **young families** and a real **alternative school** (100+ kids). Strong match for our kids plan.
- **Density of like-minded people** in one valley: LEV + ESM + Alegría + Tacotal. Matches our "hub" idea.
- Sea (<40 min) and hills both close.
- Food forest, gardens, animals: matches Nesli's work (garden, animals, food).
- Many healers and therapists: a market for our healing/yoga work.
- No guru. Governance with circles and consent.

### Weaknesses (for us)

- **Buy-in model**: no lease/join of land; van and yurt not possible (*guess*). Our path van → yurt → house does not fit.
- **Price**: lots from ~$120k (*unverified*), homes $700k+, HOA ~$350/month. Rent $2,500+/month. Above our budget.
- **Bubble**: mostly foreigners inside a private condominium.
- **Real-estate driven**: sales webinars, brokers, "luxury" words. 💶
- Transient renters and conflicts reported.
- Costa Rica is far from our focus (Europe / Turkey). Residency path for Nesli not checked.

### Short verdict

Ecovilla is a **model to learn from, not a place for us**. It shows how a school + families + shared land can grow fast. But it is a developer-led private condominium with high prices and few locals. This breaks our "not closed, integrated, lease first" rules.

## 7. KEY CHARACTERISTICS — the "Ecovilla pattern"

Use these 10 points to find similar projects in Europe, Turkey and Morocco.

| # | Characteristic (testable) | Fits us? |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | **Developer-led, planned eco-neighbourhood**: one founder/company buys big land and masterplans it | ⚠️ Conflict (💶 real-estate-driven) |
| E2 | **Private lots for sale + co-owned common land** (condominium / HOA with monthly fee) | ❌ Conflict (we want lease/join first; 🔒) |
| E3 | **Most land kept as shared nature** (reserve, food forest, river); homes only on a small part (~5–30%) | ✅ Fits |
| E4 | **Own alternative school on or next to the site** (project-based, bilingual, nature-based) | ✅ Fits strongly |
| E5 | **Many young families** from many countries (40+ nationalities) | ✅ Fits (families), ⚠️ risk of expat bubble |
| E6 | **Shared food system**: food forest, permaculture gardens, weekly harvest share, animals | ✅ Fits |
| E7 | **Shared buildings**: yoga shala, community kitchen, co-working rancho, pool, playground | ✅ Fits (if simple, not luxury) |
| E8 | **Light governance**: circles / consent / committees, no guru, independent finances | ✅ Fits |
| E9 | **Near a normal farming town**, ~1 h from capital and airport, <40 min to the sea | ✅ Fits |
