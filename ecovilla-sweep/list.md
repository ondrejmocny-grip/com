## What this sweep tells us

1. **A true Ecovilla copy (developer + school + families) does not exist yet in our countries.** The copies we found are planned, sales-driven or too expensive (Planes, Amara, Sat Village). Ecovilla Sicily could not be confirmed.
2. **The best "Ecovilla feeling" comes from resident-led ecovillages in the north** (Tempelhof, Hjortshøj, Friland, Sieben Linden). They have schools, families and shared food, and no guru. But they are flat and cold, with no sea, and home schooling is illegal in Germany.
3. **In the south, the pattern lives in clusters, not projects:** Órgiva, Benfeita, Calabacino, Penela. These fit our "rent / volunteer first" plan and our budget.
4. **The cheapest legal way to live light is France's Hameaux Légers:** a 99-year lease plus your own yurt, for about €200–300/month in total.
5. **In Turkey, avoid "eko köy" plot sales.** Always ask for the tapu (deed) and the imar durumu (zoning plan).
