- **Status:** dropped — real-estate investment product, no community; plus Cyprus drought and home schooling not allowed.

## In short
- Modular "eco" studios and villas (55–200 m²) with solar and smart homes, sold as an **investment** ("digital shares from €5,000", rental income, 0% dividend tax).
- No shared nature, no school, no community life. The best example in Cyprus of "eco" as a sales word.
- Other Cyprus eco-projects we checked are tiny or old (Tris Elies, 2015; Ekosia, no details; Pendaya host inactive).
