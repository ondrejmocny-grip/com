# Ökodorf Sieben Linden (Poppau, Altmark) — Germany

- **Status:** lead (weak for us: no mountains, no sea)
- **Location:** Next to Poppau village, municipality Beetzendorf (6 km), district Salzwedel, Saxony-Anhalt. [S1][S2]
- **Website:** https://siebenlinden.org/
- **Added:** 2026-09-28

## In short

- One of Europe's best-known ecovillages (since 1997). ~100 adults + ~40 children; 11–12 straw-bale houses + ~40 construction wagons; 100 ha (80 ha forest). [S1][S2][S3]
- Clear, open entry path: info weekend → courses → **1-year trial** → vote. New housing planned **2026–2027**. [S4]
- Forest kindergarten on site; free school 24 km away. Flat land, far from sea.

## How we could join

| Path | Possible? | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Visit | ✅ | Info weekend / info week; "Sieben Linden Intensiv" week. [S4] Camping €15/night adult, €10 child; room + full organic meals €49/day adult (from 1 May 2026). [S7] |
| Volunteer | ✅ | Federal volunteer service (BFD), FÖJ, European Solidarity Corps, work weeks. [S4] *BFD/FÖJ/ESC have age limits — check.* |
| Trial | ✅ | 2-week community course (around Easter) → application → **usually 1-year trial** with 2 mentors. [S4] |
| Join | ✅ | Per adult: **€11,275 co-op shares (refundable)** + **€1,800 entry fee**. Living costs usually **€700–1,000/month** per person; ~€130/month co-op fee included. [S1][S4] |
| Buy land | ❌ | No private land. |

- **Cost for us:** 2 × (€11,275 + €1,800) = **~€26,150** + ~€1,400–2,000/month for two (*guess from per-person range*). Fits our budget better than a house purchase.
- **Van / wagon:** ~40 construction wagons ("Bauwagen") are homes here. [S2] Newcomers use "temporary solutions" while housing is short. [S4]
- They seek craftspeople, doctors, midwives, teachers. [S4]

## Families & school

- 40+ children. Forest kindergarten in the village; schools: Freie Schule Altmark (24 km), state schools in Beetzendorf (6 km), Waldorf school Wolfsburg; school bus from Poppau. [S5]

## Relation with locals

- Linked by road to Poppau; residents started local citizen initiatives (rail, energy transition, against a turkey farm). [S1]
- *Guess: Altmark is thinly populated; few like-minded neighbours outside the village.*

## Risks

- **Flat land, no mountains, no sea** → fails our deal-breaker.
- Cold winters. Dry-toilet, simple life.
- Housing shortage until new buildings (2026–27). [S4]
- Life mostly happens inside the ecovillage.

## Not scored FYI

- Water: *north-east Germany has dry summers in recent years (guess, verify)*. Climate: continental, cold winters.
