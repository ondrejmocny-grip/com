# Gemeinschaft Schloss Tempelhof (Kreßberg) — Germany

- **Status:** lead
- **Location:** Kreßberg, north Baden-Württemberg. ~10 km east of Crailsheim. Rural, hilly land. [S1]
- **Website:** https://www.schloss-tempelhof.de/
- **Added:** 2026-09-28

## In short

- Eco-village since 2010 on an old castle estate. ~150–180 residents, ~40–50 children, 30–34 ha (26–28 ha farm land). [S1][S2][S3]
- **Own democratic school** (Schule für freie Entfaltung, ~63–80 pupils, age 6–18) + forest kindergarten, open to children from the region. [S1][S4][S5]
- No private land. You join the cooperative (€30,000 share) and rent living space. Consensus decisions. Strong on E4–E8, weak on sea and mountains.

## How we could join

| Path | Possible? | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Visit | ✅ | Info café on Sundays 14:30–18:00; "getting to know" weekend seminar. [S9] |
| Volunteer | ✅ | **Helper weeks April–November**: live and work 1 week (farm, kitchen, building). [S9] |
| Rent | ⚠️ | Only as a member. Warm rent ~€5/m²; average private space 24 m² per person. [S1] |
| Trial | ✅ | Admission: intro seminar → 2 intensive processes → **12-month approach period**; consensus after each step. [S10] |
| Buy / join | ✅ | Each adult pays a **€30,000 cooperative share** (gives lifelong right to live there) + **€250/month** for food. [S1][S10] Open to new members: "phase of opening, new living space built" (April 2024). [S7] |

- **Cost for us (guess):** 2 × €30k = €60k share + ~€500 food + ~€240 rent (48 m²) ≈ **€740/month**. Share > our savings (€12k); *guess: a bank loan for co-op shares may be possible (e.g. GLS Bank works with Tempelhof [S11]) — verify.*
- **Van / yurt:** people already live in **yurts, mobile homes, construction wagons** and an Earthship. [S1] 
- School fees (per month, 2026): 1st child €226.60, 2nd €185.40, 3rd €154.50, 4th €133.90. Both parents must attend a course or school week first. [S5]

## Families & school

- 40–50 children on site. School ~63–80 pupils, open to the region. [S1][S2][S4]

## Relation with locals

- School and kindergarten mix village and regional kids. Farm shop and café open to all. [S2][S3]
- *Guess: Hohenlohe is a conservative rural area; contact is friendly but not deep.*

## Risks

- **No sea**; hills only, no real mountains → fails 2 of our wishes.
- Cold winters (*guess: frost Dec–Feb*).
- Intense community process; life is quite "inside" one site. Closer to a closed community than we want.
- €30k × 2 buy-in.
- German language needed.

## Not scored FYI

- Water: *no problem known; central Europe, moderate rain (guess)*. Climate: continental, cold winters, warm summers.
