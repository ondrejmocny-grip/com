# Friland (Feldballe, Djursland) — Denmark

- **Status:** lead
- **Location:** Feldballe village, Syddjurs municipality, Djursland. Nearest towns: Rønde (~5 km, *guess*), Aarhus (~35 km). [S1][S3]
- **Website:** https://start.friland.org/
- **Added:** 2026-09-28 (all facts checked on this date unless another date is given)

## In short

- Resident-led eco-cooperative (founded 2002, started as a Danish TV project). ~45 houses, ~75 adults + ~35 children. [S2][S3][S4]
- Self-built, **debt-free** houses on private-use plots, next to a normal village with a **free school (friskole)** right next door. [S1][S2][S5]
- Very good on families, school and "no guru". Weak on mountains, climate and our loan plan (loans on house/land are not allowed). [S6]

## Families & school

- ~35 children (2024–26 site text), 30 in 2014. [S2][S3]
- Feldballe Friskole & Børnehus next door. [S5][S7]
- Services in Feldballe are "largely aimed at families with children". [S5]

## Relation with locals

- Municipal plan: Friland is "a notable part of Feldballe". The friskole, sports hall and shop are shared with the village. [S5]
- Friland Erhverv (business area) hosts local firms too. [S2]
- *Guess: good mixing, because the school is shared.* 

## Risks

- **No mountains** (Mols Bjerge hills only, *max ~137 m, guess*) → our deal-breaker "no mountains close".
- Cold, wet, dark winters. Not Nesli's climate. We would be here in summer only.
- Debt-free rule vs. our loan plan.
- Only a few sales per year; many applicants (*guess*).
- Danish language needed for meetings (*guess*).

## Not scored FYI

- Water: rainy climate, no drought issue; own reed-bed sewage (*guess on water security*). Climate: temperate, cool summers (~20 °C, *guess*).
