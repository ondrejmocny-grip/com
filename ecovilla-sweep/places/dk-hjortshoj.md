# Andelssamfundet i Hjortshøj (AiH) — Denmark

- **Status:** lead
- **Location:** Hjortshøj, 15 km north-east of Aarhus, view over Aarhus Bay. Train station in Hjortshøj. [S1][S2]
- **Website:** https://www.andelssamfundet.dk/
- **Added:** 2026-09-28

## In short

- Big, old eco-community (founded 1986, first houses 1992). ~300 people in 8 housing groups, ~90 children. [S1][S3][S4]
- **Mix of owner, cooperative and social rental housing** → one of the few eco-villages where you can simply **rent**. [S1][S4]
- Farm with cattle, goats, hens, grain, vegetables; next to Aarhus. Weak on mountains and climate.

## Families & school

- ~90 children. Many families with children. [S4][S7] (group 5 alone: 27 adults, 14 children)
- Hårup Skole (public) in Hjortshøj; Børnenes Akademi friskole nearby; Kernehuset kindergarten in the old village school. [S6]

## Relation with locals

- Vegetables grown together with local people; open harvest market (7 Sept 2025), festival, shops open to all. [S1][S5]
- *Guess: well mixed; Hjortshøj is a commuter village for Aarhus.*

## Risks

- **No mountains** → our deal-breaker.
- Cool, wet climate; dark winters.
- Rental waiting lists (*guess*: long).
- Near a city: less "rural" feel, higher prices (*guess*).

## Not scored FYI

- Water: rainwater collection, willow evaporation beds, public water. [S1] Climate: temperate, cool.
