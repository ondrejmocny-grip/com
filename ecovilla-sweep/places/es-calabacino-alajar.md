# El Calabacino — Alájar, Sierra de Aracena (Huelva)

- **Status:** lead (existing, resident-led)
- **Location:** Hamlet El Calabacino, municipality of Alájar, Sierra de Aracena y Picos de Aroche Natural Park. Nearest town: Alájar (village), Aracena (market town). Seville airport *~1 h 15, guess*. Beaches (Huelva coast) *~1 h 30, guess*.
- **Website:** [ecoaldeas.org/el-calabacino](https://ecoaldeas.org/el-calabacino/) (RIE member). Contact: elcalabacino@gmail.com. [RIE members](https://ecoaldeas.org/miembros-de-la-rie/)
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **See also:** [community-sweep file](../../community-sweep/places/es-aracena-calabacino.md), [main place file](../../places/es-aracena-alajar.md)

## In short
- An old hamlet rebuilt by newcomers since the 1970s–90s. ~60 adults and **~50 children**. Private homes, no leader, sociocracy. The "family of families" version of Ecovilla, without a developer.
- Kids keep the Alájar village school open. Free school La Semilla is in the next valley.
- Main problems: Natural Park building rules (court cases since 2017), sea is far, few rentals.

## Families & school
- Half the people are children. They go to the Alájar public school with local kids. [Huelva24](https://www.huelva24.com/provincia/sierra/unica-ecoaldea-provincia-huelva-20240611033805-nth.html)
- Free school La Semilla (family-run, parent work expected); Montessori Sendas in Aracena. [ludus](https://ludus.org.es/es/sendas)

## Relation with locals
- Alájar council voted to support legalising El Calabacino as "rural habitat of social and ecological interest" (Junta must still ratify). [La Voz del Sur, 2021-10-31](https://www.lavozdelsur.es/actualidad/ecologia/calabacino-ecoaldea-en-mitad-parque-natural-camina-hacia-regularizacion_266812_102.html)
- Shared school, shared village life. *Good real contact, guess from the above.*

## Risks
- Legal: 2017 demolition files against 6 families; regularisation still not confirmed (no 2025–26 news found). [Diario de Huelva](https://www.diariodehuelva.es/articulo/provincia/alajar-abre-expedientes-derribo-seis-familias-la-ecoaldea-calabacino/20171226001414167208.html), [La otra Andalucía](https://laotraandalucia.org/huelva-los-vecinos-de-la-ecoaldea-de-el-calabacino-reafirman-los-beneficios-de-su-forma-de-vida-frente-a-las-multas-y-presiones/)
- Small place, few free houses. Sea is far (Nesli wants both).
