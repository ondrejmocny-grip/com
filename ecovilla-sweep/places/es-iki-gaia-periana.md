# IKI-GAIA ecovillage ("Healing Biotope") — Periana, Axarquía (Málaga)

- **Status:** planned (under construction since 2022; small pioneer group on site). *Claude's view: lead, but check carefully.*
- **Location:** Cortijo Puerto del Sol, 29710 Periana, Axarquía, Málaga. ~925 m altitude. Nearest town: Periana. Málaga city/airport *~1 h, guess*. Coast (Torre del Mar) *~40 min, guess*. [Semilla Monte Alegre](https://semillamontealegre.es/eco-village/approach-to-our-ecoldea-iki-gaia-project/), [Telegram channel](https://t.me/s/ecoaldeaikigaia)
- **Website:** [semillamontealegre.es](https://semillamontealegre.es/) (association "Semilla Monte Alegre"). iki-gaia.org = "in maintenance" (2026-09-28).
- **Added:** 2026-09-28

## In short
- A 240 ha estate with springs and oak forest. Plan: ecovillage in "tribes" of 25–30 people + "School of Life" for kids + healing / retreat centre + bakery, bar, music and permaculture academies.
- Run by the association Semilla Monte Alegre. Moved to Periana in July 2022. Active in 2026: courses, "experience weeks", 6-month resident co-creator program.
- Entry: €50/year member, then "rights of use" (incl. lifetime) or lender models. **No plots for sale.** Van / tent / yurt pitches exist.
