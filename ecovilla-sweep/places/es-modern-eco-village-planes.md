# The Modern Eco Village (MEV) — Planes, Alicante

- **Status:** planned (paper only; permits not final). *Claude's view: watch from far away, likely not for us.*
- **Location:** Planes (El Comtat, Alicante province), next to the Beniarrés reservoir. Nearest towns: Planes (~700 people), Cocentaina / Alcoy (*~20–30 min, guess*). Alicante airport ~1 h, Valencia ~1 h 30. [MEV FAQ](https://www.modernecovillage.com/faq)
- **Website:** [modernecovillage.com](https://www.modernecovillage.com/)
- **Added:** 2026-09-28

## In short
- The **closest copy of La Ecovilla in Spain**: a developer-led, masterplanned "eco-village" for 300–500 homes, with its own school, farms, restaurants and wellness centre.
- Led by a Norwegian influencer couple. Membership €15,000, homes €165k–€1.5M (+10% VAT). Show house autumn 2026, first residents 2027.
- Strong local opposition ("Salvem Planes"): water, a "closed colony", doubling the village. Built on the land of a failed 2006 bubble project.

## Flags
- 🏘️ Yes — a new planned settlement, not a village.
- 🔒 Yes — buy-in only (membership + purchase). No rent, lease or trial found. [FAQ](https://www.modernecovillage.com/faq)
- 💶 Yes — influencer marketing, villas up to €1.47M, "EMF-free" homes, bank mortgage partners (Santander). [House info](https://www.modernecovillage.com/houseinfo)
- 🏫 Spain = home schooling grey zone. The planned private school would solve it *if it opens*. See [legal overview](../../regions/legal-overview.md).

## Relation with locals
- Mayor Javier Sendra supports it ("we want eco growth"; fear of millionaire damages if refused). [Alicante Plaza](https://alicanteplaza.es/alicanteplaza/alcoy-el-comtat/la-macrourbanizacion-eco-village-de-planes-avanza-hasta-500-casas-pero-en-parcelas-mas-grandes), [El Español](https://www.elespanol.com/alicante/vivir/20260402/planes-pueblo-dividido-ecoaldea-noruega-familias-frente-temor-vecinal-macrourbanizacion/1003744192166_0.html)
- Platform **"Salvem Planes, protegim el Comtat"** (founded 2026-03-21, people from Planes, Beniarrés, Muro, l'Alqueria, Cocentaina). Concerns: water, sewage, pools in drought, "closed colony", Serpis river landscape. Calls for mass objections. [Alicante Plaza](https://alicanteplaza.es/alicanteplaza/alcoy-el-comtat/crean-una-plataforma-contra-la-macrourbanizacion-eco-village-en-planes-que-proyecta-hasta-500-casas), [El Nostre Ciutat](https://www.elnostreciutat.com/es/salvem-planes-busca-alegaciones-masivas-contra-la-ecoaldea/), [Pàgina 66](https://es.pagina66.com/art/134116/el-projecte-de-500-xalets-per-a-estrangers-al-panta-de-beniarres-genera-una-forta-oposicio-social)
- Planes has ~700 people; the project could **double** it. [Alicante Plaza](https://alicanteplaza.es/alicanteplaza/alcoy-el-comtat/crean-una-plataforma-contra-la-macrourbanizacion-eco-village-en-planes-que-proyecta-hasta-500-casas)
- *Guess: a foreign bubble next to a divided village. This hits our deal-breaker "no real contact with locals".*

## Risks
- Permits: PAI in public review; plenary vote + environmental report still pending (March 2026). [Alicante Plaza](https://alicanteplaza.es/alicanteplaza/alcoy-el-comtat/la-macrourbanizacion-eco-village-de-planes-avanza-hasta-500-casas-pero-en-parcelas-mas-grandes)
- History: same land = "La Joya de Planes" (2006), stopped in 2008 after €3M spent; fire and €132k fine. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Modern_Eco_Village)
- Paying €15k + deposits to a project with no permits yet.
- Water in a dry comarca (see below).

## Not scored FYI
- Water: MEV claims 2 wells, underground streams, rainwater and greywater ([FAQ](https://www.modernecovillage.com/faq)); neighbours fear drought. The Marina Alta next door had severe well shortages in 2024 and July 2026 (see [../../places/es-marina-alta-valls.md](../../places/es-marina-alta-valls.md)). Climate: 400–450 m, hot summers, mild winters (*guess*).
