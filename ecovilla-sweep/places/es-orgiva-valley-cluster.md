# Órgiva valley cluster (Alpujarra, Granada) — "Ecovilla without a developer"

- **Status:** lead (existing)
- **Location:** Órgiva (~7,000 people, ~450 m) and villages around (Soportújar, Cáñar, Carataunas, Bayacas, Lanjarón). Motril beach ~40 min, Granada ~1 h. [Euro Weekly News, 2025-12-01](https://euroweeklynews.com/2025/12/01/tiny-spanish-mountain-village-where-a-third-of-its-population-are-expats/)
- **Website:** no single project. Town program [alpujarraknowmads.com](https://www.alpujarraknowmads.com/en/town/orgiva/); school [arbolmadre.org](https://arbolmadre.org/)
- **Added:** 2026-09-28
- **See also:** [main place file](../../places/es-orgiva-alpujarra.md), [Beneficio (dropped)](../../places/es-beneficio-orgiva.md)

## In short
- Not one project. A **valley cluster** like Machuca Valley: a normal market town + many cortijos, retreats, eco farms, one legal Waldorf school, 72 nationalities.
- No developer and no lots with HOA. You rent, volunteer, lease or buy a normal finca. That is the path we want.
- No planned eco-neighbourhood near Órgiva found (search 2026-09-28).

## Families & school

## Relation with locals
- Mayor welcomes diversity. Only ~35% local-born. [Euro Weekly News](https://euroweeklynews.com/2025/12/01/tiny-spanish-mountain-village-where-a-third-of-its-population-are-expats/), [Olive Press, 2022](https://www.theolivepress.es/spain-news/2022/06/26/olive-press-meets-orgiva-mayor-raul-orellana-to-find-out-why-this-spanish-town-in-la-alpujarra-south-of-granada-is-the-worlds-best/)
- Acequia traditions (UNESCO recognition). [El Independiente de Granada](https://www.elindependientedegranada.es/economia/unesco-premia-regantes-alpujarra-sierra-buen-uso-agua-con-acequias-careo)

## Risks
- Expat / hippie bubble; drifting fringe (Beneficio police cases 2017–2024). [El Debate, 2024-07-06](https://www.eldebate.com/espana/andalucia/20240706/asi-comuna-hippie-orgiva-menores-desamparados-criminales-buscados-justicia_210940.html)
- Water in a multi-year drought; summer heat.

## Not scored FYI
- Water: snowmelt + acequias; very wet 2025–26 (Rules dam spilling, May 2026). [COPE, 2026-05-27](https://www.cope.es/emisoras/andalucia/granada-provincia/motril/noticias/paradoja-rules-presa-vierte-mar-agua-no-20260527_3372437.html). Climate: hot summers, mild winters.
