# Sabina Ibiza — "ecologically inspired" villa estate (Ibiza)

- **Status:** dropped (2026-09-28) — luxury real estate (villas €4.5M–€20M+), private and gated in spirit, no families/school/locals pattern. Show-off = our deal-breaker.
- **Location:** hilltop above Cala Tarida, west coast of Ibiza. Nearest town: Sant Josep de sa Talaia (*guess*). Ibiza airport *~25 min, guess*.
- **Website:** [sabinaibiza.com](https://www.sabinaibiza.com/)
- **Added:** 2026-09-28

## In short
- The only "eco-neighbourhood" found on the Balearic Islands that looks like a developer model. It is a luxury estate: 17 ha, villas by 14 star architects, clubhouse with spa, nightclub and "temple".
- Prices €4.5M to €20M+. Not a community for us. Kept only as a warning example of the E1/E2 model at its worst.
- No Mallorca / Ibiza family eco-neighbourhood found (search 2026-09-28). Mallorca "Ciutat d'Elles" = women 65+.
