# Écohameau du Plessis — Pontgouin, Eure-et-Loir, France

- **Status:** dropped (2026-09-28) — good pattern match, but **no mountains** (deal-breaker) and it sits next to an **Amma ashram-type centre** (guru risk).
- **Location:** Pontgouin, Eure-et-Loir (Centre-Val de Loire). *~1 h 30 from Paris, guess.*
- **Website:** https://ecohameauduplessis.fr/
- **Added:** 2026-09-28

## In short

1. Eco-hamlet for **28 families** on 4.3 ha. Started 2013, building since 2017. Spring 2025: 15 households live there (23 adults, 13 children). [S1][S2]
2. **Each family owns a private plot**; common buildings run by an ASL (landowners' association). Very close to the Ecovilla model (E1–E2). [S2]
3. Next to **Centre Amma – Ferme du Plessis** (spiritual centre of the Indian guru Amma, since 2002). The wider "ecosystem" has a Montessori school, market garden, senior housing. [S1][S2]

## Why dropped

1. **No mountains** — flat Beauce/Perche edge (deal-breaker in `../../us/criteria.md`).

3. Northern climate, cold grey winters; no sea.
4. Buy-in model (private plot), no lease.
