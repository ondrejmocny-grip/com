# Hameau des Buis — Lablachère, South Ardèche, France

- **Status:** lead
- **Location:** Plateau above the Chassezac gorges, next to the Bois de Païolive. Commune Lablachère (Ardèche, 07). School address is in Berrias-et-Casteljau. [S1][S5]
- **Nearest towns:** Lablachère, Joyeuse, Les Vans (*~10–15 km, guess*). The hamlet says "10 km from the nearest town". [S2]
- **Website:** https://hameaudesbuis.org/
- **Added:** 2026-09-28 (all facts seen on this date, unless another date is given)

## In short

1. Intergenerational eco-hamlet: 43 people, ~20 straw-earth homes, 6 ha, built 2006–2011. Since Jan 2023 a **housing co-op** (SAS SCOP). [S1][S3]
2. **Montessori school on site** (ages 3–12), goat dairy, market garden, bakery, café, grocery. [S1][S4][S5]
3. You **rent**, you don't buy. They were **looking for a family** for a free T3 flat (Oct 2025, open day Jan 2026). [S2][S4]

## Families & school

- École Montessori de Païolive: ages 3–12, in the hamlet. Registered with the Education Ministry. [S5][S12]
- Fees (seen in a directory): enrolment €50, primary ~€2,400/year, middle school ~€2,500/year. [S13] *Directory data, date unknown. Check.*
- The older school "La Ferme des Enfants" (Sophie Rabhi) **left** the hamlet after a court case in 2021. It restarted in 2025 at the **Oasis de Poul'Art**, near Toulouse (Rieumes). [S3][S14]
- They want families with kids. [S2]

## Relation with locals

- Public events: cinema-debate (Sept 2026), "Farm-to-Farm" (Apr 2026), Christmas market (Dec 2025). [S1]
- Grocery and café are open to neighbours (*guess from "épicerie associative", "café-bistro"*).
- *South Ardèche has many "néo-ruraux" since the 1970s. Guess: normal mix of locals and newcomers, not a bubble.*

## Risks

- **Water:** Beaume–Chassezac basin went to **drought "crisis" level in summer 2025**. In Sept 2026 it is at "alerte renforcée". [S15][S16] This touches our deal-breaker "drought / water shortage".
- **Past conflict:** 2018 governance fight between founders and residents; Sophie Rabhi resigned 2018; Ferme des Enfants expelled by court (notice March 2021, exit deal Oct 2021). Labour case about unpaid volunteers. [S3][S7][S17] Now new co-op and new school. *Guess: calmer now, but ask residents.*
- Small group (43 people). One free flat at a time.
- Wildfire (*Mediterranean garrigue and oak woods, guess*).
- Language: French only (*guess*). We are not fast in French.
