# Hameau léger de Rocles — South Ardèche, France (Hameaux Légers network)

- **Status:** planned (first residents planned **June–summer 2026**; *not checked if they moved in yet*)
- **Location:** Rocles, village of ~250 people in the Parc naturel régional des Monts d'Ardèche. [S3]
- **Nearest towns:** *Largentière and Joyeuse (~10–15 km, guess)*. *Hameau des Buis ~20–25 km (guess).*
- **Website:** https://hameaux-legers.org/projets/rocles (network: https://hameaux-legers.org/)
- **Added:** 2026-09-28

## In short

1. The **commune owns 1 ha** and leases it for **99 years** to a residents' association. 9 "reversible" homes (yurt, tiny house, wood cabin on light foundations) + 1 common building. [S1][S2]
2. Very cheap: land rent **~€90–140/month** per household + **~€100–150/month** for common house and costs. [S1]
3. Group ECLORS (5 households) is **recruiting, preferably young people and families**. [S1][S2]

This is the best example of the **Hameaux Légers** model in our area. The network has ~30 projects, most in Brittany (no mountains). Mountain / south projects: Rocles (07), Sablière–Dieulefit (26, **full**), Suze (26), Tiyourca–Le Bourget-du-Lac (73), Saint-Julien-Molin-Molette (42), Limogne-en-Quercy (46). [S4][S5][S6]

## Relation with locals

- Project comes **from the mayor** (Alain Gibert, since 2017) and village people, to bring young households and affordable homes. [S3]
- ⚠️ A local website "Un autre avenir pour Rocles" has a page titled "Éco hameau : un projet immobilier" (a critique). [S11] *We could not open it (site down). So: some local opposition exists. Ask.*

## Risks

- **Water:** South Ardèche rivers hit drought **crisis** level in summer 2025 (Beaume–Chassezac basin). [S12] *Rocles is in the Beaume area, guess.*
- Timeline may slip (Hameaux Légers projects often take years).
- Only 9 homes. Small group, all new.
- Land is "non-urbanisable": homes **must** stay reversible. [S2]
- Some local opposition (see above).
- Cold winters at altitude? *Rocles ~400–600 m, guess.*
