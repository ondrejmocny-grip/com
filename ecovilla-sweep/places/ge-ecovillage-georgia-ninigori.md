# Ecovillage Georgia — Ninigori, Lagodekhi (Georgia)

- **Status:** lead (young, resident-led, early building phase)
- **Location:** Ninigori village, Lagodekhi municipality, Kakheti. Foot of the Greater Caucasus. Nearest town: Lagodekhi. ~2.5 h from Tbilisi. [About land](https://ecovillagegeorgia.ge/about-land/)
- **Website:** https://ecovillagegeorgia.ge/
- **Added:** 2026-09-28

## In short
- A small, **resident-led** ecovillage on 6.5 ha, bought together in 2023. Georgian + international people. No developer, no guru.
- You join with a **refundable $6,000 loan** and get the use of a 300 m² plot. Vans and tents are OK at the start.
- **Weak on the key points:** few people (~10–20), few kids, school only "planned", no cluster of other projects nearby, sea is far.

## Flags
- 🏘️ Yes — intentional community, but inside a normal village.
- 🔒 No — open door: volunteers, guests, lenders, residents.
- 💶 No — not real-estate driven. Loans are refundable in 6–12 months. [site](https://ecovillagegeorgia.ge/)
- 🏫 Home schooling in Georgia: ✅ *probably legal* (independent study / "eksternat"). [HSLDA](https://hslda.org/post/georgia) *(verify)*

## Risks
- Very early. As of early 2026 people **do not yet live** there permanently (tents + community center). [GEN](https://ecovillage.org/ecovillage/ecovillage-georgia/)
- Small group → one conflict can break it.
- Since May 2025 Georgia limits foreign funding for NGOs → project runs on volunteers. [Böll](https://ge.boell.org/en/2025/12/24/organuli-da-regeneratsiuli-soplis-meurneobis-perspektivebi-sakartveloshi-ekosopeli)
- Political situation in Georgia (2024–2026) unstable (*guess, not checked here*).
- Residency: Czech and Turkish citizens can stay visa-free a long time (*guess: 1 year, verify*).
