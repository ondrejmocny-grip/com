# Cloughjordan Ecovillage — Ireland

- **Status:** lead (on hold: no new sites for sale)
- **Location:** Cloughjordan, Co. Tipperary (Irish midlands). Built as an extension of the existing town; train station 15 min walk. Dublin ~2 h. [S1][S2][S3]
- **Website:** https://www.thevillage.ie/
- **Added:** 2026-09-28

## In short

- Ireland's first ecovillage (charity Sustainable Projects Ireland, 1999; first residents 2009). 67 acres, 55 low-carbon homes, ~100 people. [S1][S2][S4]
- Closest in our area to the Ecovilla **structure**: masterplan, **private sites + shared land**, community farm, enterprise centre, hostel — and **inside a normal town**. [S1][S2]
- **~47–50 serviced sites wait**, but cannot be sold: the town's wastewater plant is full (April 2026 register: no spare capacity). [S2][S5]

## Families & school

- Children have "two primary schools and many activities within walking distance". [S6]
- No alternative school found. Home schooling is legal → an option.

## Relation with locals

- Many town-hall meetings with the town since 2003; farm supplies local families; shops on Main Street. [S1][S6]
- *Guess: best local integration of all projects in this sweep.*

## Risks

- **No new sites** for years (*guess*) — depends on Uisce Éireann funding. [S5]
- Midlands: **no mountains in view** (Arra / Slieve Bloom hills ~30 km, *guess*), sea ~1.5 h.
- Wet, grey climate; high Irish house prices.

## Not scored FYI

- Water: plenty of rain; limit is wastewater capacity, not supply. Climate: mild, wet, cool summers.
