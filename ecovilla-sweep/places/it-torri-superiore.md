# Ecovillaggio Torri Superiore — Bevera valley, Liguria, Italy

- **Status:** lead
- **Location:** Torri Superiore, a hamlet of Ventimiglia (Imperia), Bevera valley. 11 km from Ventimiglia, 16 km from the French border. [S1][S2]
- **Nearest town:** Ventimiglia (sea). *Nice airport ~50 min (guess).*
- **Website:** https://www.torri-superiore.org/
- **Added:** 2026-09-28

## In short

1. A 13th-century stone village, rebuilt by a cultural association since 1989. **22 private flats (30–80 m²)** owned by members + a **common part** (guesthouse, kitchen, dining rooms, library) owned by the association. [S3]
2. About **20–22 residents, 7–8 of them kids/teens**. Weekly meeting, consensus. [S4][S2]
3. Open door: eco-guesthouse, volunteers, European projects. Part of the local "Terre di Confine" farm network. [S5][S6]

## Families & school

- 8 kids and teens live there [S4]. No school on site.
- *Public schools in Ventimiglia. No alternative school found nearby. Home schooling legal in Italy.*

## Relation with locals

- Works with local farmers (Terre di Confine network) [S4][S6].
- Social co-op SPES (disabled people) is a partner [S6].
- The area was dying: SNAI "inner area" municipalities; population recovered to ~290 in 2024 (paper) [S2].
- *Guess: good contact with valley people, but the group itself is a small Italian-speaking island.*

## Risks

- Very small group. Joining = consensus of all.
- No school, few families.
- Steep, narrow valley. Little flat land for our ~1 ha + animals (*guess*).
- Wildfire (*Liguria, guess*). Flood risk in Liguria valleys (*guess*).
- Busy border / mass tourism on the coast (Riviera), not in the valley.

## Not scored FYI

- **Water:** Bevera river and springs (*guess: OK, but Liguria had drought in 2022*). **Climate:** mild Mediterranean coast, one of the mildest in Italy (*guess*) ✅ Nesli. **Sea + mountains:** both, very close ✅✅.
