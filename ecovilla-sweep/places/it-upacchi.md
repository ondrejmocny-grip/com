# Ecovillaggio Upacchi — Anghiari, Valtiberina, Tuscany, Italy

- **Status:** lead (weak — most facts are old, 2014–2020)
- **Location:** Upacchi, a stone hamlet in the hills of the upper Tiber valley, commune of Anghiari (Arezzo). 6 km from Anghiari. [S1][S2]
- **Nearest towns:** Anghiari, Sansepolcro. *Arezzo ~40 min, Florence airport ~1 h 45, sea ~2 h (guess).*
- **Website:** http://www.eccoupacchi.eu/ (*not checked*), blog www.relazioniinecologia.blogspot.com [S1][S3]
- **Added:** 2026-09-28

## In short

1. **The closest thing to "Ecovilla" in Italy that we found**: one founder (Elmar Zadra) bought an abandoned hamlet in 1990 and made it an eco-village. [S1]
2. In 2002 the co-op was closed. Since then each family **owns its house and part of the land**; the common house, spring and parking stay common. [S1][S4]
3. 17 homes, 70 ha (50 ha forest), 12 families living there + 4 second homes, from Italy, Switzerland, Germany, Austria, UK. [S1][S3]

- Summer: concerts and parties in the hamlet square [S1].

## Risks

- **Old data** (RIVE page 2014, Italia che Cambia update 2020). May have changed a lot.
- Low energy as a group; "failure as a collective" said a visitor [S4].
- No school, no cluster → weak on our key points E4 / E10.
- Winter: hills, *~500–600 m, cold-ish (guess)*.
