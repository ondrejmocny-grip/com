# Madrasa Dunya WorldSchool hub — Belhouane, Ourika valley (Morocco)

- **Status:** lead (for the school / family side only — not an eco-neighbourhood)
- **Location:** Belhouane, an Amazigh village in the Ourika valley, High Atlas. Nearest towns: Tnine Ourika (Monday souk), Tahannaout. *~1 h from Marrakech (guess).* [NomadSchoolers](https://nomadschoolers.com/madrasa-dunya-worldschool-morocco)
- **Website:** https://www.md-worldschoolmorocco.com/ — info@md-worldschoolmorocco.com, +212 660 243 882
- **Added:** 2026-09-28

## In short
- The only real **"school + families" hub** we found in Morocco. Waldorf + Montessori + democratic learning, ages 1–18.
- Families come for 2–8 weeks or 1 year. There is a **camping area for vans**. It is a gated complex, not an ecovillage with plots.
- Good as a **trial stay** with kids, and as a node in the Ourika "cluster". Not a place to buy land into.

## Flags
- 🏘️ Partly — a gated family hub, not a village.
- 🔒 ⚠️ Gated complex ("all facilities within a gated complex"). Open to anyone who pays. [NomadSchoolers](https://nomadschoolers.com/madrasa-dunya-worldschool-morocco)
- 💶 No real-estate sales. Normal paid school/stay.
- 🏫 Morocco: ⚠️ no home-school law for foreigners; tolerated. School duty is for Moroccan kids. [Expat Exchange](https://www.expatexchange.com/ctryguide/20958/81/Morocco/Education-in-Morocco-Homeschooling-in-Morocco)

## Relation with locals
- Inside a real Amazigh village, local facilitators, cultural exchange. [NomadSchoolers](https://nomadschoolers.com/madrasa-dunya-worldschool-morocco)
- ⚠️ Gated complex + pools = *risk of a foreigner bubble inside a poor village (guess)*.
- ⚠️ Language: Tashelhit, Darija, French. We speak none well.

## Risks
- Transient families → hard to build long friendships.
- Depends on one small business.
- Al Haouz earthquake zone (Sept 2023). Flash floods in Ourika (1995). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ourika_Valley)
- No sea nearby → fails Nesli's "sea + mountains".

## Not scored FYI
- Water: snow-fed river valley (better than most of Morocco). Climate: hot lower valley in summer, *cool nights higher up (guess)*.
