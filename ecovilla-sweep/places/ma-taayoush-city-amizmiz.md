# Taayoush City ("Amizmiz Eco Village") — Amizmiz, Al Haouz (Morocco)

- **Status:** dropped — planned since 2016, **no sign it was built**; too big (≈3,000 homes); no lease path; 2023 earthquake zone.
- **Location:** Near Amizmiz, Province of Al Haouz. 53–55 km south of Marrakech. Nearest town: Amizmiz. [site](https://www.taayoushcity.ma/en/)
- **Website:** https://www.taayoushcity.ma/en/ (developer: Earth Development; tel. +212 661 17 45 69)
- **Added:** 2026-09-28

## In short
- A **developer-led "100% ecological city"** on 44 ha: solar, earth building, wastewater plant, school and farm in later phases.
- Presented in Marrakech in **October 2016** (COP22 side event). We found **no news after 2016**. Prices and sale model are not published.
- It is a new town for thousands, not an eco-neighbourhood with shared nature. Not for us.

## Risks
- Stalled: only 2016 press. [Article19, 2016-10-18](https://article19.ma/accueil/archives/51083), [Atalayar, 2016-10-26](https://www.atalayar.com/en/articulo/new-technologies-innovation/marruecos-proyecta-construir-una-ciudad-totalmente-sostenible/20161026080502162209.html)
