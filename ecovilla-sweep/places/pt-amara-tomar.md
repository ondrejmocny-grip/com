# Amara (Nexus Villages) — Castelo do Bode, Tomar (Portugal)

- **Status:** planned (building permits given; homes pre-sold; not finished) — **FYI only, not for us**
- **Location:** Vila Nova, Serra de Tomar, on the Castelo do Bode reservoir (lake). Nearest town: Tomar (*~20–30 min, guess*). Lisbon airport ~1 h 20.
- **Website:** https://amaravillage.co · https://www.liveinamara.com
- **Added:** 2026-09-28

## In short
- The **most Ecovilla-like project in Portugal**: one company, one masterplan, **193 homes** on ~50 ha, "alternative school on-site", wellness campus by the lake.
- Developer-led and **sales-driven** (paid ads, "82% pre-booked", call with "community curators"). Prices not published.
- Good as a model. Bad for us: buy-in only, likely far above budget, no lease / van path, no track record yet.

## Flags
- 🏘️ Yes — planned eco-neighbourhood.
- 🔒 Yes — buy-in via application; no lease found.
- 💶 **Strong.** Heavy paid ads; a writer (Feb 2026) calls the marketing "cult-like" (she says it is *not* a cult). A comment in local news calls it a "scam" and mentions **€10,000 non-refundable deposits** (*unverified comment, not a fact*). ## Relation with locals
- Mayor of Tomar supports it. Quercus (environmental NGO) asks about safeguards. Old project idea (~30 years, earlier names Urbicastelo / Urbilaguna). Reservoir master plan still under review. [Tomar na Rede, 2026-06-25](https://tomarnarede.pt/economia/sic-emite-reportagem-sobre-mega-projeto-na-albufeira-de-castelo-do-bode/)
- *Guess: a new foreign-buyer village on a lake; locals will be mostly workers and neighbours. Bubble risk high.*

## Risks
- Not built. Environmental impact and lake master plan not settled. Deposits before completion.
- Real-estate marketing, no lease, price likely over budget.
- Castelo do Bode = Lisbon's main drinking-water reservoir → strict rules (*guess*).
