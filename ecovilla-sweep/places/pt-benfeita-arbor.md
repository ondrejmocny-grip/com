# Benfeita valleys + ArBOR + Folha Verde (Arganil, Portugal)

- **Status:** lead (resident-led cluster, existing)
- **Location:** Benfeita parish, Serra do Açor, Arganil municipality (Coimbra district). 12 villages on ~21.8 km² (Luadas, Pardieiros, Sardal, Esculca…). Nearest towns: Côja and Arganil (*~15–25 min, guess*). Coimbra *~1 h (guess)*.
- **Website:** https://arborbenfeita.org · https://folhaverde.pt
- **Added:** 2026-09-28

## In short
- Not one developer project. A **valley of normal mountain villages** where 200+ newcomer adults and ~70 children live, mixed with Portuguese neighbours.
- They run their own **learning community (Folha Verde)**, a food co-op, a tool bank and a forest festival, under one association (**ArBOR**).
- The closest Portuguese match to Ecovilla's "school + families + cluster" (E4, E5, E10), but **without** private lots, HOA or developer. Big risk: **wildfire**.

- 💶 No — no developer, no sales marketing found.

## Families & school
- Folha Verde: ages 3–14, bilingual, small groups. Fees not published. [folhaverde.pt](https://folhaverde.pt/)
- Old Waldorf kindergarten Caracol ao Sol (Barril de Alva) closed in 2020. [caracolaosol.org](http://caracolaosol.org/)
- Other school cluster ~1 h away: Penela / Espinhal (see [pt-penela-espinhal-schools.md](pt-penela-espinhal-schools.md)). *(guess on time)*

## Relation with locals
- Parish became an Eco-Freguesia **together** with the newcomers (2019). [permaculturinginportugal](https://www.permaculturinginportugal.net/projects/community/)
- After the August 2025 fire: "the fire reduced cultural divisions"; locals "trust us more" (ArBOR president). [Campeão das Províncias, 2025-09-02](https://www.campeaoprovincias.pt/2025/09/02/comunidade-da-benfeita-defende-reflorestacao-com-especies-autoctones-apos-incendio-de-agosto/)
- *Local Portuguese population is small and old. Guess.*

## Risks
- ⚠️⚠️ **Wildfire**: Oct 2017 (22 families lost homes) and Aug 2025 (Arganil fire, 64,451 ha, largest on record). [Renascença, 2025-08-25](https://rr.pt/noticia/pais/2025/08/25/fogo-de-arganil-com-a-maior-area-ardida-desde-que-ha-registos/437325/)
- Nearby **Awakened Life Project** (Quinta da Mizarela) has a "spiritual director" and non-dual teachings. Fine as a neighbour, but not a place for us to join. [Fire of the Heart](https://thefireoftheheart.com/awakened-life-project/), [pureportugal](https://pureportugal.co.uk/blogs/quinta-da-mizarela/)
- Few locals; expat language bubble.
- No rental market data found.
