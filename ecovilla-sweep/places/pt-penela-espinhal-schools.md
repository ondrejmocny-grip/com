# Penela – Espinhal – Rabaçal school cluster (Portugal)

- **Status:** lead (resident-led cluster around two schools, existing)
- **Location:** Penela municipality, Coimbra district. Villages Espinhal and Rabaçal. Nearest towns: Penela, Lousã. Coimbra *~30 min (guess)*.
- **Website:** https://www.cerejeiras.eu · https://www.waterfallvalleyed.org
- **Added:** 2026-09-28

## In short
- Two parent-run **alternative learning communities** pull international families into normal Portuguese villages.
- Families live in rented or bought village houses, not in one site. The school is the "village centre".
- Strong on E4/E5, weak on shared land and shared buildings. Close to Coimbra (hospital, train).
