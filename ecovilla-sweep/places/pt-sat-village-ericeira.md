# Sat Village — near Ericeira / Sintra area (Portugal)

- **Status:** planned (pre-sale from Q4 2025; "community selection now open"; building not finished) — **FYI only**
- **Location:** hills near Ericeira; one source says "area of Sintra". Exact village not published. *Guess: Mafra or Sintra municipality.* Sea 10 min, Lisbon airport 35 min.
- **Website:** https://satvillage.com (captcha, could not read directly) · https://satvillage-sparkle.lovable.app/
- **Added:** 2026-09-28

## In short
- A small developer-style **"eco-town" of 18 private homes**, 2–4 bedrooms, car-free inner path, green roofs, next to a protected forest.
- Classic Ecovilla legal model: **buy house + land**, join an **owners association**, pay a **monthly fee** for shared facilities.
- Close to the Ericeira/Sintra alternative scene, but in one of the most expensive areas of Portugal. Almost surely over budget.
