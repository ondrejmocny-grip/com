# Tamera — Healing Biotope I, Odemira (Portugal)

- **Status:** dropped (deal-breaker: closed-ish community with a strong founders' ideology)
- **Location:** Monte do Cerro, 7630-392 Relíquias, Odemira (Alentejo). Near São Martinho das Amoreiras and Colos. Lisbon / Faro *~2 h (guess)*.
- **Website:** https://www.tamera.org
- **Added:** 2026-09-28

## In short
- Famous "Peace Research & Education Centre", founded 1978 in Germany, in Portugal since 1995. ~160 members (2026), ~134 ha, famous lakes ("Water Retention Landscape").
- It has many Ecovilla points: shared land, food, buildings, own school, children. But it is a **commune with its own world-view**, not a neighbourhood you lease into.
- Keep only as a **hub and a place to learn** (water, courses). Do not plan to live inside.
