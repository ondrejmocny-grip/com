# Zaježová ("Zaježka") — Slovakia

- **Status:** lead (needs a fresh check — most facts are from 2014–2018)
- **Location:** Scattered hill farms ("lazy") of Zaježová, part of Pliešovce municipality, near Poľana mountains, central Slovakia. *Zvolen ~20–25 km (guess).* [S1][S2]
- **Website:** https://zajezka.sk/
- **Added:** 2026-09-28

## In short

- Not a planned project. An old, emptied farm hamlet where **newcomers and old locals** live side by side. ~200 inhabitants; ~80 adults + ~45 children in the "Zaježka community". [S2][S3]
- Many small independent projects: ecovillage **Sekier** (since 1994), **education centre** (NGO Živica), a **community-restored mini school** (grades 1–4, since 2007), forest kindergarten, food bank, natural building. [S1][S4][S5]
- Best nature match in our area (mountains, springs). Weak: cold winters, no sea, small scale.

## Families & school

- One-class school + forest kindergarten (2–4 h outside daily). [S1][S4]
- Main school in Pliešovce village. [S6]

## Relation with locals

- Old locals (mostly retirees) and newcomers live together; newcomers record old people's memories with local children. [S1][S3]
- Closest to our wish "integrated in a normal village".

## Risks

- **Cold, snowy winters** (hill farms ~600–700 m, *guess*) → only ~5–6 good months.
- **No sea.**
- Data is old; school may have closed. Must visit.
- Small scale: few services; car needed.
- Slovak language needed (close to Czech → easy for Ondrej).
