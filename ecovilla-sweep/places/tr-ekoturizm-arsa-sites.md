# "Eko köy" plot and villa sites under eco-tourism zoning — Kaz Dağları / Assos / Datça (Turkey) 💶

- **Status:** dropped — real-estate greenwashing; legally you **cannot make them your home**; no community, no school.
- **Location:** Many sites. Examples: Akçin (Ayvacık), Bayramiç, Assos (Behramkale), Sofular (Yenice) in Çanakkale; Hızırşah village in **Datça**.
- **Website:** examples — [Land Art Assos Panorama](https://landart.com.tr/ekolojik-koylerimiz/land-art-assos-panorama/), [Nef Arsa Kaz Dağları](https://www.nef.com.tr/projeler/nef-arsa-kaz-daglari/), [Sofular Eko Köy](https://www.haritahaber.com/haber/kazdaglari-eteklerinde-surdurulebilir-yasam-sofular-eko-koy-projesi_3829/), Hızırşah Eko Turizm Geliştirme Kooperatifi ([search result](https://www.hizirsahekolojikkoyu.com/kopyasi-koey-hakkinda), site offline 2026-09-28)
- **Added:** 2026-09-28

## In short
- Since ~2020 many companies and "eco-tourism co-ops" sell **small parcels or shares** (300–1,000 m²) as "eko köy", "ekolojik yaşam köyü" or "tiny house village".
- The land has **eco-tourism zoning**. The law allows tourism use, **not private homes**. Experts call many of them "villa projects under the name of eco-tourism" (May 2026).
- This is the Turkish version of the Ecovilla money model (E1 + E2), without the good parts (school, families, shared life).

## Why it fails (legal, honest)
- "Ecological tourism co-operative" is not a real legal type. The real type is "Turizm Geliştirme Kooperatifi". **Members cannot get a house or holiday home** from it; splitting tourism buildings into private units for sale is **not allowed**. [MuhasebeTR, 2022-04-18](https://www.muhasebetr.com/yazarlarimiz/evrenozmen/0221/)
- Farmland bought at 150–250 TL/m² is resold at 7,000–10,000 TL/m². Courts cancelled some eco-tourism plans in 2021 ("no public benefit"). A 2025 rule asks for min. 25,000 m² parcels. [Turizm Ekonomi, 2026-05-29](https://www.turizmekonomi.com/assosta-ekoturizm-planiyla-villa-projeleri-yapiliyor)
- Shares are often **hisseli tapu** (shared title) → hard to sell, disputes. *(guess from market practice; check each deed)*
