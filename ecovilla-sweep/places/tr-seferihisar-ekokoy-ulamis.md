# Seferihisar Ekolojik Yaşam Köyü ("Ekoköy") — Ulamış, Seferihisar (Turkey)

- **Status:** lead (weak) — only as a **rent-first base** near the Urla–Seferihisar scene. Water and legal doubts.
- **Location:** Ulamış neighbourhood (Özyaren "küme evler"), Seferihisar, İzmir. ~3 km from Seferihisar town, ~10 km to Sığacık / Akarca beaches. [Emlakjet listing](https://www.emlakjet.com/ilan/ulamis-ekokoy-de-gol-manzarali-sirin-21-mustakil-ev-19697755)
- **Website:** no official site found. Instagram [@ekokoyseferihisar](https://www.instagram.com/ekokoyseferihisar/). Legal body: *S.S. Ekolojik Yaşam Köyü Arsa ve Konut Yapı Kooperatifi*. [yenifirma](https://yenifirma.com.tr/firma/sinirli-sorumlu-ekolojik-yasam-koyu-arsa-ve-konut-yapi-kooperatifi)
- **Added:** 2026-09-28

## In short
- A **housing cooperative** "eco village": ~100 detached houses on ~120 dönüm (~12 ha) next to the Ulamış irrigation pond, with shared green areas, lake, café, trails.
- Houses are **rented and sold on normal portals** → easy rent-first entry.
- "Eco" is mostly a label (heat pumps, gardens). No school, no shared food system, no community life found. Legal fights since 2018.

## Flags
- 🏘️ Yes — a planned housing estate ("site"), not a village.
- 🔒 ⚠️ Not gated as far as we know, but it is a closed co-op estate.
- 💶 ⚠️ Partly — "eko köy" label on a normal co-op estate; houses sold by estate agents.
- 🏫 Turkey: ❌ **home schooling illegal** for Turkish kids (our kids would be Turkish). [HSLDA](https://hslda.org/post/turkey)

## Families & school
- Children's park on site. No school.

## Relation with locals
- Next to Ulamış village and Seferihisar town; Sunday Sığacık market with village women producers. [Seferihisar Belediyesi](https://seferihisar.bel.tr/tepecik-pazari-acildi/)
- ⚠️ Local irrigation co-op (Ulamış Sulama Kooperatifi) opposed the project: 100 houses built in the irrigation pond area, fear of water pollution; questions about permits. [Gündem35](https://www.gundemotuzbes.com/seferihisar-al-sana-eko-koyden-ekolojik-manzaralar/37015) *(article dated 2018 per search)*
- We speak Turkish → easy daily contact.

## Risks
- **Water:** İzmir's main dam fell to 0.13% (Dec 2025), night water cuts in 2025. [İZSU](https://izsu.gov.tr/haberler/tahtali-yukselise-gecti-gece-kesintileri-sona-erdi)
- **Fire:** 2025 fires in Seferihisar area. [Wikipedia TR](https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_T%C3%BCrkiye_orman_yang%C4%B1nlar%C4%B1)
- Legal: co-op vs. contractor court case; permit doubts. Check title deed (tapu) before any purchase.
- Low hills only. Main file dropped Urla–Seferihisar for water + fire + low hills: [tr-seferihisar-urla.md](../../places/tr-seferihisar-urla.md).
