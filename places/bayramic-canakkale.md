## In short
Our first example of what we like. People came from cities (Turkish and international) to live a holistic, ecological life.

## What we like
- People from cities who **build projects**: retreats, bungalows, eco farming.
- Mix of **international and local** newcomers.
- **Locals** are authentic and kind, and help. (We don't have a deep connection with them.)
- Landscape is very good.

## What we don't like
- **Land prices are high.**
- Landscape is not fully to our taste. We want **more mountains in sight**.
- Neither is a big problem.
