## In short
A ski town under high, wild mountains (Pirin National Park, UNESCO) that became Europe's best-known **digital nomad hub**. Since ~2023 a group of **worldschooling / unschooling families** lives around Bansko and in the village **Obidim**, with self-made learning spaces. Home schooling is **legal** in Bulgaria. Land is cheap. **But:** winters are cold and snowy, the sea is far, and the eco / spiritual project scene is thin.

**Órgiva pattern:** 5/8 points — ✅ real market town (1), ✅ international newcomers (2), ✅ spread out in villages (3), ✅ weekly Sunday market in Bansko + Monday market in Razlog (5), ✅ young families with self-made alternative learning (6). Partial: easy start (8 — cheap rent, nomad infrastructure, van grey). ❌ projects (4 — few eco / healing projects found), ❌ sea close (7).

## People
- Nomads: Bansko Nomad Fest had ~700 participants in 2023; many coworkings. Community is biggest in winter and shoulder seasons. [nomads.com](https://nomads.com/bansko), [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/10104237/digital-nomad-village-bansko-bulgaria/)
- Families: Israeli, British, Bulgarian, Maltese and other families; "ownschooling", unschooling, umbrella schools. **Ubuntu Hub** (Obidim, art-therapy based, screen-free, ages 4–11, 2–3 days a week), **Craft Café** in Bansko, **Ubuntu Family Camp** in August. Some stay all year, some seasonal. [Remote Work Europe, 2025-07-31, verified 2026-07](https://www.remoteworkeurope.eu/insights/bansko-obidim-worldschooling)
- Spiritual: *not much found in articles. Yoga and hot springs yes; healing / ceremony scene unknown. Guess.*
- Grounded? *Nomads = practical, tech people; families = grounded. Some "party nomad" side. Guess.*

## Integration
- Honest article: "you can spend a month there speaking only English … and leave without a single Bulgarian friend." [Remote Work Europe, 2026-08](https://remoteworkeurope.eu/insights/bansko-digital-nomad-community/)
- Families report taking part in local village life (Obidim). [Remote Work Europe](https://www.remoteworkeurope.eu/insights/bansko-obidim-worldschooling)
- Traditions: old Bansko stone town, Orthodox churches, Pirin folk music, Bansko Jazz Festival. *Guess on intensity.* [Wikipedia – Bansko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bansko)
- Markets: Bansko Sunday farmers market (cheese, honey, vegetables) + flea market; Razlog Monday market, cheaper. [vacationbansko](https://www.vacationbansko.co.uk/home/bansko-sunday-market), [bulgariajourney](https://bulgariajourney.com/where-to-shop-in-bansko-markets-boutiques-more/)

## How we could join
- Rent: summer is the cheap season (ski town). *Easy to rent a flat or village house. Guess.*
- Join families: Ubuntu Hub / family camp as a first contact.
- Van: ⚠️ sleeping in a camper 21:00–07:00 outside campsites banned since 2021 (fine €60–600), *rarely enforced inland; on private land with owner's OK = probably fine. Guess.* [bulgariahiddengems](https://www.bulgariahiddengems.com/post/camping-rules-in-bulgaria)
- Buy: Ondrej (EU) can buy land in own name; Nesli only via a Bulgarian company. [ELRA](https://www.elra.eu/contact-point-contribution/bulgaria/limitations-to-foreigners/)
- Contribute: a real gap here — **healing, yoga, retreat, food projects**. Nesli's skills + Ondrej's community building could fill it.

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ✅ legal since 2016 ("independent form" — enrol at a school, committee approves, exams). [HSLDA](https://hslda.org/post/bulgaria). Also umbrella schools used by families here.
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ see above. Yurt: *building permit likely. Guess.*
- Wildfire: ⚠️ medium. July 2025: Bulgaria's worst fire (4,500+ ha) near Ilindentsi, **south Pirin** (*~50 km south, guess*). [BTA](https://www.bta.bg/en/news/bulgaria/940748-all-major-wildfires-in-bulgaria-contained-pirin-blaze-under-control-says-fire-), [BTA – records](https://www.bta.bg/en/news/economy/1172235-forest-fires-set-new-records-across-four-continents-bulgaria-among-worst-hit-in)
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ⚠️ yes — nomad bubble + mass ski tourism in winter (we would be away then).

## Water (honest)
- High mountains (Vihren 2,914 m) with snow and many glacial lakes; rain ~700 mm/year. [weather-and-climate](https://weather-and-climate.com/average-monthly-Rainfall-Temperature-Sunshine,bansko,Bulgaria)
- *No drought problem found for the Razlog valley. Water looks good. Guess — check village supply in August.*

## Climate
- Jan: high ~2 °C, low ~−6 °C. Jul–Aug highs ~25–26 °C. Snow cover Dec–Apr. May is the wettest month (~127 mm); August driest (~31 mm). ~111 rain days a year. [weather-and-climate](https://weather-and-climate.com/average-monthly-Rainfall-Temperature-Sunshine,bansko,Bulgaria), [search summary: weather-atlas](https://www.weather-atlas.com/en/bulgaria/bansko-climate)
- **Verdict: 🟡 Borderline.** Snow-free May–Nov, but May–June are rainy. Good months: *July–October. Guess.* Summer is **not hot** (good for Nesli). Winter is hard — we would travel then.

## Costs (2026)
- Bansko houses ~€2,000/m² in prime spots; Razlog / outskirts €800–1,100/m². [banskoestates](https://banskoestates.com/bansko-property-market-2026-news-prices-trends-and-tips/), [investropa](https://investropa.com/blogs/news/bulgaria-bansko-property-overpriced)
- Village houses in Bulgaria generally €18–39k. [bulgarianrural-properties](https://bulgarianrural-properties.com/en/204). *Obidim / Dobrinishte likely higher because of Bansko. Guess.*
