# Troodos villages (Kalopanagiotis, Kakopetria, Platres) — Cyprus

- **Status:** dropped — **water**: worst drought in 100+ years (dams 13.7% in Feb 2026, ~38% in Sept 2026). Also **home schooling not allowed** and **no newcomer / eco scene found**.
- **Country / region:** Cyprus, Troodos mountains (Marathasa and Solea valleys, Limassol hills). Villages: Kalopanagiotis, Kakopetria, Platres, Pedoulas. Also checked: Akamas / Paphos hills (same water problem).

## In short
Green, cool mountain villages with Byzantine churches (UNESCO) and agritourism. Nice for summer. **But** Cyprus has a severe water crisis, alternative newcomers are hard to find, and home schooling is only for sick / disabled children.

**Órgiva pattern:** 2/8 points — ✅ mountains + sea (7), partial: real villages (1 — small, tourist). ❌ newcomers, spread-out scene, projects, market, alternative schools, easy start: *none found. Guess from searches 2026-09-27.*

## Water (honest) — deal-breaker
- Worst drought in over 100 years; dams at record low **13.7%** in Feb 2026; people asked to cut use by 10%; 14 mobile desalination plants added. [Euronews, 2026-01-26](https://www.euronews.com/2026/01/26/cyprus-water-emergency-citizens-urged-to-reduce-water-use-as-dams-reach-record-low), [Smart Water Magazine](https://smartwatermagazine.com/news/smart-water-magazine/cyprus-confronts-a-record-drought-reservoirs-run-dry)
- Rain in May 2026 helped: dams ~42% in June, **~37–38% in Sept 2026** (still "low"). [Cyprus Mail, 2026-06-02](https://cyprus-mail.com/2026/06/02/dam-levels-almost-double-those-of-last-year), [fragmata.info](https://fragmata.info/)
