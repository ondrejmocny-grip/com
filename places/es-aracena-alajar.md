## In short
Green, low mountains with chestnut, cork oak and holm oak forests, and old stone villages. Since the 1970s–90s, Spanish and foreign newcomers rebuilt the abandoned hamlet **El Calabacino** (~60 adults, ~50 children). Around it: a free school, rural schools with "learning community" methods, small farms. More Spanish and more family-oriented than Órgiva. Water is better than most of Andalusia, but 2023 showed it is not safe.

## People
- El Calabacino: ~60 adults + ~50 children, founded as ecovillage 1992, calls itself **"a family of families"** — not one project, but families in their own houses. Workshops: music, circus, nonviolent communication, permaculture, bio-building. [RIE](https://ecoaldeas.org/el-calabacino/), [Huelva24, 2024-06-11](https://www.huelva24.com/provincia/sierra/unica-ecoaldea-provincia-huelva-20240611033805-nth.html)
- Newcomers from Spain, Europe and the world replaced the old people who left in the 1960s–70s. Barter, bread, gardens, crafts, music. [Wikipedia ES](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Calabacino)
- Spiritual orientation: *earth-based, artistic, low-key. No leader found. Guess.*
- Grounded: yes — families, school, legal fight organized together.
- Academic study (2024) on neorrurales in Fuenteheridos and tourism shows newcomers are a known group in the whole Sierra. [AIBR, 2024-08-19](https://aries.aibr.org/articulo/2024/19/6393/los-neorrurales-frente-al-turismo-de-base-local-y-la-crisis-rural-estudio-de-caso-en-la-sierra-de-aracena-andalucia-espana)

## Integration
- Alájar town council **voted to support** El Calabacino's legalisation as "dispersed rural habitat of social and ecological interest" (waiting for the Junta). This shows good local relations. [La Voz del Sur, 2021-10-31](https://www.lavozdelsur.es/actualidad/ecologia/calabacino-ecoaldea-en-mitad-parque-natural-camina-hacia-regularizacion_266812_102.html)
- Strong local traditions: Iberian ham, chestnut harvest, romerías (Peña de Arias Montano in Alájar), stone paths. *Guess from general knowledge.*
- The Sierra takes part in "Vente a vivir a un pueblo" to attract new residents. [Huelva24, 2024-02-03](https://www.huelva24.com/provincia/sierra/vente-vivir-pueblo-sierra-aracena-incorpora-plataforma-20240203105853-nth.html)

- Rent a village house or finca nearby; get to know Calabacino families. *Guess — ask on site.*
- Later: buy a small finca with a **legal well (pozo legalizado)**. Listings: 4,000 m² chestnut plot with legal well in Galaroza; 7 ha with legal well; 5 ha in Alájar. [search summary of idealista / milanuncios, 2026-09-27](https://www.idealista.com/venta-terrenos/aracena-huelva/)
- Contribute: yoga, cooking, animal care, community building, education (La Semilla is run with parent volunteer work).

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ⚠️ grey zone in Spain. Options here: **Escuela La Semilla** (free school since 2005, Castaño del Robledo, self-managed by families) — *check if it is officially registered; many free schools are not, guess*. Public rural schools **CPR Tresfuentes (Alájar)** and CPR Adersa 1 (Castaño del Robledo) use "learning community" methods. Sendas / Serendipias (Montessori) in Aracena. [ludus Huelva](https://ludus.org.es/es/projects?province_id=5)
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️⚠️ **strict — Natural Park.** 2017: the environmental prosecutor charged several El Calabacino families for "crimes against territorial planning": fines, demolition orders, prison sentences (suspended). The judge gave a **5-year moratorium** to regularise. [Wikipedia ES](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Calabacino), [Diario de Huelva, 2017-12-26](https://www.diariodehuelva.es/articulo/provincia/alajar-abre-expedientes-derribo-seis-familias-la-ecoaldea-calabacino/20171226001414167208.html). *Current status (2026) not found — ask.*
- Wildfire: ⚠️ Aug 2020 Almonaster la Real fire burned **~12,000 ha** of this Sierra. [Diario de Huelva, 2024-08-27](https://www.diariodehuelva.es/articulo/al-oido/incendio/20240827132956319655.html)
- Expat bubble: ✅ low (mostly Spanish neorrurales + some foreigners, *guess*). Mass tourism: ⚠️ weekend tourism from Seville (ham, caves in Aracena).

## Water (honest view)
- The Sierra is one of the **wettest parts of western Andalusia**, with many springs and streams (*guess ~800–1,000 mm/year, verify*). Most Calabacino houses have their own water tanks (albercas). [RIE](https://ecoaldeas.org/el-calabacino/)
- ⚠️ **July 2023:** Aracena town had **water restrictions** (night pressure cuts). Eight villages (Santa Olalla, Calas, Cumbres Mayores, Cañaveral de León, Cortelazor…) had **5–9 hour daily cuts**. The big Aracena and Zufre reservoirs only supply **Seville**, not the Sierra. [Diario de Huelva, 2023-07-04](https://www.diariodehuelva.es/articulo/provincia/aracena/20230704120903306634.html)
- **2026 status:** Huelva reservoirs at **92.8 %** (April 2026), ~30 % above the 10-year average. [Huelva Red, 2026-04-13](https://huelvared.com/2026/04/13/los-embalses-de-huelva-superan-el-92-de-su-capacidad-casi-un-30-mas-que-la-media-de-la-ultima-decada/)
- **Rule for us:** land with own spring or legal well; avoid dependence on town supply.
