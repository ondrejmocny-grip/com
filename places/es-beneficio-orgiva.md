# Beneficio (commune near Órgiva)

- **Status:** dropped — closed commune (own rules, assemblies, camp life), not integrated in a village. Repeated police and child-protection cases. Not a place to live with kids. Keep only as part of the [Órgiva hub](es-orgiva-alpujarra.md) picture.
- **Country / region:** Spain, Andalusia, Granada, Alpujarra. River valley between Órgiva and Cáñar.
- **Nearest town / airport:** Órgiva (~10 min, *guess*). Granada / Málaga airports.
- **Website / contacts:** none official found.
- **Added:** 2026-09-27 by Claude

## In short
Beneficio is often called the **largest and oldest hippie commune in Spain / Europe**. Founded in the late 1970s by hippies from Ibiza. About 200 people live in tents, tipis, self-built huts and vans. It is a well-known part of the Órgiva scene, but it is **not** the kind of place we want.

## People
- ~200 people, mostly Europeans. Tents, handmade huts, vans. (2021 figures) [Yahoo / Europa Press, 2021-02-18](https://es.finance.yahoo.com/noticias/comuna-beneficio-hippie-granada-alpujarra-113850729.html)
- Rules: assemblies twice a day with a talking stick; a big tipi ("Big Lodge") as center; alcohol and hard drugs banned, cannabis allowed. [same source](https://es.finance.yahoo.com/noticias/comuna-beneficio-hippie-granada-alpujarra-113850729.html)
- Two other communes nearby: El Morreón and La Burra Verde. [La Guía de la Alpujarra](https://www.laguiadelaalpujarra.com/vida-alternativa-en-la-alpujarra/)

## Integration
- Separate camp in a valley. Little link with village life. *Guess, but consistent with all reports.*

## Why dropped (facts)
- 2017: Guardia Civil raid; >2,000 cannabis plants seized; a French man with a murder warrant arrested. [Yahoo / Europa Press](https://es.finance.yahoo.com/noticias/comuna-beneficio-hippie-granada-alpujarra-113850729.html)
- 2021: three children taken from Finland by their mother were found there. [same](https://es.finance.yahoo.com/noticias/comuna-beneficio-hippie-granada-alpujarra-113850729.html)
- 2024: police removed five Czech children (3–13 years) from the commune. Reports mention children not in school. [El Debate, 2024-07-06](https://www.eldebate.com/espana/andalucia/20240706/asi-comuna-hippie-orgiva-menores-desamparados-criminales-buscados-justicia_210940.html), [OKDiario](https://okdiario.com/andalucia/hijos-menores-madre-hippie-orgiva-granada-comunas-europa-comuna-mas-grande-espana-13103362)
- Note: the press is sensational. But the pattern fits our deal-breakers: closed community, no village integration, social-services risk with kids.

## How we could join
- Not recommended. *It may be interesting for one visit, to understand the Órgiva scene.*

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ❌ in practice (children not in school led to police cases).
- Van / yurt: ⚠️ informal camp on rustic land.
- Wildfire: ⚠️ tents and huts in a dry valley.
- Expat bubble: ⚠️⚠️ yes.
