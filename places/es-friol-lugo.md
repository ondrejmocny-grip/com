# Friol / Lugo countryside (Galicia) — around Waldorf Meniñeiros

- **Status:** lead
- **Country / region:** Spain, Galicia, Lugo province. Friol municipality (village As Donas, Santa Marta), ~15 min from Lugo city. Wider area: Lugo mountains (A Fonsagrada, Baleira, Becerreá, Pedrafita…).
- **Nearest town / airport:** Lugo (hospital, all services) ~15 min. Airports: Santiago or A Coruña *~1h (guess)*.
- **Website / contacts:**
  - Escola Waldorf Meniñeiros: [waldorflugo.com](https://www.waldorflugo.com/), secretariaescoladonas@gmail.com, 687 500 624
  - Repopulation project "Aldeas Vivas" (Lugo mountains): [Galiciapress, 2026-08-23](https://www.galiciapress.es/articulo/movimientos/2026-08-23/5991096-fefugio-rural-gallego-llegada-nuevos-pobladores-huyen-ruido)
- **Added:** 2026-09-27 by Claude

## In short
Green, wet, cheap rural Galicia near the city of Lugo. The first Waldorf school in Galicia (since 2009) is in the forest of Friol. It goes from kindergarten to bachillerato and **pulls families** from all Spain and abroad who want to live in the countryside. Not a community: people live in normal villages. *The adult eco / spiritual scene around the school: not verified (guess).*

## People
- School: **145 pupils**, about **one third international**. Families come from Andalusia, Castile, Toledo, Madrid, Argentina, Costa Rica. Director: it attracts "people who want to live in the countryside". [El Español, 2023-10-29](https://www.elespanol.com/reportajes/20231029/colegio-gallego-sin-libros-atrae-extranjeros-alumnos-estudia-infanta-sofia-gales/805169892_0.html), [El Progreso, 2020](https://www.elprogreso.es/articulo/lugo/busca-cole-naturaleza/202006222018161445440.html)
- Newcomers in the Lugo mountains: e.g. a German family with three kids in the local school (Mañón), artisans from Madrid (A Pontenova). [Galiciapress, 2026-08-23](https://www.galiciapress.es/articulo/movimientos/2026-08-23/5991096-fefugio-rural-gallego-llegada-nuevos-pobladores-huyen-ruido)
- Spiritual orientation: *Waldorf / anthroposophy, nature, crafts. Guess: grounded, family-based, no guru.*

## Integration
- Villages are Galician-speaking, old farming population. *Locals usually warm and helpful once they know you (guess).*
- Traditions very alive: farming, "feiras", magosto, pilgrim routes (Camino Primitivo passes Lugo).

## How we could join
- Start: rent a house in a village near Friol (cheap). Volunteer at the school (parent-run culture).
- Repopulation: "Aldeas Vivas" project (14 mountain municipalities, ~22,000 people on 2,500 km²) listed houses, jobs and business offers, 2023–2024. *Check if still active.* [Galiciapress](https://www.galiciapress.es/articulo/movimientos/2026-08-23/5991096-fefugio-rural-gallego-llegada-nuevos-pobladores-huyen-ruido)
- Xunta "aldeas modelo" program puts abandoned farm land back into use. [AGADER](https://agader.xunta.gal/es/recuperacion-de-tierras/aldeas-modelo)
- Contribute: wife = animals, gardening, food; Ondrej = community, tech, education.
- Van: *common to park at a rented house or farm (guess).*

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ⚠️ grey zone (Spain). Alternative school: **Meniñeiros Waldorf** (all stages, infantil to bachillerato). [waldorflugo.com](https://www.waldorflugo.com/). *Official recognition and fees: ask.* Also CPI Castroverde (public "learning community"). [Ludus Galicia](https://ludus.org.es/es/projects?age=admits_children&autonomy_id=11)
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ Not allowed by default on rustic land (see [legal overview](../regions/legal-overview.md)).
- Wildfire risk: ⚠️ Galicia burned **~120,000 ha in August 2025** (worst this century), mostly Ourense and the Courel. O Courel burned again in Sept 2026. [Campo Galego](https://www.campogalego.es/balance-de-los-incendios-120-000-hectareas-quemadas-y-debate-sobre-lo-que-hacer/), [El Correo Gallego, 2026-09-16](https://www.elcorreogallego.es/sociedad/2026/09/16/vuelve-arder-galicia-control-380-134345196.html). *Friol plain (meadows) seems lower risk than the mountains (guess).*
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: No bubble. No mass tourism.

## Costs
- *Guess: rural houses to restore €30k–90k; rustic land very cheap (often under €1–2/m²). Verify on idealista / Aldeas Abandonadas.*
- Waldorf fees: not published.
