# Garrotxa: Olot hub + Vall d'en Bas (Catalonia)

- **Status:** lead
- **Country / region:** Spain, Catalonia, Girona province, Garrotxa county. Hub town: Olot. Villages around: La Vall d'en Bas (Les Preses, Joanetes, La Pinya), Santa Pau, Sant Joan les Fonts, Sant Feliu de Pallerols (all *~10–25 min from Olot, guess*).
- **Nearest town / airport:** Olot (hospital, all services). Girona airport *~45–50 min (guess)*. Barcelona *~1h30 (guess)*.
- **Website / contacts:**
  - Escola Waldorf Garrotxa (La Llavor), Mas Garganta, La Pinya: [Ludus list](https://ludus.org.es/es/projects?province_id=32&age=admits_children), [blog](https://escolalallavor.wordpress.com/)
  - Ses Arrels (Waldorf-inspired 0–6, Olot): [sesarrelsolot.com](https://www.sesarrelsolot.com/es/escolawaldorf)
  - Montessori Olot: [Ludus](https://ludus.org.es/es/montessori-olot)
- **Added:** 2026-09-27 by Claude

## In short
A green volcanic county in the Pyrenees foothills. Olot is a real, lively Catalan town. Around it are farm villages in a natural park. There is an old Waldorf school with families from the whole county, and other small alternative schools. *The holistic / neorural scene seems real but low-key. Not verified in person (guess).* Good "hub town + cheaper village 20–40 min away" shape, but land is **expensive**.

## People
- Mostly Catalan locals. Newcomers from Barcelona and abroad. *Mix and size of the eco / spiritual scene: unknown (guess: moderate, Catalan-speaking, not an expat bubble).*
- Waldorf school La Llavor already had **60 children "from all around the region"** in 2011, run by parents and teachers together. [Garrotxa Digital, 2011-02-24](https://www.garrotxadigital.cat/noticia/155787/estudiar-sense-notes-ni-compteicio-i-amb-alumnes-daltres-edats)
- Workaway hosts: permaculture and meditation projects near Sant Joan les Fonts (listing now offline, 2026-09-27). [Workaway](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/676661924816)
- Spiritual orientation: *probably Waldorf / anthroposophy, yoga, nature. No guru structure found (guess).*

## Integration
- Strong Catalan village life, markets, festivals (e.g. Olot "Festes del Tura"). *Guess: locals are friendly but you need Catalan for depth.*
- Local traditions: very alive (farming, volcanic cuisine "cuina volcànica", masia culture). [Garrotxa – Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garrotxa)

## How we could join
- Volunteer: Workaway / WWOOF farms in the county (check live listings).
- Rent a room or a masia flat first. *Lease a piece of a masia farm: possible but needs local contacts (guess).*
- Contribute: wife = cooking, yoga, animals; Ondrej = community, tech, education (Waldorf schools are parent-run and need help).
- Van: ⚠️ Natural Park of the Garrotxa Volcanic Zone has strict rules. *Park only on a friend's farm or licensed camping (guess).*

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ⚠️ grey zone (Spain). Alternatives nearby: Escola Waldorf Garrotxa (La Pinya, 0–6 listed + some primary), La Casa del Bosc (La Pinya, 2–5), Ses Arrels (Olot, 0–6), Montessori Olot, CEIP Lliurona (Albanyà, rural school). [Ludus Girona list, 2026-09-27](https://ludus.org.es/es/projects?province_id=32&age=admits_children)
  - *Primary 6–12 Waldorf offer today: not clear. Ask the school.*
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ Not allowed by default on rustic land in Spain (see [legal overview](../regions/legal-overview.md)). Natural park adds limits.
- Wildfire risk: ⚠️ *Medium (guess).* Green and wet for Catalonia, but lots of forest.
- Water: Catalonia had a big drought 2021–2024. In 2024 the emergency list was mainly **Alt Empordà** (34 towns), not Garrotxa. [El Món](https://elmon.cat/societat/medi-ambient/un-any-emergencia-sequera-37-municipis-encara-962362/), [3Cat](https://www.3cat.cat/3catinfo/saixeca-lemergencia-per-sequera-i-ja-hi-ha-200-municipis-sense-restriccions-consulta-el-mapa/noticia/3341474/)
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: Small bubble (*guess*). Day tourism in the volcanoes and Besalú, not mass tourism.

## Costs
- Examples (2026-09-27): 32 ha rustic farm (16 ha fields + 16 ha forest) in Vall d'en Bas **€295,000**; restored masias often **€1M+**. [Rústic Gestió](https://rusticgestio.com/es/finca-rustica-masia-vall-den-bas-garrotxa-2-2/), [search summary](https://www.idealista.com/en/venta-viviendas/girona/la-garrotxa/con-masias/)
- *Small plot with a ruin: rare, guess €80k–200k. Over our budget for a house; leasing is more realistic.*
