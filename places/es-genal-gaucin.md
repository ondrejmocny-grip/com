# Valle del Genal and Gaucín (Serranía de Ronda)

- **Status:** lead (weak — the "scene" is not confirmed yet)
- **Country / region:** Spain, Andalusia, Málaga province, Serranía de Ronda. Hub: **Gaucín**. Genal villages: Benarrabá, Algatocín, Benalauría, Benadalid, Jubrique, Genalguacil, Igualeja. Next valley west: Guadiaro (Cortes de la Frontera, Jimera de Líbar).
- **Nearest town / airport:** Ronda (~45 min, *guess*). Coast (Manilva / Sotogrande / Estepona) ~35–45 min (*guess*). Airports: Málaga (~1 h 30), Gibraltar (~50 min) (*guess*).
- **Website / contacts:** Gaucín town hall [gaucin.es](https://www.gaucin.es/9724/poblacion). No hub organisation found.
- **Added:** 2026-09-27 by Claude

## In short
White villages on steep green slopes with chestnut and cork forests, **sea and Morocco in view** from Gaucín. Very beautiful and mild. Many British and other foreigners, some yoga retreats and small eco farms. But I found **no clear network** of spiritual / eco project makers like in Órgiva. Worth a visit day, not a main target yet.

## People
- Gaucín had **~350 foreign residents in 2022**, **56 % British**. [Gaucín town hall data via search, 2026-09-27](https://www.gaucin.es/9724/poblacion). *Town population ~1,600, guess.*
- Yoga / fitness retreats run in Gaucín. [BookYogaRetreats](https://www.bookyogaretreats.com/jennifer-devonshire/6-days-fitness-yoga-retreat-in-gaucin-spain)
- Genalguacil has a contemporary **art biennial** where international artists live with villagers. [MalagaCar blog](https://www.malagacar.com/blog/leisure/white-villages-genal-valley/)
- Spiritual orientation: *probably retreat businesses + retired expats + artists. Less young families. Guess — verify.*

## Integration
- Locals described as open and willing to share with newcomers. [andaluciarustica](https://andaluciarustica.com/en/genal-valley.htm)
- Traditions alive: chestnut harvest, pig slaughter (matanza), village fiestas. *Guess from general knowledge.*
- ⚠️ British expat culture is strong in Gaucín (retirees, second homes). *May be less "project-making". Guess.*

## How we could join
- Workaway: eco farm (~3 ha) near Gaucín with rescued animals (goats, sheep, donkeys, pigs), stone walls, restoration; gives independent accommodation + Spanish lessons, wants couples mid/long term. Good fit for the wife's animal skills. [Workaway](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/397799575179)
- Rent a village house. *Rent likely higher because of Costa del Sol demand, guess.*
- Buy: *land and houses are pricier than inland Andalusia (Costa del Sol effect), guess.*

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ⚠️ grey zone in Spain. Alternative schools: **Escuela Internacional Montessori Ronda** (private, authorised foreign school, infant + primary) in Ronda, ~45 min. [infoescuelas](https://www.infoescuelas.com/espana/malaga/escuela-internacional-montessori-ronda-y-serrania-de-ronda/). Public secondary: IES Valle del Genal (Algatocín). [buscocolegio](https://www.buscocolegio.com/School/school-details.action?id=29000311). *No free school found in the Genal itself.*
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ standard Spanish rules (see [legal overview](../regions/legal-overview.md)).
- Wildfire: ⚠️⚠️ **Sept 2021 Sierra Bermeja fire** started in Jubrique, burned **~9,700 ha** (Jubrique, Genalguacil, Júzcar, Faraján, Estepona, Casares). A firefighter died. [Wikipedia ES](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incendio_de_Sierra_Bermeja), [El Español, 2022-09-08](https://www.elespanol.com/malaga/20220908/sierra-bermeja-despues-incendio-apagaron-valle-genal/701430033_0.html)
- Expat bubble: ⚠️ yes in Gaucín. Mass tourism: ⚠️ Costa del Sol nearby, but villages are quiet.

## Water (honest view)
- River Genal starts at a karst spring in Igualeja (average ~260 l/s, min ~6 l/s). [caminitodelrey entorno](https://entorno.caminitodelrey.info/naturaleza/lis_cd-13625/nacimiento-del-rio-genal)
- ⚠️ In the 2022–24 drought, parts of the Genal **dried up**; a research project (DRYvER) warned the river was close to drying. Mayors said bathing pools stayed OK (2023). [El Salto](https://www.elsaltodiario.com/malaga/rio-genal-malaga-punto-secarse-segun-proyecto-dryver), [Radio Ronda](https://radioronda.net/ayuntamientos-del-valle-del-genial-desmienten-que-el-rio-vaya-seco/)
- **2026 status:** Málaga's drought officially **ended 10 April 2026**; Málaga reservoirs at **95.7 %**. [El Español, 2026-04-24](https://www.elespanol.com/malaga/20260424/embalses-secos-rozar-lleno-junta-entierra-sequia-malaga-pantanos/1003744218764_0.html). Next door, Grazalema had **>3,600 mm** Oct 2025–Feb 2026 and floods. [Wikipedia ES](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inundaciones_de_febrero_de_2026_en_Grazalema)
- *The west Serranía is among the wettest zones of south Spain in a normal year, but swings between drought and floods are growing. Guess from the above.*
