- **Status:** dropped — **water** (aquifers overused and salty) and **prices** (~€7,400/m², far above our budget). Scene fits the Órgiva pattern on paper, but it is also partly a "new elite" scene (show-off risk).

## In short
The famous hippie north of Ibiza. Hippies settled in Sant Carles in the 1960s–70s, and some still live there. There is a Waldorf school and the Las Dalias hippie market. **But** in 2026 it is one of the most expensive countryside areas in Spain, the island's groundwater is in bad shape, and even local workers live in tents and campers because of the housing crisis.

**Órgiva pattern:** 6/8 points (on paper) — ✅ villages (1), ✅ newcomers (2), ✅ spread out (3), ✅ projects / retreats (4), ✅ weekly markets: Las Dalias (Sat, Apr–Oct, 200+ stalls) and Sant Joan Sunday market (5), ✅ Waldorf school + families (6). Partial: mountains + sea (7 — sea yes, only low hills). ❌ easy start (8 — no affordable rent or land).

## Water (honest) — deal-breaker
- 12 of 17 groundwater bodies salinised or in poor state (2023); 13 of 16 aquifers in poor condition; heavy overuse. Tap water "widely considered unsuitable" to drink. [Ara Balears](https://en.arabalears.cat/opinion/water-crisis-or-governance-crisis_129_5774804.html), [Ibicasa](https://www.ibicasa.com/en/articles/sustaining-ibiza-s-water-supply), [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/ibiza-has-a-water-problem/)
- Reserves jumped from 26% to 39% after record rain in Oct 2025; island still on water alert. [Desalination News, 2025-11-10](https://www.desalination-news.com/2025/11/10/desalination-3/)
