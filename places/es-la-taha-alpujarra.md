# La Taha (Pitres, Ferreirola, Mecina Fondales) — high Alpujarra, satellite of Órgiva

- **Status:** lead
- **Country / region:** Spain, Andalusia, Granada, Alpujarra Alta. Municipality La Taha: 7 small villages (Pitres, Mecina, Fondales, Ferreirola, Capilerilla, Atalbéitar, Mecinilla), ~769 people (2023). Ferreirola ~1,000 m. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Taha), [lovegranada](https://www.lovegranada.com/alpujarra/taha-pitres/)
- **Nearest town / airport:** Órgiva (hub) *~30–40 min (guess)*. Cádiar (~2,000 people, market town of the middle Alpujarra) *~30 min (guess)*. [VIVA Spain](https://spain.vivandalusia.com/spain-news/cadiar/). Granada / Málaga airports *~1 h 45 (guess)*.
- **Website / contacts:** Town hall: [lataha.es](https://lataha.es/pueblo/pitres/). Retreat/farm spaces: [Cortijo Casería del Mercado](https://cortijolacaseria.com/), [Las Mecías](https://www.granjalasmecias.com/)
- **Added:** 2026-09-27 by Claude (web research)

## In short
Not a new hub. It is a **"live 20–40 min from the hub" option** for Órgiva (our "best setting" in the profile). Tiny, old stone villages higher up, with springs and acequias. Cheaper than around Órgiva, cooler in summer. Kids and school = Órgiva or Cádiar.

**Órgiva pattern:** 6/8 points *if we count Órgiva as the hub* (2 newcomers ✅, 3 spread out ✅, 4 small projects ✅, 6 schools via Órgiva ✅, 7 mountains + sea ✅, 8 easy start ⚠️/✅). Own town (1) ❌ too small. Own weekly market (5) ⚠️ *unknown, markets rotate between villages (guess)*.

## People
- Foreigners live here (Casa Ana walking/creative retreats, Las Mecías run by two women, retreat farm with yoga studios). [Casa Ana](https://www.casa-ana.com/friends-family), [Las Mecías](https://www.granjalasmecias.com/), [Casería del Mercado](https://cortijolacaseria.com/)
- *Share of foreigners not found. Probably high for such small villages, but a quieter, older crowd than Órgiva (guess).*
- Spiritual: yoga retreats, creative retreats. *Less "hippie" than Beneficio (guess).*

## Integration
- Very old Moorish village structure, acequias, walking paths between villages. [andaluciarustica](https://andaluciarustica.com/en/ferreirola.htm)
- *Locals are few and old. Contact good but thin (guess).*

## How we could join
- Volunteer / work exchange at a retreat farm, then rent a village house.
- Buy later: houses in Pitres from **~€45,000** (idealista, 2026-09). Listings with **own spring + acequia** exist, e.g. ~2 ha in Pitres with ruins and spring. [idealista Pitres](https://www.idealista.com/en/geo/venta-viviendas/pitres/), [milanuncios](https://www.milanuncios.com/venta-de-fincas-en-granada/cortijo-agua.htm)
- Van: same Spanish rules as Órgiva (not legal long-term on rustic land). See [legal overview](../regions/legal-overview.md).

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ⚠️ Spain grey zone. Legal alternative: Árbol Madre (Waldorf, Órgiva) — see [Órgiva file](es-orgiva-alpujarra.md).
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ not legal without licence.
- Wildfire risk: ⚠️ medium. Dec 2020 fire ~600 ha near La Taha / Torvizcón / Almegíjar. [El Independiente de Granada](https://www.elindependientedegranada.es/ciudadania/incendio-alpujarra-ya-controlado-ha-afectado-600-hectareas)
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ⚠️ light tourism (hikers). Poqueira (Pampaneira, Bubión, Capileira) next door is busier.

## Water (honest view)
- Same source as Órgiva: Sierra Nevada snowmelt + acequias. *But higher up = closer to springs, more rain and snow than Órgiva's ~558 mm (guess).*
- Rule: only land with own spring or registered acequia rights.

## Costs
- Village houses from ~€45k; fincas with water *€60–150k (guess)*. [idealista La Taha](https://www.idealista.com/en/venta-viviendas/la-taha-granada/)
