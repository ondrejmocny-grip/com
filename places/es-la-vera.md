- **Status:** lead (strong on water; weak on sea)
- **Country / region:** Spain, Extremadura, Cáceres province, comarca La Vera (~24,000 people). Villages of interest: Villanueva de la Vera, Talaveruela de la Vera, Madrigal, Losar, Jarandilla, Garganta la Olla. Next door: Valle del Tiétar (Ávila) and Valle del Jerte.

## In short
A long green valley under the Gredos peaks (2,000+ m), full of mountain streams (*gargantas*) and natural pools. Mild microclimate for inland Spain. Spanish neorrurales (many from Madrid) live here, with two alternative schools. **Best water of all Spanish candidates**, but the **sea is far** (fails the wife's "sea nearby" wish).

## People
- Mostly Spanish newcomers (from Madrid, *guess*) + some foreigners. A family on Workaway in Villanueva de la Vera builds a home on 2.2 ha "in an area full of water with more than 30 rivers". [Workaway search, 2026-09-27](https://www.workaway.info/en/hostlist?country=ES&region=extremadura)
- Two "free / living pedagogy" schools exist (see Legal flags) → there are **young families with alternative values**.
- Population grew by 116 in 2024 (24,169). Villanueva, Losar, Jaraíz grew; Madrigal, Talaveruela, Garganta la Olla shrank. [A la Vera de Gredos, 2024-12-29](https://alaveradegredos.com/2024/12/29/la-poblacion-en-la-vera-aumento-en-116-personas-en-2024-y-se-situa-en-24-169-habitantes/)

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ⚠️ grey zone in Spain. Alternatives: **Comunidad de aprendizaje Isla Tortuga** (Villanueva de la Vera, ages 3–12, "libre/viva"); **Maisha** (Talaveruela, 3+, inspired by Rebeca and Mauricio Wild). *Legal registration unknown; many such schools are not officially registered. Verify.* [ludus Cáceres](https://ludus.org.es/es/projects?province_id=36)
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ standard Spanish rules (see [legal overview](../regions/legal-overview.md)).
- Wildfire: ⚠️⚠️ July 2023 fire in Villanueva de la Vera. [Junta de Extremadura, 2023-07-24](https://www.juntaex.es/w/incendio-forestal-villanueva-de-la-vera). Aug 2025 **Jarilla fire** burned **~15,500 ha** in the Jerte and Ambroz valleys next door; smoke reached La Vera. [A la Vera de Gredos, 2025-08-19](https://alaveradegredos.com/2025/08/19/el-incendio-de-jarilla-avanza-en-el-valle-del-jerte-y-el-humo-ya-afecta-a-la-vera/), [Junta de Extremadura](https://www.juntaex.es/w/el-plan-infoex-da-por-extinguido-el-incendio-de-jarilla?inheritRedirect=true)
- Expat bubble: ✅ low. Mass tourism: ⚠️ summer tourism at the natural pools.

## Water (honest view)
- Gredos catches Atlantic rain; many gargantas flow into the Tiétar. Abundant rain in autumn–winter, **sharp dry summer**. [Wikipedia / La Verata](https://laverata.com/comarca/villanueva/), [Villanueva de la Vera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villanueva_de_la_Vera)
- ⚠️ In dry summers flows fall; problems are **bad management** (illegal concrete pools in streams, over-extraction of wells, untreated sewage) more than lack of rain. [El Salto, 2021-08-17](https://www.elsaltodiario.com/medioambiente/aguas-y-gargantas-de-la-vera-entre-la-sequia-y-el-mal-gobierno)
- **2026:** *no restrictions found; the 2025–26 Iberian winter was very wet. Guess — verify with the Tajo basin (CHT).*
- Overall: **best water security** of the Spanish candidates. *Guess.*
