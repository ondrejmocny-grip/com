# Lakabe and Arterra Bizimodu (Navarre)

- **Status:** dropped (2026-09-27) — both are **intentional communities**. Keep only as places to visit / learn.
- **Country / region:** Spain, Navarre. Lakabe: Arce–Artzibar valley (Pyrenees foothills). Arterra Bizimodu: Artieda (Urraúl Alto, near Lumbier).
- **Nearest town / airport:** Pamplona *~45–60 min (guess)*.
- **Website / contacts:** [lakabe.org](https://www.lakabe.org/), [arterrabizimodu.org](https://arterrabizimodu.org/)
- **Added:** 2026-09-27 by Claude

## In short
The two best-known ecovillages of northern Spain, both members of the Red Ibérica de Ecoaldeas (RIE). Lakabe is an abandoned village squatted in 1980, now ~44 people, run by assembly. Arterra is a co-housing project (since 2014) with ~25 families in one large building. Good people and knowledge, but **closed / intentional communities** = our deal-breaker.

## Why dropped
- Lakabe: the village **is** the community; decisions by assembly; "no visits without notice". [okdiario](https://okdiario.com/viajes/1980-era-pueblo-abandonado-mitad-montanas-navarras-hoy-ecoaldea-hippie-donde-viven-44-personas-no-aceptan-visitas-sin-avisar-20287320), [Lacabe – Wikipedia](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lacabe)
- Arterra: intentional co-housing, sociocracy, shared 8,000 m² building + 3 ha. [EcoHabitar](https://ecohabitar.org/comunidad-intencional-arterra-bizimodu/), [ecoaldeas.org](https://ecoaldeas.org/arterra-bizimodu/)
- Sea is far (*~2h, guess*). Winters colder.

## Still useful
- Arterra offers volunteer seasons and "experience weeks". Good way to learn and meet people. [arterrabizimodu.org](https://arterrabizimodu.org/que-hacemos/)
- RIE + Navarre government run **"Rehabitar"**, a program to repopulate abandoned villages. Might lead to open village projects. [ecoaldeas.org](https://ecoaldeas.org/proyectos-de-la-rie/)
- Other RIE northern member: Matavenero (León) — also an intentional community. [RIE members](https://ecoaldeas.org/miembros-de-la-rie/)

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ⚠️ grey zone (Spain). Lakabe had a Montessori space (looked for Basque-speaking guides). *Guess.*
- Wildfire risk: *low–medium (guess).*
- Expat bubble: no; mostly Basque / Spanish.
