# Mallorca, Serra de Tramuntana (Sóller, Deià, Fornalutx)

- **Status:** dropped — **prices** (Sóller fincas ~€6,600/m², houses €700k–3M) and **water** (Deià had water cut 3 days a week in Aug 2026; Sóller bans on garden watering).
- **Country / region:** Spain, Balearic Islands, Mallorca. Hub town: Sóller (~14,000, *guess*). Villages: Deià, Fornalutx, Biniaraix. Tramuntana is UNESCO World Heritage.
- **Nearest town / airport:** Palma ~30–40 min, airport ~40 min *(guess)*.
- **Website / contacts:** Ecoescola Ses Marjades (Sóller): [search summary / charlesmarlow](https://www.charlesmarlow.com/blog/2023/07/03/alternative-schooling-in-mallorca-becoming-a-movement/)

## In short
Beautiful: a limestone mountain range with terraces and olive groves falling into the sea. Sóller is a real town with a market. There is a growing alternative-school movement on the island. **But** prices are among the highest in Spain, and the mountain villages already run short of water in summer.

**Órgiva pattern:** 5/8 points — ✅ real market town (1), ✅ international newcomers (2 — mostly wealthy), ✅ spread out (3), ✅ weekly market in Sóller (*guess*) (5), ✅ mountains + sea (7). Partial: alternative schools (6 — Ses Marjades public eco-school in Sóller; Sa Llavor in Mancor; Waldorf only in Palma). ❌ projects we can join (4 — few found), ❌ easy start (8).

## Water (honest) — deal-breaker
- **Deià**: one well + one small spring; in Aug 2026 water was **cut 3 days a week** in parts of the village; households used tankers or own cisterns. [Majorca Daily Bulletin, 2026-08-12](https://www.majorcadailybulletin.com/news/local/2026/08/12/144887/luxury-hotel-deya-faces-water-restrictions-daily-cuts-begin-this-friday-top-holiday-village.html)
- **Sóller, Fornalutx, Esporles**: bans on filling pools, garden irrigation. Restrictions are now "an established system, readjusted monthly". [mallorca.com, 2026](https://www.mallorca.com/en/advice/property/building-renovating/water-restrictions-mallorca)
- Island reservoirs were holding up in July 2026. [Majorca Daily Bulletin, 2026-07-08](https://www.majorcadailybulletin.com/news/local/2026/07/08/143875/with-mallorca-gripped-high-temperatures-the-reservoirs-are-holding-up.html)
