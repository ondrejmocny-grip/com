# Marina Alta inland valleys (Vall de Gallinera, Vall de Laguar, Vall de Pop / Xaló)

- **Status:** dropped — **water**. The whole comarca has no reservoirs and lives only on aquifers. 2024: emergency, towns without drinking water. **July 2026: Parcent, Xaló, Alcalalí, Llíber again warn their wells are running out.** Drought is our deal-breaker.
- **Country / region:** Spain, Valencian Community, Alicante province, Marina Alta. Valleys: Vall de Gallinera (~600 people in 8 hamlets), Vall d'Alcalà, Vall d'Ebo, Vall de Laguar, Vall de Pop (Xaló, Alcalalí, Parcent, Llíber).
- **Nearest town / airport:** Pego / Dénia coast 20–40 min (*guess*). Alicante or Valencia airport ~1 h (*guess*).
- **Website / contacts:** —
- **Added:** 2026-09-27 by Claude

## In short
Limestone mountains 20–40 min from the Costa Blanca. Pretty, mild, some yoga retreats and many foreign residents. Mountains + sea = perfect. But the water base is fragile and got worse in 2024 and again in summer 2026.

## Water (why dropped)
- Marina Alta has **no reservoirs**; it lives only on groundwater, with over-extraction and salinisation. [lamarina.eldiario.es, 2024-02-16](https://lamarina.eldiario.es/2024/02/16/por-que-la-sequia-de-la-marina-alta-es-peligrosa-temor-a-restricciones-mientras-se-preven-miles-de-viviendas/)
- Rain fell from ~1,135 mm (2021–22) to **~233 mm (2023–24)**. [search summary of Dialnet/MDPI study](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/17/12/5512)
- 2024: emergency; supply problems in Vall de Gallinera, Murla, Xaló; tankers/bottled water; Teulada and Benitatxell ran out of drinking water. [El Español, 2024-08-19](https://www.elespanol.com/alicante/economia/20240819/diputacion-estudia-medidas-garantizar-suministro-agua-potable-sur-marina-alta/879412464_0.html)
- March 2025: very wet (Vall de Gallinera 590 mm in one month); area left "red" level, still "orange". [lamarina.eldiario.es, 2025-04-10](https://lamarina.eldiario.es/2025/04/10/la-marina-alta-sale-oficialmente-del-nivel-rojo-por-sequia-tras-el-segundo-marzo-mas-humedo-desde-1950/)
- **15 July 2026:** Parcent, Xaló, Alcalalí, Llíber: wells "decreasing considerably", ask 90 l/person/day, no pools or garden watering. [lamarina.eldiario.es, 2026-07-15](https://lamarina.eldiario.es/2026/07/15/restricciones-de-agua-en-la-comarca-parcent-y-xalo-alertan-de-que-sus-reservas-se-agotan-e-instan-a-no-llenar-piscinas-ni-regar-jardines/)
- Nitrates in drinking water are also an issue (2026). [lamarina.eldiario.es, 2026-04-12](https://lamarina.eldiario.es/2026/04/12/el-agua-que-bebemos-la-concentracion-de-nitratos-en-cada-municipio-de-la-marina-alta/)
