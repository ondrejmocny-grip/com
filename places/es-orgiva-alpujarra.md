# Órgiva and the western Alpujarra (Sierra Nevada)

- **Status:** lead
- **Country / region:** Spain, Andalusia, Granada province, Alpujarra Granadina. Hub town: Órgiva (~7,000 people, ~450 m). Nearby villages higher up: Soportújar, Cáñar, Carataunas, Bayacas, Pampaneira, Bubión, Capileira, Lanjarón.
- **Nearest town / airport:** Motril beach ~40 min. Granada city ~1 h. Airports: Granada (~1 h 15, *guess*), Málaga (~1 h 30, *guess*). [Euro Weekly News, 2025-12-01](https://euroweeklynews.com/2025/12/01/tiny-spanish-mountain-village-where-a-third-of-its-population-are-expats/)

## In short
Órgiva is the best-known hub for alternative and spiritual newcomers in mainland Spain. It is a normal market town, not a community. Many newcomers live in farmhouses (*cortijos*) around it and run retreats, eco farms, yoga and healing work. There are legal alternative schools. Sea is 40 min away and Sierra Nevada is right above. **Main doubts:** water in a dry year, hot summers, and the "hippie" image (see [Beneficio](es-beneficio-orgiva.md)).

## People
- Numbers (town hall data, 2025-12-01): **26% foreign** (~2,300 people from **72 countries**), **38% from other parts of Spain**, only **35% born locally**. Top groups: British (754), German (253), Dutch (171), Romanian, Moroccan, French, Italian. [Euro Weekly News](https://euroweeklynews.com/2025/12/01/tiny-spanish-mountain-village-where-a-third-of-its-population-are-expats/)
- So: a real **mix of international and Spanish neorrurales**. Good for us.
- Spiritual scene: yoga, meditation, kirtan, ecstatic dance, shamanic and healing workshops, retreats in yurts. [Workaway listings, 2026-09-27](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/256651467427), [La Guía de la Alpujarra](https://www.laguiadelaalpujarra.com/vida-alternativa-en-la-alpujarra/)
- Grounded? *Mixed, guess.* Many families, farmers, builders, school founders. But also a loud "hippie" fringe (Beneficio and other camps). *Part of the scene may feel show-off or drifting. Check on site.*

## Integration
- The mayor openly says diversity is good for the town. [Euro Weekly News](https://euroweeklynews.com/2025/12/01/tiny-spanish-mountain-village-where-a-third-of-its-population-are-expats/), [Olive Press, 2022](https://www.theolivepress.es/spain-news/2022/06/26/olive-press-meets-orgiva-mayor-raul-orellana-to-find-out-why-this-spanish-town-in-la-alpujarra-south-of-granada-is-the-worlds-best/)
- Town hall runs a program to attract remote workers ("Knowmads"), with a municipal hostel and coworking. [alpujarraknowmads.com](https://www.alpujarraknowmads.com/en/town/orgiva/)
- Local traditions: acequia irrigation (Moorish), terraces, markets, fiestas. The *acequias de careo* in the Alpujarra got a UNESCO recognition. [El Independiente de Granada](https://www.elindependientedegranada.es/economia/unesco-premia-regantes-alpujarra-sierra-buen-uso-agua-con-acequias-careo)
- ⚠️ Only ~1/3 of people are local-born. *Contact with old locals may be thinner than in Bayramiç. Guess.*

- Later: buy a cortijo with **acequia water rights** (listings often say "8 hours of acequia irrigation"). [idealista Órgiva land](https://www.idealista.com/en/venta-terrenos/orgiva-granada/)

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ⚠️ grey zone in Spain. **But** Órgiva has **Árbol Madre**, an **officially authorised private school** (Junta de Andalucía code 18015791), Waldorf-based, ages **3–11**, enrolment 2026–27 open. [arbolmadre.org, 2026-09-27](https://arbolmadre.org/). Also public schools and a Montessori association. Earlier, families left because no legal alternative primary existed. [Goteo](https://en.goteo.org/project/arbol-madre-holistic-international-project). *Secondary (12+) = public IES only, guess.*
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ Spanish rules: no living on rustic land without license (see [legal overview](../regions/legal-overview.md)). *In practice many people live in vans/yurts here. Tolerated, not legal. Guess.*
- Wildfire: ⚠️ medium. Dec 2020 fire burned ~600 ha near La Taha / Torvizcón / Almegíjar (next valleys). [El Independiente de Granada](https://www.elindependientedegranada.es/ciudadania/incendio-alpujarra-ya-controlado-ha-afectado-600-hectareas). No big recent fire found in Órgiva itself.
- Expat bubble: ⚠️ yes, strong international share. Mass tourism: ⚠️ light, in the high villages (Pampaneira, Capileira) only.

## Water (honest view)
- Órgiva is **dry land fed by snow**. Water comes from Sierra Nevada snowmelt through acequias and rivers (Guadalfeo, Poqueira, Chico). [Sierra Nevada / Guadalfeo](https://www.adurcal.com/enlaces/cultura/zona/historia/general/sierra_nevada.htm)
- In dry years the Alpujarra is hit: e.g. 2019 it was one of 13 Andalusian areas in **severe drought**. [El Independiente de Granada, 2019-11-09](https://elindependientedegranada.es/economia/andalucia-pierde-1656-agua-ano-tiene-ya-13-comarcas-agrarias-sequia-severa-ellas-alpujarra). 2022–2024 was a very dry period across Andalusia.
- **2026 status:** the 2025–26 winter was extremely wet in Andalusia (storm Leonardo, Feb 2026). Rules reservoir (below Órgiva) had to **release water to the sea** in May 2026. [COPE, 2026-05-27](https://www.cope.es/emisoras/andalucia/granada-provincia/motril/noticias/paradoja-rules-presa-vierte-mar-agua-no-20260527_3372437.html), [Euronews, 2026-02-05](https://euronews.com/video/2026/02/05/spain-floods-storm-leonardo-forces-4000-evacuations-in-andalusia)
- Risk: *snow cover on Sierra Nevada is shrinking with climate change; a multi-year drought can come back. Guess based on trend.*
- **Rule for us:** only lease/buy land with **own spring or registered acequia rights**, never land that depends on rain.
