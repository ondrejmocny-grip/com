# Piloña – Parres – Nava (Eastern Asturias)

- **Status:** lead
- **Country / region:** Spain, Asturias, "Oriente". Towns: Infiesto (Piloña), Arriondas (Parres), Nava, Cangas de Onís. Between Oviedo and the Picos de Europa.
- **Nearest town / airport:** Infiesto / Arriondas (small towns). Oviedo and Gijón *~30–45 min*. Asturias airport *~1h (guess)*.
- **Website / contacts:**
  - "Volver al Pueblo" (COCEDER, local partner CDR El Prial in Piloña): [volveralpueblo.coceder.org](https://volveralpueblo.coceder.org/nuestras-comarcas/asturias/oriente-asturiano-pilona)
  - Naturalia retreat (Estivalia) at Planeta Verde permaculture farm, Infiesto: [estivalia.es](https://estivalia.es/eventos/naturalia/retiro-naturalia-verano/), info@estivalia.es
  - Workaway hosts: [yoga/permaculture family near Arriondas](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/265741612568), [world-schooling family finca in Nava](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/851149672891)
- **Added:** 2026-09-27 by Claude

## In short
Green valleys of the river Piloña/Sella, 25 min from beaches and close to the Picos de Europa. A quiet but real scene of newcomers: yoga + permaculture families, a biodanza / yoga / ecology retreat, a world-schooling family finca. A regional program helps new settlers. Not a community — normal Asturian villages. **Best climate + sea + mountains + water combination** found in the north so far.

## Integration
- Newcomer kids go to the local village school and learn Asturian. [Workaway Nava](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/851149672891)
- Traditions very alive: cider (Nava cider museum), Asturian language, livestock, "fiestas de prao", Descenso del Sella (Arriondas). *Guess: locals are open and direct.*

## How we could join
- Volunteer first at one of the hosts above (both hosts can host families).
- "Volver al Pueblo" gives free help with housing, jobs, business ideas. Covers Cangas de Onís, Parres, Piloña (2/3 of the area's people); new settlers reported in April 2025. [COPE Ribadesella, 2025-04-09](https://coperibadesella.com/09/04/2025/el-oriente-de-asturias-sigue-recibiendo-nuevos-pobladores-gracias-al-programa-volver-al-pueblo/), [search summary](https://actualidad.asturias.es/-/el-proyecto-volver-al-pueblo-para-favorecer-la-fijaci%C3%B3n-de-poblaci%C3%B3n-en-el-medio-rural-se-extiende-al-suroccidente)
- Rent a village house, lease meadow land from older farmers (*common, guess*).
- Contribute: wife = yoga, cooking, animals (a lot of livestock here); Ondrej = community, tech, retreats.

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ⚠️ grey zone (Spain). Alternatives: Tribu Raigame (forest school, 3–12, max 12 kids, ~€200/month, *location in Asturias not stated*); Colegio Andolina (free school, Gijón, ~45 min); some project-based public schools. [Ludus Asturias](https://ludus.org.es/es/projects?province_id=12), [Tribu Raigame](https://ludus.org.es/es/tribu-raigame). One family "unschools" near the Picos (Workaway search result, 2026-09-27).
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ Not allowed by default on rustic land (see [legal overview](../regions/legal-overview.md)).
- Wildfire risk: ⚠️ *Low–medium (guess).* Wet climate, but Asturias has spring fires (mostly west).
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ⚠️ Summer tourism in Cangas de Onís, Covadonga, Llanes, the Sella. Infiesto / Nava are calmer. No expat bubble.

## Costs
- *Guess: village house to restore €40k–120k; meadow land €1–4/m². Rents higher in summer near the coast. Verify on idealista.*
