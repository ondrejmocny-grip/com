## In short
Very green mountain valleys with stone cabins ("cabañas pasiegas"), 20–50 km from the sea. A recognised Waldorf school (2–12) and a Waldorf-based secondary (12–16) sit at the valley mouth, near Santander. Some newcomers restore cabins and do permaculture. The region actively looks for new residents. *The spiritual / project-maker scene is thin in what we found (guess).*

## People
- Portuguese–Dutch couple with a 3-year-old restoring stone cabins off-grid near Vega de Pas, living in a caravan; ocean ~50 km. [Workaway](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/484118172442)
- Permaculture farm in Gibaja (Ramales, east Cantabria). [Workaway](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/223451987226)
- City people restoring family houses in Liérganes, Valle del Pas, La Cavada. [faircompanies](https://faircompanies.com/articles/valles-ibericos-fuera-de-mapa-de-despoblacion-a-neorruralismo/)
- Spiritual orientation: *Waldorf families; otherwise unknown (guess).*

## Integration
- Pasiego culture: seasonal moving of cattle between cabins ("muda"), stone walls, sobaos and quesadas. Very alive. [National Geographic](https://viajes.nationalgeographic.com.es/a/valles-cantabria-que-aprendieron-a-vivir-movimiento_24125)
- *Guess: Pasiegos are known as reserved; trust takes time.*
- LEADER projects fund rural services and a database of farms / houses for new settlers. [cantabria.es](https://www.cantabria.es/web/comunicados/w/el-gobierno-financia-ocho-proyectos-leader-para-mejorar-los-servicios-rurales-y-dinamizar-la-econom%C3%ADa-de-los-valles-pasiegos)

## How we could join
- Volunteer at cabin / permaculture hosts first.
- Rent or lease a "cabaña pasiega" with meadow: *many are empty (guess).* Use the Vente a Vivir a un Pueblo / LEADER database.
- Contribute: wife = animals (cattle country), cheese / food, yoga; Ondrej = tech, community.
- Van: ⚠️ *Some hosts live in caravans on their land (tolerated? guess).*

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ⚠️ grey zone (Spain). Alternatives: **Escuela Waldorf Cantabria** — officially accredited since 2016, 2–12 years. Fees 2026: €335/month (infantil), €389/month (primary), + €300 enrolment, + €200 materials; private, has a scholarship fund. [About](https://waldorfcantabria.es/sobre-nosotors/), [Tarifas](https://waldorfcantabria.es/tarifas/). **Escuela Rafael** (Sarón, 12–16). [Ludus](https://ludus.org.es/es/projects?autonomy_id=6)
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ Not allowed by default on rustic land (see [legal overview](../regions/legal-overview.md)). *Converting a cabaña to housing needs permits (guess).*
- Wildfire risk: *Low–medium (guess).* Wet; some winter/spring pasture fires in Cantabria.
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: No bubble. Summer tourism on the coast, less in the valleys.

## Costs
- *Guess: cabaña pasiega to restore with meadow €40k–150k. Close to Santander more expensive. Verify.*
- Waldorf: ~€4,000–4,700 / year per child (from fees above).
