- **Country / region:** France, Occitanie, Gard (south-east edge of the Cévennes National Park). Hubs: Saint-Jean-du-Gard (*~2,700 people, guess*), Anduze (*~3,400, guess*), Lasalle (small), Le Vigan (*~3,800, guess*, west side). Bigger town: Alès.

## In short
The Cévennes are the classic French "back to the land" area since 1968. Green chestnut valleys, stone *mas*, rivers (Gardons). Sea and Montpellier are reachable. On paper it is the closest French copy of Órgiva. **The big problem: summer drought is now regular, with drinking-water cuts in 2026.** Also wildfire risk.

## People
- Since the 1960s, waves of néo-ruraux, "often with eco-leftist sensibilities". Mixed: some rich families buying big houses, others on low income. [maxicours (school geography summary)](https://www.maxicours.com/se/cours/les-cevennes-un-espace-rural-multifonctionnel-en-transformation/), [CQFD, 2022-09](https://cqfd-journal.org/Cevennes-tiens-voila-du-bidasse)
- Projects: Le Merlet (nature education, 35+ years), Maison des Semences Paysannes (peasant seeds), energy co-ops. [France 3, 2020-07-01](https://france3-regions.franceinfo.fr/occitanie/gard/cevennes-territoire-qui-s-invente-nouvel-art-vivre-1839198.html)
- *Many French, plus Dutch, German, British, Swiss (guess). Spiritual scene: yoga, Buddhist-inspired, healing, festivals (guess — check).*

## Integration
- Saint-Jean-du-Gard market: **Tuesday 8:00–13:00** (bigger in summer). Anduze flea market Sunday. [Cévennes Tourisme](https://www.cevennes-tourisme.fr/visites-decouvertes/saint-jean-du-gard/activites/)
- Tension exists: 2022 conflict over farmland in Saint-Jean-du-Gard sold to the Foreign Legion, "néo-ruraux vs locals". [CQFD, 2022-09](https://cqfd-journal.org/Cevennes-tiens-voila-du-bidasse)
- Traditions: Protestant (Camisard) history, chestnuts, silk, sweet onions, bull games in the lowlands *(guess on how alive)*.

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ⚠️ authorisation needed (see [france.md](../regions/france.md)). ✅ Alt schools: **Caminarem** Steiner-Waldorf (since 1994, ages 2.5–15; Monteils + Alès), **Je d'Enfants** Montessori (3–12), **École Vivante** in Bagard (since 2022). [Caminarem](https://www.ecolecaminarem.org/), [Je d'Enfants](https://www.ecole-jedenfants.fr/), [École Vivante](https://ecolevivante.org/). *Alès is ~45 min from Saint-Jean (guess).*
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ French 3-month rule. National Park rules may be stricter *(guess)*.
- Wildfire risk: ⚠️ **medium–high.** July 2025: 430 ha fire at Montdardier (near Le Vigan). Small fires near Saint-Jean-du-Gard. [France 3, 2025-07](https://france3-regions.franceinfo.fr/occitanie/gard/nimes/incendie-dans-le-gard-500-hectares-brules-le-feu-n-est-toujours-pas-fixe-les-pompiers-attendent-le-renfort-des-moyens-aeriens-3185268.html), [Objectif Gard](https://www.objectifgard.com/faits-divers/saint-jean-du-gard-10-hectares-de-foret-partent-en-fumee-80396.php)
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ⚠️ strong summer tourism (Anduze, bamboo park, steam train, rivers) *(guess)*.

## Water (honest view)
- Autumn brings huge storms ("épisodes cévenols") and floods. *Summers are now very dry. Guess from pattern.*
- **August 2026:** Gardons amont (Saint-Jean / Anduze basin) at **crisis** level. Hérault amont (Le Vigan side) at crisis. "Several communes already have **drinking-water supply cuts**." Secondary streams dry. [Objectif Sud, 2026-08-07](https://www.objectifsud.fr/actualites/gard-secheresse-les-restrictions-deau-renforcees-face-a-une-situation-qui-se-degrade-167875.php)
- Older years also had crisis levels on the Gardons. [Préfecture du Gard](https://www.gard.gouv.fr/Actualites/Secheresse-Aggravation-de-la-situation-hydrologique)
- **My honest view:** this is close to our deal-breaker. Only consider land with a proven year-round spring + big storage. *Guess.*
