# Conflent: Prades and the Canigou valleys (Pyrénées-Orientales)

- **Status:** dropped (2026-09-27) — **reason: water.** Pyrénées-Orientales has had water restrictions almost without a break since summer 2022 (4 dry years). Drought is our deal-breaker. *Re-check if 2026–27 stays wet.* Same reason for Céret / Vallespir.
- **Country / region:** France, Occitanie, Pyrénées-Orientales, Conflent (Têt valley under Mont Canigou, 2,784 m). Hub: Prades (*~6,000 people, guess*). Villages: Eus, Mosset, Corneilla-de-Conflent, Vernet-les-Bains.
- **Nearest town / airport:** Perpignan airport *~45 min (guess)*. Sea *~50 min (guess)*.
- **Website / contacts:** [conflentcanigo.fr](https://www.conflentcanigo.fr/prades)

## In short
On paper very attractive: Catalan culture, big mountain, sea under 1 hour, a big weekly market, small nature/Montessori schools. **But the department is in a long drought.**

## Water (honest view)
- Restrictions "almost continuous since summer 2022". [France 3, 2025](https://france3-regions.franceinfo.fr/occitanie/pyrenees-orientales/perpignan/secheresse-les-restrictions-d-eau-prolongees-jusqu-au-31-decembre-2025-dans-ce-departement-3252178.html), [info.fr, 2026-04](https://info.fr/secheresse-pyrenees-orientales-six-villages-liberes-28-communes-restrictions/)
- Winter 2025–26 was wet; groundwater is now near normal except one deep aquifer. On 27 April 2026, 28 communes (Aspres, Salanque — not Conflent) were still restricted. [info.fr, 2026-04](https://info.fr/secheresse-pyrenees-orientales-six-villages-liberes-28-communes-restrictions/), [Préfecture 66](https://www.pyrenees-orientales.gouv.fr/Actions-de-l-Etat/Environnement-eau-risques-naturels-et-technologiques/Eau-et-Peche/Reglementation-de-l-Eau/Secheresse/Point-sur-la-situation-et-sur-les-restrictions-d-usages-de-l-eau)
- So Conflent is better **right now**, but the 4-year trend is the reason to drop.
