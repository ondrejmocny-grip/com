## In short
The Couserans is a green, wet corner of the French Pyrenees. From the late 1960s, hippies and later néo-ruraux took over empty hamlets. Today there are many families in cabins, yurts and restored farms. It is the **wettest and cheapest** area in this French list. **Weak points:** sea is far, winters are cold and rainy, and French is needed.

## People
- Massat basin: first "hippie" wave from the late 1960s in ruined hamlets, then many later newcomers "with extremely varied profiles". [Le Journal Toulousain](https://www.lejournaltoulousain.fr/societe/dossier-lhabitat-legerlourd-de-consequence-35227/), [Geoconfluences](https://geoconfluences.ens-lyon.fr/informations-scientifiques/dossiers-regionaux/france-espaces-ruraux-periurbains/articles-scientifiques/habitat-informel-ariege-marge-innovante) *(site down 2026-09-27; facts from search snippet)*
- Néo-ruraux stopped the population loss. Montbrun-Bocage Sunday farmers' market draws néo-ruraux and local producers, "similar to Massat". [Wikipedia Massat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massat)
- Massat has had autonomy projects (energy + food self-sufficiency) for 40 years. [les-cabanes.com](https://www.les-cabanes.com/robinsons-cabane-vie-alternative/libres-enfants-de-l-ariege-vivre-en-autonomie-complete/)
- *Mix: mostly French, some British, Dutch, German, Spanish (guess). Some poverty and "marginal" fringe (per Geoconfluences title: "marginality or alternative?").*
- Spiritual side: *earth-based, simple living, some healing/yoga; less "retreat business" than Órgiva (guess).*

## Integration
- Saint-Girons main market: **Saturday 8:00–13:00**, Champ de Mars. [camping-audinaclesbains.com](https://camping-audinaclesbains.com/ou-faire-son-marche-pres-de-saint-girons/)
- Massat market: **Thursday and Sunday**. [Tourisme Couserans](https://www.tourisme-couserans-pyrenees.com/en/animations/marche-de-massat/)
- Massat school got pupil numbers back to 1956 levels thanks to newcomers. [les-cabanes.com](https://www.les-cabanes.com/robinsons-cabane-vie-alternative/massat-une-ecole-qui-repasse-a-un-taux-de-frequentation-de-1956/)
- Local traditions: pastoralism (sheep, cows up to the summer pastures), Occitan culture (Calandreta school). *Guess on how alive.*

## How we could join
- Yurt gîtes and farms exist (e.g. La Ferme Rosane, Massat–Biert, yurt rental). [ferme-rosane.com](https://ferme-rosane.com/)
- *Many hamlets have people with land who host vans / volunteers (guess).*
- Chant'Arize school sits on an 85 ha **Terre de Liens** farm (biodynamic). A place to meet families. [Jardin d'Églantine](https://jardindeglantine.fr/ecole-chant-arize-steiner-waldorf-a-campagne-sur-arize-fonctionnement-et-tarifs/)
- Contribute: vet/animal care is useful in a herding area; cooking; yoga; community building.

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ⚠️ authorisation needed (see [france.md](../regions/france.md)). ✅ Legal private schools: **Chant'Arize** Steiner-Waldorf (Campagne-sur-Arize, since 1993, ~80 pupils, nursery to 6th grade), **École Pleine Nature** (Petits Pas association, Couserans), **Calandreta deth Coserans** (Occitan). [Fédération Steiner-Waldorf](https://pedagogie-waldorf.fr/ecole/ecole-chantarize/), [petitspascouserans.org](https://petitspascouserans.org/fr/ecole-pleine-nature/) *(site did not load 2026-09-27)*
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ same French 3-month rule. But the area has a long culture of light housing and its own association (HALEM). Some people live without official rights. [Le Journal Toulousain](https://www.lejournaltoulousain.fr/societe/dossier-lhabitat-legerlourd-de-consequence-35227/), [La Gazette des communes](https://www.lagazettedescommunes.com/765818/en-plein-essor-tiny-houses-et-yourtes-doivent-encore-convaincre-les-elus/)
- Wildfire risk: ✅ *low–medium (wet, green; some winter burns) — guess.*
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ✅ low.

## Water (honest view)
- Saint-Girons: **~1,000–1,100 mm rain/year**, ~185 rain days, climate "temperate, no dry season" (Cfb). [fr.wikipedia Saint-Girons](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Girons_(Ari%C3%A8ge)), [Météo-France 1991–2020](https://donneespubliques.meteofrance.fr/FichesClim/FICHECLIM_09289001.pdf)
- But: in the hot years it also got tight. Oct 2022: supply "in tension, notably in the Couserans". Oct 2023: Salat basin at **crisis** level. June 2026: Salat on alert. [SMDEA 09, 2022-10-28](https://smdea09.fr/secheresse-en-ariege/), [Préfecture Ariège](https://ariege.gouv.fr/Actualites/Secheresse-passage-en-niveau-crise-et-alerte-renforcee-de-plusieurs-cours-d-eau)
- *Still the best water in this French list. Small hamlet springs can fail in late summer → check spring history. Guess.*
