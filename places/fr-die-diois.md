## In short
The Diois is the upper Drôme valley. Since the 1970s it has drawn "néo-ruraux" (city people who move to the land). Organic farming, herbs and eco projects are part of normal life. The whole valley calls itself "Biovallée". It fits the Órgiva pattern well, **except the sea is far** and water is tight in summer.

## People
- Newcomers are a big share. In 2011, 17% of people were newcomers of the last 5 years (15% in similar rural areas). Newcomers first settled at the valley entrance, and **from 1999 in the whole deep valley**. [INSEE 2011](https://www.insee.fr/fr/statistiques/fichier/1292672/SL_Vallee_Drome_Diois.pdf)
- The valley has "a local tradition of welcoming" protesters, resisters and néo-ruraux. [Cairn / Ethnologie française](https://cairn.info/revue-ethnologie-francaise-2004-1-page-23.htm)
- Drôme grew by 20,000 people 2013–2018, mostly by newcomers. [Médias Citoyens Diois, 2021](https://mediascitoyens-diois.info/2021/01/drome-toujours-plus-de-neo-ruraux-dans-la-vallee/)
- Mostly **French** newcomers, *few foreigners (guess)*. So less "expat bubble" than Órgiva, but you need French.
- Spiritual side: *yoga, meditation, healing workshops exist; the scene feels more eco-political than New Age (guess, check on site).*

## Integration
- Biovallée: 91 communes, 56,000 people, 1,000+ associations. Organic farming since the 1970s. Co-op shop La Carline: 620 member families. Big herb business (L'Herbier du Diois). [Diois Tourisme](https://www.diois-tourisme.com/en/pays-diois/the-biovallee/)
- Yearly "Rencontres de l'Écologie au Quotidien" in Die (24th edition in 2026). [Médias Citoyens Diois, 2026-01](https://mediascitoyens-diois.info/2026/01/52-minutes-sur-le-programme-des-24emes-reconcentres-de-die-et-de-la-biovallee-avec-joelle-vial-estelle-gilliot-michel-leon-et-claude-veyret/)
- Market: **Wednesday and Saturday mornings**, ~120 stalls, Place de la République. Summer organic evening market on Mondays. [camping-pinede.com](https://www.camping-pinede.com/fr/tourisme-drome/marches/)
- Local traditions: Clairette de Die wine, lavender, transhumance *(guess)*.

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ⚠️ France needs a yearly **authorisation** since 2022 (see [france.md](../regions/france.md)). ✅ But legal alternative schools exist: **Terresdenfance** in Die (ages 3–8, since 2013), **École Buissonnière du Diois** (primary). [terresdenfance.fr](https://terresdenfance.fr/), [ecole-buissonniere-diois.org](https://www.ecole-buissonniere-diois.org/) *(site did not load 2026-09-27)*
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ max 3 months/year without permission, then a permit (see [france.md](../regions/france.md)).
- Wildfire risk: ⚠️ *medium (dry summers, forest slopes) — guess.*
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ✅ low expat share. ⚠️ Summer tourism in the Vercors and on the river *(guess)*.

## Water (honest view)
- Summer drought is **recurring**. Diois was under drought vigilance in July 2025. [Médias Citoyens Diois, 2025-07](https://mediascitoyens-diois.info/2025/07/43506/)
- July 2026: "vallée de la Drôme" among the most affected areas, **alerte renforcée** (pool filling, lawns, car washing banned). [ICI, 2026-07-30](https://www.ici.fr/auvergne-rhone-alpes/drome-26/secheresse-dans-la-drome-des-restrictions-renforcees-sur-plusieurs-secteurs-du-departement-2100398)
- The lower Drôme river often dries up in summer (*assec*). *Guess from news snippet; not confirmed for 2026.*
- *The upper side valleys (near the Vercors) have more springs. Only take land with its own spring. Guess.*

## Costs
- *House with some land in the Diois: ~€200–350k; land alone cheaper. Guess, Drôme is pricier than Ariège.* Farm land in Drôme rose ~6% in 2025 (irrigated land). [Réussir](https://www.reussir.fr/quel-prix-des-terres-agricoles-2025-par-departement)
