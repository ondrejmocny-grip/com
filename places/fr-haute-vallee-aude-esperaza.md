## In short
A quiet, cheap valley between Carcassonne and the Pyrenees. The **Espéraza Sunday market** is the meeting point of néo-ruraux, "hippie types" and local producers. Close to Bugarach and Rennes-le-Château, which attract **esoteric / New Age** tourists. Sea is ~1 h 15. Less documented than Diois or Cévennes.

## People
- Espéraza organic market: "colorful, organic, friendly, many small producers, néo-ruraux and hippie types". [Pyrénées Mon Amour](https://www.pyrenees-mon-amour.com/2019/03/25/marche-bio-esperaza/)
- ⚠️ Bugarach and Rennes-le-Château: known for esoteric tourism (the 2012 "end of the world" hype at Bugarach). *Risk of a show-off / conspiracy fringe. Guess, check on site.*

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ⚠️ authorisation needed (see [france.md](../regions/france.md)). ⚠️ No alt school confirmed in the valley (search 2026-09-27). *Check Limoux / Carcassonne / Chalabre.*
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ French 3-month rule.
- Wildfire risk: ⚠️ **high in the Aude.** Aug 2025 Corbières fire: 17,000 ha, 36 houses destroyed, worst on the French Mediterranean in 50 years. It was east of this valley (Corbières), not in it. [Wikipedia FR](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incendie_du_massif_des_Corbi%C3%A8res_de_2025)
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ⚠️ light; esoteric tourism at Bugarach / Rennes-le-Château; rafting in summer *(guess)*.

## Water (honest view)
- Winter 2025–26: best recharge in 30 years in the Aude. But spring 2026 was very dry (–27% rain) and restrictions started in **June 2026**. [info.fr, 2026](https://info.fr/aude-premieres-mesures-restriction-eau-secheresse-precoce/), [Pyrénées FM](https://pyreneesfm.com/secheresse-dans-l-aude-le-departement-place-sous-vigilance-malgre-un-hiver-pluvi)
- *The upper valley (Aude river from the Pyrenees, Pays de Sault) is wetter than the Corbières or the coast. Guess.*
- Check current rules per village: [vigieau.gouv.fr](https://vigieau.gouv.fr/)

## Costs
- Aude is cheap for houses in the upper valley *(guess: village houses €60–120k, small farm with land €150–250k)*. Farm land average in Aude is higher because of vineyards *(~€13,000/ha per search snippet; not checked)*. [Réussir](https://www.reussir.fr/quel-prix-des-terres-agricoles-2025-par-departement)
