## In short
Green hills of inner Provence with old villages, springs and fountains. Correns turned almost fully organic and got younger (farmers aged 35–40). Barjols is an old tannery town where artists settled. **Main doubts:** hot, dry summers with yearly water restrictions, high fire risk, Var prices, no clear alternative scene, French needed.

## People
- Correns: almost all land organic; population growing; newcomers came for the organic idea. [La Bouscule, updated 2026-02-02](https://www.labouscule.com/ecologie/correns-village-bio/), [Reporterre](https://reporterre.net/A-Correns-dans-le-Var-le-bio-est-un-mode-de-vie)
- "Neo-farmers aged 30–40" rejuvenated the village; young people came back. [Tourisme varois](https://www.tourisme-varois.com/ce-village-du-var-est-le-premier-village-bio-de-france-une-initiative-pionniere)
- Barjols: artists settled in the old tanneries. [Barjols – Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barjols)
- Eco-place near Verdon (Provence Verte): permaculture, off-grid power, water management + yoga, meditation, dance; caravans/tipis, vans OK. [Workaway](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/875156594288)
- *Spiritual scene: small (guess). Cotignac has many British/foreign second homes (guess, not checked).*

## Integration
- Organic project is led by the village itself (mayor, farmers) → locals and newcomers work together. [Tourisme varois](https://www.tourisme-varois.com/ce-village-du-var-est-le-premier-village-bio-de-france-une-initiative-pionniere)
- Traditions: Provençal markets, fêtes; Barjols Saint-Marcel festival *(guess from memory, verify)*; 28–43 fountains. [MarseilleTourisme](https://www.marseilletourisme.fr/fr/que-voir/patrimoine-culture/villes-villages/barjols/)

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ⚠️ yearly authorisation in France. Correns has a public maternelle + primary with 75–80 % organic canteen. [Tourisme varois](https://www.tourisme-varois.com/ce-village-du-var-est-le-premier-village-bio-de-france-une-initiative-pionniere). Montessori-inspired École Emmanuel (3–12 y) in Brignoles *(search summary only)*.
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ 3-month rule, then permit.
- Wildfire: ⚠️⚠️ **high** — Var forests and garrigue, yearly fire season *(guess, well known; not checked for 2025–26)*.
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ⚠️ summer tourism (Verdon, "plus beaux villages") *(guess)*.

## Water (honest view)
- Rain ~820 mm/yr (Cotignac), wettest month October. [en.wikipedia – Cotignac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cotignac)
- 30 Jun 2026: whole Var on drought watch. [Mairie de Cotignac](https://mairiecotignac.fr/arrete-du-30-juin-2026-placant-en-situation-de-vigilance-secheresse-le-departement-du-var/)
- **Aug 2026: 102 Var communes under restrictions, incl. Barjols and Cotignac (Argens zone).** Restrictions are now "almost every summer". [presseagence](https://presseagence.fr/toulon-secheresse-15-nouvelles-communes-du-var-en-alerte-102-au-total-concernees-par-des-restrictions/), [Chambre d'agriculture PACA](https://paca.chambres-agriculture.fr/toutes-les-actualites/detail-de-lactualite/83-secheresse-dans-le-var)
- Karst springs feed the fountains *(guess)*. Canal de Provence water in parts of the Var. [Canal de Provence](https://canaldeprovence.com/agence-en-ligne/suivi-des-arretes-secheresse/)
- *Verdict: ⚠️ close to our deal-breaker. Only with own spring / canal connection.*
