## In short
A deep, green Alpine valley that ends at the Mediterranean. Saorge has had néo-ruraux since the 1970s. Since Storm Alex (Oct 2020) there is a new wave of self-organised projects: a rural tiers-lieu, eco-building, an agricultural Emmaüs community. **Main doubts:** small scene, no alternative school, flood + drought risk, French needed.

## People
- Saorge: "the 1970s hippies were followed by néo-ruraux looking for nature and simplicity". [villagesetpatrimoine](https://www.villagesetpatrimoine.fr/saorge/)
- After Storm Alex ~30 citizens started a tiers-lieu idea to keep links and a local economy. [France Tiers-Lieux](https://observatoire.francetierslieux.fr/tiers-lieu-dans-la-vallee-de-la-roya-reconstruire-apres-la-tempete/)
- Strong solidarity culture (help for migrants at the border). Cédric Herrou's farm became the first agricultural Emmaüs community (2019). [France Bleu](https://www.francebleu.fr/infos/economie-social/cedric-herrou-cree-la-premiere-communaute-agricole-emmaus-dans-la-roya-1565292209), [Sans Transition, 2019-10-28](https://www.sans-transition-magazine.info/social/vallee-de-la-roya-un-retour-la-terre-pour-senraciner)
- Spiritual side: *not found. Scene looks more political / ecological / artistic than spiritual (guess).* Saorge has a Franciscan monastery (now a writers' residence). [Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/france/vallee-de-la-roya/attractions/monastere-de-saorge/a/poi-sig/1229678/1323059)

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ⚠️ France needs yearly authorisation (see [france.md](../regions/france.md)). Only public schools in Breil (maternelle, primary, collège); lycée in Menton. [fr.wikipedia](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breil-sur-Roya)
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ 3 months/year free, then permit (France rules). Steep valley = little flat land *(guess)*.
- Wildfire: ⚠️ medium *(guess — Mediterranean scrub on lower slopes)*.
- Floods: ⚠️⚠️ **Storm Alex 2–3 Oct 2020**: 6 bridges gone, homes damaged, 3 deaths in Breil, ~€25 M damage. [fr.wikipedia](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breil-sur-Roya)
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ✅ low *(guess)*.

## Water (honest view)
- Rain ~950 mm/year; river Roya from the Alps. [fr.wikipedia](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breil-sur-Roya)
- ⚠️ **July 2026:** "Roya amont" basin under water restrictions (no watering 8:00–20:00). Rain deficit 15–20 % since Sept 2025; 100 communes of the département restricted on 13 Aug 2026. [Préfecture 06, July 2026](https://www.alpes-maritimes.gouv.fr/Actualites/Espace-Presse/2026/JUILLET/Secheresse-mesures-de-restriction-des-usages-de-l-eau2), [Préfecture 06, Aug 2026](https://www.alpes-maritimes.gouv.fr/Actualites/Espace-Presse/2026/AOUT/Secheresse-dans-les-Alpes-Maritimes)
- Saorge: a landslide linked to water infiltration is being studied (2025). [Fondation Carasso](https://fondationcarasso.org/nouvelles/la-parole-aux-laureats-de-composer-les-savoirs-pour-imaginer-un-avenir-durable-2025/)
- *Verdict: better than the coast, not drought-proof. Land with a spring is key (guess).*
