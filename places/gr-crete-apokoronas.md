# Apokoronas (Chania hinterland, West Crete)

- **Status:** dropped (2026-09-27) — **water: 4th dry year in a row, villages without water**. Also no legal alternative school in Greece.
- **Country / region:** Greece, Crete, Chania regional unit, Apokoronas municipality. Villages: Vamos, Kefalas, Gavalochori, Plaka, Almyrida, Kalyves, Kokkino Chorio.
- **Nearest town / airport:** Chania city 15–30 min, Chania airport ~40 min *(guess)*. [elxis.com](https://elxis.com/where-do-most-expats-live-in-crete)
- **Website / contacts:** Holon Yoga (Vamos): [holon.gr](https://holon.gr/). Light Love and Yoga (Plaka): [lightloveandyoga.com](https://lightloveandyoga.com/)
- **Added:** 2026-09-27 by Claude (web research)

## In short
The most popular expat area of Crete. Green (for Crete) hills between the White Mountains and the sea. Many yoga places and retreats. On paper it fits the Órgiva pattern well. **But water is now a crisis**, and schooling is a problem (Greek law).

**Órgiva pattern:** 6/8 points (on paper) — ✅ 1 market towns (Vamos, Kalyves), ✅ 2 many newcomers, ✅ 3 spread in villages, ✅ 4 yoga/retreat projects, ✅ 5 *markets (guess)*, ❌ 6 alternative schools, ✅ 7 mountains + sea, ⚠️ 8 easy start (only one camper per plot). **Fails deal-breaker: water.**

[holon.gr](https://holon.gr/), [lightloveandyoga.com](https://lightloveandyoga.com/), [theolivefarm.gr](https://www.theolivefarm.gr/cooking-and-yoga-retreat/)
- *Many retirees, not many young alternative families. Guess.*

## Water (honest) — the reason to drop
- 2025–26 is the **lowest-rainfall year on record** for Crete and the **4th dry year in a row**. Reservoirs and aquifers falling. Chania unit meets "dry year" criteria 4 years in a row. [argophilia, 2026-02-05](https://www.argophilia.com/news/drought-crete-2026/247766/)
- **2024: villages in "normally green" Apokoronas went months without water**, residents protested. Some village zones lack water even in normal years. [search summary: claireleesingham substack](https://claireleesingham.substack.com/p/water-water-wheres-it-gone), [greekreporter, 2024-09-11](https://greekreporter.com/2024/09/11/water-shortage-mismanagement-crete/)
- ~6 million tourists per year use water in the driest months. [argophilia](https://www.argophilia.com/news/drought-crete-2026/247766/)

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ❌ illegal in Greece. [HSLDA](https://hslda.org/post/greece). No Waldorf / free school in Chania; only a private international school (Theodoropoulos). [grekodom, 2026-06-22](https://www.grekodom.com/news/2026-06-22/european-schools-crete-expat-guide-heraklion-chania). Nearest alternative: School of Nature and Colors, Fourfouras (Rethymno) — but it is a state-frame school. [greekreporter](https://greekreporter.com/2014/06/23/greek-school-in-international-list-of-alternative-education/)
- Van: one camper per private plot (law 5170/2025). [nikana.gr](https://nikana.gr/en/blog/7342/new-camping-law-in-greece-2025-rules-restrictions-and-penalties-for-camper-vehicles)
- Wildfire: ⚠️ high in summer (Greek norm). *Guess.*
- Expat bubble: ⚠️ yes (British). Mass tourism: ⚠️ yes on the coast.
