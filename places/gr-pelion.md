# Mount Pelion (Zagora / Tsagarada east side + Argalasti in South Pelion)

- **Status:** lead
- **Country / region:** Greece, Thessaly, Magnesia. Hub villages: **Zagora** (~2,000 people, north-east), **Argalasti** (~1,400, South Pelion market center). Green villages between: Tsagarada, Kissos, Anilio, Milies. [Wikipedia – Zagora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zagora,_Greece), [Wikipedia – Argalasti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argalasti)
- **Nearest town / airport:** Volos city (hospital, university) ~45–75 min *(guess)*. Airports: Volos (Nea Anchialos, small, seasonal) or Thessaloniki / Athens ~3–4 h *(guess)*.
- **Website / contacts:** Kalikalos Holistic Centre (Anilio): [kalikalos.com](https://www.kalikalos.com/)
- **Added:** 2026-09-27 by Claude (web research)

## In short
A mountain peninsula (1,600 m) that falls straight into the sea. The east side is **very green, with springs everywhere**. It has had a small "back to nature" and holistic scene for 20+ years (Kalikalos, founded by Findhorn people in 2001). **But:** the newcomer scene is much smaller than Órgiva, there are **no alternative schools**, and **home schooling is illegal in Greece**.

**Órgiva pattern:** 4/8 points — ✅ real market villages (1), ✅ spread out (3), ✅ weekly market in Argalasti (5), ✅ mountains + sea (7). Partial: newcomers (2), projects (4), easy start (8). ❌ alternative schools (6).

## People
- Mostly Greek villagers, plus Greeks from Athens/Volos who moved here, plus some British and other foreigners (e.g. in Argalasti). [Expat Forum](https://expatforum.com/expats/kafeneio/159394-english-people-pelion.html)
- A 2012 article spoke of **"eco-communities flourishing on Mount Pelion"** after the Greek crisis. [South as a State of Mind (citing Tvxs.gr, 2012)](https://southasastateofmind.com/article/defence-urban-alienation-regression-localisation-dreams-exodus-greece-today/). *How many are still active in 2026 is not known — check on site.*
- Spiritual: **Kalikalos** — seasonal holistic community with sharing circles, workshops (healing, communication, self-enquiry), vegetarian life. Started 2001 by people from Findhorn. Kissos campus left the network by 2021; **Anilio** continues. [kalikalos.com – heritage](https://www.kalikalos.com/about-us/our-heritage/), [Kalikalos community](https://www.kalikalos.com/community/)
- Yoga retreats in Tsagarada. [Anna Zorzou](https://annazorzou.gr/yoga-retreats/tsagarada-pelion/)
- Grounded? *Probably yes — small, low-key scene, not show-off. Guess.*

## Integration
- Real Greek villages with strong traditions: stone mansions, cobbled paths (kalderimia), village festivals, chestnuts and apples. [greekreporter, 2026-01-22](https://greekreporter.com/2026/01/22/villages-pelion-greece/)
- Argalasti: "important commercial center" of South Pelion with a **weekly market day** (local olives, oil, herbs). [gtp.gr](https://www.gtp.gr/LocPage.asp?id=13017&lng=2), [search summary: ilovepelion](https://ilovepelion.gr/destinations/pagasiticview/argalasti)
- *Greeks in villages are warm but language is needed. Guess.*

So: park on a friend's / host's land = *possible*.

## Water (honest)
- East Pelion is one of the wettest places in Greece. "Springs come out of the mountain everywhere, which is why it is green when the plain below is burnt." [search summary: travelthegreekway / aroundpelion](https://www.aroundpelion.com/villages/tsagarada)
- Rain: wettest month December ~116 mm, driest June ~22 mm (Zagora). [climate-data.org](https://en.climate-data.org/europe/greece/zagora/zagora-717699/)
- ⚠️ **Flood risk:** Storm Daniel (Sept 2023) dropped **754 mm in one day** at Zagora (Greek record); roads cut, villages stranded. [greekcitytimes](https://greekcitytimes.com/2023/09/06/rain-record-in-greece-was-broken/), [greekreporter](https://greekreporter.com/2023/09/08/thousands-trapped-pelion-greece-floods/)
- South Pelion (Argalasti side) is **drier** than the east. *Guess.*
- **Verdict:** water good on the east side. Buy / lease only land with a spring, not in a stream bed.

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ❌ **illegal in Greece** (Law 1566/1985, school compulsory). [HSLDA](https://hslda.org/post/greece). After age 4 no legal option other than state-approved schools. [self-directed.org](https://www.self-directed.org/tp/defending-the-obvious/). No Waldorf school found in Volos/Pelion (search 2026-09-27). Nearest Waldorf: Athens, Thessaloniki (kindergarten).
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ one camper per private plot is OK; more = camping license.
- Wildfire: ⚠️ medium. Aug 2026 fire at Paralia Pau (South Pelion), controlled. [insider.gr](https://www.insider.gr/eidiseis/420891/pyrkagia-sto-notio-pilio-minyma-apo-112), [efsyn](https://www.efsyn.gr/ellada/1432157/pyrkagia-se-dasiki-ektasi-sto-pilio-epiheiroyn-ishyres-dynameis-katasvesis/). Green east side burns less. *Guess.*
- Expat bubble: ✅ low. Mass tourism: ⚠️ Greek summer and weekend tourism (Volos, Athens), strong in August. *Guess.*
