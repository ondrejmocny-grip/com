## In short
An eco-retreat and community on ~4 acres by the sea. Founded 1986 by a group of Osho disciples. About 20 people live and work there. Organic garden (permaculture since 2001), guest rooms, yoga, meditation, workshops, and a one-month "Argayall Experience" for young adults.
