- **Status:** lead
- **Country / region:** Spain, Canary Islands, La Gomera, north side. Hermigua, Agulo, Vallehermoso, and La Laja (upper Barranco de La Villa, San Sebastián municipality).

## In short
The green, quiet north of La Gomera: deep valleys with bananas, palms and terraces under the Garajonay laurel forest. Villages are **losing people**, so newcomers who farm may be welcome. A few small eco projects exist, including one run by a **Czech botanist**. No alternative school found. Good for a calm, local life; weak for families like us.

## People
- Hermigua lost the most population in the Canaries in 2023–24 (−2.7%). The mayor wants to buy land for public housing: few homes for sale is a main problem. [El Tambor](https://www.eltambor.es/hermigua-en-la-gomera-es-el-que-mas-poblacion-ha-perdido-de-canarias-con-un-27-menos-que-hace-tres-anos/)
- Economy: bananas, wine. Programme "Dinamiza Rural" wants young people in farming. [Diario de Avisos 2026-07](https://diariodeavisos.elespanol.com/2026/07/dinamiza-rural-hermigua-la-barberia-de-domingo/)
- Newcomers:
  - Zdenka Hájková: Czech botanist, gardener, horticultural therapist. Came 2011, helped build a permaculture project (Finca Alexandria), lives in La Laja since 2019. Workshops: permaculture, dry-stone walls, palm basket weaving, "Empowerment Week". Languages: German, Spanish, English, **Czech**. [gomeracorazonverde.com](https://gomeracorazonverde.com/en/), [Sakti Yoga](https://saktiyoga.de/2020/10/16/zdenka-hajkova/)
  - Vallehermoso farm (local Iru + Swiss Hugo, since 2020): 3,000 m², avocado, mango, papaya; permaculture + traditional farming; restoring an old kiln. Active Sep 2026. [Workaway](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/844459875183)
  - Ecofinca Hermigua: small permaculture self-learning garden inside a banana farm. [search summary / Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/ecofincahermigua/)
- Spiritual orientation: *low-key, nature-based (guess).*

## Integration
- *Villages are mostly Canarian (guess).* Traditions alive: silbo gomero (whistled language), terraces, palm honey. *(common knowledge — verify)*
- Zdenka's courses teach local crafts (palm brooms, Canarian mojo) → *good bridge to local culture (guess).*

- Visit Zdenka (Czech!) and ask about the island's network. Volunteer at the Vallehermoso farm.
- Rent in a village: west/north rentals from ~€475/month (listings). - Lease abandoned terraces from older owners. *(guess)*
- Van: strict (see [region](../regions/canary-islands.md#law-and-practical)). *Host's land with consent (guess).*

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ⚠️ grey zone (Spain). ❌ No alternative school found on La Gomera (search 2026-09-27). Public infant schools in Agulo, Vallehermoso, Valle Gran Rey. [tododecanarias](https://tododecanarias.com/colegios-en-la-gomera)
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ same Canary rules.
- Wildfire risk: ⚠️ Aug 2012 fire burned 3,600+ ha and ~20% of Garajonay. *North valleys are greener, but forest is just above (guess).* [The Ecologist](https://theecologist.org/2017/jan/30/witness-la-gomera-forest-fires)
- Water: ⚠️ Island water emergency July 2022 (dry aquifers). New desalination plants and Igualero tank. [Tenerife Weekly](https://tenerifeweekly.com/2022/07/20/la-gomera-declares-a-water-emergency/), [lagomera.es](https://www.lagomera.es/noticia/el-gobierno-culmina-la-reconstruccion-del-deposito-de-igualero-y-refuerza-la-red) North gets more rain than south. [canarianfeeling](https://www.canarianfeeling.com/la-gomera-experience/la-gomera-weather)
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ✅ low in the north *(guess)*.
