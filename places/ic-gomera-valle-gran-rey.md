# Valle Gran Rey (La Gomera) – alternative hub

- **Status:** lead (as a **hub to visit and connect**; *probably not a place to live — guess*)
- **Country / region:** Spain, Canary Islands, La Gomera, south-west. Villages: La Calera, Vueltas, La Playa, upper valley.
- **Nearest town / airport:** San Sebastián (port) ~1 h *(guess)*. Ferry to Tenerife, then TFS airport.
- **Website / contacts:** no umbrella group found. Local info: [lagomera1 blog](https://lagomera1.blogspot.com/2024/). See also [Finca Argayall (dropped)](ic-gomera-argayall.md).
- **Added:** 2026-09-27 by Claude

## In short
The best-known alternative place in the Canaries. Hippies came in the late 1960s/70s and some stayed. Today: meditation centres, yoga, sunset drumming, craft market — **and** a tourist town with holiday flats. A good place to meet people and hear about projects on the island. Honest view: part show, part real; dry; tensions with some locals.

## People
- ~4,500 inhabitants; 2nd town of the island. Most foreign buyers are German (>60%, per estate agents). [cbtuhogar 2025](https://www.cbtuhogar.es/blog/article/zonas-mas-solicitadas-para-comprar-en-valle-gran-rey-en-2025/)
- Alternative scene: meditation centres, yoga, crafts (jewellers, potters, artists), Sunday market. [canarianfeeling](https://www.canarianfeeling.com/canary-island-la-gomera/cities-and-communities), [slowtravelonline](https://slowtravelonline.com/en/tips/responsable-tourism/hippie-villages-spain)
- The "alternatives" live separate lives; sunset drumming and fire shows draw tourists. [Irish Examiner](https://www.irishexaminer.com/lifestyle/outdoors/damien-enright/where-hippie-ambience-and-vacationers-expectations-collide-467602.html)
- ⚠️ Show-off risk: sunset drum/fire shows for tourists. *Not a deal-breaker by itself, but check the vibe (guess).*

## Integration
- Split opinions: some locals say the hippies started the island's tourism; others see them as untidy and bad for "posh and elderly" tourists. [Irish Examiner](https://www.irishexaminer.com/lifestyle/outdoors/damien-enright/where-hippie-ambience-and-vacationers-expectations-collide-467602.html)
- March 2021: police evicted ~20 beach/cave campers. [lagomera1](https://lagomera1.blogspot.com/2021/03/campers-evicted-in-valle-gran-rey.html)
- Town has social "caves" housing for long-term alternative residents *(from a blog; may be satire — verify)*. [lagomera1](https://lagomera1.blogspot.com/2018/04/new-hippie-caves.html)

## How we could join
- Stay 1–2 weeks as visitors. Go to the market, yoga, talks. Ask about projects in the north (Hermigua, Vallehermoso).
- Rent: tourist flats dominate (317 holiday listings; ~€585/week). [likibu](https://www.likibu.com/es/alquiler-apartamentos-vacaciones/valle-gran-rey) Long-term rent from ~€475 in the Vallehermoso–VGR area. [idealista](https://www.idealista.com/en/alquiler-viviendas/santa-cruz-de-tenerife/la-gomera/vallehermoso-valle-gran-rey/)
- Van: ❌ beach camping is enforced.

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ⚠️ grey zone (Spain). Public school only found (EEI Valle Gran Rey). [tododecanarias](https://tododecanarias.com/colegios-en-la-gomera)
- Van / yurt on land: ❌ evictions (2021), Costas law on beaches.
- Wildfire risk: ⚠️ 2012 fire burned 100+ houses, mostly in the upper valley. [The Ecologist](https://theecologist.org/2017/jan/30/witness-la-gomera-forest-fires)
- Water: ❌ Dry (~371 mm/yr). Oct 2023 town water emergency: cuts; no water for farming, pools, cars. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valle_Gran_Rey), [Tenerife Weekly](https://tenerifeweekly.com/2023/10/11/the-valle-gran-rey-city-council-la-gomera-declares-the-situation-of-water-emergency/)
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ❌ tourist town, German-heavy.
