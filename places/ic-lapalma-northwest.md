## In short
The green, quiet northwest of La Palma. Normal Canarian farming villages, with many European newcomers (mostly German) since the 1970s. Organic farms, a permaculture academy and a busy weekend market. The hub town Los Llanos (Waldorf kindergarten, shops) is 30–40 min away. This fits our "live near a hub, where land is cheaper" model. Big minuses: **2023 wildfire here**, and the **2021 volcano** hit the hub valley.

## People
- Foreign-born share (2020): Puntagorda 27%, Garafía 25%, Tijarafe 21% (island average 11%). Most are German, then Italian, British, Dutch. [eldiario.es 2021](https://www.eldiario.es/canariasahora/lapalmaahora/sociedad/puntagorda-municipio-palma-mayor-porcentaje-residentes-extranjeros_1_8293120.html), [search summary](https://elapuron.com/blogs/idayvuelta/1623/los-extranjeros-en-la-palma-compensan-la-despoblacin/)
- Newcomers run many market stalls: organic vegetables, own meat, bread, crafts. [welikecanarias](https://welikecanarias.com/en/best-markets-flea-markets-la-palma/)
- Garafía and Puntagorda are the only La Palma towns that **gain** working people (others lose). [El Time](https://eltime.es/isla-bonita/50374-la-migracion-laboral-la-palma-pierde-mas-poblacion-trabajadora-de-la-que-recibe-excepto-en-garafia-y-puntagorda.html)
- Projects:
  - Finca Autarca (Barbara & Erich Graf, since 2007): 4 ha edible forest, PDC courses, day seminars (€45; kids €15), next date seen: 12 May 2026. [matricultura.org](http://matricultura.org/index_E.html)
  - Retreats: Vidyamor (Tijarafe, vegan detox/yoga, for individuals). [BookRetreats](https://bookretreats.com/organizers/o/vidyamor) Eco-Finca Miraflores (herbal healing). [Canary Green](https://www.canarygreen.org/a-holistic-retreat-in-the-heart-of-la-palma-eco-finca-miraflores/)
  - Ecolen (La Palma) is in the government "Fincas Faro" agroecology network. [RTVC](https://rtvc.es/presentan-el-proyecto-de-fincas-agroecologicas-fincas-faro/)
  - Serenity La Palma: GEN-listed, but only 2 members. [GEN](https://ecovillage.org/ecovillage/serenity-la-palma/)
- Spiritual orientation: *mixed — permaculture, raw-vegan wellness, herbal healing. Some retreats look more "wellness tourism" than community (guess).*
- Grounded: *yes, farming is real work here (guess).*

## Integration
- Local traditions: almonds, wine, goat cheese, "almendrados". Market since 2002. [visitlapalma.es](https://visitlapalma.es/en/shopping/la-palma/puntagorda-market/)
- ⚠️ With 20–27% foreigners, *there may be a German-speaking bubble (guess). Learning Spanish is key.*

## How we could join
- Volunteer / course first: Finca Autarca day seminar or PDC. Workaway hosts on La Palma. [Voluntouring](https://voluntouring.org/2023/09/05/permaculture-vegan-project-la-palma/)
- Rent a house in a village, or lease a finca from an older owner. *(guess — ask at the market)*
- Van: no legal long stay on public land (see [region](../regions/canary-islands.md#law-and-practical)). *Park on a host's finca (grey zone) (guess).*
- Later buy: NW fincas with irrigation ~€145k for 1.1 ha; 1 ha + house €235k; one plot in Llano Negro says "~60 tourist beds possible". [la-palma24.es, 2026-09-27](https://www.la-palma24.es/property-type/finca-agricoa/)
- Contribute: Nesli = cooking, gardening, yoga, animals; Ondrej = community, tech. The Waldorf group needs families to restart primary.

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ⚠️ grey zone (Spain). Waldorf kindergarten "Lena" in Los Llanos; **primary group closed for now**. Escuela Libre San Miguel (El Paso) no longer listed. [Waldorf blog](http://grupowaldorflapalma.blogspot.com/), [ludus](https://ludus.org.es/en/escuela-libre-san-miguel-de-la-palma)
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ same as all Canaries. Holiday rental on rustic land banned (Ley 6/2025), but green islands get 20% quota and some easing to Dec 2027. [inmomashome](https://inmomashome.com/blog/nueva-ley-vivienda-vacacional-canarias-2025), [alquilerviviendavacacional.com](https://alquilerviviendavacacional.com/canarias-100-preguntas-y-respuestas-sobre-la-ley-6-2025-de-10-de-diciembre-de-ordenacion-sostenible-del-uso-turistico-de-viviendas/)
- Wildfire risk: ❌ **High.** July 2023: fire started in the Puntagorda pine forest, ~4,650 ha, ~4,200 evacuated, 13+ houses lost. [Canarian Weekly](https://www.canarianweekly.com/posts/Forest-fire-in-La-Palma-rises-to-Level-2-emergency-and-evacuation-of-residents), [guidetocanaryislands](https://www.guidetocanaryislands.com/la-palma-wildfire-affects-4500-hectares-more-than-2-000-residents-evicted/)
- Volcano: ⚠️ 2021 eruption hit the Aridane valley (hub): 1,345 homes lost. Yellow alert still (gas in Puerto Naos). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Cumbre_Vieja_volcanic_eruption), [Infobae 2026-07](https://www.infobae.com/america/agencias/2026/07/16/el-gobierno-de-canarias-no-ve-condiciones-cientificas-para-activar-el-semaforo-volcanico-en-tenerife/)
- Water: ⚠️ North is wetter and greener. [lapalmaisland.com](https://lapalmaisland.com/en/weather-la-palma/) But drought in the Aridane valley; farmers asked for water emergency (Dec 2024). [eldiario.es](https://www.eldiario.es/canariasahora/lapalmaahora/economia/sector-platanero-palma-pide-emergencia-hidrica-afeccion-gases-volcanicos-agua-sequia_1_11893115.html) *Check water rights for any plot.*
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ⚠️ many Germans. ✅ no mass tourism.

## Costs
- Land: see above (€100–260k for 0.4–5.6 ha, asking prices + 3% agency). [la-palma24.es](https://www.la-palma24.es/property-type/finca-agricoa/)
- Housing in Los Llanos: €2,543/m² (Jul 2026), almost double since 2021; rents almost doubled. [La Región 2026-09](https://www.laregion.es/espana/cinco-anos-erupcion-volcan-palma-familias-casas-provisionales_1_20260919-4419594.html)
- *NW village rent: probably lower than Los Llanos (guess).*
