- **Status:** lead
- **Country / region:** Spain, Canary Islands, Tenerife north. Core: Tegueste, Tacoronte, El Sauzal, La Laguna edge. Wider: Anaga Rural Park (northeast), La Orotava valley.

## In short
Not one project, but a **zone**: normal Canarian towns in the green north of Tenerife, near the university city of La Laguna. It has the **most alternative schools in the Canaries**, an eco food co-op, and several permaculture farms. Anaga mountains and the sea are close. Big minus: **water emergency on the island** and high prices.

## Integration
- Mostly Spanish-speaking, local projects. *Good for real contact with locals (guess).*
- Local traditions: vineyards (Tacoronte-Acentejo wine), Anaga villages like Taganana with terraces and Malvasía vines. Anaga has 26 small settlements, ~2,500 people; many left since the 1960s. [Wikivoyage](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Anaga_Rural_Park), [bye:myself](https://byemyself.com/taganana-over-the-hills-and-far-away-in-tenerife/)
- Anaga is a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve (2015). [Hello Canary Islands](https://www.hellocanaryislands.com/nature-spaces/tenerife/anaga-rural-park/)

## How we could join
- Volunteer first: Anaga finca (Workaway), Hoya del Brezal (Anaga, 10-month agroforestry programme), off-grid permaculture in Santa Catalina. [Workaway Anaga](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/244279288174), [Workaway Santa Catalina](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/874621225539), [search result](https://www.workaway.info/en/hostlist/europe/es?region=canary-Islands)
- Join La Gangochera co-op; ask its farms about leasing land. *(guess)*
- Ilatán (Icod de los Vinos): eco-village with 1 ha forest. ⚠️ Entry process: form, video call, visits, **one-year trial** → closer to an intentional community. OK to visit, *probably not our model (guess)*. [ecobuking](https://viajes.ecobuking.es/complejo/ilatan/)
- Van: Cabildo camping zones, max 7 days with permit. Wild camping forbidden. [Cabildo de Tenerife](https://www.tenerife.es/zonas-de-acampada)
- Contribute: wife = cooking, gardening, yoga, animals; Ondrej = community, tech, education (schools are parent-run).

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ⚠️ grey zone (Spain). But several alternative schools here → less need to home-school. *Check if they are officially approved.* [legal overview](../regions/legal-overview.md)
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ Wild camping forbidden. New Canary camping decree pending (2026). Housing on rustic land only in "asentamiento rural" or linked to a farm. [Canarias7 2026-07](https://www.canarias7.es/economia/turismo/decreto-caravanas-elimina-acampada-libre-canarias-exige-20260723060000-nt.html), [Agroingenia](https://agroingeniacanarias.com/construir-en-suelo-rustico-ley-del_suelo_de_2017/)
- Wildfire risk: ❌ **High.** Aug 2023 fire (~14,900 ha) burned parts of Tacoronte, El Sauzal, La Matanza, La Victoria, Santa Úrsula, La Orotava. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Tenerife_wildfire)
- Water: ❌ **Island water emergency since May 2024**, extended into 2026. Aquifers falling for decades. [Canarian Weekly](https://www.canarianweekly.com/posts/Cabildo-officially-declares-a-water-shortage-emergency-in-Tenerife), [RTVC](https://rtvc.es/tenerife-lanzarote-la-graciosa-prorrogan-emergencia-hidrica-2026-15-octubre-2025/)
- Volcano: ⚠️ Teide quake swarms 2025–26, still green alert. [Diario de Avisos 2026-08](https://diariodeavisos.elespanol.com/2026/08/pevolca-terremotos-en-tenerife-sin-riesgo-erupcion/)
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ✅ north towns local *(guess)*. ❌ Island has mass tourism; housing crisis and protests. [Euronews 2025](https://www.euronews.com/travel/2025/05/16/inequality-and-infinite-growth-canary-islands-anti-tourism-protests-reignite-amid-record-a)

## Costs
- Tacoronte homes ~€2,040/m² (June 2026). [idealista](https://www.idealista.com/sala-de-prensa/informes-precio-vivienda/venta/canarias/santa-cruz-de-tenerife-provincia/tacoronte/tacoronte-los-naranjeros/)
- Rent: Santa Cruz de Tenerife province €14.54/m² (Apr 2025) → ~€1,000+ for 70–80 m². [Radio Las Palmas](https://www.radiolaspalmas.com/economia/2025/05/19/el-precio-del-alquiler-en-canarias-supera-los-1-100-euros-de-media/n12825.html)
- Rustic land Tenerife avg €157,491/ha (2024). [Cocampo](https://www.cocampo.com/es/es/noticias/precio-de-suelo-rustico-en-canarias/)
- School fees: not published *(check)*.
