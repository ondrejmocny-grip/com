## In short
The **birthplace of Steiner education in Ireland** (1986). Today a **state-funded** Steiner-style primary school and a Steiner-based secondary on a woodland site, plus a Camphill community. Lake Derg and low hills. Good for **families + schooling**. Weaker on sea and "many small projects" (not well documented online).

**Órgiva pattern:** 5/8 points — 1 market town ✅, 3 spread out ✅ *(guess)*, 6 alternative schools ✅✅, 8 easy start ⚠️/✅ (EU route), 2 newcomers ✅ *(Steiner families, guess)*. Weak: 4 projects ⚠️ *not well found*, 5 weekly market ⚠️ *not found*, 7 sea ⚠️ ~1 h, hills only.

## People
- Raheen Wood: first Steiner Waldorf school in Ireland (1986); now a **Community National School** (state school) keeping Steiner pedagogy since 2019. [raheenwood.org](https://raheenwood.org/), [LCETB](https://lcetb.ie/two-county-clare-steiner-national-schools-to-become-community-national-schools-under-patronage-of-the-authority/)
- ALFA: Steiner-based secondary (ages 12–17), started 2001 in a log cabin, now at Raheen Wood. [ALFA](https://alfasteinersecondary.org/our-school/about-our-school)
- Ard Aoibhinn charity supports ALFA, a youth club and **Camphill Community Mountshannon**. [LCETB / search](https://lcetb.ie/two-county-clare-steiner-national-schools-to-become-community-national-schools-under-patronage-of-the-authority/)
- *A school like this usually means many alternative families around (guess).*
