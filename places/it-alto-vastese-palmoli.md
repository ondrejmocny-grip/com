## In short
A real, fairly large cluster of neo-rural families lives in the woods here. It became famous through the "famiglia nel bosco" case. It is a **degrowth / off-grid** scene, not a retreat-and-projects scene. Water supply in 2026 was very bad.

## People
- ~60 people in the woods near Palmoli, Tufillo, San Buono; meetings around fires, music, talks. [Open](https://www.open.online/2025/11/23/neorurali-boschi-abruzzo-tribu-genitori-figli-birmingham-trevallion/)
- Mayor of Palmoli: at least ~30 more families with the same lifestyle. Between Tufillo and Palmoli ~15 families (from north/centre/south Italy, Vienna, Brazil), small children, home schooling, often university educated. [search summary – Il Messaggero](https://www.ilmessaggero.it/abruzzo/famiglia_bosco_neo_rurali_tufillo_figli_chi_sono_cosa_fanno_ultime_notizie-9203389.html), [Today.it](https://www.today.it/attualita/famiglia-bosco-palmoli-chi-sono-catherine-birmingham-nathan-trevallion.html)
- Style: "happy degrowth", self-sufficiency, some removed utilities on purpose. *More radical than us (guess).*

## Integration
- Mayor says other families have kids without problems. [Open](https://www.open.online/2025/11/23/neorurali-boschi-abruzzo-tribu-genitori-figli-birmingham-trevallion/)

## Water (honest view) — deal-breaker
- 3 Apr 2026: landslide at Acquaviva/Roccaspinalveti cut the supply; **15 towns of Alto and Medio Vastese without water**. From 15 Apr water back but **on rotation**. [Zonalocale, 2026-04-15](https://www.zonalocale.it/2026/04/15/sasi-torna-acqua-15-comuni-vastese/), [Chiaro Quotidiano, 2026-04-15](https://chiaroquotidiano.it/2026/04/15/emergenza-superata-lacqua-torna-razionata-nei-15-paesi-colpiti-dalla-crisi-idrica/)
- "Breakdowns and rotations 12 months a year." [Chiaro Quotidiano, 2026-04-06](https://chiaroquotidiano.it/2026/04/06/guasti-e-turnazioni-12-mesi-lanno-nel-vastese-lemergenza-acqua-non-finisce-mai/)
- 30 Aug 2026: Vastese still the worst hit area; Vasto up to 12 h a day without water. [WordNews, 2026-08-30](https://wordnews.it/2026/08/30/emergenza-idrica-continua-il-vastese-e-il-territorio-piu-colpito-a-vasto-anche-dodici-ore-senzacqua/)
- Note: the problem is mostly **broken infrastructure**, not only rain. A plot with its own spring could be OK *(guess)*.
