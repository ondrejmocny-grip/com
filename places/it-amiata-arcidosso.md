## In short
Arcidosso is a normal mountain market town on Monte Amiata. Since 1981 the Buddhist centre **Merigar West** is here. **Many people interested in Dzogchen moved to the area, above all to Arcidosso**, and formed an informal, spread-out community. This is the closest thing to an "Órgiva" we found in Italy. **Main doubts:** no alternative school found, colder winters, sea 1 h away, and the geothermal industry on the mountain.

## People
- Merigar: founded 1981, 50 ha on Monte Labbro above Zancona (Arcidosso). International Dzogchen Community has members in 40+ countries. [it.wikipedia – Merigar West](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merigar_West), [merigar.it](https://www.merigar.it/it/comunita-dzogchen/)
- "Nearby, above all in Arcidosso, many people interested in Dzogchen Buddhism moved in" → informal, cross-community. [Viverealtrimenti](https://viverealtrimenti.com/merigar/)
- Summer retreat brings people "from every part of the world"; later reports say 1,000+ people from Europe. [Il Giunco, 2017](https://www.ilgiunco.net/2017/06/23/ritiro-alla-comunita-dzogchen-di-merigar-e-lincontro-piu-importante-dellanno)
- Spiritual orientation: Tibetan Buddhist (Dzogchen), plus yoga, dance, meditation. Fits our "Buddhist-inspired" path. [Visit Tuscany](https://www.visittuscany.com/en/attractions/the-merigar-west-tibetan-centre/)
- ⚠️ It is a teacher lineage. The founder, Namkhai Norbu, died in 2018. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Namkhai_Norbu) *No abuse scandal about him found in one search. People live in their own homes in normal villages, so it is not a closed community. Still, ask locals how strong the "inner circle" feels. Guess.*

## Water (honest view)
- Rain: ~937 mm/year, more in winter. [climate-data.org](https://en.climate-data.org/europe/italy/tuscany/arcidosso-110216/)
- Monte Amiata is a big volcanic **aquifer** that supplies about **700,000 users** in Tuscany. [ospiteingrato.unisi.it](https://www.ospiteingrato.unisi.it/problema-acqua-studi-e-relazioni-sullacquifero-del-m-amiata/) → good sign.
- ⚠️ **Geothermal power plants** (Enel) on the mountain. A 2006 study found a link between the drinking aquifer and the geothermal field; the water table is ~300 m lower than before. Locals complain about rising **arsenic** in water. [ospiteingrato.unisi.it](https://www.ospiteingrato.unisi.it/problema-acqua-studi-e-relazioni-sullacquifero-del-m-amiata/), [Orvietonews, 2017](http://www.orvietonews.it/ambiente/2017/07/16/amiata-acqua-e-arsenico-sos-geotermia-ci-fanno-o-ci-sono-56105.html), [ARPAT monitoring 2024](https://www.arpat.toscana.it/pubblicazione/qualita-delle-acque-superficiali-sotterranee-e-sorgenti-area-geotermica-monte-amiata-anno-2024/)
- No 2024–2026 drought emergency for Arcidosso found (only one search). *Guess: water quantity OK, quality needs a test.*
- **Rule for us:** land with own spring; test for arsenic.
