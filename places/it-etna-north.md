## In short
Volcano, vineyards, forests and the sea below. Foreign and Italian newcomers come mainly for **natural wine**. We found **no clear spiritual / eco-family scene**. Water was very bad in 2023–24, better in 2026.

## People
- Castiglione: ~2,500 people now vs 14,000 in 1911 — strong decline. [La Voce di New York](https://lavocedinewyork.com/en/vetrina-italiana-en/windows-on-sicily-en/2025/09/25/castiglione-di-sicilia-a-jewel-on-the-slopes-of-mount-etna/)
- Foreign winemakers settle here: e.g. a Parisian couple with a micro farm in Randazzo started "Solo Uva", a meeting of natural winemakers from all Europe in Linguaglossa. [5gusti.it](https://www.5gusti.it/solo-uva-prima-edizione-linguaglossa-diventa-centro-di-gravita-per-i-vignaioli-di-tutta-europa-con-lintuizione-di-cole-e-puglisi/)
- Spiritual scene: *not found. Guess: small.*
- Families: a Steiner kindergarten/school "ArchINuè" started in the Etna towns and later **moved to Catania city**. [Italia che cambia, 2023-05](https://www.italiachecambia.org/2023/05/asilo-steineriana-catania-archinue/)

## Water (honest view)
- Linguaglossa normally gets ~**1,500 mm/year**. From July 2023 to June 2024 it got only **390 mm**. Sicily had a national drought emergency from **6 May 2024** for 12 months. [Renewable Matter](https://www.renewablematter.eu/siccita-sicilia-cosa-sta-succedendo), [Regione Sicilia – drought report 2024](https://www.regione.sicilia.it/sites/default/files/2025-01/Report_siccita'_2024_def.pdf)
- 2025–26 winter was exceptional: up to +650% rain vs average around Catania. Sicilian dams rose from 230 to 617 million m³. Region says water is enough for all 2026. [AetnaNet, 2026-09-17](https://www.aetnanet.org/2026/09/17/dighe-siciliane-la-grande-risalita-del-2026-da-230-a-617-milioni-di-m%C2%B3-cosa-e-cambiato-dal-disastro-del-2024-e-cosa-possono-fare-pnrr-e-nuovi-lavori/), [BeSicily, 2026-09](https://www.besicilymag.it/2026/09/societa/crisi-idrica-sicilia-invasi-2026/)
- Etna has its own big groundwater (falda), a plus vs the rest of Sicily. But the network (pipes, wells) is weak. [LiveUniversity Catania, 2026-07-27](https://catania.liveuniversity.it/2026/07/27/sicilia-senzacqua-perche-lisola-rischia-la-sete/)
- **Verdict:** ⚠️ risky. One dry year = big trouble. Only land with own well / spring.
