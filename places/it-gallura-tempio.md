# Alta Gallura (Sardinia) — Tempio Pausania, Luogosanto, Aggius, Luras

- **Status:** lead
- **Country / region:** Italy, Sardinia, Gallura hinterland. Hub: Tempio Pausania (12,878 people, 566 m, 2026). Villages: Luogosanto (<2,000), Aggius, Luras, Calangianus. [en.wikipedia – Tempio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tempio_Pausania), [SardegnaTurismo – Luogosanto](https://www.sardegnaturismo.it/it/esplora/luogosanto)
- **Nearest town / airport:** Olbia airport ~1 h from Luogosanto. [Workaway](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/949393529999). North coast (Santa Teresa, Costa Paradiso) *~30–45 min (guess)*. Monte Limbara (1,359 m *from memory*) above Tempio.
- **Website / contacts:** Off-grid homestead near Luogosanto (Till & Tzegha): [Workaway 949393529999](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/949393529999) · Rete Eco Sarda: [reteecosardi.blogspot.com](http://reteecosardi.blogspot.com/p/ecovillaggi-e-comunita.html)

## In short
Granite hills, cork-oak forests and old *stazzi* (farmhouses) between Tempio and the north coast. A few international families are building off-grid homesteads and want to grow "a local network of international people and families". This is a **pioneer area, not an established hub**. **Main doubts:** water (one main reservoir), fire, tiny scene, Italy's strict van rules.

## People
- Foreign-born in Tempio: 4.9 % (Romania, Argentina, France, Morocco, Germany). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tempio_Pausania). Luras: 5.9 % (incl. Poland, France). [search summary – Luras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luras)
- Luogosanto homestead: clay tiny house, planned seminar centre; "first homestead of a growing local network of international people and families". [Workaway](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/949393529999)
- Other family permaculture hosts and a "non-profit ecological and spiritual centre" exist in Sardinia (location not checked). [Workaway family garden](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/473536432316), [Workaway spiritual centre](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/514439963893)
- ⚠️ The old hippie cave community in Valle della Luna (Aggius/Santa Teresa) was **evicted in Sept 2023**. [Il Post, 2023-09-08](https://www.ilpost.it/2023/09/08/sgombero-hippy-valle-della-luna-sardegna/) → authorities act against informal living.

## Integration
- Strong Gallurese culture: *stazzi*, cork and granite crafts, own dialect. [Hotel Pausania Inn](https://www.hotelpausaniainn.com/en/tempio-pausania-gallura-sardinia/tempio-and-surroundings/), [Gallura Città di Paesi](https://galluracittadipaesi.it/en/municipalities/luras/)
- *Locals are known as reserved but hospitable (guess).*

## How we could join
- Volunteer at the Luogosanto homestead: "a lot of space for vans and tents", own van preferred. Spring water collected weekly. [Workaway](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/949393529999)
- Rent a stazzo; later buy. *Stazzi with land are common in the hinterland (guess).*
- Contribute: Nesli = cooking, yoga for a seminar centre; Ondrej = building community, tech, off-grid systems.

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ✅ legal (Italy, yearly notice + exam). ⚠️ Off-grid + home school = social-services risk (Palmoli case, see [italy.md](../regions/italy.md)). No alternative school found in Gallura (only public). [Comprensivo Tempio](https://www.comprensivotempio.it/index.php/l-istituto/la-scuola). A Steiner parental school "Polaris" appeared in search; location not confirmed. [polarisaps.org](https://polarisaps.org/)
- Van / yurt on land: ❌ strict in Italy (camper used as a home = new building).
- Wildfire: ⚠️⚠️ high. July 2026: 10 ha at Luras (Monte Biancu), 20 ha pine forest at Aglientu with evacuations. [EspansioneTv, 2026-07-01](https://www.espansionetv.it/2026/07/01/incendi-in-sardegna-10-ettari-andati-in-fumo-in-gallura/), [Unione Sarda](https://www.unionesarda.it/en/sardinia/a-fire-in-aglientu-engulfs-20-hectares-of-pine-forest-the-blaze-engulfs-homes-and-beaches-and-swimmers-are-evacuated-u59o1jvo)
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ✅ hinterland quiet; ⚠️ Costa Smeralda mass tourism on the coast.

## Water (honest view)
- Tempio: 882 mm/yr (Jan 96 mm, Jul 10 mm). [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tempio_Pausania)
- Gallura depends on **one dam (Liscia)**; it ended 2024 with 12 M m³ less than 2023. "Gallura is not safe." [La Nuova Sardegna, 2025-08-26](https://www.lanuovasardegna.it/olbia/cronaca/2025/08/26/news/siccita-la-gallura-non-e-al-sicuro-servono-impianti-e-nuove-dighe-1.100752878)
- Rationing hit east Sardinia (Bassa Gallura, Baronia, Ogliastra) in 2024–25. [Sardinia Post](https://www.sardiniapost.it/ambiente/siccita-la-crisi-delle-campagne-nella-sardegna-orientale-colture-a-secco-e-animali-senzacqua/)
- ✅ 28 Feb 2026: Sardinian reservoirs at 94.7 %, drought indicator "normal". [Autorità di Bacino](https://autoritadibacino.regione.sardegna.it/invasi/)
- Granite hills have many small springs *(guess)*. *Verdict: medium risk; good after wet 2025–26, but one dry cycle can bring rationing.*

## Climate check
- Csa. Jul max 30 °C, Jan 9 °C / 3 °C. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tempio_Pausania)
- ✅ **Pass (partly guess):** Jul 10 mm, Aug 26 mm; May–Sep dry. Altitude keeps summer milder than the coast. Winter cool, rare snow on Limbara *(guess)*.
