# Lunigiana (Tuscany, north) — Villafranca, Bagnone, Filattiera, Pontremoli

- **Status:** lead
- **Country / region:** Italy, Tuscany, Massa-Carrara province. Valley of the Magra river between the Apennines and the Apuan Alps. 14 municipalities incl. Aulla, Bagnone, Filattiera, Fivizzano, Licciana Nardi, Mulazzo, Pontremoli, Villafranca, Zeri. [Viverealtrimenti](https://viverealtrimenti.com/ecovillaggio-in-lunigiana/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lunigiana)
- **Nearest town / airport:** Sea (Sarzana / La Spezia) *~30–50 min (guess)*. Pisa airport *~1 h–1 h 15 (guess)*.
- **Website / contacts:** Lunigiana Sostenibile (coop AlterEco + Legambiente): [lunigianasostenibile.it](https://www.lunigianasostenibile.it/). Tourism: [lunigiana.land](https://www.lunigiana.land/en/)
- **Added:** 2026-09-27 by Claude

**Órgiva pattern:** 3/8 points (+3 partial) — ✅ 1 real market towns, ✅ 5 weekly markets, ✅ 7 mountains + sea close · ⚠️ 2 some newcomers (growing German interest), ⚠️ 3 spread in hamlets, ⚠️ 4 a few eco projects · ❌ 6 no alternative school found, ❌ 8 no clear easy-start network found.

## In short
Green, wet, quiet valley with stone villages and castles. Close to the sea and to real mountains. Cheap for Tuscany. **But** we did not find a strong alternative / spiritual scene. It is more "a good place to build something" than "a place where the scene already is".

## People
- ~55,900 people (2014). Median age 48, very old. Foreign residents grew from 1,067 (2001) to 3,923 (2012), mostly Romanian, Moroccan, Albanian — work migrants, not eco newcomers. [Città della Spezia, 2014-12-20](https://www.cittadellaspezia.com/?p=172683)
- Estate agent: Lunigiana is **"not ideal for those seeking a strong international expat community"**, but interest grew in the last 5–8 years, especially from German-speaking countries. [Italica Homes](https://www.italicahomes.com/post/lunigiana)
- Example project: "Il Tempo del Borgo" — two doctors who left medicine, micro-village of <20 people, permaculture, biodynamic garden. [Ecobnb, 2021](https://ecobnb.com/blog/2021/06/sustainable-holiday-lunigiana-italy/)
- An older "Ecovillaggio in Lunigiana" project (Meredo) — website no longer active. [Viverealtrimenti](https://viverealtrimenti.com/ecovillaggio-in-lunigiana/)
- Spiritual scene: *not found. Guess: small.*

## Integration
- ✅ Locals are mostly old Lunigiana families. Young newcomers are needed (ageing index 265%). [Città della Spezia](https://www.cittadellaspezia.com/?p=172683)
- Traditions: chestnut, testaroli, castles, village fairs. *Guess, typical for the area.*
- Art project 2026: Mulazzo + Fivizzano host a "Scuola Diffusa" artist residency (Aug 2026 – Jan 2027) — councils try to bring young people in. [Il Corriere Apuano, 2026-05](https://www.ilcorriereapuano.it/2026/05/mulazzo-e-fivizzano-una-scuola-diffusa-per-artisti-e-curatori-under-35/)

## How we could join
- *Rent a stone house first (guess: easy and cheap).*
- Van: ❌ hard (Italian rule, see [italy.md](../regions/italy.md)). Agriturismo / campsite hosts are the legal way.
- Contribute: we would be **founders**, not joiners. Good for our own retreat later, weak for "joining a scene" now.

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ✅ legal (istruzione parentale). No alternative / Steiner school found in Lunigiana. Scuola Steiner Lucca exists *~1 h+ (guess)*. [Facebook – Scuola Steiner Lucca](https://www.facebook.com/Scuolasteinerlucca/)
- Van / yurt on land: ❌ needs building permit (see [italy.md](../regions/italy.md)).
- Wildfire risk: *low–medium (guess: wet, green valley; not checked).*
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ✅ low.

## Water (honest view)
- Pontremoli: ~1,260–1,390 mm/year, oceanic climate (Cfb). July is driest (~57 mm). [climate-data.org](https://en.climate-data.org/europe/italy/tuscany/pontremoli-110281/), [Weather Atlas](https://www.weather-atlas.com/en/italy/pontremoli-climate). Other source gives ~930 mm for the wider area. [Grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/Pontremoli)
- ✅ One of the wettest candidates in our Italy search. *No recent drought emergency found (not searched in depth).*
- ⚠️ Wet also means **floods / landslides** (e.g. Aulla flood 2011, *from memory — verify*).

## Costs
- Villafranca in Lunigiana: average **€1,064/m²** (July 2026); houses €1,219/m². +3.8% in 12 months. [RealAdvisor](https://realadvisor.it/it/mercato-immobiliare/comune-villafranca-in-lunigiana-it)
- Listings: dilapidated farmhouse + 2 ha by a stream; Bagnone stone rustico + 4,000 m²; 3.5 ha flat land with house and stable. [search summary: idealista / casa.it / trovacasa](https://www.idealista.it/en/geo/vendita-case/lunigiana/con-rustici/)
- *Guess: rustico to renovate + 1 ha: €60–150k.*

## Weekly market
- Pontremoli: Wednesday. Aulla: Saturday. Villafranca: Friday. Bagnone: Monday. [TuscanDogma](https://www.tuscandogma.it/blog/i-mercati-settimanali-in-tutta-la-toscana-paese-per-paese/)
