# Western Liguria hinterland — Val Nervia, Val Bevera (Torri Superiore), Val Roia

- **Status:** dropped (2026-09-27) — **water**. Summer 2026: dry taps and rationing in Val Nervia (Pigna, Apricale) after 3 years of low rain and snow. Keep **Torri Superiore** only as a place to visit or volunteer.
- **Country / region:** Italy, Liguria, Imperia province, behind Ventimiglia, next to the French border. Valleys: Nervia (Dolceacqua, Apricale, Pigna), Bevera (Torri Superiore), Roia (Airole, Olivetta).
- **Nearest town / airport:** Ventimiglia / sea ~11 km from Torri. [Interreg Europe](https://www.interregeurope.eu/good-practices/eco-village-torri-superiore-ventimiglia-im-italy). Nice airport *~1 h (guess)*.
- **Website / contacts:** Ecovillaggio Torri Superiore: [torri-superiore.org](https://www.torri-superiore.org/en/home/)
- **Added:** 2026-09-27 by Claude

**Órgiva pattern:** 3/8 points (+2 partial) — ✅ 3 people spread in hamlets, ✅ 7 Alps + sea 15 min, ✅ 8 volunteering at Torri · ⚠️ 1 Ventimiglia is a real town, but not a newcomer hub, ⚠️ 4 small "Terre di Confine" network · ❌/❓ 2, 5, 6 not found.

## In short
Beautiful, steep valleys between the Maritime Alps and the sea. **Torri Superiore** is a restored medieval village run as an ecovillage (~25 residents of all ages) with guest house and courses. It built a small local network with farms and social projects. But the wider area has **no big alternative scene**, and **water failed in summer 2026**.

## People
- Torri Superiore: ~25 residents of all ages. [Tripadvisor review](https://www.tripadvisor.com/ShowUserReviews-g187828-d1956283-r330898227-Ecovillage_Torri_Superiore-Ventimiglia_Italian_Riviera_Liguria.html) *(number may be old)*
- "Terre di Confine" network: 6 small firms in social care, organic farming, beekeeping, ecotourism in the Val Bevera – Val Roia bioregion. [torri-superiore.org](https://www.torri-superiore.org/en/ecovillage/the-terre-di-confine-network/)
- Research paper (2026) calls Torri a "living lab for social innovation". [MDPI Sustainability 18(1):188](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/18/1/188)
- Other newcomers in Val Nervia: *not found*. Bussana Vecchia (near Sanremo) is an old 1970s artist/hippie squat village. [Wandering Liguria / search](https://www.wanderingliguria.com/articles/39/dolceacqua-sweet-water-superb-wine-and-monet)

## Integration
- Torri residents do voluntary work with locals: farm road, trails, public water channels, dry-stone walls; they created local jobs. [Interreg Europe](https://www.interregeurope.eu/good-practices/eco-village-torri-superiore-ventimiglia-im-italy)
- Traditions: olive terraces, Rossese wine (Dolceacqua), village festivals. *Guess.*

## How we could join
- Stay / volunteer at Torri Superiore (guest house, courses). [torri-superiore.org](https://www.torri-superiore.org/en/home/)
- Torri is an **intentional community** (cooperative). For us: a place to learn, not to live in.

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ✅ legal (Italy).
- Van / yurt on land: ❌ building permit needed (see [italy.md](../regions/italy.md)).
- Wildfire risk: ⚠️ *high (guess: dry Mediterranean slopes, summer drought)*.
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ⚠️ Riviera coast (Sanremo, Menton) is touristy; valleys are quiet.

## Water (honest view) — the reason for "dropped"
- Aug 2026: **7 comuni** in Imperia province in serious water crisis: **Pigna, Apricale** (both Val Nervia), Prelà, Diano San Pietro, Pieve di Teco, Vasia, Villa Faraldi. Night shut-offs, bans, water brought by tanker. Almost 5 months with no rain. [ANSA, 2026-08-06](https://www.ansa.it/liguria/notizie/2026/08/06/sette-comuni-dellimperiese-in-grave-crisi-idrica_2ed602b8-d090-4ef5-bcfa-36103452b707.html)
- Worst case: Pigna, upper Val Nervia. Cause: very low rain **and snow for the last three years**. [La Voce di Imperia, 2026-08-08](https://www.lavocediimperia.it/2026/08/08/leggi-notizia/argomenti/attualita-5/articolo/emergenza-siccita-razionamenti-dellacqua-nellentroterra-si-moltiplicano-ordinanze-e-divieti-per.html), [La Voce di Imperia, 2026-08-03](https://www.lavocediimperia.it/2026/08/03/leggi-notizia/argomenti/attualita-5/articolo/emergenza-idrica-nellentroterra-di-imperia-rubinetti-a-secco-cresce-la-protesta.html)
- Region plans a €40M "Roja-bis" pipeline and even water from Piedmont (Tanaro). [La Voce di Imperia, 2026-08-28](https://www.lavocediimperia.it/2026/08/28/leggi-notizia/argomenti/imperia-citta/articolo/roja-bis-40-milioni-per-la-sicurezza-idrica-del-ponente-ma-lestate-ha-evidenziato-le-criticita.html), [La Voce di Imperia, 2026-08-28](https://www.lavocediimperia.it/2026/08/28/leggi-notizia/argomenti/attualita-5/articolo/imperia-torna-a-guardare-al-piemonte-lacqua-del-tanaro-per-dissetare-la-riviera.html)
- Val Bevera (Torri) was **not** in the list. *Guess: still the same climate trend.*
