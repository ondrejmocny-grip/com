# Chefchaouen and the Talassemtane Rif (Akchour, Oued Laou)

- **Status:** lead (weak — does not reach 6/8; kept as Morocco's "best water" option)
- **Country / region:** Morocco, Tanger-Tétouan-Al Hoceïma, Chefchaouen Province. Hub: Chefchaouen (46,168 people, 2024; 600 m). Around: Akchour (river gorges, Talassemtane National Park), Oued Laou (Mediterranean, 48 km).
- **Nearest town / airport:** Tétouan *~1 h*; Tangier airport *~2 h, guess*.
- **Website / contacts:**
  - Gîte Talassemtane eco-lodge: [gitetalassemtane.com](https://www.gitetalassemtane.com/)
  - L'Ermitage d'Akchour (permaculture garden): [stayaltered.com](https://stayaltered.com/place/lermitage-dakchour/)
- **Added:** 2026-09-27 by Claude

**Órgiva pattern:** 4/8 points. Meets: 1 (real town), 5 (souk Monday + Thursday), 7 (Rif mountains + Mediterranean ~1 h), 8 (eco-lodges, Workaway). Missing: 2 (few foreign or Moroccan settlers found — mostly tourists), 3, 4 (only a few eco-lodges), 6 (no alternative school found).

## In short
The famous "blue town" in the green Rif mountains. It has the most rain in our Moroccan list, rivers and springs, forests, and sea one hour away. But we found **no settled newcomer scene** and **no alternative school**. Tourism is heavy in town. Fire risk is high.

## People
- Mostly Jebli (northern Arab-Amazigh) farmers and townspeople; many tourists. *Guess.*
- Small eco scene: eco-lodges in Talassemtane park, permaculture garden at Akchour, a local who returned to build a small off-grid farm. [Gîte Talassemtane](https://www.gitetalassemtane.com/), [Workaway](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/988164642618), [Worldpackers](https://www.worldpackers.com/search/africa/morocco/chefchaouen)
- ⚠️ Cannabis (kif) region. Since 2021 the "Beldia" strain may be grown legally for medical use only; recreational use still illegal. Cannabis tourism exists. [Hespress](https://en.hespress.com/108883-legal-cultivation-of-beldia-cannabis-in-morocco-triples-in-size.html), [High Times](https://hightimes.com/news/world/morocco-cannabis-history-legalization/)

## Integration
- Very traditional. Souk days Monday and Thursday. [search summary, chauen.info](https://chauen.info/en/oued-laou-travel-guide/)
- *Northern locals often speak Spanish, not French. Guess (history of Spanish protectorate).*

## How we could join
- Volunteer at eco-lodges (Workaway, Worldpackers).
- Rent in villages around. *Cheap, guess.*

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ⚠️ tolerated for foreign children; no alternative school found.
- Van / yurt: ⚠️ same Morocco rules (van max 6 months/year).
- Wildfire: ❌ **high.** Aug 2025: ~500 ha burned near Chefchaouen, plus fires in Bouhachem (80 ha) and others. ANEF lists "extreme" fire risk for Chefchaouen province. [Hespress](https://en.hespress.com/118360-morocco-contains-wildfire-that-burned-500-hectares-near-chefchaouen.html), [Morocco World News](https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2025/08/249295/wildfire-hits-chefchaouens-bouhachem-forest-teams-mobilize/), [communesmaroc](https://www.communesmaroc.com/en/ville/chefchaouen/articles/view/2025/07/chefchaouen-risque-extreme-d-incendies-a-chefchaouen-taounate-et-taza-l-anef-appelle-a-la-vigilance)
- Mass tourism: ⚠️ yes in town (Instagram "blue city").

## Water (honest view)
- ~1,082 mm rain/year (range 500–1,900 mm in 2000–2020). Most in Oct–Apr. [fr.wikipedia](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chefchaouen), [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chefchaouen)
- 💧 **Verdict: best in our Morocco list.** Springs and rivers. *Dry years still hurt (500 mm), guess.*

## Climate
- ✅ **Pass (weak).** May–Sep dry: 53, 16, 3, 7, 32 mm; 0–3 rain days. Oct 109 mm / 4 days. [fr.wikipedia](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chefchaouen)
- ❌ Heat: July–Aug highs **37–38 °C**. Winters wet, highs 17–18 °C. *Snow on peaks, rare in town, guess.* Hot summers are bad for Nesli.

## Costs
- *Rent and village houses cheap, guess.* Farmland: foreigners cannot buy.
