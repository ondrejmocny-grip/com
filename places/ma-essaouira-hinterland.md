## In short
Essaouira is a windy, mild Atlantic town with an old artistic and "hippie" past. Many foreigners live in villas and farmhouses in the villages to the south (Ghazoua, Sidi Kaouki). There are yoga and surf retreats and some permaculture projects. **Big problems for us:** very low rain and falling, salty groundwater; and no real mountains close.

## People
- Expats: "many French, British and Spanish residents", a growing community in villas south of town (Ghazoua, Ida Ougourd, Herrerta, Sidi Kaouki). [maroc-o-phile](https://maroc-o-phile.com/expat-life-in-essaouira/), [expat.com](https://www.expat.com/en/destination/africa/morocco/essaouira/)
- Mixed view from locals: some say "it's their town too", some say foreigners "want Essaouira to be like Europe". Prices went up. [Morocco World News, 2019](https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2019/08/77646/expats-essaouira-help-hindrance/)
- Spiritual scene: many yoga retreats (often run by visiting European teachers). Example: Douar Noujoum "ressourcement" center. [zenngo](https://www.zenngo.fr/guide-voyage/hebergements/details/10/214-douar-bien-etre-maroc.html), [BookYogaRetreats Sidi Kaouki](https://www.bookyogaretreats.com/all/d/africa-and-the-middle-east/morocco/sidi-kaouki)
- *Grounded? Mixed, guess. Many retirees and holiday-home owners, fewer young families than Órgiva.*

## Integration
- Villages are traditional Amazigh / Arab farming and fishing hamlets. [maroc-o-phile](https://maroc-o-phile.com/expat-life-in-essaouira/)
- Traditions alive: Gnaoua music (festival), argan cooperatives, Had Dra Sunday livestock souk (one of Morocco's biggest). [Wikipedia Had Dra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Had_Dra)
- ⚠️ Morocco is a Muslim country. Kindness and hospitality are strong. But some things we like (ceremonies, open "spiritual" events) should stay low-key. *Guess, check on site.*

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ⚠️ tolerated for foreign children, no law (see [morocco.md](../regions/morocco.md)). Local option: École Célestin Freinet (private, nursery + primary, French). [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/FreinetEssouira/), [madeinmedina](https://essaouira.madeinmedina.com/fr/groupe-scolaire-celestin-freinet-24697.html). The old Freinet/Steiner school "Les 1001 étoiles" moved to Marrakech (now Amana Montessori). [Amana](https://amana-montessori.blog4ever.com/)
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ van with foreign plates max 6 months per year; Essaouira town cracked down on overnight van parking. [whole world or nothing](https://thewholeworldornothing.com/vanlife-morocco/). Yurt on farmland: *needs permit, tolerated on a friend's land, guess.*
- Wildfire: *low–medium (argan scrub, thin forest), guess.* No big recent fire found.
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ⚠️ medium. Surf and kite tourism in Sidi Kaouki; yoga retreat industry.

## Water (honest view)
- Rain only **~300 mm/year**, almost zero June–September. [Wikipedia Essaouira climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essaouira)
- Groundwater of the Essaouira basin is falling and getting salty; Zerrar dam has siltation. [Springer, 2019](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13146-019-00497-0)
- 2024: the provincial water committee warned of a **short-term risk of shortage** for the town; deep aquifer recharges slowly. Town water now also from desalination (Bhibah plant 2023, bigger plant planned). [Le Desk](https://mobile.ledesk.ma/encontinu/essaouira-focus-sur-les-mesures-durgence-pour-faire-face-au-stress-hydrique/), [Médias24, 2023](https://medias24.com/2023/07/27/province-dessaouira-mise-en-service-de-la-station-de-dessalement-deau-a-bhibah/)
- The 2025–26 winter ended Morocco's 7-year drought. But the dry trend stays. [AGBI, 2026-02](https://www.agbi.com/opinion/agriculture/2026/02/moroccos-drought-is-over-but-the-water-crisis-isnt/)
- 💧 **Verdict: weak.** Town tap water OK (desalination), but gardens and animals on rural land depend on wells that are sinking. *Close to our deal-breaker.*

## Climate
- ✅ **Pass (best in Morocco).** Dry months Apr–Oct: 17, 9, 0.5, 0, 1, 4, 33 mm; 0–3 rain days. Highs only 19–23 °C all year, lows 11–18 °C. [Wikipedia (1991–2020)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essaouira)
- Very mild = Nesli's wish. But strong trade wind (*alizé*) most of summer (*known, guess on strength*).

## Costs
- Titled building land in Ghazoua is **expensive**: ~1,200–2,800 MAD/m² (≈ €110–260/m²) in villa zones. [Avito](https://www.avito.ma/sp/immobilier/terrains-a-ghazoua-essaouira), [Mitula](https://immo.mitula.ma/immo/terrain-ghazoua-essaouira)
- Farmland: foreigners **cannot buy** (see [morocco.md](../regions/morocco.md)). Rent or lease only.
- 1 € ≈ 10.9 MAD (Sept 2026). [Wise](https://wise.com/us/currency-converter/eur-to-mad-rate/history)
