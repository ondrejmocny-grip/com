# Ourika valley and Tahannaout (High Atlas foothills near Marrakech)

- **Status:** lead
- **Country / region:** Morocco, Marrakech-Safi, Al Haouz Province. Market towns: **Tnine Ourika** (Monday souk) and **Tahannaout** (*~900 m, guess*). Valley goes up to Setti Fatma (~65 km from Marrakech). Nearby valleys: Asni / Imlil / Ouirgane (Toubkal).
- **Nearest town / airport:** Marrakech ~30–60 min; Marrakech airport (direct flights to Prague in winter, Istanbul all year).
- **Website / contacts:**
  - Madrasa Dunya World School (worldschool hub, Ourika): [md-worldschoolmorocco.com](https://www.md-worldschoolmorocco.com/), info@md-worldschoolmorocco.com, +212 660 243 882
  - Marrakesh Organics (permaculture farm + training, 25 km south of Marrakech): [marrakeshorganics.com](https://marrakeshorganics.com/)
  - Oulad Chmicha permaculture farm & eco-lodge: [farmouladchmicha.com](https://www.farmouladchmicha.com/)

## In short
The closest thing in Morocco to Órgiva's *geography*: a snow-fed valley below big mountains, one hour from a big city. Amazigh villages, a weekly souk, and a handful of real permaculture and education projects, including a worldschooling hub for families. **Main doubts:** sea is far, 2023 earthquake zone, deadly flash floods (1995), hot lower valley, and the luxury "Marrakech villa / retreat" scene.

## People
- Valley is Amazigh (Shilha-speaking) and keeps its traditional mountain life, despite being 30 km from Marrakech. [Wikipedia Ourika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ourika_Valley)
- Projects:
  - **Marrakesh Organics**: 8 ha organic olive farm, founded 2014 by Kenza Isnasni ("first to practise permaculture in Morocco"). PDC courses, natural building, yoga, meditation, Thai massage. [About](https://marrakeshorganics.com/about/), [Courses](https://marrakeshorganics.com/courses/)
  - **Oulad Chmicha**: 20 ha permaculture farm with clay-brick rooms. [site](https://www.farmouladchmicha.com/)
  - **Madrasa Dunya**: worldschool hub in an Amazigh village; Waldorf + Montessori + democratic education; ages 1–18; camping area for van families; long-term homeschool program ~$600/child/year; short stays ~$160–260/child/week. [Worldschooly (unverified listing)](https://worldschooly.com/hub/madrasa-dunya-worldschool/), [site](https://www.md-worldschoolmorocco.com/)
  - Workaway: permaculture + eco-construction south of Marrakech. [Workaway](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/336875696231)
- ⚠️ Also luxury: "The Ranch" resort-farm in Tahannaout, villas on the Route de l'Ourika. [The Ranch](https://theranch.ma/en/home/), [Celestia](https://celestiainvest.com/en/news/route-de-lourika-trouver-votre-villa-de-montagne). *Show-off risk in the Marrakech crowd, guess.*

## Integration
- Strong local life: Monday souk at Tnine Ourika ("tnine" = Monday). [search summary / Persée on Ourika floods](https://www.persee.fr/doc/horma_0984-2616_2002_num_46_1_2050)
- Traditions alive: Amazigh villages, terraces, irrigation channels, Setti Fatma moussem (*guess*).
- Tourism: Ourika is a big day-trip from Marrakech (Setti Fatma waterfalls). Busy weekends. [morocco-escape](https://www.morocco-escape.com/guides/ourika-valley-day-trip/)

## How we could join
- Volunteer / course at Marrakesh Organics or Workaway hosts. Van families welcome at Madrasa Dunya (camping area).
- Rent a house in a douar; lease land. *Guess: common, cheap.*
- Big plots (5,000–20,000 m²) offered for eco-lodge projects near Setti Fatma. [ourika-valley.com](https://ourika-valley.com/things-to-do-in-ourika/) *(broker text, check)*
- Nesli: cooking, yoga, animals; Ondrej: education, tech — both match Madrasa Dunya type work.

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ⚠️ tolerated for foreign children (no law), and there is a worldschool hub here. See [morocco.md](../regions/morocco.md).
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ van max 6 months/year (foreign plates). Yurt on land: *needs permit, tolerated on a host's land, guess.*
- Wildfire: *low (few forests in lower valley), guess.*
- ⚠️ **Earthquake:** Sept 2023 Al Haouz earthquake (M6.8–6.9). 76 people from Ourika died; some villages deep in the valley were levelled. Asni / Imlil / Ouirgane side worse. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Al_Haouz_earthquake), [Al Jazeera, 2024-03](https://www.aljazeera.com/features/2024/3/8/six-months-after-the-earthquake-moroccos-atlas-villagers-still-in-tents)
- ⚠️ **Flash floods:** 17 Aug 1995 flood killed 200–730 people (sources differ). [Persée](https://www.persee.fr/doc/horma_0984-2616_2002_num_46_1_2050), [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/293275254_The_Ourika_floods_High_Atlas_Morocco_extreme_events_in_semi-arid_mountain_context). Never build near the riverbed.
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ⚠️ medium (day-trippers, Marrakech villas).
- Safety: *in Dec 2018 two Scandinavian hikers were murdered near Imlil by IS-inspired men (known event, verify). Rare; area is generally safe.*

## Water (honest view)
- River fed by High Atlas snow and rain. Lower Marrakech plain gets only ~240 mm/year. [Wikipedia Marrakesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marrakesh). *Valley itself wetter, guess ~400–600 mm.*
- Drought 2019–2024 hit the whole country; 2025–26 winter was very wet and ended the drought (dams 70% in Feb 2026). [Atalayar, 2026-02](https://www.atalayar.com/en/articulo/society/torrential-rains-change-the-course-of-moroccos-water-trajectory/20260218125133223475.html), [maroc.ma](https://maroc.ma/en/news/morocco-emerges-seven-year-drought-thanks-recent-rainfall-minister)
- 💧 **Verdict: medium.** Better than the coast (river + snow + old channels), but a dry cycle will come back. Rule: only land with **irrigation rights on a channel (seguia)** or a spring. *Guess.*

## Climate
- ✅ **Pass for dryness** (May–Oct dry in Marrakech: 11, 3, 2, 5, 15, 19 mm). [Wikipedia Marrakesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marrakesh)
- ⚠️ Heat: Marrakech plain July highs ~38 °C. Ourika valley ~31 °C by day in July; higher villages cooler. [weather-and-climate Ourika](https://weather-and-climate.com/ourika-marrakech-tensift-haouz-ma-July-averages), [climate-data Tahannaout](https://en.climate-data.org/africa/morocco/tahannaout-%D8%AA%D8%AD%D9%86%D8%A7%D9%88%D8%AA/tahannaout-%D8%AA%D8%AD%D9%86%D8%A7%D9%88%D8%AA-54392/)
- Winters: cold nights, snow on the peaks. *Upper valley may get snow some days, guess.* Best home season for us: spring + autumn; *summer may be too hot for Nesli in the lower valley.*

## Costs
- Farmland: foreigners cannot buy (see [morocco.md](../regions/morocco.md)). Lease or join.
- Guesthouses 300–700 MAD/night. [malika-in-morocco](https://www.malika-in-morocco.com/post/10-best-places-to-stay-in-ourika-valley). *Long-term rent in a douar: guess €200–500/month.*
