- **Status:** lead
- **Country / region:** Morocco, Souss-Massa, Agadir Ida-Outanane prefecture. Market town: **Aourir** ("Banana Village", Thursday souk). Villages: Tamraght (2 km south of Taghazout), Taghazout. Inland: Paradise Valley (Tamraght river gorge), Imouzzer Ida Outanane (Atlas foothills, *~1,200 m, guess*).
- **Nearest town / airport:** Agadir city 20–40 min; Agadir airport *~45–60 min, guess*. [agadir-properties](https://www.agadir-properties.com/en/blog/where-buy-agadir-aourir-tamraght-taghazout)

## In short
A 10 km coast of former fishing villages north of Agadir. It became Morocco's surf and yoga hub. Aourir is the local market town; Tamraght is quieter and more residential; Taghazout is the busy surf village. Mountains start right behind the coast. **Main doubts:** surf/retreat tourism and big resorts (show-off risk), water depends on desalination, few alternative schools.

## People
- Mix of "surfers, yoga practitioners, and folks who wanted somewhere more real". [DeepInMorocco](https://deepinmorocco.com/places/tamraght/)
- Buyers: retirees, Franco-Moroccan families, digital nomads, surf expats. [agadir-properties](https://www.agadir-properties.com/en/blog/where-buy-agadir-aourir-tamraght-taghazout)
- Yoga: Kalananda (Ashtanga, Yin, Qi Gong, sound healing, feminine retreats) in a permaculture garden; almost every surf camp has rooftop yoga. [Sally Kirby](https://www.sallykirby.com/blog/drop-in-yoga-classes-in-taghazout-bay), [Taghazout Guide](https://www.thetaghazoutguide.com/activities/yoga-retreats/)
- ⚠️ *Much of the scene is commercial surf-and-yoga tourism, short stays, young travellers. Guess: fewer long-term, grounded families than Órgiva.*

## Integration
- Aourir = "most local of the three villages", genuine Moroccan market town, Thursday souk. [Taghazout Guide](https://www.thetaghazoutguide.com/practical/tamraght-aourir/)
- Amazigh (Tashelhit) culture; banana gardens, argan, fishing. *Guess.*
- ⚠️ Big change: Taghazout Bay resort (Fairmont 2021, Hilton and RIU 2022). Visitors +25% in 3 years. Locals are split: gentrification fears vs. jobs. [National Geographic](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/travel/article/travel-guide-taghazout-morocco), [Changemaker Media](https://changemaker.media/rethinking-tourism-in-taghazout/)

## How we could join
- Many surf camps, hostels and yoga houses take volunteers (Workaway). *Guess, not checked one by one.*
- Rent: Tamraght guesthouses 200–350 MAD/night short-term; *monthly flats ~€300–600, guess.* [Taghazout Guide](https://www.thetaghazoutguide.com/practical/tamraght-aourir/)
- Nesli: yoga, pilates, cooking = high demand in retreat houses. Ondrej: coworking scene (Coworksurf). [Coworksurf](https://www.coworksurf.com/blog/a-digital-nomad-guide-to-taghazout)
- Later: lease a plot in the hills behind (Paradise Valley road, Imouzzer) for a small retreat. *Guess.*

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ⚠️ tolerated for foreign children (no law). Nearest alternative school: Montessori school in Agadir (Cité Suisse), expensive (~£8,300/year for 2 kids, older report). [expat.com forum](https://www.expat.com/en/forum/africa/morocco/agadir/134976-helpprimary-english-schools-and-kids-actvities-in-agadir.html), [Expat Exchange](https://www.expatexchange.com/advice/62/22586/81/Morocco/Review-of-Montessori-School--in-Agadir). Families come here for worldschooling. [Boston Tribe](https://bostontribetravels.com/taghazout-or-tamraght-travel-guide/)
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ van max 6 months/year (foreign plates). Wild camping "widely tolerated", but vans got too many on this coast (Imsouane crackdown). [whole world or nothing](https://thewholeworldornothing.com/vanlife-morocco/). Yurt on land: *needs permit; tolerated inland, guess.*
- Wildfire: *low on coast (argan scrub), medium in hills, guess.*
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ❌ **strong.** Resorts + surf tourism.
- Earthquake: Agadir was destroyed in 1960 (*known fact, verify*). Build safely.

## Water (honest view)
- Rain ~230 mm/year, almost none May–Sept. [Wikipedia Agadir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agadir)
- In the drought Agadir had a real water crisis. Now the **Chtouka desalination plant** (2022, 275,000 m³/day, goal 400,000) supplies ~2 million people and farms. [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/world/morocco-desalination-ocean-africa-spc), [Aquatech](https://www.aquatechtrade.com/water-stories/desalination/water-security-morocco-national-water-plan-agadir-plant-upgrade)
- Paradise Valley pools and Imouzzer waterfalls were **mostly dry for years**, and filled again after the Dec 2025–Feb 2026 rains. [SoussStays](https://sousstays.com/en/journal/paradise-valley-agadir/), [ExcursionMania](https://www.excursionmania.com/ttd/1105/paradise-valley-blg-1105)
- 💧 **Verdict: medium.** Tap water secure (sea water). Land water for gardens is weak unless the plot has a good well. *Guess.*

## Climate
- ✅ **Pass.** Apr–Oct dry: 14, 6, 1, 0, 3, 4, 21 mm; 0–2 rain days. Highs 23–27 °C. Winter lows ~8–10 °C. [Wikipedia Agadir (1991–2020)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agadir)
- ⚠️ Chergui (Sahara wind) can push 2–5 days above 40 °C. Record 50.4 °C (Aug 2023). [Wikipedia Agadir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agadir)

## Costs
- Land is **expensive** (surf boom). Tamraght village 100 m² ≈ 220,000 MAD (~€20k); a 4,650 m² hill plot at 8,000 MAD/m². [Avito Tamraght](https://www.avito.ma/sp/immobilier/terrains-a-tamraght-agadir)
- Farmland: foreigners cannot buy (see [morocco.md](../regions/morocco.md)).
- Food cheap in Aourir: tagine 25–40 MAD. [Taghazout Guide](https://www.thetaghazoutguide.com/practical/tamraght-aourir/)
