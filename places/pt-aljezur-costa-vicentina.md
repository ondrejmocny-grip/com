# Aljezur / Costa Vicentina (Rogil, Odeceixe, Bordeira)

- **Status:** lead
- **Country / region:** Portugal, west Algarve (Faro district), Aljezur municipality, inside the SW Alentejo & Costa Vicentina Natural Park
- **Nearest town / airport:** Aljezur. Lagos ~30 min. Faro airport ~1 h 15 *(guess)*. Monchique ~30–40 min.
- **Website / contacts:** various projects (see People). Local info: https://aljezur.life
- **Added:** 2026-09-27 by Claude (web research)

## In short
Small town on the wild west coast. Surf, cliffs, natural park. A well-known cluster of alternative, spiritual and permaculture people, with families and an international school. Saturday market where "the alternative community meets local farmers". Good "hub" to live 20–40 min from (e.g. in the hills toward Monchique, or Marmelete).

## People
- "Very cosmopolitan with many spiritual communities" plus surfers, digital nomads, local farmers. [pureportugal](https://pureportugal.co.uk/blogs/communities-in-portugal/), [Portugalist](https://www.portugalist.com/place/algarve/aljezur/)
- Projects (checked 2026-09-27):
  - **Shambala** (Aljezur) — healing community; meditation, NVC, sociocracy; open to residents, visitors, volunteers. [pureportugal](https://pureportugal.co.uk/blogs/communities-in-portugal/)
  - Off-grid community on 27 ha between Aljezur and Odeceixe (since April 2023). [Workaway](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/699249652949)
  - Off-grid "oasis" farm, Aljezur. [Workaway](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/423641437586)
  - Permaculture farm + surf, Aljezur (18 years of permaculture). [Workaway](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/775881927684)
  - **KidsTemple at Essência Nature Retreat** (between Lagos and Aljezur) — nature learning for kids. [alternativeschoolsinportugal](https://www.alternativeschoolsinportugal.com/)
- Spiritual: meditation, NVC, yoga, surf culture. *Some "show-off" / lifestyle-retreat risk near the beaches (guess).*

## Integration
- Real Portuguese town with a Saturday market (7:00–12:00) where local farmers sell. [aljezur.life](https://aljezur.life/perfect-saturday/), [Algarve Housing](https://algarvehousing.net/highlights/markets/)
- Sweet-potato (batata-doce) culture and fair. *(general knowledge, not checked)*

## How we could join
- Volunteer at Shambala or the off-grid farms, then lease nearby.
- Van: **the whole coast is Natural Park + Natura 2000 + coastal plan (POOC)** → van parking outside official places is forbidden, fine €60–300. Enforcement on this coast is known to be strict *(guess)*. On private land: council licence. [Lei 66/2021](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/lei/66-2021-170083315), [idealista news](https://www.idealista.pt/news/ferias/turismo/2021/08/26/48647-autocaravanas-com-nova-lei-em-vigor-o-que-muda)
- Building in the park is very restricted → better to join an existing licensed project. *(guess)*

## Water (honest)
- Aljezur shares the Mira / **Santa Clara dam** irrigation with Odemira; both councils fought for "fair" water in 2023 when it was at record low. [Sul Informação](https://www.sulinformacao.pt/en/2023/04/distribuicao-da-agua-da-barragem-de-santa-clara-deve-ser-justa-dizem-odemira-e-aljezur/)
- Algarve public supply: dams 68–95% in July 2026; only −5% restriction since March 2025 (2024 had −15% urban, −25% farms). [Wego, Jul 2026](https://blog.wego.com/portugal-drought/), [Portugal Resident](https://www.portugalresident.com/algarve-water-cuts-of-25-for-agriculture-and-15-for-urban-sector-officially-announced/)
- *Aljezur's public tap water comes from the Algarve regional system (guess, not checked).*
- **Verdict:** OK in 2026, but in the structural drought zone. Private wells can fail; coastal wells can turn salty *(guess)*.

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ✅ (Portugal). International school in Aljezur since 2010. Waldorf Infância Viva (Barão de São João, ~25 min), Escola Waldorf a Oliveira (Vila do Bispo). [propertyguides](https://www.propertyguides.com/portugal/news/could-you-move-to-aljezur-the-algarves-unspoilt-town/), [alternativeschoolsinportugal](https://www.alternativeschoolsinportugal.com/)
- Van / yurt: strict (natural park).
- Wildfire risk: ⚠️ high. Aug 2023 Odemira fire reached Aljezur; Sept 2025 fire in Aljezur spread to Lagos (Barão de São João forest). [Portugal Resident](https://www.portugalresident.com/aljezur-wildfire-gallops-into-municipality-of-lagos/)
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ⚠️ yes, strong international / surf scene; busy summers.

## Costs
- Coastal land is expensive and building rights are rare. *Not checked; guess €30–80k/ha for rustic land, much more with a house.*
