# Aljezur / Costa Vicentina (Rogil, Odeceixe, Bordeira)

- **Status:** ⭐ interesting (Ondrej: liked the first look, 2026-09-27) · lead. Open points checked 2026-09-27 (deep check, see below).
- **Country / region:** Portugal, west Algarve (Faro district), Aljezur municipality, inside the SW Alentejo & Costa Vicentina Natural Park
- **Nearest town / airport:** Aljezur. Lagos ~30 min. Faro airport ~1 h 15 *(guess)*. Monchique ~30–40 min.
- **Website / contacts:** various projects (see People). Local info: https://aljezur.life
- **Added:** 2026-09-27 by Claude (web research)

## In short
Small town on the wild west coast. Surf, cliffs, natural park. A well-known cluster of alternative, spiritual and permaculture people, with families and an international school. Saturday market where "the alternative community meets local farmers". Good "hub" to live 20–40 min from (e.g. in the hills toward Monchique, or Marmelete).

**Deep check (2026-09-27), short version:**
- 💧 Water: public supply OK now (dams 70–85% in Aug 2026), but it **nearly failed in 2023–24**. Structural risk stays. → 🟡
- ☀️ Climate: **clear pass** of the "6 dry months" rule (Apr–Sep). Mild. Windy. → ✅
- 👪 Family / conscious scene: real and big, but mixed with a surf / lifestyle scene. Waldorf is 25–40 min away. → ✅ / 🟡
- 🏡 Land: park rules are **very strict** (5 ha minimum to build, vans banned). Best path = rent or join, or buy **outside** the park (Marmelete / Monchique side).

## How we could join
- Volunteer at Shambala or the off-grid farms, then lease nearby.
- Van: **the whole coast is Natural Park + Natura 2000 + coastal plan (POOC)** → van parking outside official places is forbidden, fine €60–300. Enforcement on this coast is known to be strict *(guess)*. On private land: council licence. [Lei 66/2021](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/lei/66-2021-170083315), [idealista news](https://www.idealista.pt/news/ferias/turismo/2021/08/26/48647-autocaravanas-com-nova-lei-em-vigor-o-que-muda)
- Building in the park is very restricted → better to join an existing licensed project. See **Land & legal** below.

### Drought 2022–2024 in Aljezur (what really happened)

- Feb 2022: public boreholes in Lagos, Vila do Bispo and Aljezur were **switched back on**. Private wells were limited. [Sul Informação 2022-02](https://www.sulinformacao.pt/2022/02/algarve-reativa-furos-publicos-e-limita-captacoes-particulares-para-mitigar-efeitos-da-seca/)
- 2023: Bravura dam fell to **~10%**. For 2 years its water went **only to taps**; farmers got none. Bravura gave just 60 l/s to Lagos, Aljezur and Vila do Bispo. [Público 2026-01](https://www.publico.pt/2026/01/28/azul/noticia/ate-barragem-bravura-afectada-seca-extrema-agua-excesso-2162730), [Sul Informação 2023-04](https://www.sulinformacao.pt/2023/04/regantes-servidos-pela-barragem-da-bravura-procuram-alternativas-para-contornar-a-seca/)
- Apr 2023: Santa Clara at record low. Odemira and Aljezur fought for a "fair" share. [Sul Informação 2023-04](https://www.sulinformacao.pt/en/2023/04/distribuicao-da-agua-da-barragem-de-santa-clara-deve-ser-justa-dizem-odemira-e-aljezur/)
- Nov 2023: Aljezur streams (e.g. **Ribeira de Seixe**) **did not reach the sea**; hundreds of fish died. [Público 2023-11](https://www.publico.pt/2023/11/29/azul/noticia/seca-falta-agua-impede-ribeiras-aljezur-chegar-mar-dizima-peixes-2072001)
- Jan 2024: official cuts −15% urban, −25% farms. Dams ~25%. [Público 2024-01](https://www.publico.pt/2024/01/17/azul/noticia/seca-algarve-governo-anuncia-cortes-25-agricultura-15-sector-urbano-2077174), [Portugal Resident](https://www.portugalresident.com/algarve-water-cuts-of-25-for-agriculture-and-15-for-urban-sector-officially-announced/)
- Dec 2024: Bravura still only **13%**, Odelouca 30%. [Observador 2024-12](https://observador.pt/2024/12/05/seca-barragens-do-algarve-asseguram-abastecimento-para-mais-um-ano/)
- **Taps did not run dry** in Aljezur in this drought *(no report of cuts to homes found — guess)*.

### Verdict vs our "no drought" deal-breaker
- 🟡 **Not a deal-breaker, but a real risk.** Taps held in 2023–24, but only by emergency steps. Farms lost water for 2 years. Streams dried.
- 2026 is a very good water year. It will not stay like this. Expect a drought every ~5–10 years *(guess)*.
- Rule for us: **only live where there is (a) public mains, or (b) a licensed well that ran in Sept 2023/2024, plus (c) a big rain tank.** Ask sellers for the well licence and a flow test.
- Water is **better on the Monchique side** (double rain). See [pt-monchique.md](pt-monchique.md).

- **"6 dry, snow-free months" rule: ✅ PASS (strong).** Apr–Sep all under 50 mm and ≤ 6 rain days. No snow ever. Oct is borderline.
- **Winter:** mild. Highs 15–16 °C, lows 8–9 °C. Gusty Atlantic storms, 9–10 rain days/month Dec–Feb. [surfrental-aljezur 2026-02](https://surfrental-aljezur.com/blog/weather-aljezur-yearly) *Old stone houses feel cold and damp inside (guess).*
- **Wind:** strong. Summer **Nortada** (N/NW wind) most afternoons Jun–Aug. Windiest month Dec. [surfrental-aljezur](https://surfrental-aljezur.com/blog/weather-aljezur-yearly), [WeatherSpark](https://weatherspark.com/y/32189/Average-Weather-in-Aljezur-Portugal-Year-Round)
- **Sea fog:** summer mornings, low cloud on the cliffs, burns off by noon. Less in Aljezur town than at Arrifana. [surfrental-aljezur](https://surfrental-aljezur.com/blog/weather-aljezur-yearly)
- **Summer heat:** coast 24–28 °C highs — **good for Nesli**. *Inland valleys and Monchique side get hotter, 30–35 °C in heatwaves (guess).* Heatwaves reach 40 °C+ in inland Portugal (46.6 °C Mora, June 2025). [Wikipedia 2025 heatwaves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_European_heatwaves)
- Verdict: **one of the best climates on our list** for "no cold, no strong heat". Minus: wind and damp winters.

### Show-off / surf bubble vs grounded
- Coast (Arrifana, Vale da Telha, Monte Clérigo): **surf, Airbnb, digital nomads**. More "lifestyle". [Islands](https://www.islands.com/1938906/carrapateira-sleepy-surf-village-portugal-coast-surf-haven-windswept-cliffs-empty-shores/)

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ✅ (Portugal, needs a bachelor's degree — we have it). International school (secondary) in Aljezur since 2010. Waldorf Infância Viva (Barão de São João, ~25 min), Escola Waldorf a Oliveira (Vila do Bispo). [propertyguides](https://www.propertyguides.com/portugal/news/could-you-move-to-aljezur-the-algarves-unspoilt-town/), [alternativeschoolsinportugal](https://www.alternativeschoolsinportugal.com/)
- Van / yurt: strict (natural park). See Land & legal.
- Wildfire risk: ⚠️ high. Aug 2023 Odemira fire reached Aljezur; Sept 2025 fire in Aljezur spread to Lagos (Barão de São João forest). [Portugal Resident](https://www.portugalresident.com/aljezur-wildfire-gallops-into-municipality-of-lagos/)
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ⚠️ yes, strong international / surf scene (26% foreign); busy summers.

## What to check on a first visit (Nov 2026)
1. **Saturday market in Aljezur** — who is there? Families with small kids? Locals and newcomers talking? Show-off or grounded?
2. **Water on real plots:** ask 2–3 owners: did your well / spring run in Sept 2023 and 2024? Depth? Salty? Licence (APA)?
3. **Story Forest, KidsTemple, Waldorf Infância Viva:** still open? Places? Fees? Ask for the home-schooling parents' WhatsApp group.
4. **Visit Shambala and 1–2 off-grid farms** (Workaway or day visit). Check for guru structure / closed community.
5. **Drive Odeceixe → Maria Vinagre → Rogil → Aljezur → Marmelete** in one day. Feel the hills, wind, greenhouses, fire scars (2023, 2025).
6. **Ask the Câmara / a local architect:** what can we legally do with 1–5 ha (van, yurt, ruin rebuild, TER licence)?
7. **Long-term rent:** how hard is it in winter vs summer? Ask at the market board and estate agents.
8. **Winter feel:** how empty is it in November? Which cafés and shops stay open?
