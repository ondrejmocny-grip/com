# Alto Minho (Arcos de Valdevez – Ponte de Lima – Paredes de Coura)

- **Status:** ❌ fails "6 dry months" climate rule (see [climate check](../regions/climate-check.md)) · lead
- **Country / region:** Portugal, Norte, Viana do Castelo district. Lima valley, edge of Peneda-Gerês National Park (Soajo, Sistelo, Peneda).
- **Nearest town / airport:** Ponte de Lima, Arcos de Valdevez. Porto airport ~1h, Vigo ~1h *(guess)*. Sea at Viana do Castelo ~30–45 min *(guess)*.
- **Website / contacts:**
  - Na Fonte (Távora São Vicente): [nafonte.pt](https://www.nafonte.pt/), Instagram/Facebook @nafonte.pt
  - Worldpackers eco projects in Arcos: [worldpackers.com](https://www.worldpackers.com/pt-BR/search/eco/europe/portugal/arcos_de_valdevez)

## In short
The greenest and wettest part of Portugal. Mountains (Peneda, Soajo) and the Atlantic coast both close. Strong village traditions. There are small foreign permaculture families and projects, but we found **no clear "hub" with many families** yet. Best for land, water and traditions; weaker for community.

## People
- Na Fonte: Dutch couple (both 44) with a small daughter, started Aug 2023 on 2.7 ha with a spring. Aimed at 6–8 adults + kids. Practices: sharing circles, meditation, rituals, tantra/bodywork, vegetarian, unschooling. **Sept 2025: founders moved back to the Netherlands; they now rent out the place (yurt, tiny house) instead of building community.** [nafonte.pt](https://www.nafonte.pt/)
- Other: agroforestry project near Paredes de Coura (1.5 ha food forest); expat NL/AT family hosting volunteers in Arcos. [search summary, 2026-09-27](https://www.nafonte.pt/), [Worldpackers](https://www.worldpackers.com/pt-BR/search/eco/europe/portugal/arcos_de_valdevez)
- 2020 list of northern communities: Nafonte (Arcos), Aldeia de Cabrum (Amakura), Kunayala (Montalegre), Ecoaldeia Vegetariana (Cabeceiras de Basto). [pureportugal.co.uk, 2020](https://pureportugal.co.uk/blogs/communities-in-portugal/) *(may be outdated)*
- Portuguese-led family scene further south-east: **EKOA / 7 Pétalas learning community** in Fornelos, **Fafe** (~1h15 from Arcos, *guess*); Fornelos is an "eco-freguesia". [ekoa.pt](https://www.ekoa.pt/), [Fafe TV, 2023-02-10](https://www.fafetv.com/post/eco-freguesia-de-fornelos-e-comunidade-ekoa-recebem-men%C3%A7%C3%B5es-honrosas)

## Integration
- Arcos de Valdevez is a World Biosphere Reserve; granite mountain villages (Soajo, Sistelo) with community traditions. [CM Arcos de Valdevez](https://www.cmav.pt/pages/1650)
- *Locals: conservative, Catholic, but known as warm. Guess.*

## How we could join
- **Na Fonte is for rent** (yurt, tiny house on wheels, land with spring). Could be a soft start with a van, and a base to scout. Neighbouring land was said to be for sale. [nafonte.pt](https://www.nafonte.pt/)
- Workaway / Worldpackers eco farms as first contacts.
- Na Fonte as an intentional community: **paused** (not dropped — it is now a rental, so it is still useful to us).

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ✅ Portugal. No alternative school found near Arcos/Ponte de Lima (checked directory 2026-09-27). Nearest: EKOA (Fafe), Escola da Ponte (Santo Tirso, public alternative). [alternativeschoolsinportugal.com](https://www.alternativeschoolsinportugal.com/)
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ council license; National Park rules inside Peneda-Gerês.
- Wildfire risk: ⚠️ Medium–high. *The north-west has many small fires each summer (guess, verify).*
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: Low. Some summer tourism in Gerês and Ponte de Lima.

## Costs
- *Guess: land cheaper than coast, more expensive than central interior. Small, split plots (minifúndio) are common. Verify.*
