# Serra da Arrábida / Palmela – Setúbal (Biovilla area)

- **Status:** lead
- **Country / region:** Portugal, Setúbal Peninsula (Lisbon metro area, south of the Tejo). Palmela, Azeitão, Setúbal, Sesimbra.
- **Nearest town / airport:** Palmela / Setúbal. Lisbon airport ~45 min. *(guess)*
- **Website / contacts:** Biovilla: https://biovilla.org, geral@biovilla.org
- **Added:** 2026-09-27 by Claude (web research)

## In short
Limestone mountain range (Arrábida, ~500 m) that drops straight into the sea. 45 min from Lisbon. Wine villages (Azeitão, Palmela). Anchor project: **Biovilla**, a sustainability cooperative inside the natural park. Smaller alternative scene than the Alentejo coast, but more normal Portuguese life and very good access.

## People
- Mostly Portuguese (Lisbon people who left the city) plus some internationals. *(guess)*
- Projects (checked 2026-09-27):
  - **Biovilla** — cooperative since 2010, inside Arrábida Natural Park. Eco-tourism, agroforestry, organic farming, education, consulting. Volunteers all year (apply by email). ~10,000 visitors/year. [biovilla.org](https://biovilla.org/), [Goparity](https://goparity.com/project/biovilla-is-growing-175), [Hosting Transformation](https://hostingtransformation.eu/partner/biovilla/)
  - **SerVivo** (Palmela) — arts / dance / workshops. [pureportugal](https://pureportugal.co.uk/blogs/communities-in-portugal/)
  - **Pachamama** (Alcácer do Sal, ~1 h south) — farm co-living. [pureportugal](https://pureportugal.co.uk/blogs/communities-in-portugal/)
- Spiritual orientation: sustainability and personal development (Biovilla "mandala" includes personal development). *Less spiritual-scene than Odemira (guess).*

## Integration
- Biovilla works with schools and the region. Strong local traditions: Azeitão cheese, Moscatel wine, Palmela wine fair. *(traditions = general knowledge, not checked)*

## How we could join
- Volunteer at Biovilla; learn how a licensed eco-tourism co-op works.
- Van: Arrábida is Natural Park + Natura 2000 → no van parking outside official places. [Lei 66/2021](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/lei/66-2021-170083315)
- Land inside the park: almost no new building. Outside (Palmela plain, Poceirão, Águas de Moura): flat farmland, fewer views. *(guess)*

## Water (honest)
- Public water in Setúbal is 100% groundwater from ~250 m deep, from the **Tejo-Sado aquifer**, the largest in Portugal (8,000+ km²). Municipalities expect no short/medium-term problem. [SMS Setúbal](https://sms-setubal.pt/sms-aguas/abastecimento-de-agua/), [Agroportal](https://www.agroportal.pt/seca-municipios-de-setubal-nao-esperam-problemas-imediatos-no-aquifero-do-tejo-mas-querem-mais-informacao/)
- **But:** a ban on new well licences on the South Tejo bank ran from 2022 to May 2026 because groundwater levels were low; Almada had consumption limits. Government is looking for new sources. [Agroportal](https://www.agroportal.pt/apa-levanta-novas-restricoes-ao-licenciamento-de-aguas-subterraneas-incluindo-na-bacia-do-tejo-sado/), [SAPO](https://sapo.pt/artigo/palmela-pode-mudar-futuro-hidrico-da-margem-sul-com-nova-estrategia-6aad92c37a663ca5d308397a)
- **Verdict:** medium. Town water reliable; a **new private well** on rustic land may be hard to license.

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ✅ (Portugal). Apis – Escola Waldorf de Sesimbra (licensed). Many Lisbon options. [alternativeschoolsinportugal](https://www.alternativeschoolsinportugal.com/)
- Van / yurt: strict in the park.
- Wildfire risk: ⚠️ medium–high (Mediterranean scrub; *no big recent fire found, not checked well*).
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: low bubble; ⚠️ busy Lisbon weekend beaches (Portinho, Sesimbra).

## Costs
- Palmela listings: many large rustic plots (7–19 ha) on the plain; Azeitão 4.3 ha "with 1 house allowed". Prices not clear. [idealista](https://www.idealista.pt/comprar-terrenos/palmela/)
- *Guess: much more expensive than Alentejo; €180k buys a small plot, not a house with land near the hills.*
