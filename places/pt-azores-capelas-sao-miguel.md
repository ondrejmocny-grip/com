## In short
The best family spot in the Azores. Here is **Novas Rotas**, a free public "learning community" school without classes, built by parents. It is 20 minutes from the only city, airport and hospital of the Azores. The north coast is green, with crater mountains (Sete Cidades) in view. **Main doubts:** wet climate (fails our rule), expensive rents on São Miguel, and a small scene.

## People
- Novas Rotas: **60 kids** (19 pre-school, 26 Initiation, 15 Autonomy), 9 teachers (2024/25). In 2022/23: 59 kids from **43 families**. [EBI Capelas school plan](https://ebic.edu.azores.gov.pt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Plano-de-Escola-2023-2026-2.o-Ano-.pdf) (via search summary), [NEB prize page](https://prizes.new-european-bauhaus.europa.eu/node/364184)
- The school came from **civil society**: 10 years of dreaming, a petition of 1,000+ signatures, parents and 40 volunteers built a classroom in 2019. [NEB](https://prizes.new-european-bauhaus.europa.eu/node/364184) → a grounded, active parent group. ✅ This is the kind of people we look for.
- Families known from articles: "A Pequena Grande Casa" (off-grid tiny house on wheels, rainwater, solar, compost toilet) and Quinta do Bom Despacho — kids at Novas Rotas. [pureportugal 2021-12-05](https://pureportugal.co.uk/blogs/exploring-alternatives-in-azores/)
- Parent mix: Azorean and foreign *(guess, from volunteer list: Chile, Spain, Canada, England, Uruguay, Hungary, Israel, Australia)*. [search summary, Açoriano Oriental](https://www.acorianooriental.pt/noticia/projeto-novas-rotas-envolve-a-comunidade-na-construcao-da-escola-em-sao-miguel-302978)
- Spiritual: *low-key. Holistic education, nature, yoga in Ponta Delgada. No big spiritual scene found. Guess.*
- Ponta Delgada has digital nomads and remote workers; weekly yoga, meditation, dance classes. [pureportugalholidays](https://pureportugalholidays.com/blog/ecoliving-experience-for-thriving-nomads-in-the-azores/)

## Integration
- Novas Rotas is **inside the public school system** and supported by Ponta Delgada council and the Capelas parish. [search summary, Açoriano Oriental](https://www.acorianooriental.pt/noticia/projeto-novas-rotas-envolve-a-comunidade-na-construcao-da-escola-em-sao-miguel-302978) → real link with local institutions, not a bubble.
- Foreigners are only ~3% of São Miguel. [Açoriano Oriental 2026](https://www.acorianooriental.pt/noticia/numero-de-imigrantes-a-viver-nos-acores-mais-do-que-duplica-em-cinco-anos-379274) ✅ No expat bubble.
- Traditions: Holy Spirit festivals in every parish (May–July), with free soups for all. [byacores](https://byacores.com/en/festas-do-divino-espirito-santo-nos-acores-tradicao-fe-e-cultura-acoriana-en/)
- ⚠️ Language: São Miguel accent is hard. Need Portuguese for daily life. [portugalist](https://www.portugalist.com/place/the-azores/azores-living/)

## How we could join
- **Volunteer first:** Novas Rotas has a volunteer programme (2019 volunteers came via Workaway). [novasrotaseducacao](https://novasrotaseducacao.wordpress.com/), [NEB](https://prizes.new-european-bauhaus.europa.eu/node/364184) Workaway hosts nearby: family permaculture farm in **Ginetes** (~25 min west, *guess*) [636899876726](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/636899876726), Kat & Andre's 2 ha permaculture farm [727484349495](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/727484349495), co-living in Ginetes, food forest near Ponta Delgada. [Workaway list](https://www.workaway.info/en/hostlist?country=PT&region=Azores) (checked 2026-09-28)
- **Van:** we would need to **ship it** from Lisbon *(cost guess €1,000–2,000)*. Wild stays limited to 48 h and banned in protected areas. *Easier: park on a host's land while volunteering (grey, guess).* [Montepio rules](https://www.montepio.org/ei/pessoal/gestao-diaria/autocaravanas-regras-da-pernoita-e-do-estacionamento/)
- **Rent:** hard. São Miguel average asking rent **€1,200**, +50% in a year (May 2026). 1-bedroom €650–900. [Diário dos Açores](https://diariodosacores.pt/2026/05/29/acores-lideram-subida-dos-precos-das-casas-e-rendas-com-sao-miguel-a-atingir-380-mil-euros-na-compra/), [portugalist](https://www.portugalist.com/place/the-azores/azores-living/) *Tip: winter rents may be easier (guess). Ask in the families FB group.*
- **Buy later:** land near Ponta Delgada / Ribeira Grande is expensive (€100–370/m² for small plots). [casa.sapo Ribeira Grande](https://casa.sapo.pt/comprar-terrenos/mais-baratos/ribeira-grande/) *1 ha here: likely above our budget with a house (guess).* Compromise: live in Capelas while kids are small; land further east.
- **Contribute:** Ondrej = tech, community building, education (volunteer at school). Nesli = cooking, garden, yoga/pilates, animals (dairy island, many vets needed? *guess*).

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ✅ legal (national rule: school enrolment + parent with a **bachelor's degree** + exams). Azores have own student rules (Portaria 78/2023), *no different home-school rule found — verify.* [DGE](https://www.dge.mec.pt/ensino-individual-e-ensino-domestico) **But:** with Novas Rotas we may not need it.
- Novas Rotas enrolment: pre-registration form online. *Places limited (~60). Area-of-residence priority likely → live in Capelas parish. Guess — ask.*
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ needs licence; much land is in protected areas.
- Wildfire risk: ✅ very low *(guess, wet island)*.
- Earthquakes / volcanoes: ⚠️ Sete Cidades and Fogo volcanoes on São Miguel; no current crisis on this island (2026). [Portugal News 2026-03](https://www.theportugalnews.com/news/2026-03-16/seismic-volcanic-warning-issued-in-the-azores/991266)
- Expat bubble: ✅ low. Mass tourism: ⚠️ Sete Cidades crater nearby is a big tourist spot.

## Climate (vs our rule) — ❌ Fail (soft)
- Ponta Delgada (IPMA 1991–2020): dry months only **Jun (44 mm / 6.6 days), Jul (26 / 4.8), Aug (46 / 6.5)**. May 61 mm / 8.7 d, Sep 78 / 9.4 d. Humidity 78–83% all year. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ponta_Delgada)
- Temp: Jan 12.7–17.5 °C, Aug 19.9–26 °C. ✅ perfect for Nesli.
- *North coast (Capelas) is a bit wetter and windier than Ponta Delgada (guess).*
- Mould in winter in old stone houses. Rent through one winter first. [portugalist](https://www.portugalist.com/place/the-azores/azores-living/)
