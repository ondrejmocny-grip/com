# Pico island (south coast) + Horta (Faial)

- **Status:** lead
- **Country / region:** Portugal, Azores, central group ("Triângulo"). Pico towns: **Madalena** (~6,300, main services), **Lajes do Pico** (~2,000, whaling / whale watching), **São Roque do Pico** (~3,200). South-coast villages: São João, Calheta de Nesquim, Piedade, Ribeiras. Faial: **Horta** (hospital, marina), 30 min by ferry from Madalena. See [regions/azores.md](../regions/azores.md).
- **Nearest town / airport:** Pico airport (PIX) and Horta airport (HOR). *Both have some flights to Lisbon (guess, mostly via Ponta Delgada/Terceira).* [portugalist Pico](https://www.portugalist.com/place/the-azores/pico/)

## In short
The island with Portugal's highest mountain rising straight from the sea. Black lava stone villages, UNESCO vineyards, whales. Pico and Faial have the **highest share of foreigners** in the Azores (8.4% and 7.7%). A small group of creatives and remote workers is growing. **Main doubts:** no alternative school, no hospital on Pico, water shortages on Pico, wet climate, and remoteness.

## Integration

- Traditions: wine (UNESCO "Landscape of the Pico Island Vineyard Culture", 2004), whaling heritage, Holy Spirit festivals. [Wikipedia Madalena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madalena,_Azores), [Wikipedia Lajes do Pico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lajes_do_Pico)
- Expat bubble: ✅ low. Tourism: ⚠️ summer (whale watching, Pico climb), *moderate (guess)*.

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ✅ legal (degree rule). ⚠️ **No alternative school**; public schools only. [portugalist Pico](https://www.portugalist.com/place/the-azores/pico/)
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ licence; protected areas.
- Wildfire risk: ✅ low *(guess)*.
- Earthquakes: ⚠️ 1998 Faial–Pico quake (Mw 6.2, 9 dead). São Jorge next door had a crisis in 2022. [Wikipedia 1998](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_Azores_Islands_earthquake), [NASA](https://appliedsciences.nasa.gov/what-we-do/disasters/disasters-activations/sao-jorge-seismic-activity-2022)
- 💧 **Water:** Pico is one of the two islands with the **most water problems** (with Graciosa); storms break the supply. [jornalacores9 2026-07-08](https://jornalacores9.pt/2026/07/08/proposta-sobre-recursos-hidricos-suscita-debate-sobre-falta-de-agua-nos-acores/), [RTP Açores](https://acores.rtp.pt/local/mau-tempo-provoca-avarias-no-abastecimento-de-agua-na-ilha-do-pico/) *Plot needs a big rain cistern (guess).*
- Health: ⚠️ **no hospital on Pico** — Horta (ferry 30 min) or São Miguel. [portugalist](https://www.portugalist.com/place/the-azores/pico/)

## Climate (vs our rule) — ❌ Fail
- Horta (IPMA 1991–2020): only **Jul (42 mm / 5.5 days)** and **Aug (48 / 6.8)** are dry. Jun 59 / 8.4, Sep 88 / 9.7. Humidity 79–81%. Year ~1,090 mm. [Wikipedia Horta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horta,_Azores)
- Madalena ~1,024 mm/year. [Wikipedia Madalena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madalena,_Azores) Pico: 1,000–1,500 mm, humidity 75–85%, "four seasons in a day". [portugalist](https://www.portugalist.com/place/the-azores/pico/)
- Temp 14–22 °C year-round. Mountain slopes: much rain, little sun; summit snow in winter. [climatestotravel](https://www.climatestotravel.com/climate/azores)
- *South coast of Pico (lee side) is sunnier than the north (guess).*
