## In short
The quiet, green, wild end of São Miguel. Deep valleys, waterfalls, hydrangeas, fishing villages. Land is much cheaper than near Ponta Delgada. A German-led group plans an ecovillage called **"Fruit Garden"** on 27 ha by the sea in Povoação. **Main doubts:** wettest part of São Miguel ("beautiful but damp"), far from the school and hospital, and almost no scene yet.

## People
- Mostly Azorean farmers and fishers. Nordeste lost **10% of its people** 2011–2023. [Wikipedia Nordeste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nordeste,_Azores) ⚠️ Shrinking villages (watch our "dying village" deal-breaker, but services exist in the villas).
- Newcomers: *a few foreign families and hosts (Workaway: Faial da Terra garden + building project). Guess: dozens, not hundreds.* [Workaway](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/524428494771)
- **Fruit Garden** group: "nature-based regenerative culture community", mixed ages, permaculture, nature connection, community building, arts. [Cohousing Berlin](https://www.cohousing-berlin.de/schwarzes-brett/bieteoffer/fruit-garden-gruendung-ecovillage-auf-den-azoren-sao-miguel)
- Spiritual: *unknown. Guess: little.*

## Integration
- Very traditional area. Holy Spirit festivals, parish life. [byacores](https://byacores.com/en/festas-do-divino-espirito-santo-nos-acores-tradicao-fe-e-cultura-acoriana-en/)
- *Locals likely welcome young families who bring kids to emptying schools (guess, like inland mainland).*
- ✅ No expat bubble. Tourism: ⚠️ Furnas (hot springs) is busy; Povoação/Nordeste are quiet *(guess)*.

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ✅ legal (degree rule). Novas Rotas ~1 h away → *daily drive too long; home school or local public school + Novas Rotas later (guess).*
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ licence needed; PDM Povoação/Nordeste decide.
- Wildfire risk: ✅ very low *(guess)*.
- Landslides: ⚠️ steep valleys + heavy rain = landslide risk on São Miguel. [EGU 2023 study](https://presentations.copernicus.org/EGU23/EGU23-445_presentation.pdf) Furnas volcano is in Povoação municipality. *(Furnas last erupted 1630, guess.)*
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ✅ low / ⚠️ Furnas only.

## Climate (vs our rule) — ❌ Fail
- No station found. *Wetter than Ponta Delgada (east and north face the wind; valleys hold cloud). Guess: 1,200–2,000 mm on the coast, much more on the heights.* Ponta Delgada already fails (only Jun–Aug dry). [Wikipedia Ponta Delgada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ponta_Delgada)
- "Rural east (Furnas, Nordeste): beautiful but damp and distant from services." [portugalist](https://www.portugalist.com/place/the-azores/azores-living/)
- Mild temperatures (no frost at the coast, *guess*).
