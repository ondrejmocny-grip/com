## In short
A mountain valley in a normal Portuguese parish. Since years, many "eco-immigrants" (foreign and Portuguese) live in and around the villages. They run a learning community for kids, a food co-op, a tool bank, a forest festival, and an umbrella association. **Not** an intentional community: people live in their own houses in normal villages. This is the closest match to "Bayramiç, but in Portugal" we have found so far.

## People
- About **200+ adults and ~70 children** in the eco-immigrant community (source: community blog, *date of count unknown*). [permaculturinginportugal.net](https://www.permaculturinginportugal.net/projects/community/)
- Mix: Portuguese, Dutch, Belgian, British, German, French, Irish, Scandinavian, Eastern European, plus people from South America, USA, Japan and others. English is a lingua franca. [same source](https://www.permaculturinginportugal.net/projects/community/), [Workaway host](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/298817812742)
- Skills in the community: artists, musicians, eco-builders, carpenters, teachers, nutritionists, energy experts, foragers, chefs, holistic health practitioners. [Folha Verde search summary, 2026-09-27](https://folhaverde.pt/)
- Spiritual orientation: *probably mixed and low-key (holistic health, nature). No single path or leader found. Guess.*
- Grounded: very practical. After the 2017 fires they organised housing, clean-up and crowdfunding together. [permaculturinginportugal.net](https://www.permaculturinginportugal.net/projects/community/)

## Integration
- The **Junta de Freguesia** itself applied to be an **Eco-Freguesia** (2018) and got the title in **June 2019** — first in Arganil. This shows real cooperation with local government. [permaculturinginportugal.net](https://www.permaculturinginportugal.net/projects/community/)
- ArBOR (founded 9 July 2020) has Portuguese and foreign founders (3 PT, 2 NL, 1 BE, 1 EN, 1 SC, 1 BR). Board 2025–2026 is mixed too. [ArBOR](https://arborbenfeita.org/en/who-are-we/)
- Restoring an old olive press (lagar) as a meeting place — news in Público, 2018. [Público](https://www.publico.pt/2018/10/01/sociedade/noticia/a-benfeita-esta-a-recuperar-um-antigo-lagar-para-criar-um-espaco-de-encontro-1845772)
- Local traditions: schist villages, old terraces, Mata da Margaraça native forest, Peace Tower. [ArBOR](https://arborbenfeita.org/en/who-are-we/)
- *Local Portuguese population is small and old (guess, typical for the interior).*

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ✅ (Portugal, *ensino doméstico*). Folha Verde exists **because** the village school closed; it supports home-educating families. Groups: kindergarten (3+) and "Big Kids" (6–~14). Site says "spaces open for children of all ages" (checked 2026-09-27). [folhaverde.pt](https://folhaverde.pt/)
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ needs a council license (see [legal overview](../regions/legal-overview.md)). *Tolerance in Arganil unknown.*
- Wildfire risk: ⚠️⚠️ **Very high.**
  - Oct 2017: fires destroyed homes; 22 families lost their homes. [Folha Verde / Público](https://www.publico.pt/2018/10/01/sociedade/noticia/a-benfeita-esta-a-recuperar-um-antigo-lagar-para-criar-um-espaco-de-encontro-1845772)
  - Aug 2025: the Arganil fire (started in Piódão by lightning) burned **64,451 ha**, the **largest in Portugal's records**. It reached Benfeita parish and the upper Mata da Margaraça. [Renascença, 2025-08-25](https://rr.pt/noticia/pais/2025/08/25/fogo-de-arganil-com-a-maior-area-ardida-desde-que-ha-registos/437325/), [Diário de Coimbra, 2025-08-15](https://www.diariocoimbra.pt/2025/08/15/fogo-ja-andou-na-mata-da-margaraca-em-arganil/)
  - *Need to ask: how did the community get through 2025? Any houses lost?*
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ⚠️ Expat share is high (English as lingua franca). No mass tourism.
