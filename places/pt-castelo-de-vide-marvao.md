# Castelo de Vide / Marvão (Serra de São Mamede)

- **Status:** lead (low priority — small scene, no sea)
- **Country / region:** Portugal, Alto Alentejo, Portalegre district. Serra de São Mamede Natural Park, on the Spanish border. Castelo de Vide ~3,400 people (2011). Marvão = walled village on an 870 m rock, ~100 people inside the walls. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castelo_de_Vide), [Traveler's Itch](https://travelersitch.com/alentejo-portugal-travel-guide/)
- **Nearest town / airport:** Portalegre *~20 min (guess)*. Lisbon airport *~2 h 30 (guess)*.
- **Website / contacts:** [Casa Fluxus](https://pureportugal.co.uk/blogs/communities-in-portugal/) (Castelo de Vide); regenerative off-grid project in the Serra ([Workaway](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/822745546255))
- **Added:** 2026-09-27 by Claude (web research)

## In short
Beautiful green granite hills, chestnut woods and springs in the north of the Alentejo. A few eco projects, some foreigners. But no real "hub scene" found, the sea is far, and summers are hot.

**Órgiva pattern:** 4/8 points — 1 town ✅, 3 spread out ✅ *(guess)*, 4 some projects ⚠️, 8 volunteering ✅. Missing: 2 many newcomers ⚠️ *not shown*, 5 market ⚠️ *not found*, 6 alternative school ❌ *not found*, 7 sea ❌.

## People
- Casa Fluxus: small community of "mostly nomads", open to guests and volunteers. [pureportugal](https://pureportugal.co.uk/blogs/communities-in-portugal/)
- Off-grid regenerative farm with permaculture and yurt building. [Workaway](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/822745546255)
- Castelo de Vide school group has a 2025 guide for migrant pupils → some newcomer families. [AECV](https://aecv.edu.gov.pt/wp/emaei-guia-de-acolhimento-2025-inclusao-de-alunos-migrantes/)

## Integration
- Old Portuguese towns with Jewish heritage (Castelo de Vide), medieval Marvão. *Good local life, traditional (guess).*

## How we could join
- Volunteer on the projects above. Van: council licence needed; Natural Park rules. See [legal overview](../regions/legal-overview.md).

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ✅ legal in Portugal. No alternative school found nearby.
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ licence; park zoning.
- Wildfire risk: ⚠️ *medium (guess, not checked)*.
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ✅ low; day tourists in Marvão.

## Water (honest view)
- Rain ~633 mm/yr, July ~5 mm. [climate-data.org Marvão](https://en.climate-data.org/europe/portugal/marvao/marvao-7065/)
- *The Serra has many springs (Castelo de Vide is known for fountains) (guess).* Still a dry-summer region.
