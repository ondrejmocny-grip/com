# Idanha-a-Nova (Beira Baixa)

- **Status:** lead (low priority — heat, cold winters, no sea)
- **Country / region:** Portugal, Centro, Castelo Branco district. Huge, empty municipality (1,416 km², 9,716 people in 2011). Villages: Monsanto, Idanha-a-Velha, Penha Garcia, Medelim, Alcafozes. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idanha-a-Nova)
- **Nearest town / airport:** Castelo Branco *~30 min (guess)*. Lisbon airport *~2 h 45 (guess)*.
- **Website / contacts:** Town "Restart" program (Idanha Live / Idanha Experimenta) — the council helps newcomers one by one. [URBACT](https://urbact.eu/good-practices/restart-integrated-strategy-attracting-residents)
- **Added:** 2026-09-27 by Claude (web research)

## In short
Portugal's first "Bio-Region" (2018) and home of the Boom Festival. The council actively invites new families (free childcare, alternative school project). Real pull for eco and family people. **But:** hot summers (July highs ~34 °C), cold winter nights (~1.5 °C), and the sea is far. Fails Nesli's climate wish and the "sea close" must-have.

**Órgiva pattern:** 5/8 points — 1 town ✅ (small), 2 newcomers ✅ (migration positive since 2019), 3 spread out ✅, 4 projects ✅, 6 schools/families ✅. Missing: 5 weekly market ⚠️ *not found*, 7 sea ❌, 8 easy start ⚠️. **Below our 6/8 rule.**

## People
- Since 2019 more people move in than out (2020: +98). Still the **oldest** municipality in Portugal. [Reconquista](https://www.reconquista.pt/articles/demografia-idanha-a-nova-regista-segundo-ano-de-fluxos-migratorios-positivos), [Marktest](https://www.marktest.com/wap/a/n/id~22ca.aspx)
- Projects: **Spirala** ecovillage (self-development), **Idanha-à-Vida** (~1,600 ha regeneration around Idanha-a-Velha). [pureportugal](https://pureportugal.co.uk/blogs/communities-in-portugal/), [ecovillagetours](https://www.ecovillagetours.com/portugal)
- Boom Festival (since 2002, 40,000 people, 150 countries) shares money with locals via Boom Karuna. [Boom](https://www.boomfestival.org/environment/socio-economic)
- ⚠️ *Boom may bring a party / psytrance crowd, not only grounded families (guess).*

## Integration
- Council strongly pro-newcomer (Idanha +Bebé free childcare 3 months–3 years). [URBACT](https://urbact.eu/good-practices/restart-integrated-strategy-attracting-residents)
- Bio-Region: organic farming push, local products ("Idanha Made In"). [ecoregion.info](http://www.ecoregion.info/bio-regiao-idanha-a-nova-portugal/)
- Traditions: Monsanto and Idanha-a-Velha historic villages; UNESCO City of Music (2015). [Food4Sustainability](https://www.food4sustainability.org/post/why-idanha-a-nova)

## How we could join
- Council "Idanha Experimenta" = try-living program. Volunteer at eco projects.
- Van: needs council licence (Portugal rules). See [legal overview](../regions/legal-overview.md).
- Land: *cheap, large plots (guess, not checked)*.

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ✅ legal in Portugal. Also **Clonlara School** campus (since 2021) and council **VOAR** alternative-learning project. [Clonlara PDF](https://clonlara.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Education-Document-EngVersion_January2023.pdf)
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ licence needed.
- Wildfire risk: ❌ high. Jul 2025: Penamacor–Idanha fire ~3,000–3,467 ha. Jul 2025: another fire, 170+ firefighters. [TVI](https://tvi.iol.pt/noticias/incendio/fogo/autarca-estima-que-arderam-3-000-hectares-em-penamacor-e-idanha-a-nova/20250730/688a142ad34e3f0baea1194e), [Renascença](https://rr.pt/noticia/pais/2025/07/06/fogo-em-idanha-a-nova-dominado-mobilizou-oito-meios-aereos-e-mais-de-170-operacionais/431691/)
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ✅ low, except Boom week.

## Water (honest view)
- Rain ~644 mm/yr, almost none in summer. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idanha-a-Nova)
- Big irrigation dam (Marechal Carmona, 77 hm³). Old canals **lose up to 40% of water**; €95M repair planned. [Vida Rural](https://www.vidarural.pt/producao/perimetro-rega-idanha-a-nova/), [Wikipedia dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marechal_Carmona_Dam)
- 2022: Castelo Branco was one of the districts with the **biggest public water supply problems** in the drought. [Observador 2022](https://observador.pt/2022/09/16/situacao-de-seca-meteorologica-diminuiu-significativamente-ate-15-de-setembro/), [ECO 2022-10](https://eco.sapo.pt/2022/10/17/situacao-de-seca-extrema-no-pais-regista-diminuicao-acentuada-em-setembro/) *(seen in search snippet; check which article)*
- Verdict: ⚠️ not a deal-breaker if land has irrigation rights or a good well, but **dry-land risk**.
