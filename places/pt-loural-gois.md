## In short
A retreat centre and "intentional community" on **30 ha with a small river**, in an old hamlet. Aims for food and energy self-sufficiency. Offers retreats, permaculture, and training in integrative medicine and therapies. Registered tourism business (Loural Village Lda.). Soft opening May 2022, full opening 2024. [sustainableliving.pt](https://www.sustainableliving.pt/projects/loural-ecovillage), [loural-eco-village.com](https://loural-eco-village.com/en/)
