## In short
The sunny, dry side of Madeira. Steep green mountains (up to 1,800 m) fall into the sea. Old **levadas** (water channels) bring water from the wet north. Since 2021 Ponta do Sol has a **Digital Nomad Village** with thousands of remote workers passing through. There are a few permaculture and retreat projects. **But:** it is a nomad / tourist scene more than a settled "neorural" scene, and **prices rose very fast** (houses +20–26% in two years).

**Órgiva pattern:** 5/8 points — ✅ real small towns (1), ✅ spread out on terraces and hamlets (3), ✅ weekly farmers market in Prazeres (5), ✅ mountains + sea (7). Partial: newcomers (2 — many international nomads, few long-term settlers), projects (4 — a few). ❌ alternative schools (6 — one hub, but in the east of the island), ❌ easy start with a van (8).

## People
- Remote workers: over 10,000 digital nomads have lived in Madeira since 2021; the Ponta do Sol village runs coworking, BBQs, sunrise yoga, reiki, tai chi. [Startup Madeira](https://digitalnomads.startupmadeira.eu/), [search summary: portugalproperty.com 2025](https://www.portugalproperty.com/news-blog/madeira-digital-nomad-village-day-in-life-2025)
- Also foreign retirees and buyers (44% of buyers are foreign: UK, US, Brazil). [jarniascyril.com 2026](https://www.jarniascyril.com/international-real-estate/madeira-real-estate-market-2026-analysis-forecasts-investing/)
- Retreats: Yeotown Madeira (Calheta, wellness retreat — upmarket), small yoga retreats in Ponta do Sol. [Global Retreat Co.](https://www.theglobalretreatcompany.com/yeotown-madeira), [BookRetreats](https://bookretreats.com/s/yoga-retreats/ponta-do-sol)
- Grounded? *Mixed. Many nomads stay only 1–3 months. The long-term, family, "build a project" group is small. Guess.*

## Integration
- Madeiran villages are traditional and Catholic: terraces with bananas, vines, sugar cane; village festivals. [visitmadeira – Calheta](https://visitmadeira.com/en/where-to-go/madeira/west-coast/calheta/)
- The Tábua permaculture farm says volunteers get "immersion into Madeiran village life". [Workaway](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/866549972224)
- ⚠️ Nomad bubble: *many nomads mix mainly with each other. Guess.*

## How we could join
- Volunteer: Tábua permaculture farm (7 ha, food forest, natural building, animals). **Accepts families and camper vans.** Note: it calls itself an "intentional community" with consensus circles — only 3 permanent adults, so small and open. [Workaway](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/866549972224) (checked 2026-09-27)
- Van: ⚠️ sleeping in a vehicle outside official places is **illegal** (Portuguese Road Code art. 48, 50-A); only tolerated at some spots; enforcement rising. On a host's private land: *probably OK as a guest, guess.* [hikingmadeira.com](https://hikingmadeira.com/blog/wild-camping-in-madeira-whats-legal-whats-not-and-how-to-avoid-fines/), [madeira.rentals](https://madeira.rentals/blog/wild-camping-madeira/)
- Yurt / building: Portuguese rules (PDM, licence) — see [legal overview](../regions/legal-overview.md). Much of the island is Natural Park.
- Rent: hard. 2–3 bedroom in the south rarely under €1,500/month in 2026. [jarniascyril.com – cost of living](https://www.jarniascyril.com/expatriation/settling-in-madeira-complete-guide-expats/cost-of-living-madeira-expats-budgets-rents-expenses/)
- Contribute: Nesli = yoga, cooking for retreats, animals. Ondrej = remote work fits the nomad infrastructure; community building.

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ✅ legal in Portugal (*ensino doméstico*), see [legal overview](../regions/legal-overview.md). One homeschool / worldschool hub on the island (Campus da Terra, Gaula, ages 5–12, English + Portuguese) — **far east side, ~50–60 min from Calheta** *(time: guess)*. [madeiraislandnews, 2025-08](https://www.madeiraislandnews.com/2025/08/campus-da-terra-new-nature-immersed-homeschooling-hub-opening-spots-for-september.html)
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ see above. Strict.
- Wildfire: ⚠️ **high**. Aug 2024 fire burned **5,116 ha** incl. the highlands of **Ponta do Sol**, Ribeira Brava, São Vicente. No homes lost. [Wikipedia – 2024 Madeira wildfires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Madeira_wildfires)
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ⚠️ yes, strong tourism on the whole island; nomad bubble in Ponta do Sol.

## Water (honest)
- Madeira has >1,000 km of levadas bringing water from the wet north and the mountains. [Wikipedia – Levada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levada_(Madeira))
- Investment: new irrigation reservoir at Lombo do Salão (Calheta) for 716 users (2025); Ponta do Sol repaired an irrigation well. [JM Madeira](https://www.jm-madeira.pt/not%C3%ADcias/regi-o/18207442/calheta-lombo-do-salao-novo-reservatorio-de-agua-de-rega-beneficia-716-regantes-e-673-exploracoes-agricolas.html), [JM Madeira](https://www.jm-madeira.pt/not%C3%ADcias/regi-o/127620/ponta-do-sol-requalifica-poco-de-rega-que-beneficiara-mais-de-uma-centena-de-agricultores.html)
- Water utility ARM says dry years force more pumping from wells; drought contingency on irrigation was lifted recently. [ARM plan 2025–29 (PDF)](https://arm.pt/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/PAO_2025-29.pdf)
- A study found real drought periods in Madeira over 80 years. [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2214581818303677)
- **Verdict:** *good, not perfect. South side is dry in summer; water depends on levada rights (hours of irrigation). Buy / lease only land with levada water rights. Guess.*

## Climate
- Funchal (south coast, IPMA 1991–2020): Jun–Aug almost no rain (17, 4, 4 mm; 1–2 days). May and Sep ~45 mm, 5 days. Mean temp 17 °C (Feb) to 24 °C (Aug). [Wikipedia – Funchal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Funchal)
- **Verdict: ✅ Pass** (May–Sep dry, Apr / Oct in-between). Very mild — good for Nesli. *Ponta do Sol / Calheta are sunnier than Funchal; higher villages cooler and cloudier. Guess.*
- North side (Porto Moniz, São Vicente): much wetter, *borderline. Guess.*

## Costs (2026)
- Calheta houses: ~€4,100–4,500 per m² (+19.6% in one year to Aug 2026). Calheta = most expensive municipality on the island. [idealista report](https://www.idealista.pt/media/relatorios-preco-habitacao/venda/madeira-ilha/calheta-madeira/), [jarniascyril.com](https://www.jarniascyril.com/international-real-estate/madeira-real-estate-market-2026-analysis-forecasts-investing/comparison-real-estate-prices-cities-madeira/)
- Ponta do Sol: only municipality with slight price fall (−1.6%). [jarniascyril.com](https://www.jarniascyril.com/international-real-estate/madeira-real-estate-market-2026-analysis-forecasts-investing/comparison-real-estate-prices-cities-madeira/)
