# Serra de Monchique (Algarve)

- **Status:** lead
- **Country / region:** Portugal, Algarve (Faro district), Monchique municipality. Villages: Monchique, Caldas de Monchique, Alferce, Marmelete.
- **Nearest town / airport:** Monchique (town). Portimão (~30 min, hospital). Faro airport ~1 h. *(times are guesses)*
- **Website / contacts:** Toca do Coelho: https://tocadocoelho.eu. Monchique Homestead: https://monchiquehomestead.com. Several Workaway hosts (see sources).
- **Added:** 2026-09-27 by Claude (web research)

## In short
The only real mountain range of the Algarve (Fóia, 902 m). Green, wetter, cooler than the coast. A Portuguese mountain town with an international group of people restoring old farms. Sea is ~30 min away (Portimão / Aljezur coast). The best mix of **water + mountains + sea** in the south — but a **very high fire risk**.

## People
- Portuguese mountain people plus "a warm, international community of residents working to restore abandoned farms". [Workaway listings](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/987228946268)
- Projects (checked 2026-09-27):
  - **Toca do Coelho** — "open community" of arts and agroforestry; visitors join 2 work days/week and a weekly talking circle. [tocadocoelho.eu](https://tocadocoelho.eu/our-philosophy/)
  - Permaculture goat-cheese farm. [Workaway](https://www.workaway.info/748999847833-en.html)
  - Off-grid eco retreat in Foz do Farelo, 20 min north of Monchique. [Workaway](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/885246413692)
  - Self-sustainable community on an old farm, 30 min walk from Monchique. [Workaway](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/987228946268)
  - New permaculture + holistic well-being project in Monchicão. [Workaway](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/257828425685)
  - Monchique Homestead (permaculture homestead). [site](https://monchiquehomestead.com/)
  - Awakeland — yoga / meditation / tantra retreat, reported at the foothills. [pureportugal](https://pureportugal.co.uk/blogs/communities-in-portugal/)
- Spiritual orientation: yoga, tantra, arts, permaculture. *Less dense than São Luís or Aljezur (guess).*

## Integration
- Monchique is a living Portuguese town: medronho (strawberry-tree brandy), smoked ham / sausage fair, thermal spa since Roman times. [Portugalist](https://www.portugalist.com/place/algarve/monchique/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monchique)
- *Locals are old and few; depopulation of the high villages (guess).*

## How we could join
- Volunteer on one of the farms above, then lease.
- Van: on private land needs council licence. Serra de Monchique is Natura 2000 → no van parking outside official places. [Lei 66/2021](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/lei/66-2021-170083315)
- Land is cheaper than coast; many abandoned terraced farms with water. *(guess)*
- Hub distance: Aljezur hub ~30–40 min; Lagos / Barão de São João (Waldorf) ~40 min *(guess)*.

## Water (honest)
- Best in the Algarve. The mountains catch Atlantic rain: **~900–1,200 mm/year at altitude, about double the coast**. Springs and thermal water. [algarvetips](https://www.algarvetips.com/algarve/geography/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serra_de_Monchique)
- The big Odelouca dam (Algarve's main public supply) is in Monchique municipality. Algarve dams 13 Jul 2026: 68–95%, average ~84% (Jan 2024: ~25%). Restriction now only −5%. [Wego, Jul 2026](https://blog.wego.com/portugal-drought/)
- **Verdict:** best water in the south, but the region still had a serious drought in 2023–24. Choose land with a spring or stream that runs in September.

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ✅ (Portugal). Schools nearby: Waldorf Infância Viva (Barão de São João, Lagos), Escola Waldorf a Oliveira (Vila do Bispo), KidsTemple (Lagos–Aljezur). [alternativeschoolsinportugal](https://www.alternativeschoolsinportugal.com/)
- Van / yurt on land: council licence; Natura 2000.
- Wildfire risk: ⚠️⚠️ **very high**. 2018 fire burned ~27–28,000 ha in 7 days. Also 2003, 2023 (426 ha from Odemira fire), Aug 2025 fires. July 2026: Monchique at "maximum" fire danger. [VOST](https://vost.pt/en/monchique-2018/), [Safe Communities](https://www.safecommunitiesportugal.com/44371-2/), [Portugal Resident](https://www.portugalresident.com/wildfires-break-out-in-portimao-and-monchique/), [Sul Informação 2026-07](https://www.sulinformacao.pt/en/2026/07/seis-concelhos-do-algarve-estao-hoje-em-perigo-maximo-de-incendio/)
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: low–medium. Coast (Portimão) has mass tourism 30 min away.

## Costs
- Idealista examples (2025): 2.9 ha with river view €64,900 (~€22k/ha); 23 ha next to Funcho dam €105,000 (~€4.6k/ha). [idealista](https://www.idealista.pt/en/comprar-terrenos/monchique/monchique/?ordem=precos-asc)
