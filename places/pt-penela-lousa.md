- **Status:** lead
- **Country / region:** Portugal, Centro, Coimbra district. Penela (Espinhal, Rabaçal), with Lousã and Miranda do Corvo nearby. Between the Sicó hills and Serra da Lousã.

## In short
A **hub** area, not a single project. Two alternative learning communities for kids (Waterfall Valley and Cerejeiras) draw international families to the villages around Penela. Lousã, with its schist villages, had a wave of "lifestyle migrants" in the past. Close to Coimbra (hospital, university, train). Good "live 20–40 min from the hub" setting.

## People
- Waterfall Valley: "families from around the world", multicultural. Parents help with teaching, cooking, gardening, money. Portuguese + English, goal = Portuguese fluency. [waterfallvalleyed.org](https://www.waterfallvalleyed.org)
- Espinhal: mostly Portuguese, some foreigners; "an area of schools and alternative lifestyles". [search summary, 2026-09-27](https://www.waterfallvalleyed.org)
- Serra da Lousã: history of foreign "lifestyle migrants / countercultural" settlers in abandoned hamlets (Talasnal, Cerdeira…). [Folk, Knowledge, Place](https://folkknowledgeplace.org/article/143258-entangled-ruralities-remaking-a-portuguese-mountain-hamlet-through-craft-and-translocalism)
- Colectivo Germinal (Lousã): ecological group, mostly Portuguese. [Communities for Future wiki](https://wiki.communitiesforfuture.org/wiki/Community-led_initiatives_in_Portugal), [Redeconvergir](https://redeconvergir.net/iniciativas)
- Waterfall Valley offers yoga in its program. *Spiritual scene otherwise unknown — guess: mixed, family-focused, grounded.*

## Integration
- Waterfall Valley takes part in **village festivals, markets and events**. Village House is inside Espinhal. [waterfallvalleyed.org](https://www.waterfallvalleyed.org)
- Cerejeiras mentions **Portuguese integration**. [alternativeschoolsinportugal.com](https://www.alternativeschoolsinportugal.com/schools/cerejeiras)

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ✅ Portugal. Cerejeiras: children stay enrolled at a school of record under *ensino doméstico*. [alternativeschoolsinportugal.com](https://www.alternativeschoolsinportugal.com/schools/cerejeiras)
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ council license needed. See [legal overview](../regions/legal-overview.md).
- Wildfire risk: ⚠️ High in Serra da Lousã (Oct 2017 Lousã/Vilarinho fire ~53,000 ha). [Renascença](https://rr.pt/noticia/pais/2025/08/25/fogo-de-arganil-com-a-maior-area-ardida-desde-que-ha-registos/437325/). *Sicó limestone hills near Penela: guess medium.*
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: Low–medium. Mostly Portuguese villages. Lousã schist villages get weekend tourists.
