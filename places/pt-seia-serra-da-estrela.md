## In short
Portugal's highest mountains, with old traditional villages (shepherds, cheese). A scattered scene of permaculture projects and off-grid newcomers. Casa de Santa Isabel is a long-running (since 1981) international anthroposophic community with a small Waldorf-style school. Quinta das Moitas wants a **network of neighbouring permaculture projects** — a good fit for "integrated, not closed".

## People
- Casa de Santa Isabel: co-workers and volunteers from Portugal, Spain, Brazil, Chile, Germany, **Czech Republic**, Netherlands. ~10 children in its elementary school (adapted Waldorf). [casasantaisabel.org](https://www.casasantaisabel.org/about-us), [search summary](https://www.freunde-waldorf.de/en/waldorf-worldwide/organisations-worldwide/europe/portugal/sao-romao/)
- Quinta das Moitas: off-grid, holistic management, syntropic farming. Pilot of "Earth Neighbours" — adjoining projects that cooperate. [quintadasmoitas.com](https://quintadasmoitas.com/projects/earth-neighbours-project)
- Other listed projects: Tir Na Si (off-grid, natural building, seeking members), Rainbow Lodge (spiritual retreat). [pureportugal.co.uk, 2020](https://pureportugal.co.uk/blogs/communities-in-portugal/) *(may be outdated)*
- *Spiritual scene: anthroposophy + earth-based. Seems grounded. Guess.*

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ✅ Portugal.
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ council license; Natural Park rules at higher altitudes.
- Wildfire risk: ⚠️⚠️ High. *Aug 2022 Serra da Estrela fire (~25,000 ha, from memory — verify).* Aug 2025 Arganil fire also reached **Seia** and Oliveira do Hospital. [Observador, 2025-08-25](https://observador.pt/2025/08/25/fogo-que-comecou-em-arganil-apresenta-a-maior-area-ardida-de-sempre-em-portugal/)
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: Low bubble. ⚠️ Snow tourism at Torre in winter (localized).
