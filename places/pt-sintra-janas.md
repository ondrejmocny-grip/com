# Sintra hills / Janas – Colares (Lisbon region)

- **Status:** lead
- **Country / region:** Portugal, Lisbon district, Sintra municipality (Janas, Colares, Azóia, near Cabo da Roca)
- **Nearest town / airport:** Sintra. Lisbon airport ~40 min *(guess)*
- **Website / contacts:** Ecoaldeia de Janas (Quinta do Luzio): https://www.facebook.com/ecoaldeiadejanas/. Terra Alta: https://www.terralta.org. Escola da Terra: https://www.escolaterra.com
- **Added:** 2026-09-27 by Claude (web research)

## In short
Green, misty Atlantic hills (Serra de Sintra, ~530 m) right next to the sea. The wettest and greenest option in the south/Lisbon area. Strong family / alternative-education scene (Waldorf school, permaculture school). Very close to Lisbon → expensive and semi-suburban.

## People
- Portuguese and international families; many "alternative families" around the Waldorf school. *(guess)*
- Projects (checked 2026-09-27):
  - **Ecoaldeia de Janas** (Quinta do Luzio, Janas) — ecovillage + rural tourism; guests, volunteers, interns and residents; non-formal education, crafts, natural building, bees. Residents only after an **extensive application**. [NuMundo](https://numundo.org/center/portugal/ecoaldeia-de-janas), [pureportugal](https://pureportugal.co.uk/blogs/communities-in-portugal/)
  - **Terra Alta** — permaculture education (PDCs all year), volunteering in 2026; founded 2008 by Pedro Valdjiu and Rita Seixas. [terralta.org](https://www.terralta.org/about)
  - **Escola da Terra** — Waldorf school and kindergarten (since 2008), 50+ children from many countries, recognised by the Ministry, up to 4th grade. [Rede Convergir](https://redeconvergir.net/iniciativas/escola-da-terra), [alternativeschoolsinportugal](https://www.alternativeschoolsinportugal.com/schools/licensed)
- Spiritual orientation: anthroposophy (Waldorf), permaculture, Sintra's old "mystic mountain" reputation.

## Integration
- Real villages (Colares wine, Janas chapel festival) but inside a very touristy UNESCO area. *(traditions = guess)*
- Sintra is also a Lisbon commuter town → less village feeling.

## How we could join
- Volunteer / course at Terra Alta or Janas; kids at Escola da Terra later.
- Van: Sintra-Cascais Natural Park + coastal plan → no van parking outside official places. [Lei 66/2021](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/lei/66-2021-170083315)
- Buying: very hard with our budget. Renting a house nearby is more realistic. *(guess)*

## Water (honest)
- Wet Atlantic microclimate, "misty and green most of the year". [NuMundo](https://numundo.org/center/portugal/ecoaldeia-de-janas)
- Tap water from the Lisbon system (EPAL, Castelo de Bode dam, centre of Portugal). *(guess, not checked here)*
- National dams ~77% in mid-Sept 2026. [Portugal News](https://www.theportugalnews.com/news/2026-09-15/dams-in-portugal-have-an-average-water-storage-capacity-of-77/1086300)
- **Verdict:** the safest water of all southern options.

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ✅ (Portugal). Plus many licensed alternative schools in Sintra / Lisbon (14+). [alternativeschoolsinportugal](https://www.alternativeschoolsinportugal.com/)
- Van / yurt: strict (natural park).
- Wildfire risk: ⚠️ medium–high. In July and August 2025 the forest and monuments of Serra de Sintra were closed for high fire danger. [Parques de Sintra](https://www.parquesdesintra.pt/pt/sobre-nos/noticias/monumentos-na-serra-de-sintra-encerramento-risco-de-incendio-agosto-2025/), [CM Sintra](https://cm-sintra.pt/atualidade/noticias-institucional/perigo-de-incendio-encerra-monumentos-e-perimetro-florestal-da-serra-de-sintra-de-domingo-a-terca)
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ⚠️⚠️ mass tourism (Sintra palaces, Cabo da Roca).

## Costs
- Colares rustic plots: 2,160 m² near Cabo da Roca asks €160,000; an 11,640 m² plot listed at a very high price. [RB Real Estate](https://www.rbrealestate.pt/imovel/terreno-rustico-11640m2-penedo-colares-sintra/21556061), [idealista](https://www.idealista.pt/en/comprar-terrenos/sintra/colares/)
- **Far above our budget for 1 ha.**
