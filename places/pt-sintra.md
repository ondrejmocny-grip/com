# Sintra bioregion (Sintra, Colares, Azóia)

- **Status:** lead
- **Country / region:** Portugal, Lisbon district, Sintra municipality (Sintra hills, Colares, Azóia near Cabo da Roca). Nearby: Mafra / Ericeira.
- **Nearest town / airport:** Sintra town; Lisbon airport ~30–45 min *(guess)*.
- **Website / contacts:**
  - Escola da Terra (Waldorf school + kindergarten, Azóia): [escolaterra.com](https://www.escolaterra.com/)
  - Terra Alta (off-grid permaculture education centre): [terralta.org](https://www.terralta.org/about), [GEN](https://ecovillage.org/project/terra-alta-permaculture/)
  - Tribe Portugal (forest school, Sintra): [alternativeschoolsinportugal.com](https://www.alternativeschoolsinportugal.com/)
- **Added:** 2026-09-27 by Claude

## In short
A green, hilly area by the sea, 30–45 min from Lisbon. Strong alternative family scene: a Waldorf school with **50+ children from all over the world**, a forest school, and Terra Alta, a permaculture centre. Many international families. But: **expensive, touristy, and close to the city.** Good as a first stop or "hub" to visit; hard as a place to buy land.

## People
- Escola da Terra: 50+ kids "from all over the world"; founded by Pedro Valdjiu and Rita Seixas (Portuguese) for their own kids. School founded 2008. [search summary / escolaterra.com](https://www.escolaterra.com/), [terralta.org](https://www.terralta.org/about)
- Terra Alta: off-grid education and research centre in a protected valley, same founders; together = "Waldorf Association of Sintra". [GEN](https://ecovillage.org/project/terra-alta-permaculture/)
- A family blogger named Sintra (Escola da Terra) as the **first place to visit** for international families. [Roxane Maar, Medium](https://roxanemaar.medium.com/3-family-friendly-communities-worth-visiting-in-portugal-716d858f6cb2)
- Spiritual: anthroposophy (Waldorf), permaculture, yoga scene. ⚠️ *Near Lisbon, some "show-off" / wealthy wellness scene is likely. Guess.*

## Integration
- Escola da Terra uses the **old village school of Azóia**. [escolaterra.com](https://www.escolaterra.com/single-post/2014/09/16/Escola-da-Terra-na-antiga-escola-da-Az%C3%B3ia)
- Traditions: Colares wine, old quintas. *Village life is weaker near the city (guess).*

## How we could join
- Terra Alta runs courses and a "Get Involved 2026" program (volunteers / courses). [terralta.org](https://www.terralta.org/about)
- Renting in Sintra is expensive. *Guess: live 20–40 min away (Mafra hinterland, Ericeira inland) is cheaper.*
- Van: ⚠️ hard near Lisbon (protected natural park Sintra-Cascais, *guess*).

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ✅ Portugal. Waldorf school also available.
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️⚠️ Natural park rules + council license. *Likely strict.*
- Wildfire risk: ⚠️ Medium *(Sintra hills have burned in the past — guess, verify)*.
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ⚠️⚠️ **Mass tourism** (Sintra palaces, Cabo da Roca). Strong expat presence.

## Costs
- *Guess: among the highest in Portugal outside Lisbon/Cascais. Land 1 ha with license = likely above our budget. Verify.*
