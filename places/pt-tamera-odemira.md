# Tamera – Healing Biotope I (Odemira)

- **Status:** dropped (as a place to join) — kept as a **hub reference**
- **Why dropped:** deal-breaker. Closed-ish intentional community with a strong ideological frame ("Healing Biotopes Plan") built by its founders. Not an open, integrated village project.
- **Country / region:** Portugal, Alentejo, Odemira municipality, Monte do Cerro, 7630-392 Relíquias
- **Nearest town / airport:** Relíquias / Odemira / São Luís (~20 min, *guess*). Lisbon or Faro ~2 h *(guess)*
- **Website / contacts:** https://www.tamera.org
- **Added:** 2026-09-27 by Claude (web research)

## In short
Tamera is a "Peace Research & Education Centre". Founded 1978 in Germany by Dieter Duhm, Sabine Lichtenfels and Charly Rainer Ehrenpreis. Moved to Portugal in 1995. About 180 people on ~330 acres (~134 ha). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamera), [ic.org](https://www.ic.org/directory/community/tamera-healing-biotope-i/)

## Why it still matters to us
- It is a **magnet**: it pulls many people to the Odemira area. Many of them later start their own projects nearby (São Luís, Relíquias, Colos). *(guess, typical pattern)*
- **Water lesson:** its "Water Retention Landscape" (2006–2015) made 29 lakes; water surface grew from 0.62 ha to ~8.3 ha. EU adaptation platforms cite it for drought resilience. Good course to take before we buy land in the south. [BASE](https://base-adaptation.eu/water-retention-landscape-restore-water-cycle-and-reduce-vulnerability-droughts-tamera-portugal), [tamera.org](https://www.tamera.org/water-retention-landscape/)
- **School:** Escola da Esperança (at Tamera) wants to be the first international school in the Alentejo, open also to local children. Status: in development / pending licensing. [escola-da-esperanca.org](http://escola-da-esperanca.org/about-us/who-we-are/), [alternativeschoolsinportugal](https://www.alternativeschoolsinportugal.com/)

## Flags
- ⚠️ Strong ideology and founder-led vision. Its model "is deemed controversial by mainstream science" (Wikipedia). Known for its own ideas on love and sexuality *(from general knowledge, not checked here)*.
- ⚠️ Same water and fire risks as the whole Odemira area (see [pt-sao-luis-odemira.md](pt-sao-luis-odemira.md)).

## How to use it
- Visit an open day or a short course (water retention). Do **not** plan to live inside.
- Use it to meet people who live in the region around it.
