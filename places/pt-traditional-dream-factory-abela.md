# Traditional Dream Factory (TDF) — Abela, Santiago do Cacém (Alentejo)

- **Status:** researching (deep dive done 2026-09-28). Good place to **visit, volunteer and learn**. Weak as a place to **root** (few families, hot, dry, one campus).
- **Country / region:** Portugal, Alentejo Litoral (Setúbal district), municipality Santiago do Cacém, parish Abela.
- **Nearest town / airport:** Santiago do Cacém (~15 km). Train: Ermidas-Sado (~15 min). Airports: Lisbon ~1.5 h, Faro ~2 h.
- **Website / contacts:** https://www.traditionaldreamfactory.com · team: space@traditionaldreamfactory.com · press: press@traditionaldreamfactory.com · Telegram https://t.me/traditionaldreamfactory · Substack https://traditionaldreamfactory.substack.com · Address: Cerca do Aviário / Largo do Chafariz 12, 7540-011 Abela.
- **Added:** 2026-09-28 by Claude (web research). All facts seen on 2026-09-28 unless another date is given. *Italic = guess.*
- **Órgiva pattern:** **3.5/8** (fails the 6/8 rule — see below)
- **Climate:** 🟡 **Borderline** — passes the "6 dry months" rule, but inland summer heat is strong (TDF itself closes in July–August).
- **Score (Claude's first guess):** ***71 / 125***
- **Short file (Ecovilla sweep):** [../ecovilla-sweep/places/pt-traditional-dream-factory-abela.md](../ecovilla-sweep/places/pt-traditional-dream-factory-abela.md)

The real pieces are: **OASA** (Swiss non-profit), **TDF DAO**, the **$TDF token** (Celo blockchain), and **Closer** (their booking / token software). [team](https://www.traditionaldreamfactory.com/team), [whitepaper](https://oasa.earth/papers/OASA-Whitepaper-V1.2.pdf), [samueldelesque.me](https://samueldelesque.me/)

## In short
- A **25 ha former chicken farm** (built 1972, abandoned ~15 years) next to the small village of Abela. Since 2021 it is a **co-living "regenerative village"**: glamping, van spots, co-working, café, sauna, event hall, bio-pool, food forest, mushroom farm.
- It is paid for with a **crypto "utility" token ($TDF)**: 1 token = 1 night per year, forever, plus 1 vote. 30 tokens + time on the land + vouches = **"Citizen"**.
- It is still **half built**. The hotel-grade rooms, restaurant and the planned **23-home co-housing** do not exist yet. Construction is planned for **spring 2027**, if the money comes.
- The founder is **seriously ill** (cancer, 2026). The team is small. Money is tight but honest reporting is good.

### Citizenship
- Needs: **30+ tokens** (≈ **€7,900**, *+ maybe 8.1% VAT → ~€8,500, guess*), **14 days on the land**, vouches, sign the Citizen Agreement. [citizenship](https://www.traditionaldreamfactory.com/citizenship)
- ⚠️ The website says **3 vouches**; the booking system config says **6 vouches + space-host vouch**. [config](https://api.traditionaldreamfactory.com/config)
- Duties: 1 community call per quarter, 2+ weeks per year (config: 28 nights in 2 years), annual Citizen Gathering. [citizenship](https://www.traditionaldreamfactory.com/citizenship), [config](https://api.traditionaldreamfactory.com/config)
- Rights: book up to 1 year ahead, up to 180 days/year stays, vote, early co-housing access. *How many nights at "utility cost" beyond your token count is not clear — ask.*

## 8. Land and nature

### Water
- Region: Alentejo = structural drought zone. Sam: "a territory officially becoming a desert"; "six months without rain is the baseline". [Substack 2026-07-21](https://traditionaldreamfactory.substack.com/p/record-heat-continued-purpose), [regions/portugal.md](../regions/portugal.md)
- **Campilhas dam** (Santiago do Cacém, parishes Cercal / São Domingos) is **irrigation only**; it hit its **lowest ever, 3.2%**, in Oct 2022; low since 2013. Plan to link it to Alqueva. [Rádio Sines 2022-11-06](https://radiosines.sapo.pt/noticias/barragem-de-campilhas-atingiu-o-nivel-mais-baixo-de-sempre), [Ambiente Online](https://www.ambienteonline.pt/noticias/regantes-de-campilhas-e-alto-sado-avancam-com-projetos-para-reforcar-abastecimento-de-agua-e-modernizar-bloco-de-rega)
- TDF has **city (mains) water** since 2021, plus rain harvesting (1.2–1.5M litres). **No borehole used** until late Aug 2025. Well water table rose from ~20 m to **~3 m** below ground (2025) after swales/lakes. [2021 report](https://www.traditionaldreamfactory.com/pdf/2021-TDF-report.pdf), [Substack 2026-03-03](https://traditionaldreamfactory.substack.com/p/tdf-2026-an-honest-note-on-regeneration)
- 2025/26 winter: very wet (300% rain anomaly, floods). IPMA Aug 2026: drought worse in interior Baixo Alentejo. [IPMA Aug 2026](https://www.ipma.pt/pt/media/noticias/news.detail.jsp?y=2026&f=boletim_climatologico_agosto_2026.html)
- **Verdict:** ⚠️ weak. Good water work on site, but the region is dry and swings between drought and flood.

- Dry months (<50 mm): **May–Sep in a row (5) + March** → ✅ passes the "6 dry months" rule.

- Problem for our rhythm: the good TDF seasons (spring, autumn) are **not** the dry months; the dry months are **too hot** (Nesli: no strong heat).
- **Verdict: 🟡 Borderline** (rain ✅, heat ❌).
