# Alınca / Karaağaç (above Kabak and Faralya), Fethiye

- **Status:** lead
- **Which Karaağaç?** Almost sure: **Karaağaç mahallesi of Fethiye**. Alınca is a *mevki* (named part) of it. Proof: the glamping address is "Karaağaç mah., Alınca mevkii, Alınca sok. 55, Fethiye". [search summary / Türkdomes listing](https://turkdomes-glamping.aegeanhotelspage.com/en/). Karaağaç was a hamlet of Faralya; in 1954 it joined with the hamlets **Dokuzgöl, Kirme and Alınca** to become a village; since 2012 a mahalle of Fethiye. [Wikipedia TR](https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karaa%C4%9Fa%C3%A7,_Fethiye). *Other "Karaağaç" places exist in Turkey, but none fits "Alınca / Karaağaç" (guess).* ⚠️ News calls **Kirme** part of Faralya and **Kozağaç** part of Karaağaç — borders between the hamlets are fuzzy. [search summary / Habertürk](https://www.haberturk.com/yerel-haberler/mugla-haberleri/fethiye-kirme-ve-kozagac-icme-suyu-hatlari-yenileniyor-118027516)
- **Country / region:** Turkey, Muğla, Fethiye district. Mountain hamlets on the west slope of Babadağ, high above Kabak bay. Alınca ~650–800 m. [search summary / Wikipedia + Lycian Way pages](https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karaa%C4%9Fa%C3%A7,_Fethiye), [Novo-Monde](https://www.novo-monde.com/en/lycian-way-fethiye-xanthos/)
- **People (official):** Karaağaç **343** (2025). Faralya (next door) 700. [nufusune / TÜİK](https://www.nufusune.com/fethiye-ilce-nufusu-mugla). Karaağaç had 253 in 2000 → **growing**. [Wikipedia TR](https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karaa%C4%9Fa%C3%A7,_Fethiye)
- **Nearest town / airport:** Fethiye ~50 km *(search summary; Wikipedia says 70 km)*, *~1 h by car (guess)*. Faralya / Kabak 10–20 min down *(guess)*. Dalaman airport *~1 h 45 (guess)*. Minibus Fethiye–Faralya: 3×/day in winter, hourly in summer. [Workaway Faralya host](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/255153397935)

**Órgiva pattern:** **3/8** — ❌ 1 no town (Fethiye ~1 h), ⚠️ 2 some newcomers (villa and glamping owners; *few, guess*), ✅ 3 spread hamlets (Alınca, Kirme, Dokuzgöl, Kozağaç) next to the Faralya/Kabak scene, ⚠️ 4 few projects here (pensions, domes); many in Kabak/Faralya below, ❌ 5 market = Fethiye Tuesday (~1 h), ❌ 6 no families / school found, ✅ 7 **best mountain + sea view in the area**, ✅ 8 easy start via Faralya/Kabak Workaway hosts and pensions.

**Best way to see it:** not a hub. A **cheaper, cooler, very quiet satellite** of the Kabak–Faralya yoga-camp area. This fits our "live 20–40 min from a hub" idea only if we accept that the "hub" (Kabak/Faralya) is **seasonal** and the real town (Fethiye) is ~1 h.

## In short
Small Yörük mountain hamlets on a balcony 700–800 m above the sea, on the Lycian Way. Views over Kabak and Butterfly Valley. Life = goats, gardens, a few family pensions for hikers, some new villas and glamping. Cooler summers than the coast. **But:** tiny, far from town, few families, no school found, fire-prone pine slopes, and the yoga scene below closes in winter.

## Village vibe
- Mountain village on the Lycian Way, "between Fethiye and Kalkan", sees the sea "from very high". 11 km walk from Faralya (3–4 h). [search summary / gezintihaberleri](https://www.gezintihaberleri.com/?Syf=18&Hbr=1052460)
- From Kabak beach the trail climbs ~800 m to Alınca. [Sunrise Odyssey](https://sunriseodyssey.com/trekking-the-lycian-way-in-turkey)
- Hikers describe **kind, hospitable villagers** (stayed with a family, dinner + breakfast). Simple family pensions. [Likya Yolu Rehberi](https://likyayolurehberi.com/yol-guncesi/ovacik-faralya-alinca), [Lotus Eaters, 2025](https://lotuseaters.travel/2025/07/29/a-guide-to-lycian-way-accommodation-where-to-stay/)
- A **small grocery** (noodles, beans, bread, nuts). [search summary / Lotus Eaters](https://lotuseaters.travel/2025/07/29/a-guide-to-lycian-way-accommodation-where-to-stay/)
- New: luxury villas with pools ("Villa Alınca"), Airbnb "pure luxury" house. [Tatil Premium](https://www.tatilpremium.com/tr/fethiye-karaagac-alincada-ozel-havuzlu-deniz-manzarali-kiralik-luks-villa), [Airbnb](https://www.airbnb.com/rooms/29425832)
- *Feel (guess): authentic, quiet, very small. Real village life, but limited social life for us.*

## Water (honest)
| Fact | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|

| Network | MUSKİ renewing **1,400 m** of drinking-water pipes in **Kirme** (Faralya) and **Kozağaç** (Karaağaç), with bigger diameter, "because of rising population and summer demand" and to stop summer cuts | [search summary / Habertürk, May 2026](https://www.haberturk.com/yerel-haberler/mugla-haberleri/fethiye-kirme-ve-kozagac-icme-suyu-hatlari-yenileniyor-118027516), [Gerçek Fethiye](https://www.gercekfethiye.com/fethiye-kirme-ve-kozagac-icme-suyu-hatlari-yenileniyor/122550) |
| Rain | Fethiye coast 843 mm/year. *Mountain slope at 700–800 m likely gets more (guess, maybe 900–1,100 mm).* | [Wikipedia: Fethiye (NOAA)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fethiye) |
| 2025 / 2026 | 2025 = worst drought in 50 years; 2026 water year = wettest in 66 years (Turkey +29%, Muğla wettest in Aegean) | [Wikipedia TR](https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_T%C3%BCrkiye_su_krizi), [Sabah / MGM, 2026-05-11](https://www.sabah.com.tr/trend/galeri/yasam/mgm-duyurdu-turkiyede-yagislar-rekor-kirdi-son-66-yilin-en-yuksek-seviyesi) |

- **Verdict:** ⚠️ **Unknown, probably OK for a house** (springs from Babadağ karst + upgraded pipe + more rain at altitude). **Summer shortage exists** (that is why the pipe is being enlarged). *Garden irrigation in Aug–Sep: likely limited (guess).* Ask about each hamlet's spring (Dokuzgöl = "nine ponds" — a hint of water? pure guess).

## Climate
- No station. *Guess from Fethiye + altitude (−0.6 °C per 100 m): Jul max ~30–31 °C (Fethiye 35), Jan min ~2–3 °C. Frost some nights; snow a few days in some winters (guess).*
- Rain pattern as Fethiye: May–Oct dry (1–4 rain days/month). [Wikipedia (NOAA)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fethiye)
- **6 dry months rule: ✅ Pass (guess, from Fethiye station).** Summer **cooler than the coast** → better for Nesli than Kayaköy. Winter colder, wetter, windy *(guess)*.

## Land, zoning, legal
- **Kabak bay (below) = 1st-degree natural site**: no building, only wooden structures + paid camp areas. [search summary / Habertürk Hayat](https://hthayat.haberturk.com/kabak-koyu-gezi-rehberi-1074362). *Whether Alınca / Karaağaç is inside a sit zone: not found → check at Fethiye municipality (guess: parts may be).*
- **Bağ evi on farmland: plot ≥ 2 ha** since April 2026 (was 5 ha). [Sözcü](https://www.sozcu.com.tr/tarim-arazisinde-bungalov-yapma-sartlari-degisti-yeni-arazi-siniri-belli-oldu-p342136), [Hürriyet](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/bilgi/galeri/tarlaya-ev-yapma-sartlari-degisti-mi-bag-evi-sartlarinda-onemli-degisiklik-43262965). Most Karaağaç plots are <1 ha → no bağ evi.
- 💡 **Key idea: "köy içi" plots.** Several listings say *köyiçi / köy içinde* (inside the village). Inside the official **village settlement area** (*köy yerleşik alanı*) a house can be built under simpler rules than on farmland. *(Known rule in Turkish zoning, but I have not checked it for this mahalle — verify with a lawyer. Since 2012 the village is a Fethiye mahalle, which may change the rules.)*
- Van / yurt as home: ❌ not legal. Glamping domes exist, so *a licensed tourism route may exist (guess)*.
- Buy in **Nesli's name**. [tarimhukuku.org](https://www.tarimhukuku.org/turkiyede-yabancilara-tarim-arazisi-satisi-mumkun-mu/)

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ❌ illegal.
- Van / yurt on land: ❌ as a home. Faralya Workaway host gives a tent; vans not stated.
- Wildfire: ⚠️ **high** — steep pine slopes, one narrow road. A fire in a Kabak camp burned 15 + 4 bungalows (put out before the forest). [sondakika](https://www.sondakika.com/3-sayfa/haber-kabak-koyu-ndaki-yanginda-cok-sayida-bungalov-kul-13670856/) *(date not clear, ~2020)*. July 2026 fire near Göcek (other side of Fethiye). [Hürriyet](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/gundem/son-dakika-fethiyede-orman-yangini-havadan-ve-karadan-mudahale-ediliyor-43247946)
- Expat bubble: ✅ low (village). Mass tourism: ⚠️ hikers + Kabak crowds in summer; paragliding on Babadağ.

## How we could join
- **Try first:** 2–4 weeks at the Faralya Workaway bungalows (garden, building, animals) → drive up to Alınca often. [Workaway](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/255153397935)
- **Winter test:** rent a pension room / house in Nov–Mar to feel the cold and emptiness.
- **Later:** buy a *köyiçi* plot or a village house (if zoning OK) in Nesli's name; small guesthouse for Lycian Way hikers + yoga = a real, local business model *(guess)*.
- **What we bring:** yoga (Nesli), cooking for hikers, animal care (goat village), community building for the few year-round people.
