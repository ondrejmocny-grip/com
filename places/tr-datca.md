## On-site water check (we are here — ask locals)

Ask 3–5 people: a farmer, a well driller, the muhtar, a newcomer family, a water shop.

| Question | Turkish (to ask) | Good sign | Bad sign |
|---|---|---|---|
| Does this village have its own spring or network? | Köyün kendi kaynağı / şebekesi var mı? | Spring, all year | Only tanker |
| How often is water cut in summer? | Yazın su ne sıklıkla kesiliyor? | Rarely | Days, every summer |
| Is the well water salty? How deep are wells now? | Kuyu suyu tuzlu mu? Kuyular kaç metre? | Sweet, stable depth | Salty, getting deeper |
| Cost of a water tanker? | Tanker su kaça? | – | You need it often |
| Do people use cisterns (sarnıç) for rain water? | Sarnıç kullanan var mı? | Old cisterns work | – |

## In short
Very popular with İstanbul and Ankara people who "leave the city". Almonds, olives, honey, silk weaving, calm stone villages. A real town with 12 neighbourhoods. Datça is **like an island without rivers**. But rain is not tiny (~686 mm/year) and a lot of groundwater flows into the sea. The problem is **coastal wells turning salty, weak pipes, and summer crowds** (4–10× population). The east–center (Datça plain: İskele, Reşadiye, Kızlan, Karaköy, Hızırşah) is better. The west (Betçe: Mesudiye, Sındı, Yaka, Cumalı, Yazı, Palamutbükü) is on tankers in 2026.

### Salinisation
- Coastal wells turning salty: Palamutbükü coast, Billurkent (30 m → now 80 m), Cumalı drinking well EC 1,700 µS/cm. [Su Dostu](http://sudostudatca.blogspot.com/p/hidrojeoloji.html)
- MUSKİ runs a reverse-osmosis plant for the salty Palamutbükü well; above ~2,500 µS/cm more RO plants will be needed. [search summary of Su Dostu](http://sudostudatca.blogspot.com/p/hidrojeoloji.html)

### Verdict (honest)
- **House: yes.** A household with a **50–80 m³ cistern, a big roof, greywater and careful use can live well**, with a few tankers in bad years. *(guess, based on the numbers above)*
- **Green, irrigated farm: no.** Only dry-farming (olive, almond, carob, fig, herbs) and a small mulched garden.
- **Where:** center plain (Kızlan, Reşadiye, Hızırşah, Karaköy). **Not** the Betçe coast for now.
- **Timing trick:** if Datça is our "home half", live here **Oct–Apr** (rain, low crowds, mild) and travel in summer (the water crunch). *(guess — fits our 6+6 rhythm)*
- **Against our rule:** criteria say "water shortage = deal-breaker". The public system **is** short in summer. Our own cistern makes us independent, but it needs work and money. **This is our call → decide together after the on-site check.**

## Land & legal

### Protected area (ÖÇK)
- Whole peninsula is in the **Datça–Bozburun Special Environmental Protection Area** (Özel Çevre Koruma Bölgesi), declared 22 Oct 1990, 1,443 km². [ÖÇK – ÇŞB](https://ockb.csb.gov.tr/datca-bozburun-ozel-cevre-koruma-bolgesi-i-2747)
- What it means: **plans and permits are made by the Ministry** (Tabiat Varlıklarını Koruma GM), not the town. Slow and strict. [search summary / Muğla ÇŞB](https://mugla.csb.gov.tr/datca-bozburun-ozel-cevre-koruma-bolgesi-1-25000-olcekli-cevre-duzeni-plani-revizyonu-plan-hukmu-degisikligi-duyuru-447358)
- 1/25,000 plan: 2014 revision (contested by planners), approved 2020, changed 2021 and 2023. [TMMOB ŞPO](http://www.tmmob.org.tr/icerik/spo-mugla-ili-datca-bozburun-ozel-cevre-koruma-bolgesi-125000-olcekli-cevre-duzeni-plani)
- Permits in Cumalı and Karaköy were **frozen from 30 Mar 2021**, **reopened 2 Apr 2026** under the 2014 plan (only in rural settlement / rural development zones). [Muğla Gazetesi](https://www.muglagazetesi.com.tr/datca-haberleri/datca-cumali-ve-karakoyde-imar-sureci-yeniden-basladi-ruhsat-islemleri-210890h)
- ⚠️ State is selling 384,000 m² of treasury land in Emecik (June 2026). [Muğla Gazetesi](https://www.muglagazetesi.com.tr/mugla-haberleri/datca-bozburun-ozel-cevre-koruma-bolgesindeki-araziler-icin-bakanlara-215124h)

### Bağ evi and small houses (national rules, changed April 2026)
- A *bağ evi* on farmland needs **≥ 2 ha (20 dönüm)** now (was 5 ha). Must serve farming. Needs agriculture office + soil commission + municipality. [Sözcü](https://www.sozcu.com.tr/tarim-arazisinde-bungalov-yapma-sartlari-degisti-yeni-arazi-siniri-belli-oldu-p342136), [Hürriyet](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/bilgi/galeri/tarlaya-ev-yapma-sartlari-degisti-mi-bag-evi-sartlarinda-onemli-degisiklik-43262965)
- Size: sources **disagree** (30 m², 60 m² on two floors, or 150 m² / 5%). [Hürriyet](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/bilgi/galeri/tarlaya-ev-yapma-sartlari-degisti-mi-bag-evi-sartlarinda-onemli-degisiklik-43262965), [haritahaber](https://www.haritahaber.com/haber/tarla-ve-zeytinlik-sahipleri-icin-guncel-bag-evi-ruhsati-sartlari-2026_6266/) → **ask a local lawyer**.
