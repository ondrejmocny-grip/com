## On-site water check (we are here — ask locals)

Ask 3–5 people: a farmer, a well driller, the muhtar, a newcomer family, a water shop.

| Question | Turkish (to ask) | Good sign | Bad sign |
|---|---|---|---|
| Does this village have its own spring or network? | Köyün kendi kaynağı / şebekesi var mı? | Spring, all year | Only tanker |
| How often is water cut in summer? | Yazın su ne sıklıkla kesiliyor? | Rarely | Days, every summer |
| Is the well water salty? How deep are wells now? | Kuyu suyu tuzlu mu? Kuyular kaç metre? | Sweet, stable depth | Salty, getting deeper |
| Cost of a water tanker? | Tanker su kaça? | – | You need it often |
| Do people use cisterns (sarnıç) for rain water? | Sarnıç kullanan var mı? | Old cisterns work | – |
| Any new water project (DSİ, desalination)? | Yeni su projesi var mı (DSİ, arıtma)? | Funded, working | Failed (like Betçe) |
| Which villages have the best water? | Hangi köylerin suyu en iyi? | – | – |

Also check: which months it rains, olive / almond trees health, green vs dry gardens in September.

## In short
Very popular with İstanbul people who "leave the city". Almonds, olives, honey, calm villages. But Datça is **like an island without rivers**. Groundwater is small, sinking and turning salty. Summer population grows 4–5×, and then water runs out.

## People
- Well-known people and many white-collar İstanbul people moved here. [Hürriyet](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/kelebek/stil/unlu-oyuncu-datcaya-yerlesmisti-koy-hayati-kolay-degil-42652057)
- *Many are second-home owners, retirees and villa people. Less of a project / spiritual scene than Kaz Dağları (guess).*
- Kindergarten with rhythm workshops and "own pace" learning: Mor Kanguru. [morkanguru.com](https://www.morkanguru.com/)

## Water (honest) — the reason for dropping
- No rivers, limited aquifer. Winter rain is falling, summer evaporation rising. [Su Dostu Datça](http://sudostudatca.blogspot.com/p/hidrojeoloji.html)
- **Coastal wells are turning salty** and it moves fast. Reverse-osmosis plants will be needed. [Su Dostu Datça](http://sudostudatca.blogspot.com/p/blog-page.html)
- July 2026 article: groundwater sinking, illegal wells, ~40% loss in pipes, summer cuts, pools and lawns of villas. [Datça Havadis, 2026-07-27](https://datcahavadis.com/datcanin-susuzluk-drami-havuzlu-villalar-cimli-bahceler-ve-o-garibim-esekler/)
- Betçe: a 2023 water project "of the century" stopped working after 3 years; people back on tanker water. [Datça Havadis](https://datcahavadis.com/yuzyilin-projesinde-husran-datca-betce-halki-yeniden-tanker-suyuna-mahkum-oldu/)
- 3-day water cuts in Belen neighbourhood (tanks empty). [Şikayetvar](https://www.sikayetvar.com/mugla-buyuksehir-belediyesi/datcada-3-gundur-suren-su-kesintisi-tatilimizi-kabusa-cevirdi)

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ❌ illegal in Turkey.
- Van / yurt on land: ❌ not legal as a home. See [Turkey note](../regions/turkey.md).
- Wildfire: ⚠️ medium. 2021: only 9 ha burned in Datça (vs 13,600 ha in Marmaris). [soL](https://haber.sol.org.tr/haber/muglada-kac-hektar-alan-yandi-belediye-tabloyu-acikladi-310878). Local paper warns "before the fire, not after". [Datça Havadis](https://datcahavadis.com/yangindan-sonra-degil-once/)
- Expat bubble: ✅ low (Turkish). Mass tourism: ⚠️ summer 4–5× population.
