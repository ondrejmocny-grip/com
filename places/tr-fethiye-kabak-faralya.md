# Fethiye hinterland: Kabak, Faralya, Kayaköy, Üzümlü

- **Status:** lead
- **Country / region:** Turkey, Muğla province, Fethiye district (Lycian coast). Hub: Fethiye town. Hinterland: Kayaköy, Üzümlü, Faralya (above Butterfly Valley), Kabak valley (25 km south of Fethiye). Seydikemer (inland farm district) next door.
- **Nearest town / airport:** Fethiye (big town, hospital). Dalaman airport *~45 min–1 h (guess)*.
- **Website / contacts:**
  - Huzur Vadisi (yoga holidays since 1993, British founders): [Daily Sabah, 2016](https://www.dailysabah.com/expats-corner/2016/04/12/fethiyes-peaceful-valley-for-tranquility-nature-and-yoga)
  - Eco Yuva Faralya yoga retreats: [ecofaralya.com](https://ecofaralya.com)
  - Retreat overview (Kabak, Faralya, Çıralı): [Explore Lycia](https://explorelycia.com/retreats?amp=&amp=)
- **Added:** 2026-09-27 by Claude (web research)

**Órgiva pattern:** 6/8 points — ✅ 1 Fethiye is a real town (but big, ~170k *guess*), ✅ 2 many newcomers (Turkish + ~6,000 foreigners), ✅ 3 spread in villages, ✅ 4 many yoga / eco camps, ✅ 5 big Tuesday market, ❌ 6 no alternative school found, ✅ 7 mountains + sea, ⚠️ 8 easy start mostly seasonal (camp work Apr–Nov).

## In short
Fethiye's back country is Turkey's oldest yoga-retreat area. Kabak valley has had yoga camps since the early 2000s. Faralya and Huzur Vadisi since the 1990s. High mountains (Babadağ) fall into the sea. **But:** the scene is **seasonal** (camps close Nov–Apr), the foreigners are mostly **British retirees**, and there is **mass tourism** in Ölüdeniz / Hisarönü. No alternative school.

## People
- ~6,167 resident foreigners in Fethiye, mostly British; earlier (2018) ~7,600, of which 5,200 British, 1,050 German. They live mostly in Ölüdeniz, Çalış, Göcek, Çiftlik, Üzümlü. [Gerçek Fethiye](https://www.gercekfethiye.com/fethiyede-6-bin-167-yerlesik-yabanci-yasiyor/22247/), [Haberler.com](https://www.haberler.com/guncel/fethiye-de-7-bin-500-yerlesik-yabanci-yasiyor-2937961-haberi/) *(dates not clear, verify)*
- Kabak: ~80 year-round residents in ~30 households; several hundred camp guests plus seasonal yoga teachers May–Oct. [Explore Lycia](https://explorelycia.com/kabak-valley)
- Spiritual scene: yoga, meditation, retreat camps (Kabak, Faralya). [Discover Ölüdeniz](https://discoveroludeniz.com/yoga-and-meditation-camps-in-the-mystical-countryside-of-fethiye-renew-your-soul/), [BookYogaRetreats](https://www.bookyogaretreats.com/all/d/africa-and-the-middle-east/turkey/fethiye)
- *Grounded? Mixed. Many retreat businesses serve tourists; fewer year-round families (guess).*

## Integration
- Fethiye has a strong normal Turkish life: big Tuesday market beside the stadium, locals + expats both shop there. [Fethiye Tours](https://www.fethiyetours.com/best-of-fethiye/tuesday-market/), [Turkey's For Life](https://www.turkeysforlife.com/2011/06/celebration-of-fethiye-market.html)
- Kayaköy = abandoned Greek village (1923), now a tourism and heritage site. [search summary](https://www.fethiye-turlari.com/fethi-ye-market-kayakoy)
- *Village life (Yörük shepherds in the mountains, goat farming) still alive in Faralya / Seydikemer (guess).*

## How we could join
- Seasonal work / volunteering in yoga camps (Nesli: yoga, cooking). *Many camps take staff Apr–Nov (guess).*
- Van: *camps in Kabak / Faralya may let you park (guess).*
- Yurt / tiny house on own farmland: ❌ not legal as a home (see [Turkey note](../regions/turkey.md)).
- Land: buy in Nesli's name.

## Water (honest)
- Wetter than the North Aegean (mountains catch rain). *~800–900 mm/yr (guess, verify).*
- But summer water cuts happen in Fethiye because of growth, pools and hills. MUSKİ is renewing lines to Faralya and Karaağaç to "increase capacity". [Marmaris Manşet](https://marmarismanset.com/haber/28035749/muskiden-fethiyeye-1400-metrelik-icme-suyu-hatti-yatirimi), [Şikayetvar](https://www.sikayetvar.com/mugla-buyuksehir-belediyesi/fethiye-su-kesintisi)
- *Kabak has springs but it is a steep dry slope in summer (guess).*
- **Verdict:** ⚠️ OK-ish, not great. Check springs on site.

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ❌ illegal in Turkey. Private schools in Fethiye (e.g. Fethiye Ata, "nature-based" learning claim). [fethiyeata.k12.tr](https://fethiyeata.k12.tr/). No Waldorf / BBOM school found. Nearest BBOM: Antalya Çocuk Köyü (Döşemealtı) *~3 h (guess)*. [BBOM](https://www.baskabirokulmumkun.org/okullar/)
- Van / yurt on land: ❌ not legal as a home.
- Wildfire: ❌ high. 2021 Muğla fires: Marmaris 13,600 ha, Köyceğiz 12,001 ha, Seydikemer 2,011 ha. [soL](https://haber.sol.org.tr/haber/muglada-kac-hektar-alan-yandi-belediye-tabloyu-acikladi-310878). A fire in Bayır (Seydikemer) forced evacuation of ~300 households *(date in source unclear: 2025 or 2026)*. [Wikipedia 2025 fires](https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_T%C3%BCrkiye_orman_yang%C4%B1nlar%C4%B1)
- Expat bubble: ⚠️ strong British retiree bubble in Çalış / Ölüdeniz / Üzümlü. Mass tourism: ❌ Ölüdeniz, Hisarönü, Kabak in summer.

## Costs (listings, 1 € ≈ 56 TL)
- Seydikemer farmland (July 2025): 450–870 TL/m² (**~€8–15/m²**). E.g. 9,817 m² for 8.5M TL (~€150k). [search summary / Emlak Fabrika](https://www.emlakfabrika.com/)
- Fethiye Karaağaç 9,809 m² tarla for 11M TL (~€196k). [Emlakjet](https://www.emlakjet.com/satilik-arsa/mugla-fethiye)
- *Kabak / Faralya land with sea view: much higher (guess).*
