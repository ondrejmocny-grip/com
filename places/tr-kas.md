- **Country / region:** Turkey, Antalya province, Kaş district. Coast from Kalkan / Patara in the west to Kekova in the east, plus the mountain villages up to the Akdağ range (Gömbe ~1,200 m). **Órgiva pattern:** **7/8** (point 4 only medium) — ✅ 1 real small town (Kaş ~9,000; ~4,000–7,000 in winter), ✅ 2 many newcomers (Turkish city leavers, British, German, Russian since 2022), ✅ 3 spread over the peninsula and 10+ villages, ⚠️ 4 projects exist but few (rammed-earth / earthbag builders, one eco farm, yoga house, retreat house) and much is tourism, ✅ 5 Friday market in Kaş + Thursday in Kalkan, ❌ 6 no alternative school found, ✅ 7 **best mountains + sea so far in Turkey** (Akdağ ~3,000 m right behind the sea), ✅ 8 easy start (4 active Workaway hosts that take vans).

## In short
Kaş is a small, lively harbour town with a **diving, film, jazz and yoga scene**, loved by İstanbul people, British and German home owners and, since 2022, many Russians. Behind it the land rises fast to high mountains with **real farming villages** (Gömbe apples at 1,200 m, Bezirgan granaries at ~750 m, the green Kasaba valley). Water comes from **mountain springs**, not from a thin aquifer like Datça. The public pipes break often, though. Big minuses: **tourism and villa building on the coast**, **no alternative school**, and **fire** (two fires in Kaş in summer 2026).

**Best villages for us (first guess):**
1. **Çukurbağ village** — eco farm + retreat house already there, 15 min to Kaş.
2. **Kasaba** — green valley, cheapest land, real village, 25 min to sea.
3. **Gömbe** — best water and cool summers, school with 100 kids; but far and cold in winter.
4. **Sarıbelen / Gökçeören** — near Kaş, some newcomers; watch the villa boom.
*Avoid Kalkan (water, British villa bubble) and Uçağız / Limanağzı (no building).*

### The fix
- **ASAT 1.8 billion TL project (June 2026):** new network in all **54 neighbourhoods**; Çayköy–Kalkan 12 km line (60% done); Saklıkent–Kınık 12 km line (almost done); tanks 5,000 + 2,500 m³; SCADA remote control; "50 years of water for Kalkan"; goal "end summer shortages". [Akdeniz Gerçek](https://www.akdenizgercek.com.tr/kas/asat-kasta-dugmeye-basti-18-milyarlik-su-hatti-yenileniyor/379393), [MyGazete](https://www.mygazete.com/antalya-kas-a-dev-icme-suyu-yatirimi-1-8-milyar-liralik-projede-kritik-asama/783082/)
- The network is **~40 years old**. [NNC Haber](https://www.nnchaber.com/kas-ta-40-yillik-altyapi-yenileniyor-saglikli-ve-kesintisiz-icme-suyu-icin-buyuk-yatirim)

### Verdict
- **Better than Datça.** The water **exists** (mountain snow + karst springs, 834 mm rain). The problem is **old pipes and summer villa demand**, and the state is spending big to fix it.
- **Best: inland villages near the source** (Gömbe, Kasaba). **Worst: Kalkan.**
- ⚠️ With our rule ("water shortage = deal-breaker"): *OK-ish, if the plot has its own spring, well or cistern (guess).* Check on site.

- ⚠️ **Heat (Nesli):** Jul–Aug max 33 °C **and warm nights (min 25 °C)**. Humid coast *(guess)*. Beaches "up to 40 °C". [Hürriyet](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/seyahat/antalyada-sicaktan-bunalanlarin-ilk-adresi-gombe-yaylasi-41581710)
- **Highlands:** no station found for Gömbe (1,200 m) or Bezirgan (~750 m). Nearest: Elmalı (1,100 m): ~487 mm/year, **cold, somewhat snowy winters**, hot dry summers. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elmal%C4%B1). Snow on the Gömbe heights (~2,000 m) already on 6 Sep 2026. [MyGazete](https://www.mygazete.com/antalya-da-mevsimin-ilk-kari-kas-gombe-yaylasi-beyaza-burundu/785325/)
  - *Gömbe: summer ~5–7 °C cooler than the coast; winter snow Dec–Mar → dry-months rule passes (May–Oct), but winter too cold for Nesli (guess).*
  - *Bezirgan / Kasaba (~700–800 m?): a middle way — cooler nights in summer, frost but little snow in winter (guess).*
- **Idea (fits our 6+6 rhythm):** *summer in a mountain village, winter on the coast — the locals do this ("yayla" life) (guess).*

## Land & legal
- **Kaş–Kekova Special Environmental Protection Area (ÖÇK):** declared **18 Jan 1990** (decision 90/77), ~**260 km²** — coast, islands and some inland villages. Plans + permits by the Ministry, not the town. [ÇŞB ÖÇK](https://ockb.csb.gov.tr/kas-kekova-ozel-cevre-koruma-bolgesi-i-2752)
- 2023 ministry 1/25,000 plan to open areas for building: **cancelled by court**. [search summary / Antalya Ekspres](https://www.antalyaekspres.com.tr/yargidan-kas-karari)
- ⚠️ **Aug 2026:** planning power for **1st-degree natural sites (~85,000 ha, Patara → Kekova)** moved from Kaş town to the Ministry; eco groups fear downgrades to 2nd/3rd degree and new hotels. [Batı Akdeniz TV, 2026-08-15](https://www.batiakdeniztv.com/kasta-devredilen-yetki-tartisma-yaratti-sit-alanlarinda-planlama-artik-bakanlikta)
- Archaeological sites everywhere (Phellos, Antiphellos, Patara, Kekova, Pirha near Bezirgan). *Every plot: ask the Antalya heritage board (koruma kurulu) (guess).*
- **Bağ evi on farmland:** since April 2026 plot must be **≥ 2 ha** (was 5 ha). [Sözcü](https://www.sozcu.com.tr/tarim-arazisinde-bungalov-yapma-sartlari-degisti-yeni-arazi-siniri-belli-oldu-p342136), [Hürriyet](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/bilgi/galeri/tarlaya-ev-yapma-sartlari-degisti-mi-bag-evi-sartlarinda-onemli-degisiklik-43262965). In Kasaba 2 ha ≈ *€340k at €17/m² (guess)* → too much; **our 1 ha plan → buy a plot with an existing legal house, or inside the village area (köy yerleşik alanı)** — *check with a lawyer*. [Turkey note](../regions/turkey.md)
- Van / yurt / tiny house as a home: ❌ not legal on farmland. At a Workaway host: ✅ offered.
- Buy **in Nesli's name** (foreigner farmland rule). [tarimhukuku.org](https://www.tarimhukuku.org/turkiyede-yabancilara-tarim-arazisi-satisi-mumkun-mu/)
