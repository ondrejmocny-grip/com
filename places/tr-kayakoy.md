- **Status:** ⭐ interesting (Ondrej: asked to research) · lead

## In short
A green, quiet valley under an empty Greek stone town. It is a **tourist stop by day** and calm at night. Newcomers (Dutch, British, Turkish artists) run restaurants, pensions, an art camp and yoga glamping. It is **15 minutes from Fethiye**, so all services are close. **But:** almost no young families, the old town is a protected archaeological site (no building), land is very expensive, and summers are hot (~35 °C).

## Village vibe
- ~500 ruined stone houses and two churches on the hill, protected by the state. Population exchange in 1923; the Turks who came settled on the flat land below. [Wikipedia EN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kayak%C3%B6y), [dergipark](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/download/article-file/3225)
- Tourism is "the most important economic factor". Tour groups, jeep safaris, quad bikes, horse rides. [Wikipedia EN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kayak%C3%B6y), [Daily Sabah, 2014](https://www.dailysabah.com/travel/2014/08/12/kayakoy-turkeys-famous-ghost-village-comes-to-life)
- Cafés and restaurants in old houses: Levissi Garden, Blue Butterfly, Totem, Jazibe (Sunday brunch + live music). [Daily Sabah, 2014](https://www.dailysabah.com/travel/2014/08/12/kayakoy-turkeys-famous-ghost-village-comes-to-life) *(12 years old — check which still exist)*
- Many holiday villas with pools (62 rental villas listed). [search summary / tatildekirala](https://www.tatildekirala.com/fethiye-kiralik-villa/kayakoy)
- 8 km old forest path to Fethiye; walks to Gemiler and Soğuksu bays. [Daily Sabah](https://www.dailysabah.com/travel/2014/08/12/kayakoy-turkeys-famous-ghost-village-comes-to-life)
- *Feel (guess): pretty, calm in winter, busy with day tourists in summer. More "holiday valley" than farming village.*

## Climate
- Fethiye normals: Jul–Aug max **35 °C**, min 22 °C. Jan max 16 °C, min 6 °C. Rain days: Dec–Jan ~10/month; May–Sep 1–3.5/month. [Wikipedia (NOAA)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fethiye)
- **6 dry months rule: ✅ Pass** (May–Oct: 30, 5, 1, 2, 18, 73 mm; 1–4 rain days). No snow.
- ⚠️ Nesli: "no strong heat". The dry months are the **hot** months (Jun–Sep ≥32 °C). *Kaya valley may be a bit cooler at night than the coast (guess).* Winter is mild but rainy.

## Land, zoning, legal
- **Old town = 1st-degree archaeological site since 1988.** No new building. [Daily Sabah, 2014](https://www.dailysabah.com/travel/2014/08/12/kayakoy-turkeys-famous-ghost-village-comes-to-life)
- 2014: state tendered one third of the old town for a **300-bed hotel** (49-year lease). Locals formed "Kayaköy Defense"; lawyers sued in Muğla court. [Hürriyet](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/ekonomi/tarihi-kayakoy-e-otel-projesine-tepki-28030683), [Wikipedia EN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kayak%C3%B6y). *Final result not found — ask locally.*
- Valley plain: *probably partly sit area and partly farmland (guess). Every plot needs a check at the Fethiye municipality + Muğla heritage board.*
- **Bağ evi (farm house) on farmland:** since April 2026 the plot must be **≥ 2 ha** (was 5 ha). [Sözcü](https://www.sozcu.com.tr/tarim-arazisinde-bungalov-yapma-sartlari-degisti-yeni-arazi-siniri-belli-oldu-p342136), [Hürriyet](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/bilgi/galeri/tarlaya-ev-yapma-sartlari-degisti-mi-bag-evi-sartlarinda-onemli-degisiklik-43262965). In Kayaköy 2 ha costs *~€1.5–2M (guess from prices below)* → not for us.
- Realistic path: **buy or rent an existing legal stone house** (tapu says house), or lease space at a licensed camp.
- Van / yurt / tiny house as a home: ❌ not legal on farmland. Van at a Workaway host / camp: ✅ offered. [Workaway](https://www.workaway.info/en/host/191487571833)
- Buy in **Nesli's name** (foreigner farmland rule). [tarimhukuku.org](https://www.tarimhukuku.org/turkiyede-yabancilara-tarim-arazisi-satisi-mumkun-mu/)

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ❌ illegal.
- Van / yurt on land: ❌ as a home; ✅ at Workaway hosts / camps.
- Wildfire: ⚠️ **high**. July 2026: fire near Göcek / Gökçeovacık, 10 homes evacuated in İnlice, 4 bungalows hit; 417 staff, 19 aircraft. [Hürriyet, 2026-07-22](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/gundem/son-dakika-fethiyede-orman-yangini-havadan-ve-karadan-mudahale-ediliyor-43247946), [AA](https://www.aa.com.tr/tr/gundem/fethiyede-cikan-orman-yanginina-mudahale-ediliyor/4005697). A fire on the Fethiye–Kayaköy road is also reported *(date not clear)*. [search summary / Milliyet](https://www.milliyet.com.tr/gundem/fethiyede-sehir-merkezine-yakin-noktada-orman-yangini-havadan-ve-karadan-mudahale-baslatildi-7650193). Pine forest all around.
- Expat bubble: ⚠️ medium (British / Dutch holiday owners). Mass tourism: ❌ **yes** — day trips + Ölüdeniz / Hisarönü 5 km.
