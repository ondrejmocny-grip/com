# Kaz Dağları south slope: Küçükkuyu / Ayvacık (Adatepe, Yeşilyurt, Assos)

- **Status:** lead
- **Country / region:** Turkey, Çanakkale province, Ayvacık district. South side of Kaz Dağları (Mount Ida), on the Gulf of Edremit. Hub: Küçükkuyu (~10,600 people, 2021). Villages around it: Adatepe, Yeşilyurt, Zeytinli, Mıhlı, Gökçetepe, Kozlu, Kayalar, Sazlı. Assos / Behramkale ~30–40 min west *(guess)*. Bayramiç (our benchmark) is on the north side of the same mountain.
- **Nearest town / airport:** Küçükkuyu (market town). Ayvacık 25 km. Çanakkale 95 km. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%BC%C3%A7%C3%BCkkuyu,_Ayvac%C4%B1k). Airport: Edremit Koca Seyit *(~40 min, guess)*, Çanakkale *(~1 h 30, guess)*. İstanbul by car *~5–6 h (guess)*.

**Órgiva pattern:** 7/8 points — ✅ 1 Küçükkuyu is a real town (olive oil, fishing, tourism), ✅ 2 city people (mostly Turkish, some foreign) settled here since the 1980s, ✅ 3 spread in many villages, ✅ 4 small projects (eco farms, yoga camps, bungalows, olive oil), ✅ 5 weekly market (Küçükkuyu Friday, Ayvacık Tuesday + Friday village market), ❌ 6 no alternative school found, ✅ 7 Mount Ida right above + sea, ✅ 8 easy start (TaTuTa, Workaway, camps).

## In short
The "sunny side" of Kaz Dağları. Villages like Adatepe and Yeşilyurt were almost empty in the 1980s. Then city people (intellectuals, white-collar workers) bought and restored the stone houses. Now many eco farms, yoga camps and bungalow places are spread around. It is the same scene as Bayramiç, but with **more mountain in view and the sea right below**. **Main doubts:** schooling, water in drought years, land prices, fire.

## People
- Adatepe was on the way to becoming a ghost village in the 1980s. A group of intellectuals bought houses and restored them. SİT (protected) status in 1989. [Biz Evde Yokuz](https://www.bizevdeyokuz.com/adatepe-koyu-kaz-daglari-gezilecek-yerler/)
- Yeşilyurt: same story, "white-collar people from big cities" brought it back to life. [search summary / Biz Evde Yokuz](https://www.bizevdeyokuz.com/kazdaglari-gezilecek-yerler/)
- Kaz Dağları is known as a place for "physical and spiritual cleansing": women's retreats, meditation, yoga, breathwork, permaculture courses. [Altınoluk camps page](https://www.altinolukcadirkamplar.org/kamp/kazdaglari-yoga/)
- Mix: mostly **Turkish newcomers** (İstanbul, İzmir). Some foreigners (e.g. a Swiss–Turkish family farming organic near Bayramiç, 2020). [Hürriyet, 2020-11-12](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/seyahat/galeri-isvicreyi-birakip-kazdaglarina-yerlestiler-cocuklarimiz-hic-olmadigi-kadar-mutlu-41660162)
- ⚠️ Part of the newcomers are **wealthy retirees** in concrete villas. Adatepe now draws "high-income families". [AA](https://www.aa.com.tr/tr/yasam/kazdaglarinda-kentlilerin-yasadigi-koy-/912318) *Grounded eco people and weekend villa owners live side by side (guess).*

## Integration
- Reports of friction: some newcomers built big concrete houses and were not tolerant of the villagers' animals. Some land deals caused disputes. [Paradigma Akademi PDF, 2022](https://www.paradigmaakademiyayinlari.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/kaz-daglari-ve-geleneksel-ekolojik-bilgi.pdf)
- Traditions alive: Yörük roots, olive harvest, olive oil mills, local herbs (thyme, sage). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C3%BC%C3%A7%C3%BCkkuyu,_Ayvac%C4%B1k), [Cumhuriyet, 2026-08-15](https://www.cumhuriyet.com.tr/yasam/sehirden-kacanlarin-yeni-siginagi-kazdaglari-nin-oksijen-sarhosu-yapan-koyleri-2529028)
- We speak Turkish → integration is much easier here than anywhere in Europe.

## How we could join
- Volunteer: TaTuTa farms in Kaz Dağları give food + bed for work. [search summary](https://www.ekoharita.org/project/bayramic-yenikoy-kazdaglari-ekolojik-yasam-ve-tohum-dernegi/), [wwoof.tr](https://wwoof.tr/tr/). Workaway hosts (see above).
- Nesli: yoga, cooking, gardening → fits the many retreat camps (seasonal work).
- Van: *parking on a friend's land or a camp is common and tolerated (guess).*
- Yurt / tiny house on farmland: ❌ not legal as a home (see [Turkey note](../regions/turkey.md)). Legal routes: a licensed camp / eco-tourism site, or a *bağ evi* permit.
- Land: buy **in Nesli's name** (foreigners need an approved farm project within 2 years). [tarimhukuku.org](https://www.tarimhukuku.org/turkiyede-yabancilara-tarim-arazisi-satisi-mumkun-mu/)

## Water (honest)
- Good side: Kaz Dağları is the water tower of the region. A spring-fed pipeline (Şarlak and Tilkeşdere springs) now brings drinking water to **14 Ayvacık villages**. [Çan'ın Sesi](https://www.caninsesi.com.tr/haber/28198616/kazdaglarinin-suyu-14-koyle-bulustu), [Çanakkale Bölge Haber](https://www.canakkalebolgehaber.com/kazdagilarindan-gelen-su-ayvacikin-14-koyune-hayat-verdi/13675)
- Bad side: 2024–25 was a severe drought in Çanakkale. Dec 2025: Bayramiç dam at **12% ("dead level")**, Atikhisar dam at 37%. Experts warned of cuts. [Vatan, 2025-12-02](https://www.gazetevatan.com/galeri/canakkale-bayramic-barajinda-su-bitmek-uzere-kesintiler-her-an-baslayabilir-su-seviyeleri-inanilmaz-derecede-dustu-2330261). A dam tracker showed Bayramiç at **10.8%** on 2026-09-27. [barajdolulukoranlari.com](https://barajdolulukoranlari.com/il/canakkale) *(unofficial site, verify)*
- Across the gulf (Edremit, Ayvalık) there were water cuts in Sept 2026. [politikam, 2026-09-24](https://www.politikam.com/balikesir-ayvalik-edremit-su-kesintisi-24-eylul-2026-sular-ne-zaman-gelecek)
- ⚠️ Gold mining near the Bayramiç dam (Koza Altın) worries locals. [Çan'ın Sesi](https://www.caninsesi.com.tr/haber/26603211/canakkale-barajlarda-doluluk-seviyeleri-alarm-veriyor)
- **Verdict:** better than most of the Aegean (mountain springs), but not safe. Only take land with an **own spring or stream that runs in September**.

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ❌ illegal in Turkey. Only public schools here (e.g. Küçükkuyu İlkokulu). [MEB](https://kucukkuyu.meb.k12.tr/). No Waldorf / BBOM school found in the area. Nearest BBOM school: İzmir-Bornova (~3 h, *guess*). [BBOM](https://www.baskabirokulmumkun.org/okullar/)
- Van / yurt on land: ❌ not legal as a home. Tolerated in camps *(guess)*.
- Wildfire: ⚠️ medium–high. Fires in Ayvacık in 2024 (small) and Aug 2025 (Çanakkale). 2026: ~500 ha fire in Havran (east Kaz Dağları). In 2025 the Kazdağı National Park (19,000 ha) was closed because of fire risk. [search summary / BirGün](https://www.birgun.net/haber/kaz-daglarinda-orman-yangini-kontrol-altina-alindi-536743), [Ekşi, 2025-08-11](https://eksisozluk.com/11-agustos-2025-canakkale-orman-yangini--8014876)
- Expat bubble: ✅ low (mostly Turks). Mass tourism: ⚠️ summer on the coast (Altınoluk, Küçükkuyu beaches). Adatepe/Yeşilyurt = day-trip tourism.
