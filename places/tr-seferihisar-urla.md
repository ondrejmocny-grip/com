## In short
The İzmir coast is where many city people "go rural" without leaving the city far behind. Seferihisar has a real eco-culture scene (Doğa Okulu, cittaslow). But in 2025 İzmir's main dam almost emptied, 13 districts had night water cuts, and huge fires hit İzmir.

## People and projects
- Orhanlı: largest village of Seferihisar (~1,150 people), spread out, olive groves. [Son Mühür](https://www.sonmuhur.com/izmire-55-kilometre-uzaklikta-zeytini-tas-evleri-ve-doga-okulu-ile-farkli-bir-koy)
- **Seferihisar Doğa Okulu** (since 2014): adult courses on traditional ecological knowledge — olive growing, water ecology, natural building. Built with the municipality, the village association, the Alakır River Brotherhood and many volunteers. Still active. [Doğa Derneği](https://dogadernegi.org/dogaokulu), [dogaaskina.org](https://dogaaskina.org/orhanli-koyu/)
- "Another farming is possible" movement in Seferihisar. [Son Dakika](https://www.sondakika.com/guncel/haber-seferihisar-da-baska-bir-tarim-mumkun-8520962/)
- Waldorf-based play groups reported in Güzelbahçe–Urla. [search summary / alternatifokullar](http://alternatifokullar.com/tag/waldorf-izmir/). BBOM Renkli Orman (kindergarten + primary) in Bornova. [BBOM](https://www.baskabirokulmumkun.org/okullar/)

## Water (honest) — main reason for dropping
- Tahtalı dam (İzmir's main source) active fill: **0.13% on 2025-12-30**, then 14.88% after rain. [İZSU](https://izsu.gov.tr/haberler/tahtali-yukselise-gecti-gece-kesintileri-sona-erdi), [İzmir BB](https://www.izmir.bel.tr/tr/Haberler/tahtali-yukselise-gecti-gece-kesintileri-sona-erdi/57821/156)
- Night water cuts (23:00–05:00) in **13 districts from 2025-08-06**, ended around Feb 2026. [same sources], [AA](https://www.aa.com.tr/tr/gundem/izmirde-11-ilcede-planli-ve-donusumlu-su-kesintisi-uygulanacak/3651271)
- 2025 was Turkey's "worst drought in 50 years"; rain −27% vs 30-year average. [Wikipedia](https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_T%C3%BCrkiye_su_krizi)

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ❌ illegal in Turkey. Alternative: BBOM (private, legal) in Bornova.
- Van / yurt on land: ❌ not legal as a home. A local site explains the tiny-house rules. [Seferihisar Bahçeleri](https://seferihisarbahceleri.com/blog/kucuk-evlerin-tiny-house-hukuki-statusu/)
- Wildfire: ❌ high. 2025: **26,260 ha burned in İzmir** (1 Jun–7 Jul), incl. fires in Seferihisar, Çeşme, Menderes. [Wikipedia 2025 fires](https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_T%C3%BCrkiye_orman_yang%C4%B1nlar%C4%B1)
- Expat bubble: ✅ low. Mass tourism: ⚠️ summer (Sığacık, Çeşme close).
