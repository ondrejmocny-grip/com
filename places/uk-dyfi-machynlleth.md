## In short
The "green capital" of Wales. CAT (since 1974) brought eco people for 50 years. Weekly Wednesday market. Real mountains and sea close. Wales has a unique law, **One Planet Development**, that lets people **legally live on a low-impact smallholding** in open countryside. **But:** UK visa is the blocker, climate is wet and cool, and Welsh language matters.

**Órgiva pattern:** 7/8 points — 1 market town ✅, 2 newcomers ✅, 3 spread out ✅, 4 projects ✅ (CAT, OPD smallholdings), 5 Wednesday market ✅, 7 mountains + sea ✅, 8 easy start ✅ (volunteering, OPD path). Weak: 6 alternative school ⚠️ *none found in search; home ed legal*.

## People
- Machynlleth = "a focus for green living", alternative and eco crowd, "art meets agriculture". [Visit Mid Wales / Wikipedia via search](https://www.midwalesmyway.com/machynlleth), [The Traveler](https://www.thetraveler.org/machynlleth-reimagined-waless-ancient-capital-goes-green/)
- CAT: eco centre, courses, gardens, since 1974. [Visit Mid Wales](https://www.visitmidwales.co.uk/things-to-do/centre-for-alternative-technology-group-visits-p1725111)
- OPD smallholding **Tir Heddwch** in the Dyfi valley (approved 2018); several more in Ceredigion (Llanddewi Brefi near Lampeter, Llanarth, Cardigan area). [One Planet Council](https://www.oneplanetcouncil.org.uk/approved-applications/)
- *Spiritual side smaller than Totnes; more practical eco people (guess).*

## Integration
- Strong Welsh culture. Owain Glyndŵr's parliament (1404). Welsh-medium school Ysgol Bro Ddyfi. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ysgol_Bro_Ddyfi)
- *Learning Welsh helps a lot with locals (guess).*

## How we could join
- Volunteer at CAT or on OPD smallholdings (visitor rules limit this; see country note).
- **One Planet Development (Wales only):** new home on open-country land if you meet strict tests — most of your basic needs/income from the land, own energy/water/waste, zero-carbon building, management plan. 68 such smallholdings in Wales (Jan 2025). [One Planet Council](https://www.oneplanetcouncil.org.uk/approved-applications/), [gov.wales TAN 6 guide](https://www.gov.wales/planning-permission-one-planet-developments-open-countryside), [Lammas](https://lammas.org.uk/en/planning/)
- *Hard work and years of paperwork; not a quick start (guess from sources).*

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ✅ legal in Wales (parents must give "suitable education"; LA can act if not). The 2026 Act covers England and Wales; register *earliest 2027*. [HSLDA Wales](https://hslda.org/post/wales), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Children%27s_Wellbeing_and_Schools_Act_2026)
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ planning permission, except via OPD.
- Wildfire risk: ✅ low *(guess)*.
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ✅ low; ⚠️ summer tourism on the coast.

## Water (honest view)
- ✅ *Very wet west Wales (~1,500+ mm/yr in hills, guess). Water is not a problem; mud and rain are.*

## Costs
