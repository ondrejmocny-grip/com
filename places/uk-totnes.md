# Totnes (South Devon, England)

- **Status:** lead — ⛔ **blocked by UK visa rules** (see [uk-ireland.md](../regions/uk-ireland.md))
- **Country / region:** UK, England, Devon, South Hams. Market town on the River Dart, *~9,000 people (guess)*. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Totnes)
- **Nearest town / airport:** Exeter airport *~45 min (guess)*. Torbay coast *~20–30 min (guess)*. Dartmoor next door.
- **Website / contacts:** [Transition Town Totnes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transition_town), [South Devon Steiner School](https://waldorfeducation.uk/waldorf-schools/independent-waldorf-schools/south-devon-steiner-school), [Totnes markets](https://www.totnestowncouncil.gov.uk/your-community/markets/)
- **Added:** 2026-09-27 by Claude (web research)

## In short
The classic "alternative town" of England. First Transition Town (2006). Steiner school since 1980, Schumacher College, Dartington. Market Friday + Saturday. Very close to the Órgiva pattern **in the people**. But: **no legal way for us to live in the UK** without a UK job sponsor, **house prices are very high**, and there are no real mountains.

**Órgiva pattern:** 6/8 points — 1 market town ✅, 2 many newcomers ✅, 3 spread around ✅ *(Dartington, villages)*, 4 many projects ✅, 5 weekly market ✅, 6 Steiner + families ✅. Weak: 7 sea ✅ but only hills (Dartmoor, 621 m) ⚠️, 8 easy start ❌ (visa, prices).

## People
- Known since the 1990s for "alternative" values: retreat centres, organic farming, mind/body therapy, peace work. [Christopher Titmuss](https://www.christophertitmussblog.org/a-transition-in-totnes)
- Transition Town Totnes founded 2006 by Rob Hopkins and Naresh Giangrande. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_Hopkins)
- Schumacher College: accredited courses closed Aug 2024; college taken over by the **Satish Kumar Foundation** in July 2025, short courses go on. [Dartington](https://www.dartington.org/dartingtons-schumacher-college-closes-academic-courses/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schumacher_College)
- ⚠️ *Some "wellness shop" and show-off side, plus rich incomers (guess).*

## Integration
- Real market town with local life. Market Fri + Sat 9–3, plus Sunday food market 1st and 3rd Sunday. [Visit Totnes](https://visittotnes.co.uk/eat-and-drink/food-markets/totnes-market-fridays-and-saturdays/)

## How we could join
- Visit only: up to 6 months as a visitor. Since 31 Jan 2024 **incidental** remote work for a foreign employer is allowed, but it must not be the main purpose. [Fragomen](https://www.fragomen.com/insights/2024-immigration-policy-changes-uk-visitors.html), [DavidsonMorris](https://www.davidsonmorris.com/remote-work-immigration-visa-rules/)
- Living here = a UK visa (see country note). *Realistic only if Ondrej gets a UK sponsored job (guess).*
- Van on farmland: English planning rules — *living in a caravan on land long-term needs planning permission (guess, not checked)*.

## Legal flags
- Home schooling: ✅ legal in England. New law (Children's Wellbeing and Schools Act 2026) adds a **register** + home visits, *in force earliest 2027*. [UK Parliament](https://www.parliament.uk/business/news/2026/march-2026/childrens-wellbeing-and-schools-bill-lords-consideration-of-commons-amendments/), [Home Ed Daily](https://www.thehomeeddaily.co.uk/Childrens-Wellbeing-and-Schools-Act-2026-what-happens-now)
- Van / yurt on land: ⚠️ planning permission.
- Wildfire risk: ✅ low *(guess; moorland fires happen on Dartmoor)*.
- Expat bubble / mass tourism: ⚠️ many incomers from cities; Devon summer tourism.

## Water (honest view)
- Rain ~900 mm/yr. [climate-data.org](https://en.climate-data.org/europe/united-kingdom/england/totnes-8641/)
- ⚠️ Summer droughts now hit South West England: South West Water hosepipe ban for Devon and Cornwall from 8 August; Environment Agency put the South West in drought after a record-dry July. *Year shown as 2026 in search results — verify.* [South West Water](https://www.southwestwater.co.uk/about-us/latest-news/south-west-water-announces-temporary-restrictions-for-hosepipe-use-across-the-region)
- Verdict: ✅ OK for house/garden, but not "always wet".

## Costs
- Average sold price in Totnes **~£427,000** (last 12 months, Rightmove, seen 2026-09-27). [Rightmove](https://www.rightmove.co.uk/house-prices/totnes.html)
- ❌ Far above our budget for house + land.
