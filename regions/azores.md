# Azores (Açores), Portugal

- **Updated:** 2026-09-28 by Claude (web research). Prices are listing snapshots from this date. *Italic* = guess or not checked. Verify before acting.
- Part of Portugal and the EU. Autonomous region with its **own government and some own laws** (education, tourism, land planning). 9 islands, ~1,400 km west of Lisbon.
- Place files: [Capelas / north-west São Miguel](../places/pt-azores-capelas-sao-miguel.md) · [Povoação / Nordeste – east São Miguel](../places/pt-azores-povoacao-nordeste.md) · [Pico + Faial](../places/pt-azores-pico-faial.md)

## Short answer (for the busy reader)

- ✅ **Very mild**: 12–26 °C all year. No cold, no strong heat. Best temperature fit for Nesli of all regions so far.
- ✅ **Sea + real mountains everywhere.** Pico mountain 2,351 m. Green, water-rich.
- ✅ **Kind, traditional locals.** Holy Spirit festivals (*Festas do Espírito Santo*) in every parish.
- ✅ **One very good school:** Novas Rotas, a free public "learning community" school (60 kids).
- ❌ **Climate fails our "6 dry months" rule.** Rain on 5–15 days every month. Humidity ~80% all year. Mould in houses in winter.
- ❌ **Small alternative scene.** No Órgiva-like hub. Scattered farms, a few Workaway hosts, one ecovillage idea (Fruit Garden).
- ❌ **São Miguel housing crisis:** rents +50% in one year (avg €1,200/month, May 2026).
- ⚠️ **Far away:** no direct flights to Prague or Istanbul. Ryanair left in March 2026.
- ⚠️ **Earthquakes / volcanoes:** Terceira and São Jorge had seismic crises (2022–2026).

### Locals and traditions
- **Festas do Espírito Santo** (May–July, from Pentecost): every parish has an *Império* (small chapel). Free **Holy Spirit soups** for everyone, volunteers, street decoration. Roots in the 13th century. [byacores](https://byacores.com/en/festas-do-divino-espirito-santo-nos-acores-tradicao-fe-e-cultura-acoriana-en/), [Cultura Açores](http://www.culturacores.azores.gov.pt/ea/pesquisa/Default.aspx?id=8535)
- Strong village life: bullfights on a rope (*touradas à corda*, Terceira), whaling heritage (Pico), dairy farming, wine (Pico, UNESCO). [Wikipedia Madalena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madalena,_Azores) *Touradas: common knowledge, not checked.*
- **Locals:** described as friendly and close-knit. In small places "integration happens on Azorean terms, in Portuguese, at Azorean pace". [portugalist São Miguel](https://www.portugalist.com/place/the-azores/azores-living/), [portugalist Pico](https://www.portugalist.com/place/the-azores/pico/)
- **Language:** the São Miguel accent is "hard even for people from Lisbon". Doctors, offices and builders need Portuguese. [portugalist](https://www.portugalist.com/place/the-azores/azores-living/)
- **Expat bubble:** ✅ low. Foreigners are only **3.8%** of people (2025). Biggest groups are Brazilian and Cape Verdean workers; Germans 591, Americans 626. [Açoriano Oriental 2026](https://www.acorianooriental.pt/noticia/numero-de-imigrantes-a-viver-nos-acores-mais-do-que-duplica-em-cinco-anos-379274)
- **Mass tourism:** ⚠️ growing on São Miguel (Sete Cidades, Furnas, Ponta Delgada). Low on the other islands. *(guess)*

## Families and schooling

### Novas Rotas (public alternative school) — ✅ active in 2026
- **What:** a public "learning community" inside the public school group **EBI de Capelas** (Ponta Delgada municipality). No classes and no fixed subjects; kids learn through projects, in mixed-age groups, at their own pace. Inspired by Escola da Ponte (Porto) and Projeto Âncora (Brazil); pedagogical guide José Pacheco. UNESCO Associated School. [novasrotaseducacao](https://novasrotaseducacao.wordpress.com/), [Açoriano Oriental 2019-09-23](https://www.acorianooriental.pt/noticia/projeto-novas-rotas-envolve-a-comunidade-na-construcao-da-escola-em-sao-miguel-302978)
- **Where:** Edifício Fábrica das Artes, Rua do Monte Alegre, 9545-148 Vila de Capelas. Tel. 296 298 134. [Eco-Escolas](https://ecoescolas.abaae.pt/escola/projeto-novas-rotas/) Email: projetonovasrotas@gmail.com
- **Ages:** pre-school (3+) to 2nd cycle (~12). [Eco-Escolas](https://ecoescolas.abaae.pt/escola/projeto-novas-rotas/) *After 12: normal public school (guess).*
- **Size:** 38 kids (2018/19) → 59 kids, 43 families, 8 tutors (2022/23) → **60 kids** (19 pre-school, 26 "Initiation", 15 "Autonomy") in the current school plan; 9 teachers in 2024/25. [NEB prize page](https://prizes.new-european-bauhaus.europa.eu/node/364184), [EBI Capelas school plan 2023–26](https://ebic.edu.azores.gov.pt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Plano-de-Escola-2023-2026-2.o-Ano-.pdf) (via search summary)

### Home schooling in the Azores — ✅ legal, ⚠️ details not fully checked
- National rule (Portaria 69/2019): *ensino doméstico* is legal. Enrol at the school of your area, sign a protocol with it, exams at the end of each cycle. The teaching parent (*responsável educativo*) needs **at least a licenciatura (bachelor's degree)**. [DGE](https://www.dge.mec.pt/ensino-individual-e-ensino-domestico), [Portaria 69/2019](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/portaria/69-2019-120272926), [CGD summary](https://www.cgd.pt/Site/Saldo-Positivo/formacao-e-tecnologia/Pages/ensino-individual-e-ensino-domestico.aspx) → this confirms the degree rule flagged in [portugal.md](portugal.md).
- **Azores:** education is a regional power. Student rules are in the regional **RGAPA, Portaria 78/2023** (29 Aug 2023). [SPRA](https://www.spra.pt/regulamento-de-gestao-administrativa-e-pedagogica-de-alunos-portaria-n-o-78-2023-de-29-de-agosto-de-2023/), [edu.azores.gov.pt PDF](https://edu.azores.gov.pt/wp-content/uploads/2017/04/Portaria-n.o-78-2023-29-agosto.pdf) *I could not read the PDF text. I found no separate regional home-school law. Guess: the Azores apply the same model (school enrolment + degree + exams). Ask the Direção Regional da Educação or EBI Capelas.*
- **Home-school groups:** none specific to the Azores found. National: [ANPED](https://anped.pt/ensino-domestico/), [MEL](https://educacaolivre.pt/). The FB group "São Miguel Azores Expat Immigrant Families with children" is the best local start.

## Law and practical

- **Residency / visa for us:** same as mainland Portugal (national law, AIMA). Ondrej registers at the Câmara; Nesli applies as spouse of an EU citizen. See [legal overview](legal-overview.md#portugal). *AIMA has offices in Ponta Delgada (guess).*
- **Living in a van — legal? Where?** National rules apply: IMT-approved motorhomes may stay overnight up to **48 h** in one municipality, unless local rules say no. **Forbidden in protected areas and Natura 2000** (fines €120–600). [Montepio](https://www.montepio.org/ei/pessoal/gestao-diaria/autocaravanas-regras-da-pernoita-e-do-estacionamento/), [Código da Estrada art. 50.º-A](https://www.segurancarodoviaria.pt/codigo-da-estrada/titulo-ii-do-transito-de-veiculos-e-animais/capitulo-i-disposic%C3%B5es-comuns/sec%C3%A7%C3%A3o-v-algumas-manobras-em-especial/subsec%C3%A7%C3%A3o-vi-paragem-e-estacionamento/artigo-50o-a-proibicao-de-pernoita-e-aparcamento-de-autocaravanas/) ⚠️ **In the Azores a big part of each island is a protected area** (Natural Parks). *So wild van stays are harder than on the mainland (guess).* Campsites exist on São Miguel. [byacores camping](https://byacores.com/acampar-sao-miguel/)
  - ⚠️ **Getting our van there:** only by **cargo ship** from Lisbon. *Costly (~€1,000–2,000 one way, guess, not checked).* The passenger/car ferry to the mainland does not exist. [portugalist Santa Maria](https://www.portugalist.com/place/the-azores/santa-maria/)
  - Living in a van on your own rural land: same problem as mainland (needs a licence; PDM decides). [legal overview §3](legal-overview.md)
- **Yurt / tiny house on rural land:** counts as a building → needs council licence. One family on São Miguel lives off-grid in a self-built **tiny house on wheels** (2021). Legal status not mentioned. [pureportugal](https://pureportugal.co.uk/blogs/exploring-alternatives-in-azores/)
- **Building on rustic land:** each island has its own PDM (e.g. [PDM Povoação](https://portal.azores.gov.pt/en/web/draac/aae-pdm-povoacao), [PDM Nordeste](https://ot.azores.gov.pt/Instrumentos-de-Gestao-Territorial-Documento.aspx?id=73)). A search summary says some PDMs need **5,000 m² for a house** and **1,400 m²** for a farm building. *Which municipality this is from is not clear. Verify.* Farm-linked housing and **rural tourism** can be allowed on rustic land. *The Azores have their own land-planning law (guess: DLR 35/2012/A); it is not sure the mainland DL 117/2024 applies. Ask for a PIP first.* [search summary](https://ot.azores.gov.pt/ot/38/ordenamento-territorio-urbanismo)
- **Rural tourism / Alojamento Local (AL):**
  - Azores have their own AL rules: **Portaria 83/2016**. Register at the Câmara → inspection → Regional Tourism Directorate gives the number. [azoreanliving 2025-06](https://azoreanliving.com/licenciamento-alojamento-local/), [alesclarecimentos](https://www.alesclarecimentos.pt/a%C3%A7ores/)
  - ⚠️ **New limits coming:** a draft regional law (2026) wants a **ratio of AL units to homes** by zone, and incentives to turn AL back into long-term rentals. The architects' order sent its opinion on 9 April 2026. Not yet law. [Ordem dos Arquitectos 2026-05-15](https://www.ordemdosarquitectos.org/sr_acores/noticias/srazo_007)
  - *Guess: limits will hit Ponta Delgada and busy towns first, not rural parishes. Rural tourism (TER) is a different licence.*
- **Buying land as a foreigner:** no limits for EU citizens. *Most rural land is sold off-market or via local agents; few big plots online (guess, from listing search).*

[search summary / climate-data](https://en.climate-data.org/europe/portugal/sao-miguel/sao-miguel-290075/), [EGU landslides study](https://presentations.copernicus.org/EGU23/EGU23-445_presentation.pdf) The rural east (Furnas, Nordeste) is "beautiful but damp". [climatestotravel](https://www.climatestotravel.com/climate/azores/santa-maria) Hurricane **Lorenzo (Oct 2019)** destroyed the port of Lajes das Flores, ~€330 M damage. [Instituto Hidrográfico](https://www.hidrografico.pt/en/infoteca/special-misoes/furacao-lorenzo/), [Wikipedia list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Azores_hurricanes)
- **Mould / damp houses:** humidity rarely under 75%. Winter mould on walls and behind furniture in uninsulated stone houses. Advice: **rent through one winter before buying**. [portugalist](https://www.portugalist.com/place/the-azores/azores-living/) Dehumidifiers raise power bills. [portugal.com](https://www.portugal.com/moving-to-portugal/humidity-in-portugal-how-to-solve-humidity-issues-in-portuguese-houses/)

**Honest summary:** temperature is perfect, but the Azores are **green because they are wet**. Summer (Jun–Aug) is fine. May, September and October are showery. Winter is grey, windy and damp, not cold.

### Water
- ✅ Mostly plenty. Rain all year, springs, crater lakes.
- ⚠️ But "occasional shortages on almost all islands" now appear. **Graciosa and Pico** have the biggest problems. São Jorge has water but lacks storage for summer. [jornalacores9 2026-07-08](https://jornalacores9.pt/2026/07/08/proposta-sobre-recursos-hidricos-suscita-debate-sobre-falta-de-agua-nos-acores/), [Açoriano Oriental](https://www.acorianooriental.pt/noticia/proposta-sobre-recursos-hidricos-suscita-debate-sobre-falta-de-agua-378758)
- Regional government approved a **drought and water-scarcity plan** (PSE-Açores) in Sept 2025. [Diário dos Açores 2025-09-18](https://diariodosacores.pt/2025/09/18/governo-aprova-plano-regional-para-gerir-secas-e-escassez-de-agua-nos-acores-e-cria-comissao-de-resposta-rapida/)
- Storms can break water supply on Pico. [RTP Açores](https://acores.rtp.pt/local/mau-tempo-provoca-avarias-no-abastecimento-de-agua-na-ilha-do-pico/)
- *Pico's young lava soil is very porous → few rivers, many houses use rain cisterns (guess, well known but not checked).*
- *Some crater lakes on São Miguel (Sete Cidades, Furnas) have algae problems from cattle farming (guess, not checked in this round).*

### Wildfire — ✅ low
- *Wet, green islands; no big wildfire found in searches (2026-09-28). Guess: lowest risk of all our regions.* Fires are monitored; farmers' burning is the main cause. [Agroportal](https://www.agroportal.pt/incendios-federacao-de-bombeiros-dos-acores-pede-prudencia-na-realizacao-de-queimadas/)

### Earthquakes and volcanoes — ⚠️ FLAG
- The Azores sit on a triple plate junction. 2025: **20,000+ quakes**, 148 felt. [Portugal News 2026-03-16](https://www.theportugalnews.com/news/2026-03-16/seismic-volcanic-warning-issued-in-the-azores/991266)
- **Terceira:** seismic-volcanic crisis at Santa Bárbara volcano since **June 2022**. Alert V3 in Nov 2025, back to **V2 in March 2026**. Eruption "not likely soon"; small steam explosions possible. [Portugal News 2026-03](https://www.theportugalnews.com/news/2026-03-16/seismic-volcanic-warning-issued-in-the-azores/991266), [Portugal News 2025-11](https://www.theportugalnews.com/news/2025-11-07/azores-volcanic-alert-rises/913991)
- **São Jorge:** crisis March 2022, 40,000+ quakes, magma dike, no eruption. [NASA](https://appliedsciences.nasa.gov/what-we-do/disasters/disasters-activations/sao-jorge-seismic-activity-2022), [ADS](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2022AGUFMNH13A..02F/abstract)
- **Faial/Pico:** 1998 quake Mw 6.2, 9 deaths, 5,000+ people affected. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1998_Azores_Islands_earthquake)
- **São Miguel:** three active volcanoes (Sete Cidades, Fogo, Furnas). [climate-data search summary](https://en.climate-data.org/europe/portugal/sao-miguel/sao-miguel-290075/) *Furnas last erupted 1630 (guess). Landslides after heavy rain are a real local risk.* [EGU 2023](https://presentations.copernicus.org/EGU23/EGU23-445_presentation.pdf)
- *For us: build/buy with anti-seismic standards; avoid steep slopes and cliff-foot fajãs (guess).*

### Sea + mountains — ✅✅
- Every island: ocean all around, volcanic mountains in view. São Miguel: Pico da Vara ~1,100 m, Sete Cidades and Fogo craters. Pico: 2,351 m (highest in Portugal). [casa.sapo Nordeste listing text](https://casa.sapo.pt/comprar-terrenos/mais-baratos/nordeste/), [Wikipedia Madalena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madalena,_Azores)
- Sea is cool for swimming: 17–23.5 °C. [climatestotravel](https://www.climatestotravel.com/climate/azores/santa-maria)

Ferries (Atlânticoline): **Horta–Madalena all year** (3–4/day in winter, ~30 min), Velas (São Jorge) linked too; the other islands only in summer. Private CUF hospital in Lagoa (São Miguel). Pico, São Jorge, Flores, Santa Maria: health centres only; serious cases fly out. [Observador 2025-02](https://observador.pt/2025/02/11/urgencia-do-hospital-de-ponta-delgada-podia-ter-reaberto-em-julho-de-2024-diz-relatorio-interno/), [portugalist](https://www.portugalist.com/place/the-azores/azores-living/) *Birth with a planned child: best on São Miguel, Terceira or Faial (guess).*

[azoresgetaways](https://azoresgetaways.com/en-us/destination/azores/general-articles/digital-nomads-remote-azores-islands), [chilleddigitalnomad](https://chilleddigitalnomad.com/the-ultimate-digital-nomad-guide-to-the-azores-portugal/) ✅ fine for Ondrej's job.

- **Time zone:** UTC−1 (one hour behind Lisbon, 2 h behind Prague). *(common knowledge)*
