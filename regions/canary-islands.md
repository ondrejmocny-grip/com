# Canary Islands (Spain)

- **Updated:** 2026-09-27 by Claude (web research). No visits yet. *Italic* = guess or not checked. Prices and laws change: check again before acting.

## General vibe
- **Spiritual / alternative scene:** yes, but small and spread over islands. Strongest clusters:
  - **La Gomera – Valle Gran Rey:** old hippie hub since the late 1960s/70s. Meditation centres, drumming at sunset, craft market. [Irish Examiner](https://www.irishexaminer.com/lifestyle/outdoors/damien-enright/where-hippie-ambience-and-vacationers-expectations-collide-467602.html), [canarianfeeling](https://www.canarianfeeling.com/canary-island-la-gomera/cities-and-communities)
  - **La Palma – northwest (Puntagorda, Tijarafe, Garafía):** many Germans and other Europeans, organic farms, permaculture (Finca Autarca / Matricultura since 2007), weekend farmers' market. [eldiario.es 2021](https://www.eldiario.es/canariasahora/lapalmaahora/sociedad/puntagorda-municipio-palma-mayor-porcentaje-residentes-extranjeros_1_8293120.html), [matricultura.org](http://matricultura.org/index_E.html)
  - **Tenerife north (La Laguna, Tegueste, Tacoronte, La Orotava):** most alternative schools in the islands, an eco food co-op, several permaculture farms. [ludus.org.es – Canarias](https://ludus.org.es/es/projects?autonomy_id=5), [La Gangochera](https://lagangochera.wordpress.com/nuestra-comunidad/)
  - **Gran Canaria interior/north:** some yoga retreats (Teror), a Waldorf school in Arucas. [La Tejana](https://latejana.es/en), [waldorf islas canarias](https://waldorfislascanarias.wixsite.com/escuelas/gran-canaria)
  - **El Hierro:** very small scene. PapaGaia permaculture school; "100% renewable island" project. [Estatefy](https://www.estatefy.com/canary-islands/what-is-life-like-on-el-hierro-for-foreigners), [Gorona del Viento](https://www.goronadelviento.es/en/)
- **How open are locals:** *Generally warm, but tired of mass tourism and high rents (guess, based on the protests below).* On El Hierro, a study says most islanders welcome newcomers. [ScienceDirect 2025](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666957925000060)
- **Big social topic 2024–26: tourism and housing.**
  - 20 April 2024: ~200,000 people marched under "Canarias tiene un límite" (the Canaries have a limit). [Taylor & Francis study](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/14742837.2025.2512718), [Canarian Weekly](https://www.canarianweekly.com/posts/2024-in-the-Canary-Islands-Protests-housing-crisis-and-tragedies-at-sea)
  - May 2025: new protests, while tourist numbers set records. [Euronews 2025-05-16](https://www.euronews.com/travel/2025/05/16/inequality-and-infinite-growth-canary-islands-anti-tourism-protests-reignite-amid-record-a)
  - Tenerife has 5,000+ vehicles used as homes. Workers live in vans because rent is too high. [Diario de Avisos 2025-08](https://diariodeavisos.elespanol.com/2025/08/el-uso-de-autocaravanas-como-viviendas-ya-suma-un-municipio-mas-a-tenerife-el-32/), [Diario de Avisos 2026-05](https://diariodeavisos.elespanol.com/2026/05/trabajadores-tenerife-sur-autocaravanas/)
  - **For us:** newcomers who buy or rent may be seen as part of the problem. *Being useful to the village and living there all year (not as a tourist) will matter (guess).*

**Water is the main question for all Canaries.** In 2024 five or more islands were in water emergency at the same time. Our deal-breaker is "drought, dryness or water shortage". Only La Palma (north) and La Gomera (north) have good natural water, and even they had dry years. *Needs a check on site: does the plot have water rights (acción de agua / comunidad de regantes)?*

## Law and practical
- **Residency / visa for us:** same as mainland Spain (EU family route, form EX-19 for Nesli). See [legal overview](legal-overview.md#spain-incl-canary-islands).
- **Home schooling:** ⚠️ **grey zone, same as Spain.** Spain's Constitutional Court (STC 133/2010) said home schooling is not a protected right. Families risk "absentismo" cases with social services; many cases get dismissed. [CEPC](https://www.cepc.gob.es/publicaciones/revistas/revista-espanola-de-derecho-constitucional/numero-94-eneroabril-2012/ideario-educativo-constitucional-y-homeschooling-proposito-de-la-sentencia-del-tribunal-2), [homeschooling.es](https://www.homeschooling.es/denuncia-por-educar-en-casa-o-hacer-homeschooling-en-espana/), [legal overview](legal-overview.md#2-home-schooling)
  - Most private alternative schools in the islands are *not officially approved (guess, typical for "escuelas libres" in Spain)*. Check each one.
- **Living in a van — legal? Where?**
  - Wild camping is **forbidden** on Tenerife. Free Cabildo camping zones: max **7 days**, online permit. [Cabildo de Tenerife](https://www.tenerife.es/zonas-de-acampada), [AJJ Vans](https://www.ajjvanscanarias.com/en/is-wild-camping-legal-in-tenerife-complete-guide-2025/)
  - New Canary decree on campings and motorhomes: draft June 2025. It bans all "free camping" for tourist use; parking in normal allowed places is OK if you don't set up (no blocks, no dumping). Still **not in force** in July 2026. [Canarias7 2025-06](https://www.canarias7.es/politica/canarias-regula-caravanas-norma-gusta-acaba-campo-20250620195938-nt.html), [Canarias7 2026-07](https://www.canarias7.es/economia/turismo/decreto-caravanas-elimina-acampada-libre-canarias-exige-20260723060000-nt.html)
  - "Camping" (not just parking) fines: €500–5,000 (Tenerife). [Diario de Avisos 2025-08](https://diariodeavisos.elespanol.com/2025/08/el-uso-de-autocaravanas-como-viviendas-ya-suma-un-municipio-mas-a-tenerife-el-32/) Beaches: Ley de Costas, min €40 per m² per day. [Diario de Avisos 2026-08](https://diariodeavisos.elespanol.com/2026/08/la-dgt-dormir-autocarava-frente-a-playa-en-canarias/)
  - Van-dwellers are already a political issue (housing crisis). Valle Gran Rey police evicted ~20 beach campers in March 2021. [lagomera1 blog](https://lagomera1.blogspot.com/2021/03/campers-evicted-in-valle-gran-rey.html)
  - **For us:** the van works for travel and short stays. For months, *park on the land of a host/project with their consent (grey zone), or rent a room (guess)*.
- **Yurt / tiny house / glamping on rural land — allowed?** Ley 4/2017 (Canary land law). A house on rustic land only in **asentamiento rural** (rural settlement) zones, or when needed for a real farm. Protected landscape land: no. Every build needs a council licence. [BOE Ley 4/2017](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2017-10295), [Agroingenia](https://agroingeniacanarias.com/construir-en-suelo-rustico-ley-del_suelo_de_2017/) *A yurt that stays is treated as a building (guess, same as mainland Spain).*
- **Holiday rentals / retreat business:** Ley 6/2025 (in force 12 Dec 2025, changed by Ley 7/2026 of 31 Aug 2026): holiday flats max 10% of homes per town (20% on La Palma, La Gomera, El Hierro). **Holiday rental on rustic land is banned** except special cases in the local plan. [BOE Ley 6/2025](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2025-26358), [inmomashome](https://inmomashome.com/blog/nueva-ley-vivienda-vacacional-canarias-2025), [alquilerviviendavacacional.com](https://alquilerviviendavacacional.com/canarias-100-preguntas-y-respuestas-sobre-la-ley-6-2025-de-10-de-diciembre-de-ordenacion-sostenible-del-uso-turistico-de-viviendas/) *Licensed rural tourism (casa rural / hotel rural) is a separate licence — check it. (guess)* ⚠️ This matters for our "own small retreat" plan.
- **Renovating a ruin / stone house — rules?** Same Ley 4/2017 + town plan (PGO). *An old legal house in an asentamiento rural is the safest buy (guess).*
- **Buying land as foreigner:** no special limits for EU citizens *(guess, same as Spain)*.

## Money
- **Rural land price:** Canary rustic land is the **most expensive in Spain**: €148,415/ha average in 2024 (14× Spain's average). Tenerife €157,491/ha. Bananas push it up. [Cocampo](https://www.cocampo.com/es/es/noticias/precio-de-suelo-rustico-en-canarias/), [MAPA](https://www.mapa.gob.es/es/prensa/ultimas-noticias/detalle_noticias/el-precio-medio-de-la-tierra-mantiene-su-tendencia-al-alza--con-un-aumento-del-2-8---en-2024-/ec377f05-ac29-4fc9-9b99-915afb7b9387)
  - Real asking prices, La Palma NW (2026-09-27): 1.1 ha with irrigation €145k; 1 ha + 70 m² house + water tank €235k; 5.6 ha + 50 m² ruin €260k (+3% agency). [la-palma24.es](https://www.la-palma24.es/property-type/finca-agricoa/)
  - **For us:** 1 ha is at the top of our budget (~€180–190k loan + savings), or above. Leasing first is the real option. *(guess)*
- **Rent:** Canary average €14.72/m² (Apr 2025), ~€1,100 for 80 m². Capitals ~€1,050–1,170. [Radio Las Palmas 2025-05](https://www.radiolaspalmas.com/economia/2025/05/19/el-precio-del-alquiler-en-canarias-supera-los-1-100-euros-de-media/n12825.html) Small islands cheaper: La Gomera west from ~€475. [idealista](https://www.idealista.com/en/alquiler-viviendas/santa-cruz-de-tenerife/la-gomera/vallehermoso-valle-gran-rey/)
- **Monthly living cost:** *Food and imported goods cost more on small islands; lower VAT (IGIC) helps. Guess: similar to mainland Spain.*

## Climate & nature
- **Best climate we have seen so far.** No real winter at low and middle heights; summer rarely very hot in the north (trade winds). This fits Nesli's "no cold, no strong heat". [Climates to Travel](https://www.climatestotravel.com/climate/canary-islands/tenerife)
- Hot days come with Saharan air (calima). Sep 2026: fire alerts on 5 islands during a heat wave. [Canarias7 2026-09](https://www.canarias7.es/canarias/gobierno-declara-alerta-incendios-forestales-gran-canaria-20260922180645-nt.html)
- Water and fire: see the table. Fire is mostly in pine forest at mid height.
- Volcanoes: La Palma (2021), El Hierro (2011), Tenerife (unrest, green alert).
