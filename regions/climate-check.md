# Climate Check: "6 dry, snow-free months" rule

> Checked: 2026-09-27 by Claude (web research). Rule from Ondrej and Nesli: wet or snowy is OK only if there are ~6 months each year that are not wet and snowy.
> Method: "dry month" = under ~50 mm rain and ≤ ~6 rain days, no snow. **Pass** = ≥5 dry months in a row + 1 in-between. **Borderline** = ~4 dry months. **Fail** = ≤3.

## Key insight: two of our rules pull against each other

- **"6 dry months"** favours dry-summer places (south Spain, Canaries, Aegean Turkey).
- **"No drought / reliable water"** favours wet places (north Spain, Minho, Ireland) — which fail the dry-months rule.
- **Sweet spot:** a place with a **long dry summer** but **water from mountains** (snowmelt, springs, old irrigation channels) — e.g. Alpujarra (Órgiva, La Taha), Serra de Monchique, Sierra de Aracena. There the **plot's own water source** (spring, legal well, acequia rights, cistern) decides everything.
