# France (south: Drôme, Cévennes, Pyrenees, Aude)

- **Updated:** 2026-09-27 by Claude (web research). *Italic* = guess or not checked. Short note only.

## General vibe
- Strong **"néo-ruraux"** tradition since 1968: city people moving to empty mountain villages. Mostly **French** people, fewer foreigners than Órgiva. So less expat bubble, but **French is a must**.
- Scene is more **eco / political / simple living** than New Age. *Spiritual side exists but is quieter (guess).*
- Locals: long history of mixing. Some tension (land prices, Legion land case in the Cévennes). [CQFD 2022](https://cqfd-journal.org/Cevennes-tiens-voila-du-bidasse)

## Law and practical

### Home schooling (⚠️)
- Since school year **2022–23** home schooling (*instruction en famille*, IEF) needs a **yearly authorisation**. Before, a simple declaration was enough. [Cour des comptes, 2025-06](https://www.ccomptes.fr/sites/default/files/2025-06/20250626-S2025-0795-Instruction-dans-la-famille.pdf)
- Apply **1 March – 31 May** before the school year. Allowed reasons: child's health, disability, intensive sport/art, **family travelling (itinérance) in France**, too far from a school, or a "situation specific to the child". [service-public.gouv.fr](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/R61887)
- Refusal rate: 11% (2022–23), 23% (2024–25). 2025–26: 79% accepted. Refusals are mostly for the "specific situation" reason. [Cour des comptes](https://www.ccomptes.fr/sites/default/files/2025-06/20250626-S2025-0795-Instruction-dans-la-famille.pdf), [Akademos](https://www.akademos.app/blog/ief-autorisation-guide-2025)
- School is compulsory from age **3** *(since 2019; guess from memory, verify)*.
- ✅ **Private "hors contrat" schools** (Steiner, Montessori, nature schools) are legal and common in all our areas.
- *Our 6 months travel: "itinérance" only covers travel inside France. Travelling abroad with a school-age child needs another solution (CNED distance school, or the Czech system). Guess, verify.*

### Residency for us
- Ondrej (EU): no permit needed. Must have work/income and health insurance *(EU rule, see [legal overview](legal-overview.md))*.
- Wife (Turkish, spouse of EU citizen): card **"membre de famille d'un citoyen de l'UE"**. Apply online on **ANEF** after arrival, within 3 months. Free, **5 years**, work allowed. Permanent after 5 years. [demarchesetrangers.fr](https://demarchesetrangers.fr/titre-de-sejour/conjoint-membre-famille-citoyen-ue), [Documents list (interieur.gouv.fr)](https://administration-etrangers-en-france.interieur.gouv.fr/particuliers/assets/docs/Liste_Justificatifs_MembreFamilleCitoyenUE.pdf), [Your Europe](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/residence/documents-formalities/non-eu-family-members-residence-card/index_fr.htm)

### Van / yurt on rural land
- A van, caravan or yurt on private land: **free up to 3 months per year** (total). Longer → declaration or permit, and usually only on land where the town plan (PLU) allows it. Permanent light housing in farm/nature zones only in special zones (**STECAL**, loi ALUR). [construires.fr](https://www.construires.fr/mobil-home-terrain-prive-2026-loi-alur-pastilles/), [SeLoger](https://edito.seloger.com/conseils-d-experts/reglementations/autorisations-d-urbanisme-installer-une-caravane-propriete)
- Many mayors are still wary of yurts and tiny houses. [La Gazette des communes](https://www.lagazettedescommunes.com/765818/en-plein-essor-tiny-houses-et-yourtes-doivent-encore-convaincre-les-elus/)

## Money
- Farm land is cheap by EU standards: Occitanie average **€7,260/ha** (2025). Drôme rising (+6%). [Réussir](https://www.reussir.fr/quel-prix-des-terres-agricoles-2025-par-departement)
- *House to restore + 1 ha: Ariège ~€80–180k; Aude ~€100–250k; Cévennes / Drôme ~€200–400k. Guesses.*
- ✅ Tip: **SAFER** has a pre-emption right on farm land sales. *Terre de Liens* buys farms and leases them to farmers. [Jardin d'Églantine (Chant'Arize on a Terre de Liens farm)](https://jardindeglantine.fr/ecole-chant-arize-steiner-waldorf-a-campagne-sur-arize-fonctionnement-et-tarifs/)

## Climate & nature (honest)
- **Water:** the whole French south now has **summer restrictions** most years. Worst: Pyrénées-Orientales (restrictions since 2022). Gard/Cévennes: crisis + drinking-water cuts Aug 2026. Drôme: alerte renforcée July 2026. Ariège/Couserans: wettest (~1,000–1,100 mm/yr), but crisis on the Salat in Oct 2023. Sources in each place file.
- **Fire:** Aug 2025 Corbières (Aude) fire, 17,000 ha. [Wikipedia FR](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incendie_du_massif_des_Corbi%C3%A8res_de_2025)
- **Cold:** the wetter places (Ariège, Diois) have real winters (Saint-Girons ~55 frost days/yr). [fr.wikipedia](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Girons_(Ari%C3%A8ge))
- **Rule of thumb:** wetter = colder and farther from the sea. The places near the sea are dry.
