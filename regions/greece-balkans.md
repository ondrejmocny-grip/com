# Greece and the Balkans (short note)

- **Updated:** 2026-09-27 by Claude (web research). *Italic* = guess. Verify before acting.

## General vibe
- **No true "Órgiva" found** in this area. Scenes exist but are small and scattered.
- Greece: holistic / yoga places in Crete, Pelion, Mani. But **home schooling is illegal** and there are **almost no legal alternative schools**.
- Slovenia and Croatia (Istria): Waldorf schools exist, but few alternative international newcomers.
- Bulgaria: many cheap villages and some Western newcomers, a few off-grid eco projects (Rhodopes). No clear hub town with a mixed scene found.

## Law and practical

### Home schooling
| Country | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Greece | ❌ Illegal (Law 1566/1985). Fines, custody cases. After age 4 no legal option except state-approved schools | [HSLDA](https://hslda.org/post/greece), [self-directed.org](https://www.self-directed.org/tp/defending-the-obvious/) |
| Croatia | ❌ Banned for nationals except illness / disability. Waldorf schools legal (Zagreb, Rijeka, Osijek, Brtonigla, Vodnjan) | [HSLDA](https://hslda.org/post/croatia), [Glas Istre](https://www.glasistre.hr/istra/prva-generacija-u-brtonigli-skola-u-kojoj-nema-ocjena-niti-ponavljanja-a-uci-se-za-zivot-a-ne-za-ocjene-786438) |
| Slovenia | ✅ Legal. Notify school 3 months before year; state curriculum; exams | [HSLDA](https://hslda.org/post/slovenia) |
| Bulgaria | ✅ Legal since 2016 ("independent form"). Enrol at a school; regional expert committee approves; exams | [HSLDA](https://hslda.org/post/bulgaria), [Sofia Globe](https://sofiaglobe.com/2018/02/12/education-ministry-2-of-bulgarian-pupils-being-home-schooled/) |
| Albania | ⚠️ In law (69/2012, art. 17), but in practice only for disability | [HSLDA](https://hslda.org/post/albania) |
| Montenegro | ⚠️ Unclear (HSLDA "updating"). *Probably not allowed. Guess* | [HSLDA](https://hslda.org/post/montenegro) |

### Residency (Czech EU citizen + Nesli (Turkish spouse))
- **Greece, Croatia, Slovenia, Bulgaria = EU** → EU family route (Directive 2004/38). See [legal overview](legal-overview.md).
- **Albania (not EU):** EU citizens staying 90+ days register online and get a residence certificate. Non-EU spouse of an EU citizen can get a **residence card up to 5 years**. [howalbania](https://howalbania.com/family-reunification-albania/), [Karanovic & Partners](https://www.karanovicpartners.com/news/albania-adopts-amendments-to-the-law-on-foreigners/)
- **Montenegro (not EU):** no EU family route. Options: **digital nomad permit** (min. €1,350/month remote income, 2 years + 2) with family reunification for Nesli; or property ≥ €150,000 (1 year, renewable, no work). [digitalnomads.gov.me](https://digitalnomads.gov.me/article/legal-status-for-nomads), [IMI Daily](https://www.imidaily.com/program-updates/montenegro-sets-e150000-minimum-for-property-based-residency/)

### Van / yurt on rural land
- **Greece:** wild camping banned since 1976; law 5170/2025 bans campers in public places. **One camper per private property is allowed.** [nikana.gr](https://nikana.gr/en/blog/7342/new-camping-law-in-greece-2025-rules-restrictions-and-penalties-for-camper-vehicles)
- **Croatia:** camping outside campsites banned, also on private land. Fines ~€300–1,000. [caravanya](https://www.caravanya.com/en/wildcamping-in-europe/free-standing-with-a-camper-in-croatia/)
- **Slovenia:** banned also on private land with owner's OK; fines up to ~€500. [caravanya](https://www.caravanya.com/en/wildcamping-in-europe/free-standing-with-a-camper-in-slovenia/)
- **Bulgaria:** staying in a camper 21:00–07:00 outside campsites banned since 2021 (fine €60–600); rarely enforced inland. [momgoescamping](https://momgoescamping.com/wild-camping-laws-europe/), [bulgariahiddengems](https://www.bulgariahiddengems.com/post/camping-rules-in-bulgaria)
- Yurts everywhere: *building permit likely needed. Guess.*

### Buying land
- **Bulgaria:** EU citizens can buy land in own name; non-EU (Nesli) only via a Bulgarian company. [ELRA](https://www.elra.eu/contact-point-contribution/bulgaria/limitations-to-foreigners/), [bulgarianproperties](https://www.bulgarianproperties.com/property-buying-procedure.htm)
- Others (EU): *no special limit for Ondrej as EU citizen. Guess, verify.*

## Climate & nature
- **Greece water:** national plan for water shortages (July 2026); groundwater "facing collapse" (2025). Crete: record-dry 2025–26, 4th dry year. [Greek City Times 2026-07-20](https://greekcitytimes.com/2026/07/20/greece-launches-nationwide-plan-to-address-water-shortages), [Greek City Times 2025-07-04](https://greekcitytimes.com/2025/07/04/water-emergency-greece/), [argophilia](https://www.argophilia.com/news/drought-crete-2026/247766/). **Islands = high risk.** Wettest: east Pelion, north-west Greece *(guess)*.
- **Greece fire:** severe fires every summer (2023: 80+ fires, 28 dead; 2024 Attica). [Wikipedia 2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Greece_wildfires), [Wikipedia 2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Greek_wildfires)
- **Slovenia:** biggest fire in its history in Kras, July 2022 (3,700 ha). [Slovenia Times](https://sloveniatimes.com/40672/two-years-after-wildfire-restoration-in-kras-ongoing)
- **Istria:** summer water limits in 2022 and 2026. [Croatia Week](https://www.croatiaweek.com/istria-water-restrictions-heatwave-demand-2026/)
- Balkan mountains (Soča, Rhodopes, Pirin): *cold, snowy winters — hard for Nesli. Guess.*

## Language
- Greek (hard, new alphabet). Croatian, Slovene, Montenegrin, Bulgarian = Slavic → **easier for Ondrej** (Czech, some Russian). Albanian = unique.
- Turkish helps in **Bulgaria** (large Turkish minority, e.g. Kardzhali in the eastern Rhodopes) and a little in Albania / Greece (loanwords). [Wikipedia – Turks in Bulgaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turks_in_Bulgaria)
