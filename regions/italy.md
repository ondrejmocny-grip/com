# Italy (short note)

- **Updated:** 2026-09-27 by Claude (web research). *Italic* = guess or not checked. Verify before acting.

## General vibe
- Italy has **neorurali** (people who leave cities for the land), ecovillages (~20 co-living projects + 16 being built, 2026), parent-run schools, forest kindergartens. [L'Indipendente, 2026-03-12](https://www.lindipendente.online/2026/03/12/ecovillaggi-in-italia-quanti-sono-e-come-si-vive-davvero/), [Viaggiare con lentezza, 2025-11](https://www.viaggiareconlentezza.com/2025/11/26/chi-sono-i-neorurali/)
- But: we found **no clear "Órgiva"** in Italy — no market town with a big, mixed, family-rich alternative scene around it. The scene is spread thin across many small projects.
- Closest match: **Monte Amiata / Arcidosso** (Buddhist centre Merigar, people moved around it).

## Law and practical
- **Home schooling:** ✅ legal (*istruzione parentale*). Yearly written notice to the nearest school head; yearly exam as external candidate. [Ministero dell'Istruzione](https://www.istruzione.it/istruzione-parentale/index.html), [D.Lgs 62/2017 art. 23](https://www.notiziedellascuola.it/legislazione-e-dottrina/indice-cronologico/2017/aprile/DLGS_20170413_62/cap4-art23)
  - ⚠️ **Warning case — "famiglia nel bosco", Palmoli (Abruzzo):** a British–Australian family, living off-grid and home schooling since 2021. Nov 2025 the juvenile court removed the 3 children to a foster home. In 2026 the court set a step-by-step return, **with public school enrolment as a condition**. Parents appealed that condition. [Today.it](https://www.today.it/attualita/famiglia-bosco-palmoli-chi-sono-catherine-birmingham-nathan-trevallion.html), [ANSA, 2026-09-02](https://www.ansa.it/sito/notizie/cronaca/2026/09/02/famiglia-nel-bosco-giudici-percorso-genitori-ancora-incompiuto_56e92c65-d798-439c-9f5c-08d098f2ba40.html), [Orizzonte Insegnanti](https://orizzonteinsegnanti.it/il-caso-famiglia-nel-bosco-il-nodo-della-scuola-pubblica-e-il-percorso-a-tappe-per-il-ricongiungimento-dei-minori/) → Home schooling is legal, but off-grid living + home schooling can draw social-services attention. *Do the paperwork exactly.*
- **Residency (Czech EU citizen + Nesli (Turkish spouse)):** EU family route.
  - Ondrej: after 3 months, register at the town hall (*iscrizione anagrafica*) as worker / self-employed or with enough money + health insurance (D.Lgs 30/2007). *Remote job for a foreign employer: bring contract and payslips (guess).*
  - Nesli: after entry, ask the **Questura** for the *Carta di soggiorno di familiare di un cittadino dell'Unione* (art. 10 D.Lgs 30/2007). Documents: passport, marriage certificate, proof of Ondrej's registration, photos. Valid 5 years, then permanent. [Gazzetta Ufficiale – art. 10](https://www.gazzettaufficiale.it/atto/serie_generale/caricaArticolo?art.progressivo=0&art.idArticolo=10&art.versione=1&art.codiceRedazionale=007G0033&art.dataPubblicazioneGazzetta=2007-03-27&art.idGruppo=0&art.idSottoArticolo1=10&art.idSottoArticolo=1&art.flagTipoArticolo=0), [Questura di Arezzo](https://questure.poliziadistato.it/it/Arezzo/articolo/11045a3bc9df06fea548589081), [InfoImmigrazione 2026](https://www.infoimmigrazione.com/carta-di-soggiorno-familiare-di-cittadino-unione/)
  - Marriage certificate from Turkey needs apostille + Italian translation (*guess, standard*).
- **Van / yurt on rural land:** ❌ strict. A camper, caravan or mobile home **used as a home** counts as **new construction** → needs a building permit, unless it is only for a short temporary need or inside a licensed tourist campsite. [DPR 380/2001 art. 3 e.5 – BibLus](https://biblus.acca.it/art-3-dpr-380-01/), [LavoriPubblici](https://www.lavoripubblici.it/news/Camper-roulotte-e-case-mobili-Per-i-privati-e-reato-ancorarle-al-suolo-senza-permesso-di-costruire-22517), [Ediltecnico](https://ediltecnico.it/caravan-roulotte-terreno-agricolo-legittimita-ordine-rimozione/). Legal start: rent, or stay with an *agriturismo* / campsite host. *Regional "agricampeggio" rules may help (guess).*

## Climate & water
- North & centre Apennines: more rain (Lunigiana ~1,300 mm). [climate-data.org](https://en.climate-data.org/europe/italy/tuscany/pontremoli-110281/)
- **South and islands:** Sicily had a national drought emergency from May 2024; Linguaglossa got 390 mm instead of ~1,500 mm. Wet winter 2025–26 refilled dams. [Renewable Matter](https://www.renewablematter.eu/siccita-sicilia-cosa-sta-succedendo), [AetnaNet, 2026-09-17](https://www.aetnanet.org/2026/09/17/dighe-siciliane-la-grande-risalita-del-2026-da-230-a-617-milioni-di-m%C2%B3-cosa-e-cambiato-dal-disastro-del-2024-e-cosa-possono-fare-pnrr-e-nuovi-lavori/)
- **West Liguria:** water crisis Aug 2026 in 7 hill towns. [ANSA, 2026-08-06](https://www.ansa.it/liguria/notizie/2026/08/06/sette-comuni-dellimperiese-in-grave-crisi-idrica_2ed602b8-d090-4ef5-bcfa-36103452b707.html)
