# Morocco

- **Updated:** 2026-09-27 by Claude (web research). Brief note. *Italic* = guess. 1 € ≈ 10.9 MAD (Sept 2026, [Wise](https://wise.com/us/currency-converter/eur-to-mad-rate/history)).

## General vibe
- Scene: surf + yoga + retreat tourism on the Atlantic (Taghazout, Essaouira, Sidi Kaouki, Imsouane). A few real permaculture pioneers (Marrakesh Organics, Fertile Roots). Moroccan interest in permaculture is growing. [Middle East Eye](https://www.middleeasteye.net/features/bio-morocco-permaculture-movement-grows-despite-pressure)
- Newcomers are mostly **French, British, Spanish, German** retirees, surfers and nomads. Few Órgiva-style settled families. *Guess.*
- Locals: very hospitable. Muslim, conservative in the countryside. Spiritual events (ceremonies, plant medicine) must stay quiet; proselytising is illegal (*known, verify*).
- Alternative schools growing, but mostly in big cities (Marrakech, Casablanca, Rabat). [Jeune Afrique](https://www.jeuneafrique.com/1378368/societe/maroc-lenseignement-alternatif-seduit-de-plus-en-plus-de-parents/), [Edumag](https://www.edumag.ma/ecoles-alternatives-maroc-190291.html)

## Law and practical
- **Entry:** Czech and Turkish citizens are both **visa-free for 90 days**. [Wikipedia visa policy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visa_policy_of_Morocco). ⚠️ Some sites say 30 days for Turks — check with the embassy. [evisaguides](https://evisaguides.com/en/visa/turkey-to-morocco/)
- **Residency:** no EU family route (Morocco is not EU). Both of us need our own **carte de séjour / carte d'immatriculation**, applied at the police (DGSN) **within 90 days** of arrival. [morocco-guide](https://www.morocco-guide.com/information/morocco-entry-requirements/), [La France au Maroc](https://ma.diplomatie.gouv.fr/fr/vivre-venir/permis-de-sejour)
  - Type **"visiteur"**: proof of own income, lease or address, medical certificate, criminal record; **no local work allowed**. Valid 1 year, renewable. [demarchesmaroc](https://www.demarchesmaroc.com/titres-de-sejour/), [retraite-etranger](https://www.retraite-etranger.fr/visa-maroc-guide-complet/)
  - Nesli could later join as spouse of a resident (family reunification). [Cabinet Jawhari](https://avocat-jawhari.com/2022/05/20/lentree-et-le-sejour-des-etrangers-au-royaume-du-maroc/)
  - **No digital-nomad visa** yet (mid-2026). Remote work for a foreign employer = grey zone. Over 183 days/year → tax resident. [Citizen Remote](https://citizenremote.com/visas/morocco-digital-nomad-visa/), [MarocMama](https://marocmama.com/digital-nomad-life-in-morocco/)
  - ⚠️ Nesli's small seasonal work (food, healing) would need a work permit. *Guess: hard.*
  - *Our 6+6 rhythm: 90 days, leave, come back is common but officials may refuse repeated "visa runs". Guess.*
- **Home schooling:** ⚠️ no law for foreigners. Compulsory school 6–15 applies to **Moroccan** children. Foreign families homeschool without known problems; follow the home country's program. [Expat Exchange](https://www.expatexchange.com/ctryguide/20958/81/Morocco/Education-in-Morocco-Homeschooling-in-Morocco), [Worldly Tribe 2026](https://worldlytribe.com/worldschooling-legal-guide-by-country/), [Hijrah to Morocco](https://hijrahtomorocco.com/homeschool-in-morocco/). Our kids (Czech/Turkish) → *tolerated, guess. Czech distance schooling could cover it.*
- **Van:** foreign-plated vehicle may stay **max 6 months per 12 months** (temporary admission). Wild camping officially not allowed, **widely tolerated**, but Essaouira and Imsouane cracked down. [expat.com forum](https://www.expat.com/en/forum/africa/morocco/1064352-temporary-admission-of-a-vehicle-and-residency.html), [whole world or nothing](https://thewholeworldornothing.com/vanlife-morocco/)
- **Yurt / tiny house on rural land:** *needs a building permit; farmland is protected; tolerated on a host's land. Guess, not verified.*
- **Buying land as foreigner:**
  - ❌ **Agricultural land outside town zones: foreigners may not buy** (Dahir 1-73-213 of 1973, Dahir 1975). Renting farmland to a foreigner also needs an administrative authorisation. [le360](https://fr.le360.ma/politique/nouvelles-restrictions-pour-lacquisition-de-terres-agricoles-par-des-etrangers-259606/), [jurisprudence.ma](https://www.jurisprudence.ma/code/dahir-portant-loi-n-1-73-213-du-2-mars-1973-relatif-au-transfert-a-letat-de-la-propriete-des-immeubles-agricoles-ou-a-vocation-agricole-appartenant-aux-personnes-physiques-etrangeres-ou-aux-per/)
  - Way around: land with a **VNA certificate** (*vocation non agricole*, not farmland). [Korte Law](https://korte-law.com/real-estate-acquisition-foreigners-morocco)
  - **Titré** (titre foncier at ANCFCC) = safe. **Melkia** (traditional adoul deed) = weak, disputes common; buy only if converted to titre before signing. [buypropertymorocco](https://buypropertymorocco.com/can-foreigners-buy-property-in-morocco/), [Rio Times 2026](https://www.riotimesonline.com/buying-property-morocco-foreigners-2026/)
  - **Collective (soulaliyate / tribal) land:** *cannot be sold to foreigners. Guess (known rule), verify.*
  - Since 2021 (Law 62-19) some companies may buy farmland for projects. [le360](https://fr.le360.ma/politique/nouvelles-restrictions-pour-lacquisition-de-terres-agricoles-par-des-etrangers-259606/)
  - **For us:** lease or join a project. A 1 ha farm of our own is **not realistic** in Morocco.

## Climate & nature
- **Water:** 2019–2024 = longest drought on record; 2023–24 driest year in 60+ years (106 mm). Winter 2025–26 ended it: dams ~70% (Feb 2026). But aquifers are overused; experts say the water crisis is not over. A strong El Niño is forming for winter 2026–27; effect on Morocco unclear (the Atlantic NAO matters more). [phys.org, 2026-09](https://phys.org/news/2026-09-drought-north-africa-super-el.html), [Atalayar, 2026-02](https://www.atalayar.com/en/articulo/society/torrential-rains-change-the-course-of-moroccos-water-trajectory/20260218125133223475.html), [Fanack](https://water.fanack.com/morocco-water-crisis-after-drought/)
- Coast cities now drink **desalinated** water (Agadir; Essaouira soon). Goal: 50–60% of drinking water from the sea by 2030. [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/world/morocco-desalination-ocean-africa-spc)
- 💧 **Honest view:** Morocco is a **dry country**. Only the Rif (north) and High Atlas valleys have real rivers. This hits our deal-breaker hard.
- **Fire:** high in the Rif (Chefchaouen 2025). Low–medium in the argan south. [Hespress](https://en.hespress.com/118360-morocco-contains-wildfire-that-burned-500-hectares-near-chefchaouen.html)
- **Earthquake:** Atlas and Agadir are seismic (2023 Al Haouz M6.8, ~2,960 dead, *number from memory, verify*; Agadir 1960). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Al_Haouz_earthquake)
- Climate rule: coast (Essaouira, Agadir) = ✅ pass, mild. Inland (Marrakech, Chefchaouen) = dry enough but **~38 °C summers**.
