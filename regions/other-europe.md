# Other Europe (short note): Cyprus, Madeira / Azores, Bulgaria, Romania, Georgia, Montenegro, Albania, North Macedonia, Balearics

- **Updated:** 2026-09-27 by Claude (web research). *Italic* = guess. Verify before acting.
- More detail on Bulgaria, Albania, Montenegro (and Greece, Croatia, Slovenia) is in [greece-balkans.md](greece-balkans.md).

## General vibe
- **No true "Órgiva" found** in this area.
- Closest: **Madeira south-west** (mild, sea + mountains, legal home school, nomad village — but expensive and a nomad bubble) and **Bansko / Obidim** (worldschool families, legal home school, cheap — but cold winters, sea far).
- **Ibiza north** fits the pattern on paper (hippie history, Waldorf school, markets), but fails on **water** and **price**. **Mallorca Tramuntana**: same problems. **Cyprus**: severe drought.
- Outside the EU (Georgia, Albania, Montenegro, North Macedonia): cheap and beautiful, but small or no newcomer scenes, and **no EU family route for Nesli**.

## Law and practical

### Cyprus (EU)
- **Home schooling:** ❌ only for special needs / illness, with Ministry approval. [thenomadresidences, 2025](https://thenomadresidences.com/2025/11/19/homeschooling-in-cyprus-is-it-legal/)
- **Residency:** EU family route (Directive 2004/38). See [legal overview](legal-overview.md).
- **Van / yurt:** *permits likely needed. Guess.*
- **Land prices:** *mid-high. Guess.*
- **Language:** Greek; English widely spoken *(guess)*.

### Portugal islands — Madeira, Azores (EU)
- **Home schooling:** ✅ legal (*ensino doméstico*). See [legal overview](legal-overview.md), [portugal.md](portugal.md).
- **Residency:** EU family route; Madeira and Azores are full EU. Nesli applies via AIMA like on the mainland *(guess: same process)*.
- **Van:** Madeira — sleeping in a vehicle outside official places is illegal (Road Code art. 48, 50-A), enforcement rising. [hikingmadeira](https://hikingmadeira.com/blog/wild-camping-in-madeira-whats-legal-whats-not-and-how-to-avoid-fines/). Yurt / building: PDM + licence; much land is Natural Park.
- **Land prices:** Madeira high — Calheta houses ~€4,100–4,500/m² (+20% in a year); island average ~€3,825/m² (Feb 2026). [idealista](https://www.idealista.pt/media/relatorios-preco-habitacao/venda/madeira-ilha/calheta-madeira/), [jarniascyril](https://www.jarniascyril.com/international-real-estate/madeira-real-estate-market-2026-analysis-forecasts-investing/). Azores: *lower. Guess.*
- **Language:** Portuguese.

### Bulgaria (EU)
- **Home schooling:** ✅ legal since 2016 (enrol at a school, committee approves, exams). [HSLDA](https://hslda.org/post/bulgaria)
- **Residency:** EU family route.
- **Van:** camper stays 21:00–07:00 outside campsites banned since 2021 (fine €60–600), rarely enforced inland. [bulgariahiddengems](https://www.bulgariahiddengems.com/post/camping-rules-in-bulgaria). Yurt: *permit likely. Guess.*
- **Land:** cheapest in the EU; village houses €18–39k. Ondrej can buy land; Nesli only via a Bulgarian company. [bulgarianrural-properties](https://bulgarianrural-properties.com/en/204), [ELRA](https://www.elra.eu/contact-point-contribution/bulgaria/limitations-to-foreigners/)
- **Language:** Bulgarian (Slavic, Cyrillic — easier for Ondrej). Turkish minority in parts (Kardzhali, north-east).

### Romania (EU)
- **Home schooling:** ⚠️ grey. In law only for children who cannot attend school; in practice many families use foreign umbrella schools. [HSLDA](https://hslda.org/post/romania), [cambrilearn 2026](https://cambrilearn.com/blog/homeschooling-in-romania-legal-guide-for-parents-2026)
- **Residency:** EU family route.
- **Van:** wild camping generally allowed (200 m from homes, not in protected areas / Natura 2000, one night per spot); on private land only with permission; fines up to ~€5,000. [is-this-legal](https://is-this-legal.com/is-wild-camping-legal-in-romania/), [caravanya](https://www.caravanya.com/en/wildcamping-in-europe/free-standing-with-a-camper-in-rumania/)
- **Land:** *cheap in most villages; Viscri-type famous villages expensive. Guess.* [Romania Insider](https://www.romania-insider.com/bbc-feature-viscri-romania-2020)
- **Language:** Romanian (Latin; Spanish / Italian help a little).

### Georgia (not EU)
- **Home schooling:** ✅ *probably legal* — law "on general education" allows independent study (*eksternat*); parents choose. [HSLDA – Georgia (country) page, via search summary](https://hslda.org/post/georgia). *Verify: HSLDA page may mix US state and country.*
- **Residency:** Czech and Turkish citizens: **1 year visa-free**. Then temporary residence permit (renewable up to 6 years, then permanent), or "Remotely from Georgia" remote-worker permit. [georgia-spirit](https://www.georgia-spirit.com/guides/visa-requirements/), [Wikipedia – Turkish visa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visa_requirements_for_Turkish_citizens). No EU family route.
- **Van:** wild camping legal and common; not on private land without permission; designated spots in national parks. [georgia.travel](https://georgia.travel/wild-camping-in-georgia)
- **Land:** *cheap. Foreigners face limits on buying agricultural land. Guess, verify.*
- **Language:** Georgian (own alphabet, hard). Russian widely known → helps Ondrej. Turkish useful in Adjara *(guess)*.

### Montenegro (not EU)
- **Home schooling:** ⚠️ unclear (HSLDA updating). [HSLDA](https://hslda.org/post/montenegro)
- **Residency:** no EU family route. Digital nomad permit (≥ €1,350/month remote income, 2+2 years) with family reunification, or property ≥ €150,000. See [greece-balkans.md](greece-balkans.md).
- **Van / land / language:** see [greece-balkans.md](greece-balkans.md). Montenegrin = Slavic.

### Albania (not EU)
- **Home schooling:** ⚠️ in law, but in practice only for disability. [HSLDA](https://hslda.org/post/albania)
- **Residency:** EU citizens register after 90 days; non-EU spouse of an EU citizen can get a residence card up to 5 years. See [greece-balkans.md](greece-balkans.md).
- **Van:** *wild camping widely tolerated. Guess.* Land: *cheap inland, rising fast on the Riviera. Guess.*
- **Language:** Albanian (unique). Italian and Greek known in the south *(guess)*.

### North Macedonia (not EU)
- **Home schooling:** ❌ not permitted. [HSLDA](https://hslda.org/post/north-macedonia)
- **Residency:** Turkish citizens 90 / 180 days visa-free. [Turkish MFA](https://www.mfa.gov.tr/makedonya-seyahat-edecek-turk-vatandaslarinin-dikkatine.en.mfa). Temporary residence (1 year) for work, family reunification, etc.; EU / OECD citizens buying a home ≥ €40,000 can get it. [Lawyers Antevski](https://lawyers-antevski.com.mk/2023/03/25/kinds-of-residence-permits-for-foreigners-in-north-macedonia/)
- **Van / land:** *cheap; rules unknown. Guess.*
- **Language:** Macedonian (Slavic, Cyrillic).

### Spain — Balearics (EU)
- **Home schooling:** ⚠️ grey zone (see [legal overview](legal-overview.md)). Schools: Waldorf Ibiza (Sant Joan / Sant Rafael), Escolasofia Waldorf (Palma), Sa Llavor (Mancor), Ses Marjades eco-school (Sóller).
- **Residency:** EU family route (EX-19).
- **Van / yurt:** ❌ rustic-land rules; very strict and heavily checked on the islands *(guess)*.
- **Land:** extreme — Sóller fincas ~€6,600/m², Sant Joan (Ibiza) ~€7,400/m².
- **Language:** Catalan + Spanish.

## Money (2026-09-27 snapshots)
- Cheapest: Bulgaria, Romania, Georgia, North Macedonia, inland Albania.
- Mid: Cyprus, Montenegro coast *(guess)*.
- Very high: Madeira south, Mallorca, Ibiza.

## Climate & nature
- **Pass** (≥5 dry months): Madeira south, Ibiza, Mallorca, Cyprus, Albania coast. But the dry ones (Cyprus, Balearics, Albanian Riviera) have **water problems**.
- **Borderline:** Bansko (snow Dec–Apr, rainy May–Jun), Kakheti.
- **Fail:** Azores (only ~3 dry months), Adjara, Transylvania, Veliko Tarnovo *(rainy early summer + snow)*.
- **Fire:** Madeira 2024 (5,116 ha), Pirin 2025 (4,500+ ha). Cyprus, Balearics: pine forests *(guess: high)*.

## Language
- Slavic (BG, ME, MK) → easier for Ondrej. Georgian, Albanian, Greek → hard. Portuguese, Catalan / Spanish → both plan to learn some. Turkish helps a little in Bulgaria, Adjara, North Macedonia *(guess for the last two)*.
