# Portugal

- **Updated:** 2026-09-27 by Claude (web research). Prices are listing snapshots from this date. *Italic* = guess or not checked. Verify before acting.

## General vibe
- **Spiritual / alternative scene:** strong, one of the biggest in Europe. Many eco-villages, permaculture farms, retreat centres and learning communities. [GEN Portugal](https://ecovillage.org/gen_country/portugal/), [pureportugal – 40+ communities](https://pureportugal.co.uk/blogs/communities-in-portugal/)
- Main clusters:
  - **Centre** (Coimbra district, Serra da Estrela foothills, Castelo Branco, Fundão, Idanha-a-Nova): many eco projects and nature-based learning communities (Penela, Espinhal, Benfeita). [alternativeschoolsinportugal.com](https://www.alternativeschoolsinportugal.com/)
  - **SW coast** (Odemira, Aljezur, Monchique, Lagos): very alive, many families, but more expat. Tamera, São Luís hub, Aljezur market. [Tamera](https://www.tamera.org/our-future/)
  - **Lisbon area** (Sintra, Ericeira, Mafra, Arrábida): most alternative schools, yoga, retreats. More urban and expensive. [alternativeschoolsinportugal.com – licensed](https://www.alternativeschoolsinportugal.com/schools/licensed)
  - **North (Minho)**: scene is small. Strong local traditions. *Few eco projects found online (search 2026-09-27).*
- **Locals:** *generally kind and helpful to newcomers who learn some Portuguese (guess, based on common reports).* Inland villages are old and emptying, so newcomers with kids are often welcome. *(guess)*
- **Expat bubble risk:** in some inland places expats meet mostly other expats. [Serra da Estrela search summary / Medium](https://roxanemaar.medium.com/3-family-friendly-communities-worth-visiting-in-portugal-716d858f6cb2). Strongest bubble: Aljezur/Lagos, Silver Coast, Sintra/Cascais. *(guess)*

## Law and practical
- **Residency / visa for us:** EU family route. See [legal overview](legal-overview.md#portugal). Don't duplicate here.
- **Living in a van — legal? Where?** Short version: camping outside official sites needs a council licence; fixed setups need a building licence; the PDM decides. Coastal natural parks (Costa Vicentina, SW Alentejo) are strict. Details: [legal overview §3](legal-overview.md#3-van--yurt-on-private-rural-land), [Aljezur place file](../places/pt-aljezur-costa-vicentina.md).
- **Yurt / tiny house / glamping on rural land — allowed?** A wooden house or yurt that stays in place counts as a building. There is **no "mobile structure" exemption**. It needs council approval and must fit the PDM. Legal path for guests: rural tourism (*turismo em espaço rural*) or *parque de campismo* licence. [MF Casas de Madeira guide 2026](https://mfcasasdemadeira.pt/guia-estrategico-2026-como-construir-uma-casa-de-madeira-em-terreno-rustico-ao-abrigo-do-decreto-lei-n-o-108-2026/)

### "Rústico" vs "urbano" land
| Type | What it means | Can we build? |
|---|---|---|
| **Urbano** | Inside the building zone of the PDM | Yes, with a licence. Usually small plots in villages. |
| **Rústico** | Farm or forest land | Only if the local PDM allows it. No single national rule. [ac-arquitetos](https://www.ac-arquitetos.com/post/construir-em-terreno-rustico-o-que-precisa-de-saber) |
| **Misto** | One plot with both parts | The urbano part can be built on. Good for us. |
| **RAN / REN** (overlays) | National farm reserve / ecological reserve | Very hard. Exceptions only for farming, forestry or rural tourism. [MF Casas](https://mfcasasdemadeira.pt/guia-estrategico-2026-como-construir-uma-casa-de-madeira-em-terreno-rustico-ao-abrigo-do-decreto-lei-n-o-108-2026/) |

- **2025 "Lei dos Solos"** (DL 117/2024): councils can reclassify rústico → urbano for housing. But it is **not** for a private person building one house. [idealista news](https://www.idealista.pt/news/imobiliario/habitacao/2025/01/23/67855-raio-x-a-lei-dos-solos-guia-para-entender-o-que-esta-em-causa), [Tiago Correia](https://tiagorcorreia.com/en/land-law-housing-rustic-land/), [DR](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/decreto-lei/117-2024-901535572)
- **Tip:** ask the council for a **PIP** (*pedido de informação prévia*) before buying. Under the 2026 rules a positive PIP is binding for 3 years. [MF Casas](https://mfcasasdemadeira.pt/guia-estrategico-2026-como-construir-uma-casa-de-madeira-em-terreno-rustico-ao-abrigo-do-decreto-lei-n-o-108-2026/)

### Renovating a ruin / stone house
- A ruin on rústico land **can** usually be rebuilt. It must be on record (*matriz*, land registry, or clear proof). Same footprint. Small extensions (often up to ~20%) may be allowed. [ac-arquitetos](https://www.ac-arquitetos.com/post/construir-em-terreno-rustico-o-que-precisa-de-saber), [linktoleaders](https://linktoleaders.com/uma-casa-em-terreno-rustico-ja-pode-ser-uma-opcao-saiba-como/)
- **New in 2026:** DL 108/2026 makes rebuilding a ruin to its last legal state **free of prior licence**. Start date reported as **1 Oct 2026** (delayed by DL 155-B/2026). [MF Casas](https://mfcasasdemadeira.pt/guia-estrategico-2026-como-construir-uma-casa-de-madeira-em-terreno-rustico-ao-abrigo-do-decreto-lei-n-o-108-2026/) *Only a builder's blog as source. Verify in Diário da República.*
- **Check before buying:** is the ruin in the *caderneta predial* as *urbano*? Does it have a *licença de utilização* or is it pre-1951? Is it in RAN/REN? *(common advice, guess)*
- Rural tourism licence: needed for our retreat plan. Some listings already have it (e.g. Aljezur: 600 m² for rural tourism). [algarveprop](https://algarveprop.com/property/21-hectare-plot-of-land-with-ruin-for-sale-in-aljezur-algarve-5/)
- **Fire rule:** in rural areas owners must clear fuel in a 50 m band around buildings. Councils check in May in the ~1,000 "priority parishes". [Despacho 4717/2025](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/despacho/4717-2025-915239753)

### Buying land as foreigner
- No limits for EU (or other) citizens. You need a NIF (tax number). *(common knowledge; see [doinportugal](https://doinportugal.pt/en/blog/220125-kak-inostrantsu-kupit-zemlyu-v-portugalii.htm))*

## Climate & nature

### Water and drought
- **North vs south:** Rain is high in the north and falls toward the south. Braga ~1,450 mm, Porto ~1,250 mm, Coimbra ~900 mm, Lisbon ~800 mm, Algarve ~500 mm. [Climates to Travel](https://www.climatestotravel.com/climate/portugal)
- Three zones: **humid north of the Tejo**; sub-humid (interior Douro, parts south of Tejo); **arid** (Guadiana basin, part of Sado, Algarve). Water problems are in the arid and sub-humid zones. [NTUA summary](http://environ.chemeng.ntua.gr/wsm/Newsletters/Issue3/Portugal.htm)
- **Structural drought = Algarve + Alentejo.**
  - 2024: government ordered cuts of 15% (urban), 25% (farming), 25% (tourism) in the Algarve. [portugal.gov.pt](https://www.portugal.gov.pt/en/gc23/communication/news-item?i=new-measures-to-tackle-the-drought-in-the-algarve-and-alentejo), [Cuatrecasas](https://www.cuatrecasas.com/en/portugal/art/response-measures-for-algarves-ongoing-drought)
  - 2025: dams recovered after a wet spring. But experts say Algarve and Alentejo must "prepare for future droughts". [Portugal News 2025-03](https://www.theportugalnews.com/news/2025-03-05/drought-restrictions-to-ease/96020), [Portugal News 2025-05](https://www.theportugalnews.com/news/2025-05-10/algarve-and-alentejo-need-to-prepare-for-future-droughts/97547)
  - Big projects (Alqueva links, desalination, "Água que Une") show the problem is long-term. [Portugal Resident](https://www.portugalresident.com/portugal-will-face-water-shortage-crisis-primarily-affecting-algarve-and-alentejo/)
- **Now (IPMA, 2026):**
  - Dec 2025 – Apr 2026: no drought anywhere.
  - End of July 2026 (driest July since 2000): **whole mainland** in drought (60% moderate).
  - August 2026 was very wet (2.5× normal). Drought eased in most places, **but got worse in interior Baixo Alentejo and eastern Algarve (Sotavento)**. [IPMA Aug 2026 bulletin](https://www.ipma.pt/pt/media/noticias/news.detail.jsp?y=2026&f=boletim_climatologico_agosto_2026.html), [tempo.pt July 2026](https://www.tempo.pt/noticias/actualidade/ipma-revela-que-o-mes-de-julho-de-2026-foi-o-julho-mais-seco-desde-2000-teresa-abrantes-apresenta-os-destaques-do-mes.html)
  - Live map: [IPMA drought monitoring](https://www.ipma.pt/pt/oclima/observatorio.secas/)
- **For us:** north of the Tejo (Minho, Centre) passes the water deal-breaker. South (Odemira, Aljezur, Monchique) = ⚠️ water risk. Only with own springs / wells / water retention, and still a risk. *(my reading)*

### Wildfire
- **Official map:** ICNF *Carta de Perigosidade de Incêndio Rural*, 5 classes (very low → very high), 25 m cells, based on 44 years of burned areas. [ICNF map viewer](https://sig.icnf.pt/portal/home/item.html?id=65e7a435415e467b82f84b0640205409), [DGT](https://www.dgterritorio.gov.pt/atividades/paisagem/ptp/carta-perigosidade-incendio-rural)
  - **Use it for every plot before buying.** In "high/very high" areas, new building outside villages is mostly not allowed (DL 82/2021). *(the building rule is a guess from the law's aim; verify)*
- **Highest-risk zone:** the **central interior** pine + eucalyptus belt (Pinhal Interior: Pedrógão, Góis, Arganil, Pampilhosa, Lousã, Oliveira do Hospital, Seia, Castelo Branco, Fundão). Also Monchique and the northern mountains.
  - June 2017 Pedrógão: 64+ dead, 46,000 ha. ~70% of burned area was eucalyptus/pine. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/June_2017_Portugal_wildfires), [EDJNet](https://www.europeandatajournalism.eu/cp_data_news/portugal-wildfires-and-the-eucalyptus-curse/)
  - Oct 2017: 45 dead in Portugal, mainly Coimbra and Viseu districts. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/October_2017_Iberian_wildfires)
  - 2025: ~278,000 ha burned in Portugal. Arganil fire 64,451 ha = biggest ever. [Wikipedia 2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Portugal_wildfires), [Portugal News](https://www.theportugalnews.com/news/2025-08-25/fire-the-largest-ever-in-portugal/604015)
- 2025: 988 "priority parishes" (34% of all parishes, 32% of the mainland). [Despacho 4717/2025](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/despacho/4717-2025-915239753), [Seia Digital](https://seiadigital.pt/2025/04/30/governo-identifica-191-freguesias-prioritarias-na-zona-de-seia-para-fiscalizacao-de-limpeza-de-terrenos/)
- **Lower risk:** coastal strips, green farmed valleys near villages, land with oak/chestnut/fields instead of eucalyptus. *(guess)* Minho burns less often than the Centre, but not never (Gerês 2025).
