## General vibe
- **Spiritual / alternative scene:** big, but spread out. Strong clusters:
  - **Andalusia south:** Alpujarras (Órgiva: *~700 people* in 3 alternative settlements: Beneficio, El Morreón, Los Tablones). [Olive Press 2022](https://www.theolivepress.es/spain-news/2022/01/02/viable-alternative-a-look-at-the-hippy-communities-around-orgiva-in-spains-alpujarras/). Sierra de Aracena (El Calabacino ecovillage, 100+ people, 40 years). [RIE](https://ecoaldeas.org/el-calabacino/)
  - **Catalonia:** many "free schools" and eco projects. XELL network: ~500 children in its projects, maybe up to 1,500 in total. [Diari de l'Educació](https://diarieducacio.cat/el-model-descoles-lliures-guanya-cada-cop-mes-pes/)
  - **North (Navarre, León):** old ecovillages Lakabe, Arterra Bizimodu (Navarre), Matavenero (León, has its own free school). [RIE members list](https://zapatealasuelaecoaldeas.blogspot.com/2020/09/ecoaldeas-espana-miembros-red-iberica-ecoaldeas-zapatea-la-suela.html), [pueblosbonitos](https://pueblosbonitos.net/ecoaldeas-en-asturias/)
  - **Asturias:** "neo-rural" villages like Cabranes. A dying school now has dozens of children from newcomer families (~30 British families + Spanish from other regions). [Atlántica XXII](https://www.atlanticaxxii.org/cabranes-meca-neorrural/)
  - **Galicia:** rural hubs and small projects (Ribeira Sacra innovation hub, eco-village plans in rural Ourense). Scene is smaller. [La Región 2026](https://www.laregion.es/comarca-de-ourense/formacion-vivienda-empleo-financiacion-son_1_20260103-4111693.html), [Los libros del salvaje](https://loslibrosdelsalvaje.com/blog/nuestro-proyecto-una-ecoaldea-en-el-rural-ourensano)
- ⚠️ Many famous Spanish projects are **closed intentional communities** (our deal-breaker). But they are good places to visit and learn, and they attract like-minded families to the villages around them. *(my reading)*
- **Locals:** *Rural Spain is emptying ("España vaciada"). Most villages welcome families with kids, because they keep the school open (guess, supported by the Cabranes case).* Local resistance exists when newcomers come in big groups (e.g. a Norwegian "ecovillage" plan for 500 families split the village of Planes, Alicante). [El Español 2026](https://www.elespanol.com/alicante/vivir/20260402/planes-pueblo-dividido-ecoaldea-noruega-familias-frente-temor-vecinal-macrourbanizacion/1003744192166_0.html)
- **Expat bubble:** strongest on the Costa del Sol and Axarquía. Cómpeta: 44.5% foreign residents, Canillas de Albaida ~36%. Málaga province: 1 in 5 people are foreign. [Wikipedia Cómpeta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%B3mpeta), [Olive Press 2025](https://www.theolivepress.es/spain-news/2025/05/05/census-reveals-over-20-of-people-living-in-malaga-are-foreign/)

## Law and practical
- **Residency / visa for us:** EU family route (EX-19 for Nesli). See [legal overview §1](legal-overview.md#spain-incl-canary-islands). Not repeated here.
- **Living in a van — legal? Where?** Not on your own rustic land long-term. A van or yurt that stays is treated like a building. See [legal overview §3](legal-overview.md#3-van--yurt-on-private-rural-land).
  - Real-life risk: police cleared a big alternative camp in Beneficio (Órgiva) in Dec 2023. [Seaside Gazette](https://www.theseasidegazette.com/2023/12/102988/police-dismantle-encampment/)
  - *Practical start: join a project that has a campsite or rural-tourism licence, or rent a house in the village while looking (guess).*
- **Yurt / tiny house / glamping on rural land — allowed?** Same as van: needs a licence. Prefab houses are OK in Asturias **inside a "núcleo rural"** or inside the volume of an old building, with a project. [Construcciones MBL 2026](https://construccionesmbl.com/construccion-casa/casas-prefabricadas-y-minicasas-en-asturias-es-legal-dnde-se-pueden-instalar-gua-2026/) Glamping for guests = rural-tourism licence from the region.

### Land types (short)
| Type | What it means | Can we build? |
|---|---|---|
| **Suelo urbano** | Inside the town/village plan | Yes, with a licence |
| **Núcleo rural** (Galicia, Asturias) | Small traditional hamlets marked on the plan | ✅ Yes, new house or rebuild allowed, with strict style rules. [Asturias forum/architect](https://www.soloarquitectura.com/foros/threads/edificar-en-nucleo-rural-de-asturias.106069/), [Galicia Ley 2/2016](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2016-3191) |
| **Suelo rústico / no urbanizable** | Farm, forest, protected land | Only limited cases (farm buildings, rebuild of existing houses, special licence). Rules differ by region. |

### Home schooling and alternative schools
- ⚠️ **Grey zone.** No law allows or bans it. School is compulsory 6–16. See [legal overview §2](legal-overview.md#2-home-schooling).
- Constitutional Court (2010): the Constitution "neither protects nor explicitly prohibits" home education. Courts decide in different ways. *~4,000–8,000 families* do it. Usual risk: a truancy (*absentismo*) case → social services check. Most cases close with no penalty. [homeschoolingspain 2026](http://www.homeschoolingspain.com/2026/02/legalidad-del-homeschooling-en-espana.html)
- **Per region (conflicting sources):**
  - One source: **more tolerant** = Catalonia, Basque Country, Navarre. **Stricter** = Madrid, Andalusia. [homeschoolingspain 2026](http://www.homeschoolingspain.com/2026/02/legalidad-del-homeschooling-en-espana.html)
  - Another source: Basque Country, Cantabria **and Andalusia** are "taking positive steps". [homeschooling.es 2025](https://homeschooling.es/legalidad-del-homeschooling/)
  - *Andalusia is unclear. Ask local families. (open question)*
- ⚠️ Many "escuelas libres" are **not officially licensed**. Legally the child may still count as "not in school". *(guess, common in Spain; verify per school)*

This keeps the Czech record. **But it does not replace the Spanish duty to go to school** if the family lives in Spain. *(the second part is my reading, verify)* [MZV ČR](https://mzv.gov.cz/jnp/cz/srp/studium_v_zahranici_a_cr/pro_zajemce_o_dochazku_do_skoly_typu.html) Czech schools exist in Madrid and Seville, a Czech club in Asturias. ## Climate & nature

### Water and drought
- **Two Spains:** wet "green Spain" in the north (Galicia, Asturias, Cantabria, Basque Country, north Navarre) vs. dry south and east. *(common knowledge)*
- **Structural drought zones:** Murcia, Almería and their mountains = highest desertification risk. Rain often <350 mm, some places <250 mm. Aquifers over-used for decades (Níjar aquifer officially "overexploited" in 2004). [phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2023-08-spain-lifeless-desertification.html), [ScienceDirect Almería](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2351989420302250)
- **Recent crises:**
  - **Catalonia 2021–2025:** worst drought on record. Internal basins at 14.4% in March 2024 → emergency. Ended in spring 2025. [catalannews](https://www.catalannews.com/drought/item/drought-emergency-measures-lifted-for-most-of-catalonias-inhabitants), [Euro Weekly News](https://euroweeklynews.com/2025/04/05/catalonia-ends-drought-crisis/)
  - **Axarquía 2022–2024:** La Viñuela at ~8% in Nov 2023; tap water cut to 180 l/person/day in 2024. [AxarquíaPlus](https://www.axarquiaplus.es/el-embalse-de-la-vinuela-ya-tiene-el-triple-de-agua-que-hace-un-ano-pero-sigue-en-sequia-grave-con-35-hectometros-cubicos/)
  - **Sierra de Aracena 2022–2023:** daily water cuts, tanker trucks. [Giahsa](https://www.giahsa.com/wps/portal/giahsa/News/newsitem/Actuaciones%20Sierra%20Sequia)
  - **Mallorca:** summer restrictions every year since 2024/25. [mallorca.com](https://www.mallorca.com/en/advice/property/building-renovating/water-restrictions-mallorca), [Majorca Daily Bulletin 2026-08](https://www.majorcadailybulletin.com/news/local/2026/08/26/145277/mallorca-water-crisis-manacor-introduces-water-saving-measures-tackle-the-drought-alert.html)
- **Now (Sep 2026):** Spain's reservoirs at **60.3%** (22 Sep), well above the 10-year average. Peak 84% in May 2026. [MITECO](https://www.miteco.gob.es/es/prensa/ultimas-noticias/2026/septiembre/la-reserva-hidrica-espanola-se-encuentra-al-60-3---de-su-capacid.html), [ecoticias](https://www.ecoticias.com/medio-ambiente/reserva-hidrica-espanola-embalses)
  - South is full after very wet winters: Guadalquivir 70.3%, Andalusian Med. 65.1%, La Viñuela 85%. [tutiempo](https://www.tutiempo.net/noticias/reserva-hidrica-espana-estado-embalses-22-septiembre-2026.html), [AxarquíaPlus](https://www.axarquiaplus.es/la-axarquia-se-bebe-casi-cinco-hectometros-del-embalse-de-la-vinuela-en-julio-y-agosto/)
  - Catalonia internal basins 76.7%, no restrictions planned. [Diari de Catalunya](https://diaricatalunya.cat/en/baix-camp/general/catalan-reservoirs-767-capacity-at-summers-end)
  - Ebro lowest at 47.9%. [tutiempo](https://www.tutiempo.net/noticias/reserva-hidrica-espana-estado-embalses-22-septiembre-2026.html)
  - ⚠️ A May 2026 article (moncloa.com) claims Catalonia was in drought emergency again. It conflicts with official ACA data. *I think it is not reliable.* [moncloa.com](https://www.moncloa.com/2026/05/21/embalses-cataluna-hoy-sau-foix-susqueda-3378648/)
- **For us:** Good years in the south hide a long-term problem. Wet years come and go (Axarquía went from 8% to 85% in 3 years). North = passes our water deal-breaker. South = ⚠️ only with own springs / acequia rights, and still a risk. *(my reading)*

### Wildfire
- **2025 = worst season this century:** ~403,000 ha burned by early September. 4 deaths, 31,000 people evacuated. [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2025/08/21/spains-sees-worst-wildfire-season-since-1994-with-382000-hectares-burned-so-far-in-2025)
  - **Ourense (Galicia):** Larouco / A Rúa fire 44,424 ha; 12 big fires at the same time. [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2025/08/21/spains-sees-worst-wildfire-season-since-1994-with-382000-hectares-burned-so-far-in-2025)
  - **León / Zamora:** Benuza 32,596 ha, Uña de Quintana 40,781 ha. Old chestnut trees and brown-bear habitat burned. [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2025/08/21/spains-sees-worst-wildfire-season-since-1994-with-382000-hectares-burned-so-far-in-2025), [Ara](https://en.ara.cat/society/more-than-200-000-hectares-burned-in-spain-in-the-last-four-days_1_5473520.html)
  - **Cáceres (Extremadura):** Jarilla fire ~16,000 ha in Jerte + Ambroz; Casares de las Hurdes fire. [Junta Extremadura](https://www.juntaex.es/w/el-plan-infoex-da-por-extinguido-el-incendio-de-jarilla?inheritRedirect=true)
- **Earlier:** Sierra de Gata/Hurdes May 2023 (10,864 ha); Sierra Bermeja / Genal Sep 2021 (~8,400–10,000 ha). [Junta Extremadura](https://www.juntaex.es/w/superficie-incendio-hurdes-gata), [Olive Press](https://www.theolivepress.es/spain-news/2022/09/08/sierra-bermeja-re-emerges-one-year-after-worst-fire-in-spain-in-2021/)
- **Long-term:** Galicia had 34% of Spain's fires and ~46% of deliberately burned area (1983–2021). Castilla y León >15%. [Civio](https://civio.es/en/environment/forest-fires-map/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wildfires_in_Galicia)
- **Lower risk:** *Basque Country, Cantabria, coastal Asturias, green farmed valleys near villages (guess).*
- Maps: [Civio fire map](https://civio.es/en/environment/forest-fires-map/), [Copernicus ESOTC 2025](https://climate.copernicus.eu/esotc/2025/wildfires). *Check each plot's burn history before buying.*
