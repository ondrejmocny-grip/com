- Both have old "alternative towns" from the 1960s–90s: Totnes, Glastonbury, Frome, Stroud (England); Machynlleth, Lampeter (Wales); West Cork, East/North Clare, Leitrim (Ireland).

## Ireland (EU)

- **Residency for us:** ✅ EU family route (Directive 2004/38). Ondrej lives in Ireland as worker / self-employed / with enough money. Wife applies **in Ireland** on form **EUTR1** → **Stamp 4 EUFAM** card (live + work). Decision within 6 months. ⚠️ Ireland is **not in Schengen**: she needs an Irish visa to enter (*free/fast for EU spouse, guess*). [Irish Immigration](https://www.irishimmigration.ie/coming-to-join-family-in-ireland/joining-an-eea-or-swiss-national/eu-treaty-rights/), [Wikipedia Stamp 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stamp_4)
- **Home schooling:** ✅ legal. Constitution Art. 42 (family = primary educator). Must **register with Tusla** (Section 14, Education (Welfare) Act 2000), ages 6–16; an assessor checks "a certain minimum education". [gov.ie](https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-education/policy-information/home-schooling-or-attendance-at-non-recognised-schools/), [HEN Ireland](https://henireland.org/what-is-home-education/legalities/)
- **Van / yurt on rural land:** ❌ Living in a caravan or mobile home on land needs **planning permission**. Only storage (1 caravan, max 9 months/year, not lived in) is exempt. [Irish Examiner](https://www.irishexaminer.com/property/homeandgardens/arid-40975916.html), [Irish Times](https://www.irishtimes.com/life-and-style/homes-and-property/if-i-buy-a-plot-of-land-do-i-need-planning-permission-for-a-caravan-1.3507296)
- **Land price (2025):** national good land ~€14,126/acre, poor ~€6,963/acre. Cork good ~€17,250; Clare good up to ~€11,333; Leitrim poor ~€3,772, good ~€8,167. (1 ha = 2.47 acres.) [Farmers Journal / SCSI-Teagasc 2026](https://www.farmersjournal.ie/news/news/average-price-for-good-agricultural-land-up-7-in-2025-913310)
- **Language:** English (Irish Gaelic in some areas).

## United Kingdom (NOT EU since Brexit)

- **Residency for us:** ⛔ **This is likely a blocker.**
  - Free movement ended 31 Dec 2020. EU citizens who came later need a visa like everyone else. [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/the-uks-points-based-immigration-system-information-for-eu-citizens)
  - Main route: **Skilled Worker visa** = a **UK employer** must sponsor Ondrej. Since July 2025 only degree-level jobs (RQF 6); from Jan 2026 English B2. The wife could come as his dependant *(usual rule, guess)*. [Commons Library](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10267/), [DavidsonMorris](https://www.davidsonmorris.com/can-eu-citizens-work-in-uk/)
  - **No digital nomad / remote-work visa.** A remote job for a foreign company does not give residence. Global Talent / Innovator routes exist but are hard.
  - Visitor: up to **6 months** per visit; since 31 Jan 2024 **incidental** remote work for a foreign employer is allowed, but not as the main purpose. [Fragomen](https://www.fragomen.com/insights/2024-immigration-policy-changes-uk-visitors.html)
  - Entry: Ondrej needs an **ETA** (£20). *Wife (Turkish) needs a UK visitor visa (guess — check GOV.UK tool).* [GOV.UK ETA](https://www.gov.uk/eta)
- **Home schooling:** ✅ legal in England and Wales ("school or otherwise"). New **Children's Wellbeing and Schools Act 2026** (Royal Assent 29 Apr 2026) adds a council register + home visits, *in force earliest 2027*. [UK Parliament](https://www.parliament.uk/business/news/2026/march-2026/childrens-wellbeing-and-schools-bill-lords-consideration-of-commons-amendments/), [Home Ed Daily](https://www.thehomeeddaily.co.uk/Childrens-Wellbeing-and-Schools-Act-2026-what-happens-now), [HSLDA Wales](https://hslda.org/post/wales)
- **Van / yurt on rural land:** ⚠️ needs planning permission *(guess for England)*. **Wales:** One Planet Development lets you live on a low-impact smallholding in open countryside if strict tests are met; 68 such holdings (Jan 2025). [One Planet Council](https://www.oneplanetcouncil.org.uk/approved-applications/), [gov.wales](https://www.gov.wales/planning-permission-one-planet-developments-open-countryside)
- **Water:** ⚠️ South West England: drought declared and hosepipe ban in Devon/Cornwall from 8 August (*2026 per search result, verify*). [South West Water](https://www.southwestwater.co.uk/about-us/latest-news/south-west-water-announces-temporary-restrictions-for-hosepipe-use-across-the-region)
- **Land price:** ❌ high. Totnes average sold price ~£427k. [Rightmove](https://www.rightmove.co.uk/house-prices/totnes.html). *Wales cheaper (guess).*
- **Language:** English; Welsh in Wales (strong in Machynlleth area).
