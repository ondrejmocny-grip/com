## Claude's first view

- **Best cluster:** the **Centre** (Benfeita, Penela/Lousã, Góis). Many families, learning communities, good water, cheap land. But this is **exactly the highest fire zone**. Each plot must be checked on the ICNF fire map.
- **South** (Monchique + Aljezur hub): best mix of sea, mountains and mild winter, but drought risk.
- **North** (Minho): safest for water and fire, but you would need to build the community yourselves.

## Proposed first scouting route (by van, from Czechia)

About **6–8 weeks**, north → south. Autumn and winter are outside the fire season.

| Stop | Area | Days | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Alto Minho (Arcos de Valdevez / Ponte de Lima) | 5–7 | Water + fire safe option. Visit Na Fonte (yurt rental) |
| 2 | Penela / Lousã | 7 | Learning communities, meet families |
| 3 | Benfeita / Arganil + Góis (Loural) | 10 | Top lead. See the fire damage honestly. Check Loural |
| 4 | *(optional)* Seia | 3 | Volunteering option |
| 5 | Sintra | 4–5 | Schools, Terra Alta. Compare price vs. quality |
| 6 | Monchique + Aljezur | 10–14 | Southern option, winter base |

Tip: volunteer (Workaway / WWOOF) for 1–2 weeks at one place in stops 3 and 6. That gives real contact, not just a tourist view.
