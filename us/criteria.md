# How We Score a Place

> Status: **v4 — confirmed** (2026-09-27). Weights are a first guess by Claude. Change them together.

## Deal-breakers (any one = drop)

- Closed intentional community, or strong guru structure.
- Show-off / performative spiritual scene.
- No real contact with local people.
- Drought, dryness or water shortage.
- No mountains in view or close by.
- Very small, dying village with no services.

## Flags (not deal-breakers, always note them)

- ⚠️ Home schooling legal status (Spain/Canaries = grey zone, Greece/Turkey = illegal). See [legal overview](../regions/legal-overview.md).

- ⚠️ Wildfire risk. (Confirmed as flag, not deal-breaker.)
- ⚠️ Strong expat bubble (few locals).
- ⚠️ Mass tourism nearby.

## Must-haves

- Spiritually inclined people who are also grounded.
- Mix of international and local newcomers who **build projects**.
- Kind, authentic locals.
- Reliable water.
- Legal way to stay with a van at the start.
- Other young families with kids nearby.
- Like-minded neighbors nearby.
- Sea and mountains both within reach.
