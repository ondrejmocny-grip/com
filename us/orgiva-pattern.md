# The "Órgiva Pattern"

> Added 2026-09-27. Ondrej liked the concept of Órgiva. This file says what the concept is, so we can look for more places like it.
> See [`../places/es-orgiva-alpujarra.md`](../places/es-orgiva-alpujarra.md).

## What makes Órgiva special

1. **A real, normal market town** (a few thousand people). Local life goes on: shops, health center, schools, festivals.
2. **Many newcomers settled around it** over decades. International **and** national ("neorrurales").
3. **Spread out, not one community.** People live on their own fincas and in hamlets 5–40 min around the town.
4. **Many small projects:** eco farms, retreats, yoga, healing, workshops, cafés, crafts.
5. **A weekly market** where everyone meets (locals and newcomers).
6. **Alternative schools and many young families.**
7. **Mountains and sea** close.
8. **Easy start:** volunteering, renting, parking a van on a friend's land.

## Watch out (Órgiva's weak points)

- 💧 Water (snow-fed, dry years).
- Hippie / expat bubble.
- Hot summers.

## Search rule for "more places like Órgiva"

A place counts if it has **at least 6 of the 8 points above**, and none of our deal-breakers (see [`criteria.md`](criteria.md)).
